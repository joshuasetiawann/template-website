Our story

About PawSteps Dog Training

Calm dogs, confident owners, kinder methods — and the people, place and principles that make it happen every day.

How it began

Started for the love of it

PawSteps Dog Training grew from a simple belief: Greenfield Park deserved dog training that puts the animal first and the clock second. What began as one person and a lot of passion is now a trusted local fixture.

We've grown carefully — never faster than our standards allow — so that every pet still gets the unhurried, individual attention we promised on day one.

Our mission

To make excellent dog training feel calm, clear and genuinely kind — for pets and the people who love them.

Our promise

  • Honest advice, every time
  • Transparent, upfront pricing
  • A familiar, friendly face
