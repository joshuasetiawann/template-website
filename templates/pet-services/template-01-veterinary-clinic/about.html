Our story

About Maplewood Veterinary

Trusted neighbourhood animal care since 2009 — and the people, place and principles that make it happen every day.

How it began

Started for the love of it

Maplewood Veterinary grew from a simple belief: Maplewood District deserved veterinary clinic that puts the animal first and the clock second. What began as one person and a lot of passion is now a trusted local fixture.

We've grown carefully — never faster than our standards allow — so that every pet still gets the unhurried, individual attention we promised on day one.

Our mission

To make excellent veterinary clinic feel calm, clear and genuinely kind — for pets and the people who love them.

Our promise

  • Honest advice, every time
  • Transparent, upfront pricing
  • A familiar, friendly face
