Our story

About TrailTails Dog Walking

Daily adventures on four paws, rain or shine — and the people, place and principles that make it happen every day.

How it began

Started for the love of it

TrailTails Dog Walking grew from a simple belief: Riverbend deserved dog walking that puts the animal first and the clock second. What began as one person and a lot of passion is now a trusted local fixture.

We've grown carefully — never faster than our standards allow — so that every pet still gets the unhurried, individual attention we promised on day one.

Our mission

To make excellent dog walking feel calm, clear and genuinely kind — for pets and the people who love them.

Our promise

  • Honest advice, every time
  • Transparent, upfront pricing
  • A familiar, friendly face
