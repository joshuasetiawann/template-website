Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Arrive any time after 1:00 pm — just tell us your train; check-out is by 11:00 am, and we'll hold your bags.

Cancellation

Free until 5 days before arrival; in-season festival weeks we ask for 14 days' notice.

Meals

A home breakfast is included every day; lunches and dinners with the family can be added for a small charge.

Our home

This is our family home, so we keep quiet hours after ten; the kitchen and courtyard are yours to share.
