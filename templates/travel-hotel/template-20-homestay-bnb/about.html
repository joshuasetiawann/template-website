Our story

Stay like family, not like a guest

Marigold House is our family home, and has been for three generations. We opened four of its rooms to guests not as a business plan but because the house was made for people — for long breakfasts, full tables and the door always on the latch. You arrive a guest and leave, we hope, rather more than that.

The heart of the house is the courtyard, and the heart of the courtyard is Amma's kitchen. Breakfast is whatever the morning and the market suggest, cooked fresh and served under the terracotta arches until everyone has eaten too much. There is no menu and no buffet; there is simply what is good today, and plenty of it.

The four rooms are homely rather than hotel-like, each one part of the old house: thick walls that keep the heat out, shuttered windows onto the courtyard, hand-block quilts and a marigold left at every door. They are comfortable and clean and entirely without pretence — the rooms of a family home, kept for friends.

What we really offer is our town. We will walk you to the temple at the right hour, send you to the cousin who makes the best sweets, sit you down with chai when the afternoon is too hot to move, and tell you which festival is worth changing your plans for. Stay with us and you stay with locals who are glad you came.

Heritage

An old house, a living home

Marigold House is a traditional terracotta home, and we keep it the old way — lime-washed walls that breathe, shuttered windows instead of air-conditioning where we can, and repairs made with local craft and materials. It is a living family house, not a museum, and looking after it is simply part of family life.
