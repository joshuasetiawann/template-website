Our story

A century on the old quay

The Meridian Crest opened its doors in 1924, when the harbour was a forest of masts and the quay still smelled of tar and salt. The founding family wanted a hotel that felt less like a hotel and more like the grandest private house on the waterfront — one where the porter knew your name before your trunk reached the lift.

A hundred years later, that intention survives every renovation. The marble in the lobby was quarried a full century before the first electric lift was fitted, and we have never seen a reason to replace it. The brass is polished by hand each morning; the lift gates still close with the same satisfying clatter they made when the building was new.

What has changed is everything you cannot see. Behind the period detail runs a quietly modern hotel: climate control in every room, soundproofing that erases the harbour, and a service team trained to anticipate rather than react. The result is a building that wears its age with grace and none of the inconvenience.

We are still family-run, still ninety-six rooms, and still convinced that luxury is mostly a matter of attention. A butler who remembers your anniversary, a fire lit before you ask, a car waiting at the arch — these are not extras here. They are simply how the Crest has always kept its guests.

Heritage

Keeping a landmark alive

The Crest is a listed building, and we treat that responsibility seriously. Restoration work uses the original lime plaster, the same quarry for replacement marble, and craftspeople who still cut brass by hand. Where modern comfort and historic fabric disagree, we hide the modern and keep the fabric.
