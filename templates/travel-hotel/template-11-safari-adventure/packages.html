Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Arrival / departure

Fly-in to the conservancy airstrip; your first and last game drives are the transfers to and from camp.

Cancellation

Full refund to 75 days before arrival, less deposit; staged thereafter — safari insurance is required.

Child policy

Children six and over are welcome on the family safari; walking trails are sixteen and over.

Health & malaria

The reserve is in a malaria area; we send a health briefing and recommend prophylaxis before travel.
