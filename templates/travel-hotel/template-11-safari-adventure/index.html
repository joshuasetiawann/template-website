How it works

The rhythm of a safari day

Two game drives, a slow middle and the fire at the end.

05:30 · Wake-up callCoffee and rusks in the dark, then out before the predators rest.
Morning · Game driveTracking with your guide and tracker across the waking plains.
Midday · Camp & siestaBrunch, a plunge pool and the heat of the day under canvas.
Sundowners · & bomaA drink as the sun drops, dinner around the fire under the stars.

The safari difference

The details that turn a safari into the trip you remember.

Private guide & tracker

A naturalist and a tracker dedicated to your vehicle.

Open game vehicles

Three guests a row, every seat a window on the wild.

Lantern-lit camps

Tented suites under canvas, no fences, no light pollution.

Bush dinners & boma

Fireside feasts and stories beneath the southern stars.

Armed walking rangers

Trails on foot, safely led by armed, trained guides.

A private conservancy

No crowds, off-road tracking and night drives permitted.
