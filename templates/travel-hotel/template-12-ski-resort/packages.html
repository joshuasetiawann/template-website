Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Check-in from 4:00 pm on changeover day, check-out by 10:00 am; ski storage available either side.

Cancellation

Free until 30 days before arrival; peak and holiday weeks require 60 days' notice.

Lift passes

Discounted area passes are arranged with your stay and collected from the base station.

Snow guarantee

If the home lifts cannot open, we rebook or refund the affected nights — the glacier rarely closes.
