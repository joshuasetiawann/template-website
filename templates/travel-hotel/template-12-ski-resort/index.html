Getting there

Getting to the snow

Two hours by road from the nearest international airport, with a winter rail link to the village.

By road

Resort transfers run from the airport; the village is car-free at its heart.

By rail

A scenic mountain railway connects to the village base station.

On arrival

Ski-check your gear at the base and ride the gondola to your lodge.
