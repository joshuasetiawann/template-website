Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Check-in from 3:00 pm; check-out by 11:00 am. Arrive early for tea and the gardens in the meantime.

Cancellation

Free until 7 days before arrival; festive and garden-festival weekends require 21 days' notice.

Listed building

The manor is a protected building with original stairs and some low beams; lift access is limited.

Children & dogs

Well-behaved children are welcome; country dogs may stay in the Gardener's and Steward's rooms by arrangement.
