A restored country manor · House since 1681

Three centuries of welcome, gently kept.

Getting there

Finding the manor

At the end of a long lime avenue, in forty acres of garden and parkland off the village road.

By car

A long lime avenue leads to the house; ample parking sits behind the stables.

By train

The county station is a fifteen-minute taxi away; we'll arrange the car.

On foot

Footpaths from the parkland lead to the village inn and the old church.
