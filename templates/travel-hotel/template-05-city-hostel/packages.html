Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Reception is 24 hours; check-in from 2:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am. Bags held free either side.

Cancellation

Free until 24 hours before arrival; festival weekends are non-refundable.

Age policy

Dorms are for ages 18–39; families and all ages are welcome in private rooms.

Payment

Pay on arrival by card or cash; a small deposit holds festival beds.
