Getting there

By the old tram depot

Two tram stops from the old town and a five-minute walk from the night-train station.

By tram

Lines 4 and 11 stop outside; the old town is two stops away.

On foot

The night-train station is a five-minute walk with the lockers ready.

By bike

Free hostel bikes and a city-share dock at the corner.
