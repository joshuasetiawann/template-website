The Boot Room Bunk

1 guest per bunk · 6-bed room

A single warm bunk in the shared room beside the drying racks and the boot store. The cheapest, most social way to stay — pack light, hang your gear to dry overnight and roll out to the trail at first light.

  • Bedding & towel
  • Communal breakfast
  • Trail route briefing

Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Check-in 3:00–7:00 pm at the lodge; check-out by 10:00 am so cabins can be readied.

Cancellation

Free until 14 days before arrival; winter weeks require 30 days' notice.

Access

The final road is unsealed; use the shuttle and never the rental's road-tyres in winter.

Dogs

Mountain dogs are welcome in the cabins by arrangement — the mudroom expects them.
