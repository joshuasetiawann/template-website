Getting there

On the house reef

A short transfer from town to the dive jetty; we collect divers from the harbour on request.

From the jetty

All boat dives leave from our own jetty, steps from the kit room.

Getting here

The dive village is a short transfer from the island airport; we can arrange it.

House reef

Certified divers can shore-dive the house reef any time the flag is up.

Times & policies

The practical details worth checking before you arrive.

Check-in / startBoat 8:00 am
Check-out / endBoat 1:30 pm

Dive days

Two-dive morning trips leave at 8:00 am and return by early afternoon.

Certification

Bring your card and logbook; a check dive may be asked for after a long break.

Cancellation

Free until 48 hours before; weather cancellations are always refunded.

What's included

Tanks, weights, guide and the boat; full kit hire is available.
