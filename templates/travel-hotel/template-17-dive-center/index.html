How it works

The shape of a dive day

Two morning dives, a surface interval and the reef after dark.

07:30 · Kit & briefCheck in at the kit room and brief the morning's two sites.
Morning · Two-tank diveOut by boat to the wall and the coral garden, small group.
Afternoon · House reefShore-dive the jetty reef at your own pace, or rest and refill.
After dark · Night diveTorch-lit corals, octopus and bioluminescence on the reef.

Why dive with us

The details that turn a dive into the trip you remember.

Daily dive boats

Two fast boats reach forty sites, small groups, twice daily.

Five-star instruction

Courses from first try-dive to divemaster, safety first.

House reef

Shore-dive a living reef straight off the centre's jetty.

Full equipment

Serviced rental gear, nitrox fills and a kit room on site.

Marine guides

Dive guides who know every wall, swim-through and turtle.

Warm-water diving

Comfortable temperatures and good visibility all year.
