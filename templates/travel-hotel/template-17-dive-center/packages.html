Discover Scuba (Try-Dive)

Per person · age 10+Half-day

Your very first breath underwater, with no experience needed: a pool-style briefing in the shallows, then a guided dive in the sheltered bay alongside an instructor, one-on-one or in a tiny group. The start of everything.

Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Booking & check-in

Reserve dives and courses online; check in at the kit room thirty minutes before each boat departure.

Medical & certification

A medical questionnaire applies to all divers; bring proof of certification for guided fun dives.

Cancellation

Free until 48 hours before a dive or course start; weather cancellations are fully refunded or rebooked.

Safety & no-fly

We dive within conservative limits and require an 18–24 hour surface interval before flying.
