Our story

The ocean, at a civilised scale

Aurelia Ocean Voyages was founded on a simple objection: the modern cruise ship had become a floating city, and something had been lost in the scaling-up. So we built small. Our ships carry a few hundred guests, not a few thousand, and the whole experience is cut to that more human measure.

Small ships go where large ones cannot. We slip into shallow harbours and island anchorages the megaships sail straight past, tie up in the heart of old ports rather than at an industrial terminal miles away, and change the itinerary when the weather offers something better. The sea, not the schedule, sets the pace.

On board, the scale changes everything. There are no queues, no crowds at the rail, no scramble for a lounger. The crew learn your name by the second night, the dining room never needs a second sitting, and the open decks are wide enough that you can always find a quiet stretch of horizon to yourself.

We are a line for travellers rather than passengers — people who care more about the port than the water-slide, who want a lecturer on the bridge wing and a sommelier at dinner. Boutique, at sea, simply means the ocean given back its sense of occasion.

Sustainability

A lighter way to sail

Small ships carry a smaller footprint. Ours run cleaner fuel, plug into shore power where ports provide it, and land guests in numbers the harbours can absorb without strain. We work with each port on waste and water, because the coastlines we sail to are precisely the reason anyone books the voyage.
