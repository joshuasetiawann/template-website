Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Booking & start

A deposit secures your place; trips begin with a briefing dinner on day one at basecamp.

Cancellation

Full refund to 60 days before departure, less deposit; staged thereafter — travel insurance required.

Group size

Every departure is capped at twelve travellers and runs with a minimum of four.

Fitness & age

Grades run easy to moderate; minimum age is twelve, and we advise on fitness per trip.
