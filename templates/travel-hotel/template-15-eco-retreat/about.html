Our story

Off the grid, deep in the woods

Mosswood Eco Retreat sits at the end of a track in a fold of ancient woodland, far enough from the road that the loudest thing you will hear is the stream. We bought the wood to protect it, then built lightly within it — a handful of timber cabins that touch the ground gently and leave the trees exactly where they stand.

Off-grid is the whole point, not a hardship. The cabins run on the sun and a battery wall, the showers are heated rainwater, the stoves burn fallen timber from the wood itself, and the composting loos keep the ground clean. You give up nothing that matters and lose a great deal that you will not miss — starting with the road noise.

We manage the woodland as carefully as the cabins. Most of the estate is left wholly wild; we coppice a little, plant natives, and keep the night sky dark so the owls, bats and badgers carry on as they always have. Guests are part of the place for a few days, not masters of it.

What people take away is the quiet and the dark. No traffic, no light pollution, no notifications — just wood-smoke, birdsong, a stove ticking in the evening and more stars than most people have seen in years. It is, we are told, the best night's sleep our guests have had in a long time.

Sustainability

Off-grid, and proud of it

Everything at Mosswood is designed to leave the wood as it found it. Solar and battery power run the cabins, rainwater is harvested and heated, composting loos return nutrients to the ground, and the great majority of the estate is left entirely wild. We bought this woodland to protect it; the cabins simply let a few people share it.
