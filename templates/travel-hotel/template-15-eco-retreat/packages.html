Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Arrive 3:00–6:00 pm at the forest gate; leave by 11:00 am. We walk you in and out by lantern at dusk.

Cancellation

Free until 21 days before arrival; the treehouse and weekends require 35 days' notice.

Off-grid living

Cabins run on solar and rainwater; we ask guests to tread lightly and keep to the marked paths.

No-screens ethos

Wifi reaches the lounge only, by design — cabins are deliberately a place to unplug.
