Getting there

At the end of the track

Ten minutes from the nearest village down a woodland track; park at the gate and we will walk you in.

By car

Park at the forest gate; we'll walk you in to your cabin by lantern if it's dusk.

By train & lift

Car-free guests take the branch-line train; we collect from the station by arrangement.

By bike

A waymarked cycle route follows the old railway right to the gate.

Check-in / out

Check-in from 3:30 pm, check-out by 10:30 am. Arrive in daylight; the track is unlit.

Cancellation

Free until 14 days before arrival; we never charge for a closure of our own.

Off-grid living

Solar power, rainwater showers and composting loos — we will show you the ropes.

Dogs

One well-behaved dog is welcome in each cabin and on every trail.
