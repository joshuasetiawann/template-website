Getting there

Finding the forest

A rewilding forest at the end of a green lane, deliberately a little hard to reach.

By car

Park at the forest gate; we'll walk you in to your cabin by lantern if it's dusk.

By train & lift

Car-free guests take the branch-line train; we collect from the station by arrangement.

By bike

A waymarked cycle route follows the old railway right to the gate.
