Our story

Trips built around you, not a brochure

Wayfare & Co. is a small studio of travel designers, not a booking website with a phone number. We do not sell off-the-shelf packages; we sit down with you, work out what a good trip actually means for the way you travel, and then build it from scratch, piece by piece.

Tailor-made is the only thing we do. Your pace, your budget, the hotels you would actually like, the day with nothing planned because you will want it — all of it shaped around you rather than squeezed into a template. We have walked most of the ground ourselves, and we book nothing we would not send our own family to.

The value is in the details and the relationships. We know which room to ask for, which guide is worth the money, which connection is too tight to risk. Years of doing this has built a little black book of fixers, hosts and drivers in dozens of countries — and that network is quietly working in the background of every trip we plan.

And we do not vanish once you have paid. A real person is on call throughout your trip, in your time zone when it matters, ready to rebook the flight, move the reservation or simply tell you where to eat tonight. Tailor-made travel should feel looked-after from the first call to the flight home.
