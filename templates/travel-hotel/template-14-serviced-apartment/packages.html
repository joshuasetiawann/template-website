Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Self check-in from 3:00 pm with a digital key; check-out by 11:00 am. The desk is staffed daily.

Minimum stay

A three-night minimum applies; weekly and monthly rates unlock at seven and twenty-eight nights.

Housekeeping

A full clean and fresh linen are included weekly; extra cleans can be added at any time.

Cancellation

Free until 7 days before arrival on flexible rates; monthly stays follow the terms in your agreement.
