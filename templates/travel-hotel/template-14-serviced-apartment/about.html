Our story

A home for a week or a season

The Lanyard Residences was built for a kind of guest the ordinary hotel serves badly: the one who is staying for weeks rather than nights. The relocating family, the consultant on a long contract, the couple between homes — people who need somewhere to live, not just somewhere to sleep, and who are tired of eating every meal out.

So we built apartments, not rooms. Every residence has a proper kitchen with a full-size fridge and a real cooker, a separate living room to work or unwind in, a washer-dryer of its own, and storage enough to actually unpack. You can cook a Sunday roast, host a colleague for dinner, and live at your own pace.

Around the apartments runs the service of a hotel. There is a staffed front desk, weekly housekeeping, parcel handling for everything you order in, and fast wired internet for the days you work from the sofa. The independence of a flat with the back-up of a hotel, in other words — and none of the loneliness of either.

Because we are built for the long stay, the longer you stay the less you pay. Weekly and monthly rates fall well below the nightly price, bills are bundled into one simple figure, and there are no surprises on the folio. It is, quite deliberately, the sensible way to be away from home for a while.

Sustainability

Built for the long haul

Longer stays let us run greener than a nightly hotel. Linen is changed weekly rather than daily, energy-efficient appliances fill every kitchen, and residents recycle properly because they are genuinely living here. Bundling the bills also lets us manage the building's energy as a whole, not room by room.
