Our story

Keys, a kettle and the open road

Rambler Vans started in a draughty barn with one old van, a jigsaw and a stubborn belief that a campervan should be simple enough to drive away without a manual. A dozen conversions later we still build them all ourselves, by hand, the way we would want them for our own trips.

Every van is ready to go the moment you collect it. The bed is made up, the fridge is cold, the water tank is full, and the kit you actually need — stove, pans, chairs, table, bedding — is already packed in its place. We walk you round it once, hand you the keys, and the rest of the trip is yours.

We keep the fleet small and the vans honest. Each one is mechanically checked between every hire, easy to drive for first-timers, and compact enough for the narrow lanes where the best spots hide. No sprawling motorhomes, no baffling gadgets — just a tough, tidy van that does exactly what a campervan should.

And we send you off knowing where to go. Every hire comes with our hand-drawn map of favourite places to park up — the quiet pitches, the wild spots that welcome vans, the farm with the honesty-box eggs and the beach you can wake up beside. The road is yours; we just give you a running start.

Sustainability

Travel that treads lightly

We build vans to last and to leave no trace.
