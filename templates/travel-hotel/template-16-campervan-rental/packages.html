Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Pick-up / drop-off

Collect from 1:00 pm, return by 11:00 am; a 30-minute handover walks you through the van on day one.

Minimum hire

A three-day minimum applies (five days on the Homestead); peak weeks may carry a Saturday-to-Saturday rule.

Driver & deposit

Drivers must be 23+ with two years' licence; a refundable security deposit is held against the van.

Cancellation

Free until 28 days before pick-up; inside that, the deposit is retained against the booking.
