Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Check-in from 2:00 pm after the boat transfer; check-out by 11:00 am to meet the launch.

Cancellation

Free until 30 days before arrival in green season, 45 days in high season.

Transfers

Boat transfers are arranged with your booking and billed per person, return.

Children

Welcome resort-wide; the reef rangers club runs daily in school holidays.
