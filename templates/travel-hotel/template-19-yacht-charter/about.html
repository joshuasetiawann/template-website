Our story

Your own yacht, your own horizon

Meridian Yacht Charters began with a single sloop and a love of these islands seen from the water. Years and many seasons later we run a fleet of sailing and motor yachts from the old harbour, but the idea has not changed: the best way to see this coast is from its own deck, anchored each night in a different bay.

We charter both ways. Take a yacht bareboat if you hold the tickets and want the freedom of your own command, or take her crewed — with a skipper who knows every anchorage and a cook who provisions from the island markets — if you would rather simply step aboard and enjoy it. Either way the yacht, and the horizon, are yours.

The fleet is chosen and kept to a high standard. Every yacht is maintained in our own yard between charters, rigged and serviced by people who sail them, and handed over spotless with full safety gear and a thorough briefing. We charter nothing we would not happily take our own families out on.

And we know this water intimately. Our skippers can take you to the anchorages the day-boats never reach, the tavernas you can only arrive at by sea, and the quiet bays to yourself for a swim before breakfast. Whether crewed or bareboat, you sail with decades of local knowledge tucked into the chart table.

Sustainability

Sailing the coast gently

We charter in a way the coastline can sustain. Our yachts use mooring buoys and holding tanks rather than anchoring on seagrass or pumping in the bays, the crew brief responsible anchoring on every bareboat handover, and we support the local harbours and tavernas we send guests to. The sea is the whole point; we mean to keep it clean.
