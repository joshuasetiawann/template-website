Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Embarkation

Board from 5:00 pm on the Saturday; the yacht is yours after handover, with departure usually the next morning.

Qualifications

Bareboat charters require a recognised skipper certificate and a sailing résumé; skippered and crewed do not.

Deposit & cancellation

A deposit confirms the charter; a refundable security deposit applies to bareboats. Cancellation is staged by date.

Provisioning & fuel

Provisioning, fuel, harbour fees and the end clean are billed at cost; crewed yachts include a daily cook.
