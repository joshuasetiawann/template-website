Our story

A whole villa, all to yourselves

Casa Terramar is one house, not a hotel, and it is yours for the length of your stay. It was built by an architect for her own family on a shoulder of cliff above the sea, and it still feels like a home that has simply been left open for you — books on the shelves, salt in the air, the pool warm by mid-morning.

Privacy is the whole proposition. There is no lobby, no neighbours through the wall, no timetable but your own. The infinity pool runs to the cliff edge, the terraces face nothing but water, and the only sound at night is the sea working the rocks below the garden.

We look after the house so you do not have to. A housekeeper comes daily but invisibly, a cook will come in for the evenings you would rather not, and a manager is a phone call away for the boat, the table booking or the case of wine — present when you want them, absent when you do not.

It suits the occasions that deserve a house of their own: a family summer, a milestone, a group of friends who would rather share a table than a corridor. You arrive, the gate closes behind you, and for a week the cliff and the sea belong to you alone.

Heritage

An architect's house, kept as built

Casa Terramar was designed as a single, considered piece — the way the light moves, the line of the pool, the frame each window makes of the sea. We maintain it exactly in that spirit, repairing with the original stone and lime and changing nothing that the architect intended to last.
