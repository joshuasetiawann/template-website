Good to know

Policies & the practical bits

The terms worth reading before you book, in plain language.

Check-in / out

Arrive 3:00–6:00 pm at the welcome barn; leave by 11:00 am. We barrow your bags to the pitch.

Cancellation

Free until 21 days before arrival; peak summer weekends require 35 days' notice.

Campfires

A fire ring comes with every stay; logs are delivered and we ask fires stay within the ring.

Dogs

Two dogs per pitch are welcome on the bell-tent and cabin stays; the meadow lodges are dog-free.
