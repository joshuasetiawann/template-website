Getting there

Down the valley lane

Fifteen minutes from the nearest village down a single farm track; park by the meadow gate.

By car

Park at the welcome barn; we ferry your bags to the pitch by trailer.

On foot

The village pub and shop are a twenty-minute walk along the brook.

Getting here

Detailed directions follow your booking — sat-nav stops short of the gate.

Cancellation

Free until 14 days before arrival; we never charge for a washout we close for.

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the cabins and on every trail.
