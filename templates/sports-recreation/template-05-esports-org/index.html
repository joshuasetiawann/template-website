About Nullpoint Esports

Aim higher. Clutch harder.

Nullpoint Esports builds disciplined, data-driven competitors and welcoming communities across multiple titles and skill tiers.

Nullpoint Esports formed in 2017 when a group of ranked players decided that talent alone wasn't enough — that esports deserved real coaching, real structure and real care for the people behind the keyboards.
