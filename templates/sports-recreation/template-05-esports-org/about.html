Our story

How Nullpoint Esports came to be

Nullpoint Esports formed in 2017 when a group of ranked players decided that talent alone wasn't enough — that esports deserved real coaching, real structure and real care for the people behind the keyboards.

Today we field competitive rosters across several titles, run academy bootcamps for rising players, and operate a public arena packed with tournament-grade PCs, consoles and a streaming studio.

We've lifted regional trophies and sent players to national finals, but our deeper mission is healthier competition: sleep, ergonomics, mindset and teamwork coached as seriously as mechanics and macro.

Our mission

What we're here to do

To develop disciplined, healthy, high-performing competitors — and to give players of every rank a structured, welcoming path into competitive gaming.
