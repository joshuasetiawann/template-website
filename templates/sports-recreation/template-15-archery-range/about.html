Our story

How Truenock Archery came to be

Truenock Archery was established in 1996 by a small group of target archers who wanted a club that took both safety and welcome seriously. Three decades on, those twin values still define everything we do.

We shoot across an indoor range and a full outdoor field, catering for recurve, compound, longbow and barebow archers from their very first arrow through to county and national competition.

Archery is a sport of focus, patience and quiet satisfaction, and our Archery-GB-qualified coaches share it with hundreds of beginners each year — many of whom discover a calm, absorbing passion that lasts a lifetime.

28years on the field
Our mission

What we're here to do

To share the focus and joy of archery safely with everyone, coaching confident archers of every bowstyle within a calm, welcoming club.

Facilities

Where it all happens

20-boss indoor range

A floodlit indoor range shooting to 18m, open year-round whatever the weather.

Outdoor field

A full outdoor range with distances to 90m, plus a field-archery course in the woods.

Equipment & hire

Club bows, arrows and all safety equipment for hire, with expert fitting.

Clubhouse & lounge

A warm clubhouse, tea bar and the easy company of fellow archers.

Bow-tuning workshop

A dedicated workshop for tuning, repairs and building your own setup.

Competition & league

Recognised competition rounds, club leagues and Archery-GB classifications.
