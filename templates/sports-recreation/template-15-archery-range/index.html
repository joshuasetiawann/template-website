About Truenock Archery

Find your centre. Hit your mark.

Truenock Archery introduces newcomers to the bow and develops competitive archers, all in a calm, welcoming and safety-first club.

Truenock Archery was established in 1996 by a small group of target archers who wanted a club that took both safety and welcome seriously. Three decades on, those twin values still define everything we do.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

20-boss indoor range

A floodlit indoor range shooting to 18m, open year-round whatever the weather.

Outdoor field

A full outdoor range with distances to 90m, plus a field-archery course in the woods.

Equipment & hire

Club bows, arrows and all safety equipment for hire, with expert fitting.

Clubhouse & lounge

A warm clubhouse, tea bar and the easy company of fellow archers.

Bow-tuning workshop

A dedicated workshop for tuning, repairs and building your own setup.

Competition & league

Recognised competition rounds, club leagues and Archery-GB classifications.
