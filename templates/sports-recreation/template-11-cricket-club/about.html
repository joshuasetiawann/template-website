About Willowmere Cricket Club

Played the right way since 1921.

Willowmere Cricket Club has been the beating heart of village cricket for over a hundred years, run by members, for members.

Our story

How Willowmere Cricket Club came to be

Willowmere Cricket Club was founded in 1921 on the village meadow, and we've played beneath the same oaks ever since. A century on, the club remains entirely member-run, volunteer-powered and proud of its traditions.

We field six senior teams across the county leagues, an All Stars and Dynamos junior section, women's and girls' sides, and a flourishing colts academy with indoor nets and professional coaching.

Our timbered pavilion, square-leg boundary and legendary teas are part of the village's fabric. But what truly defines Willowmere is its welcome — to every age, every background and every level of cricketer.

Our mission

What we're here to do
