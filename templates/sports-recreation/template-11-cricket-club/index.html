About Willowmere Cricket Club

Played the right way since 1921.

Willowmere Cricket Club has been the beating heart of village cricket for over a hundred years, run by members, for members.

Willowmere Cricket Club was founded in 1921 on the village meadow, and we've played beneath the same oaks ever since. A century on, the club remains entirely member-run, volunteer-powered and proud of its traditions.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Championship square

A hand-rolled grass square and outfield maintained to league standard.

Indoor net centre

Four lanes with bowling machines for year-round batting and bowling practice.

Outdoor practice nets

Six all-weather lanes for pre-match warm-ups and academy sessions.

Heritage pavilion

Changing rooms, members' bar, scorers' box and the famous tea room.

Strength & fitness room

Conditioning and rehab space for senior and academy players.

Honours boards

A century of captains, centuries and silverware proudly displayed.
