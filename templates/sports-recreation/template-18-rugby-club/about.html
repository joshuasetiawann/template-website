About Granite Rugby Club

Hard as granite. Tight as family.

Granite Rugby Club forges tough, skilful players and a fierce, family club spirit — from first tag try to first-team glory.

Our story

How Granite Rugby Club came to be

Granite RFC was founded in 1924 by quarrymen who played as hard on Saturdays as they worked all week. A century later the quarry's gone, but the granite spirit — graft, grit and brotherhood — runs through everything we do.

We field senior men's and women's XVs across the leagues, a colts side, a full minis and juniors section from age four, and a walking-rugby group — all coached to play tough, smart, ball-in-hand rugby.

Our clubhouse is the beating heart of Harrowdale: the third half, the sing-songs, the legends retold over a pint. But ask any member what Granite means and they'll say the same thing — family that happens to play rugby.

100years of grit
Our mission

What we're here to do

To develop tough, skilful rugby players of every age and gender, and to be a fierce, welcoming club family where everyone belongs.

Facilities

Where it all happens

Two floodlit pitches

A main grass pitch with stand and a floodlit training pitch for all squads.

Strength & conditioning barn

A serious S&C facility with platforms, sleds and a contact area.

The Quarry Clubhouse

Members' bar, function room and the rowdiest third half in the league.

Physio & rehab suite

On-site physiotherapy, injury rehab and return-to-play screening.

Scrum & contact zone

Scrum machines, tackle bags and a padded contact-skills area.
