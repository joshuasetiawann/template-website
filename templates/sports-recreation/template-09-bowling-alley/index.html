About Strike Avenue Bowl

Good times, right up your alley.

Strike Avenue Bowl is where Maple Falls comes together — birthday parties, date nights, league rivalries and weekend family fun.

Strike Avenue opened its doors in 1998 with sixteen lanes and a milkshake bar, and quickly became the place Maple Falls gathered. Birthdays, first dates, work parties and league nights — the soundtrack of a community.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

24 glow-bowling lanes

Computer-scored synthetic lanes with bumpers, ramps and lightweight balls available.

Arcade & redemption

Classic and modern arcade games with a prize-redemption counter.

The Avenue Diner

Burgers, shakes, loaded fries and a full bar served right to your lane.

Private party rooms

Decorated rooms and packages for birthdays, work dos and celebrations.

Glow nights & laser

Cosmic bowling with music, lights and laser tag in the back arena.

Accessible lanes & ramps

Step-free lanes, ball ramps and assistance for bowlers of all abilities.
