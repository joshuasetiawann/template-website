Our story

How Strike Avenue Bowl came to be

Strike Avenue opened its doors in 1998 with sixteen lanes and a milkshake bar, and quickly became the place Maple Falls gathered. Birthdays, first dates, work parties and league nights — the soundtrack of a community.

We've grown to twenty-four computer-scored lanes with bumpers and ramps for the little ones, a roaring arcade, a classic American diner and private party rooms — but kept the friendly, family-first feel that started it all.

From toddlers' first gutter-busters to our fiercely contested Thursday-night leagues, Strike Avenue is built on one belief: that everyone deserves an easy, joyful night out, whatever their score.
