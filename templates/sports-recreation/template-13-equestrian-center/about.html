About Ashford Equestrian Centre

Where horse and rider grow together.

Ashford Equestrian Centre is a refined, BHS-approved yard where riders of every age and stage develop alongside beautifully cared-for horses.

Our story

How Ashford Equestrian Centre came to be

Ashford Equestrian Centre has occupied its corner of the vale since 1987, growing from a small riding school into one of the region's most respected BHS-approved centres — without ever losing its calm, horse-first ethos.

Set across forty acres, the centre offers indoor and floodlit outdoor arenas, a cross-country course, immaculate stabling and a string of schoolmaster horses and ponies suited to everyone from nervous beginners to competitive riders.

Whether you ride for the joy of it, compete at affiliated level or keep your own horse on full livery, Ashford pairs exceptional facilities with genuine expertise — and a deep, quiet respect for the welfare of every horse in our care.

Facilities

Where it all happens

Indoor & outdoor arenas

A 60×30m indoor school plus two floodlit outdoor surfaces for year-round riding.

Cross-country course

A schooling XC course with fences and water for eventing practice.

Stabling & full livery

Spacious American-barn stabling, turnout paddocks and full livery care.

Horse welfare & vet care

On-site vet visits, farrier days and a dedicated equine welfare team.

Clubhouse & viewing café

A warm clubhouse and café overlooking the indoor arena.

Tack shop & competition

On-site tack shop, lecture room and regular in-house competitions.
