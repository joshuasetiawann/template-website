About Oakhaven Links

Where the game is played as it should be.

Oakhaven Links pairs a celebrated championship course with welcoming coaching and a members' club proud of its traditions.

Oakhaven Links was laid out in 1962 across the rolling vale, its fairways framed by the ancient oaks that give the club its name. Designed in the classic links tradition, it has tested amateurs and tour professionals alike for over sixty years.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Championship 18

A par-72 links of 6,840 yards, immaculately presented across the seasons.

Floodlit driving range

Thirty covered, matted and grass bays open from dawn until late.

Short-game academy

Practice greens, bunkers and a par-3 course for honing the scoring game.

Members' clubhouse

Restaurant, bar and the famous terrace over the eighteenth green.

Pro shop & fitting

Full retail, custom club fitting and trolley & buggy hire.

Locker rooms & spikes bar

Changing facilities, lockers and a relaxed post-round spikes bar.
