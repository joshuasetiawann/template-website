About Oakhaven Links

Where the game is played as it should be.

Oakhaven Links pairs a celebrated championship course with welcoming coaching and a members' club proud of its traditions.

Our story

How Oakhaven Links came to be

Oakhaven Links was laid out in 1962 across the rolling vale, its fairways framed by the ancient oaks that give the club its name. Designed in the classic links tradition, it has tested amateurs and tour professionals alike for over sixty years.

Beyond the championship eighteen we offer a par-3 academy course, a floodlit driving range and a short-game complex — everything a golfer needs to build a game, whether scratch or just starting out.

Our members' clubhouse, with its long terrace over the eighteenth green, remains the heart of the club: a place for celebration, commiseration and the easy company of people who love the game.

60years of the game
Our mission

What we're here to do

To protect a great golf course, grow the game for all ages and abilities, and offer members and visitors a club defined by warmth, not formality.

Where it all happens

Championship 18
