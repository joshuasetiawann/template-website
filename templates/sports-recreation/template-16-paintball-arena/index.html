About Blitzfield Paintball

Suit up. Take cover. Dominate.

Blitzfield Paintball delivers heart-pounding, safety-first action across eight cinematic battlefields for groups of every age.

Blitzfield Paintball carved its first battlefield out of Foxhollow woodland in 2013, founded by a crew of adrenaline junkies who believed a great game day should be cinematic, safe and unforgettable.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Eight themed battle zones

Cinematic battlefields from a downed jet to a village siege and a speedball arena.

Full safety kit

Goggles, masks, overalls and body armour issued and checked for every player.

Base camp & safe zone

A covered base with seating, lockers, briefings and a catering kiosk.

Low-impact zone

Junior-friendly markers and gentler games so kids from eight can play safely.

Airsoft skirmish ground

A dedicated airsoft area with scenario games for the dedicated skirmisher.

Marshals & first aid

Qualified marshals on every game and first-aiders on site all day.
