About Tidewater Watersports Centre

Your adventure starts at the waterline.

Tidewater Watersports Centre opens the water to everyone, with expert RYA instruction, premium kit and a glorious sheltered bay to learn on.

Tidewater Watersports Centre has launched from Harborlight Marina since 1995, growing into one of the coast's most respected RYA-recognised training centres — without ever losing the salt-in-the-air joy of being on the water.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Sheltered training bay

A safe, sheltered bay with consistent breeze — ideal water for learning every sport.

Premium boat & board fleet

Dinghies, keelboats, paddleboards, windsurfers and kayaks, all immaculately kept.

RYA training rooms

Shore-based classrooms for theory, navigation and safety qualifications.

Changing rooms & drying

Warm changing, hot showers, wetsuit drying and secure kit storage.

Members' pontoon & café

A private launch pontoon, boat park and a café overlooking the marina.

Safety & rescue cover

Powerboat safety cover on every session, run by qualified rescue crews.
