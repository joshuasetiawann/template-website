FAQ

Questions, answered

Basic swimming ability and water confidence are needed, and buoyancy aids are worn at all times; let us know your level and we'll keep you safe and supported.

Yes — boats, boards, wetsuits and buoyancy aids are all included with lessons and courses, and members enjoy unlimited board and kayak hire.

Junior courses and the academy welcome children from age eight, with family sessions for younger ones; most adult courses have no upper age limit.

We're an RYA-recognised training centre, so our sailing, windsurfing and powerboat courses lead to nationally and internationally recognised certifications.

Safety comes first: sessions may move to sheltered water, switch to shore-based theory or be rescheduled at no charge if conditions are unsafe.
