About Apex Lane Karting

Lights out and away you go.

Our story

How Apex Lane Karting came to be

Apex Lane Karting laid its first stretch of tarmac in 2012, built by motorsport fans who wanted a circuit with real racing pedigree — proper timing, proper karts and a layout that rewards skill, not just bravery.

Today our floodlit multi-level circuit runs both rapid electric and punchy petrol karts, with arrive-and-drive sessions, a junior academy, endurance team races and a full championship for the seriously quick.

From an eight-year-old's first cadet session to a fifty-lap team enduro under the floodlights, Apex Lane is where the racing bug bites — and where lap times, podiums and rivalries are made.

Facilities

Where it all happens

Floodlit multi-level circuit

A 1,100m two-storey circuit with banking, hairpins and a flat-out main straight.

Electric & petrol karts

Rapid electric karts and powerful petrol machines, plus cadet karts for juniors.

Pro race-control & timing

Transponder timing, live leaderboards and printed results after every race.

Pit-lane café & viewing

A trackside café and grandstand with a full view of the circuit and big screens.

Race kit & briefing

Helmets, balaclavas and race suits provided, with a full safety briefing for all.

Podium & event suite

A podium, prize ceremony space and private suites for parties and corporate days.
