Our story

How Skyline Hoops came to be

Skyline Hoops started in a single rented gym in 2009 with eight kids and one mission: teach the game the right way. No shortcuts, no hero-ball — fundamentals, footwork and a defensive identity that travels.

Today we run development academies, competitive travel teams and adult rec leagues out of our four-court Dome, with strength coaches, shooting machines and film sessions that used to be reserved for the pros.

Our alumni have earned college roster spots and scholarships, but our scoreboard is wider than that: kids who learned to lead, to lose well, and to put the team first. That's the Skyline standard.
