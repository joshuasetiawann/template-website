About Meridian Sports Complex

Every sport, under one roof.

Meridian Sports Complex brings an entire community of sport together — from learn-to-swim and family fun to elite training and league competition.

Meridian Sports Complex opened in 2010 as Kingsmere's flagship leisure destination: a single, beautifully designed home for swimming, racket sports, fitness, team sports and family activity all year round.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Competition & learner pools

An eight-lane 25m pool plus a warm teaching pool for lessons and families.

Two sports halls

Flexible halls for basketball, netball, badminton, five-a-side and events.

120-station gym

Cardio, resistance and free-weights zones with a functional-training rig.

Racket & squash courts

Eight badminton and four glass-backed squash courts for clubs and casual play.

Studios & spa

Group-exercise studios, a spin room and a sauna, steam and relaxation suite.

Climbing wall & café

An indoor climbing wall, soft-play and a café at the heart of the complex.
