About Saltline Surf School

Catch more. Surf better. Stay stoked.

Saltline Surf School gets people of every age standing up, surfing safely and falling in love with the ocean at Crescent Bay.

Saltline began in 2011 as one instructor, a van full of foamies and a belief that anyone can learn to surf with the right coaching and a lot of encouragement. Crescent Bay's gentle, consistent breaks did the rest.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Beachfront base

Right on the boardwalk at Crescent Bay's most beginner-friendly break.

Full gear fleet

Soft-tops, performance boards and wetsuits in every size, all included.

Warm changing rooms

Hot showers, heated changing and secure storage after every session.

Surf club lounge

Members' deck, board racks and a café serving the best post-surf coffee.

Video coaching bay

Wave footage reviewed on the big screen to fast-track your progression.

Forecast & safety hub

Daily surf reports, tide charts and lifeguard-led safety briefings.
