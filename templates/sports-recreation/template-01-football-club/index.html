About Ironside FC

Forged on the pitch. Bonded for life.

Ironside FC has shaped Northgate footballers for three decades, blending elite coaching with a community that never quits.

Ironside FC was founded in 1994 on a single ash pitch behind the old foundry, by a handful of steelworkers who wanted somewhere for their kids to play. The name stuck, and so did the attitude: work rate over ego, the team over the individual.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Two full-size 3G pitches

FIFA-quality floodlit all-weather surfaces, lined for 11-a-side and small-sided games.

Performance gym

Strength, speed and rehab suite run by our sports-science team.

2,000-seat stadium

Covered terracing, matchday catering and the loudest home end in the league.

Video analysis room

Every senior fixture filmed and broken down clip by clip.

Physio & recovery

On-site treatment, ice baths and return-to-play screening.

Trophy clubhouse

Members' bar, function room and thirty years of silverware.
