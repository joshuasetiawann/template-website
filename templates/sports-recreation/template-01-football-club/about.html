About Ironside FC

Forged on the pitch. Bonded for life.

Ironside FC has shaped Northgate footballers for three decades, blending elite coaching with a community that never quits.

Our story

How Ironside FC came to be

Ironside FC was founded in 1994 on a single ash pitch behind the old foundry, by a handful of steelworkers who wanted somewhere for their kids to play. The name stuck, and so did the attitude: work rate over ego, the team over the individual.

Three decades later we field eighteen squads from Under-7s to a senior men's and women's side, all coached to the same standard and the same philosophy. Promising players are developed, not bought; character is coached as hard as technique.

Our academy is FA-charter accredited, our pitches are full-size 3G and grass, and our floodlit stadium holds two thousand of the loudest supporters in the county. But what we are proudest of is simpler: nobody who wants to play is ever turned away.
