About Greenfield Recreation Centre

Where the whole community plays.

Greenfield Recreation Centre is the warm, welcoming heart of Brookvale, where every age and ability finds a way to play, move and belong.

Our story

How Greenfield Recreation Centre came to be

Greenfield Recreation Centre was built by and for the people of Brookvale in 2001, with one guiding idea: that a community is healthiest, happiest and closest when it has somewhere to come together, play and look after one another.

Today the centre hums from dawn to dusk — toddler groups and learn-to-swim, after-school clubs and teen nights, fitness classes and sports leagues, and a busy programme of activities keeping our older neighbours active and connected.

We're a not-for-profit run for the whole community, with concession rates, accessible facilities and a genuine open door. Whatever your age, budget or ability, there's a place for you at Greenfield.

24years serving Brookvale
Our mission

What we're here to do

To keep Brookvale active, connected and well — an inclusive, affordable community centre where everyone, of every age and ability, belongs.

Facilities

Where it all happens

Multi-use sports hall

A flexible hall for badminton, basketball, five-a-side, indoor bowls and events.

Community swimming pool

A four-lane pool for lessons, family swims, lane swimming and aqua classes.

Friendly fitness suite

An approachable, fully-equipped gym with free inductions and a welcoming feel.

Activity & class studios

Bright studios for fitness, dance, yoga, youth clubs and community groups.

Community café & lounge

A warm café and social lounge at the heart of the centre — all welcome.

Outdoor courts & play

Floodlit outdoor courts, a playground and a community garden out back.
