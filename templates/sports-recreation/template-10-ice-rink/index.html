About Frostgate Ice Arena

Find your glide.

Frostgate Ice Arena welcomes wobbly first-timers, figure skaters and hockey teams onto the same gleaming ice, all year round.

Frostgate Ice Arena opened in 2003 to bring year-round skating to Wintergreen, and it's been a community fixture ever since — the place where countless locals took their first, slightly terrified, glide.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Olympic-size ice pad

A full 60×30m rink kept in pristine condition for skating, hockey and figure.

Skate hire & sharpening

Hundreds of hire skates in every size, plus a professional sharpening service.

Warm spectator gallery

Heated seating, a café and a clear view over the whole rink.

Off-ice training studio

Dryland conditioning, jumps and edge work for figure and hockey skaters.

Penguin aids & support

Skating aids and gentle ice for nervous beginners and little ones.

Hockey changing rooms

Team changing rooms, benches and a pro shop for sticks and gear.
