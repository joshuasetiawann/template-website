About Concrete Wave Skatepark

Drop in. Push your limits.

Concrete Wave is a community skatepark where first drop-ins and sponsored riders session the same ramps with the same respect.

Our story

How Concrete Wave Skatepark came to be

Concrete Wave poured its first quarter-pipe in 2014, built by skaters for skaters in a former bus depot. The goal was simple: a safe, welcoming park where the next generation could learn, progress and find their crew.

Today we run 2,000 square metres of indoor and outdoor terrain — street plaza, bowls, mini-ramps and a vert wall — plus coaching academies for skate, scooter and BMX, and an inclusive all-wheels policy.

We've hosted regional jams and sent groms to national finals, but the heart of the park is everyday: the older skaters teaching the little ones, the chalkboard of session times, and nobody ever skating alone.
