About Concrete Wave Skatepark

Drop in. Push your limits.

Concrete Wave is a community skatepark where first drop-ins and sponsored riders session the same ramps with the same respect.

Concrete Wave poured its first quarter-pipe in 2014, built by skaters for skaters in a former bus depot. The goal was simple: a safe, welcoming park where the next generation could learn, progress and find their crew.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Street plaza

Ledges, rails, stairs and manual pads for technical street progression.

Bowls & transitions

Flowing bowls, a vert wall and mini-ramps for every level of air.

Pads & helmet hire

Full protective gear for hire, with safety briefings for newcomers.

Viewing mezzanine & café

Parents' lounge, café and a clear view over the whole park.

Foam pit & resi ramp

Land new tricks safely into the foam pit and resi landing.

Pro shop & repairs

Decks, wheels, bearings and on-site setup, repairs and grip.
