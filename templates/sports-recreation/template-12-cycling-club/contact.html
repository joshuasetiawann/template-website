Before you visit

Yes! Our no-drop beginner rides are paced for new and returning riders with ride leaders and a café stop, and we genuinely never leave anyone behind.

A roadworthy bike in good condition is enough to begin; our mechanics offer free safety checks, and we can advise on bikes as you progress.

Newcomers complete a coached accreditation course to learn the velodrome safely before joining open track sessions — no prior track experience needed.

Our Women's Wheels sessions are women-led and women-only, designed to build skills and confidence in a supportive, sociable group.

Our Youth Go-Ride programme coaches eight- to sixteen-year-olds in a safe, traffic-free environment, building bike handling, racing skills and confidence.
