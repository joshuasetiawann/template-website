About Velocity Cycling Club

Ride harder. Go further. Together.

Velocity Cycling Club takes riders from their first nervous group ride to racing fitness, with a peloton that never drops anyone.

Velocity Cycling Club was founded in 2008 by a handful of friends who wanted structured, sociable riding without the elitism. The 'no-drop' promise we made on day one still defines us: nobody gets left behind.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

Velodrome access

Track-accredited sessions on Ridgeway's 250m indoor velodrome.

Turbo training studio

Smart-trainer studio for structured indoor classes and winter base miles.

Workshop & wash bay

Full mechanic workshop, tools and a bike wash for members.

Clubhouse & café

Riders' lounge, secure bike storage and the all-important post-ride coffee.

Bike fit & physio

Professional bike fitting and cycling-specific physiotherapy.

Race team HQ

Kit room, race briefings and a wall of podiums and palmarès.
