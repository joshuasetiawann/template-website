About Velocity Cycling Club

Ride harder. Go further. Together.

Velocity Cycling Club takes riders from their first nervous group ride to racing fitness, with a peloton that never drops anyone.

Our story

How Velocity Cycling Club came to be

Velocity Cycling Club was founded in 2008 by a handful of friends who wanted structured, sociable riding without the elitism. The 'no-drop' promise we made on day one still defines us: nobody gets left behind.

We now run road, track, gravel and time-trial sections, coached turbo classes through the winter, and a youth go-ride programme — based out of the Ridgeway velodrome with a full workshop and clubhouse.

Our racers have stood on regional podiums and our sportive riders have conquered mountain passes, but the heart of Velocity is the Sunday café stop: the laughter, the stories and the friendships forged at speed.

16years in the saddle
Our mission

What we're here to do

To get more people riding, faster and further, in a club where every level is welcome, every ride is safe, and no one is ever dropped.
