About Baseline Tennis Academy

Footwork. Focus. Finish.

Baseline Tennis Academy develops complete players through technical coaching, match play and a culture that values effort over ego.

Baseline Tennis Academy opened its courts in 2005 with a simple conviction: that great tennis is built on great fundamentals — footwork, balance and decision-making — coached patiently and progressively.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

12 floodlit courts

Acrylic and clay courts, four covered, lined and maintained to tournament standard.

Ball-machine bays

Programmable ball machines for solo drilling and rep-based practice.

Strength & movement studio

Speed, agility and injury-prevention work tailored to tennis.

Video analysis suite

Stroke and serve analysis with side-by-side technical comparison.

Clubhouse & café

Members' lounge, viewing balcony and a café overlooking centre court.

Stringing & pro shop

Same-day racket stringing, demo rackets and full retail.
