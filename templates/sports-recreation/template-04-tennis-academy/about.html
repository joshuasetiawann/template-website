Our story

How Baseline Tennis Academy came to be

Baseline Tennis Academy opened its courts in 2005 with a simple conviction: that great tennis is built on great fundamentals — footwork, balance and decision-making — coached patiently and progressively.

Two decades on we run a full performance pathway alongside thriving recreational and junior programmes, across twelve floodlit acrylic and clay courts, four of them covered for year-round play.

Our players have reached national rankings and county finals, but our proudest results are the hundreds who simply fell in love with the game here — and the welcoming, competitive community they built together.
