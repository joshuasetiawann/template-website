About Apex Holds Climbing

Read the wall. Trust the move.

Apex Holds is a community-driven climbing centre where first-timers and competition climbers share the same walls and the same stoke.

Apex Holds was bolted into a disused quarry warehouse in 2015 by three climbers who wanted a gym that felt like a crag, not a fitness chain. Raw industrial space, brilliant setting and a community at the heart of it.

Facilities

Built for the game

Everything you need under one roof, kept to the highest standard.

15m lead & top-rope walls

Towering routes with auto-belays and dedicated lead lanes, reset weekly.

Bouldering arena

A vast spread of problems from slab to steep, graded for every climber.

Training board room

Campus boards, hangboards and a moonboard for focused strength work.

Yoga & mobility studio

Recovery, flexibility and antagonist training to keep you climbing injury-free.

Café & community space

Specialty coffee, a sun deck and the best beta in town.

Gear shop & rental

Shoes, chalk and harnesses for hire and sale, plus expert fitting.
