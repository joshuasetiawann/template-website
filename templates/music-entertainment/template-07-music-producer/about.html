The Producer

The producer behind the records, not in front of them.

KOZA is a producer and beatmaker crafting genre-blurring records for artists who want their sound to stand out — from the first idea to the final master.

Our story

How it all comes together

KOZA is the production project of Kofi Asare, who started flipping samples on a cracked laptop and ended up with credits that span hip-hop, R&B, afrobeats and pop. The throughline is simple: make the artist sound like the best version of themselves.

The KOZA sound is hard to pin down on purpose — warm low-end, unexpected chord changes and drums that sit just behind the beat. It is built to serve a song, not to announce a producer.

From a first voice memo to a delivered master, KOZA handles the whole chain: beats, arrangement, recording, mixing and the final polish. Artists can step in at any point and leave with something finished.

Multi-platinum placements opened doors, but the work that matters most is often the smallest — the unsigned artist with one great idea and no budget, who walks out with a record they are proud of.

KOZA splits time between studio sessions, remote production for clients worldwide, and a steady stream of beat drops for independent artists. If you have a sound in your head, the job is to get it out.
