The Band

Four friends, one amp too many, no plan B.

Voltage Hollow is a four-piece alternative rock band from Detroit — raw guitars, anthem choruses and a live show that leaves the floor shaking.

Our story

How it all comes together

Voltage Hollow started in a leaking Detroit basement in 2014, four friends who would rather play one honest song badly than ten polished ones for show. That stubbornness became the whole identity of the band.

We write about the things people actually live through — the dead-end jobs, the long drives home, the friendships that hold a city together when nothing else does. If a lyric does not feel true at full volume, it does not make the record.

The live show is where everything we are makes sense. We have played sweat-soaked clubs and open-air festival stages, and we treat them exactly the same: leave nothing in the tank, and never look down at the setlist when the room is singing it back.

Recording 'Ashfall' nearly broke us. Eleven months, two studios, a hard drive we almost lost, and a producer who pushed until the takes hurt. It is the most honest thing we have ever made and the reason we still do this.

We are independent and unsigned by choice. Every poster, every pressing and every merch run is built by the four of us and a small crew who feel like family. If you are in the room, you are part of it too.
