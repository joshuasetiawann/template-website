The Band

Four friends, one amp too many, no plan B.

Voltage Hollow started in a leaking Detroit basement in 2014, four friends who would rather play one honest song badly than ten polished ones for show. That stubbornness became the whole identity of the band.

We write about the things people actually live through — the dead-end jobs, the long drives home, the friendships that hold a city together when nothing else does. If a lyric does not feel true at full volume, it does not make the record.
