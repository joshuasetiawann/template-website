The Orchestra

Six decades of bringing the score to life.

The Meridian Symphony is a full professional orchestra performing the great symphonic repertoire and bold new commissions in its landmark concert hall.

Our story

How it all comes together

The Meridian Symphony was founded in 1962 by a group of musicians who believed their growing city deserved a world-class orchestra of its own. From a handful of subscription concerts, it has become one of the region's defining cultural institutions.

Under successive music directors, the orchestra has built a reputation for interpretations of unusual depth — Beethoven and Brahms played with fire and tenderness, and twentieth-century masterworks delivered with fearless commitment.

The Meridian is equally devoted to the music of our own time. Each season includes new commissions and premieres, performed alongside the classics so that audiences experience the living, evolving art of orchestral music.

Home is Meridian Hall, an acoustically celebrated auditorium designed so that the softest pianissimo and the fullest tutti reach every seat with clarity. The hall and the orchestra were, in a real sense, built for each other.

Beyond the concert platform, the orchestra is committed to its community — schools programmes, free open rehearsals and education concerts that bring the symphony to tens of thousands of young listeners every year.
