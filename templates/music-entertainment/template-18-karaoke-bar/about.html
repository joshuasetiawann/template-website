The Bar

Twelve rooms, one stage, zero judgement.

Goldenote is the city's favourite karaoke bar — private rooms, a 60,000-song book, killer cocktails and the best stage in town to find your voice.

Our story

How it all comes together

Goldenote was born from a simple truth: everybody wants to sing, and everybody deserves a room where they can do it badly, brilliantly and without a shred of judgement. We built exactly that, and then we added cocktails.

Choose your night. The main stage is for the brave — a proper PA, a spotlight and a crowd that cheers for everyone. Our twelve private rooms are for crews who want the party to themselves, each with its own screens and sound.

The song book runs 60,000 deep and grows every week, from power ballads and pop bangers to deep cuts, duets and the guilty pleasures you would never admit to in daylight. If it has a tune, it is probably in the machine.

Behind the bar, a serious cocktail list and ice-cold beer keep the energy up, while our hosts read the room, hype the crowd and make sure the mic always finds the next star.

From birthday blowouts to office parties to a random Tuesday that gets out of hand, Goldenote is where the city comes to let go. Pick a song, grab the mic, and own the night.
