The Club

One address. Three rooms. Every kind of night.

Pulse Avenue is the city's premier nightclub — three rooms, a world-class sound system and resident DJs taking you from first drink to last dance.

Our story

How it all comes together

Pulse Avenue opened to give the city a nightclub worthy of its name — a single address with three distinct rooms, so every night out can be exactly the night you are in the mood for.

The main room is built around a custom sound system tuned by engineers who design festival rigs, with a light and laser show that turns peak time into pure adrenaline.

Upstairs, the Velvet Room is slower and more intimate — house, disco and soul for the people who came to dance close. Downstairs, the Basement is dark, loud and strictly for the techno faithful.

Our resident DJs are the backbone, joined every weekend by touring names who choose Pulse for its sound and its crowd. We program with care: the right music, the right room, the right hour.

VIP tables, bottle service and a fast-track door make a big night effortless, while a genuinely welcoming team make sure everyone — VIP or not — has the time of their lives.
