The Label

Twenty-five years of putting the needle on the right groove.

Goldgroove Records is an independent label pressing soul, funk and modern roots to wax — championing artists who play it live and mean every note.

Our story

How it all comes together

Goldgroove Records began in 1998 above a record shop in Memphis, founded on a simple belief: that great music deserves a physical home, and that an independent label can be an artist's ally rather than their landlord.

We sign for the long run, not the quick spike. Our roster spans soul revivalists, funk collectives and modern roots artists who share one thing — they can do it live, in one room, with the tape rolling.

Everything we release is cut and pressed with care. We still master for vinyl first, work with the same plating house we have used for a decade, and obsess over a warm, present sound you can feel in your chest.

Beyond the records, Goldgroove is a community. We run label showcases, in-store sessions and a vinyl club that puts our pressings directly in the hands of the people who love them most.

A quarter century in, we are still a small team that answers its own email and listens to every demo. If you make honest music with a pulse, there may be a place for you in the groove.
