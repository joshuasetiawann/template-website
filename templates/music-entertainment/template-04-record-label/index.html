Goldgroove Records
The Label

Twenty-five years of putting the needle on the right groove.

Goldgroove Records began in 1998 above a record shop in Memphis, founded on a simple belief: that great music deserves a physical home, and that an independent label can be an artist's ally rather than their landlord.

We sign for the long run, not the quick spike. Our roster spans soul revivalists, funk collectives and modern roots artists who share one thing — they can do it live, in one room, with the tape rolling.
