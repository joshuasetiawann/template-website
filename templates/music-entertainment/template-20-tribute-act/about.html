The Show

A labour of love, played note for note.

Rumours of Fleetwood is a critically acclaimed tribute show recreating the magic of a legendary band — note-perfect, full-production and unforgettable, night after night.

Our story

How it all comes together

Rumours of Fleetwood began as five musicians united by an obsession: to recreate the sound, the look and the feeling of one of the greatest bands in rock history, with a respect for the music that borders on the religious.

This is not an impression — it is a recreation. Every harmony, every guitar tone, every drum fill is studied and reproduced with painstaking accuracy, so that for two hours you genuinely feel transported to the records you grew up on.

The show is a full production: authentic instruments, period-perfect staging, the costumes, the lighting and the between-song magic that made the original band a phenomenon. Audiences regularly tell us they forgot they were not at the real thing.

We tour theatres, festivals and clubs nationwide, and we treat every room the same — give the audience the night they came for, the songs they love, performed with the care those songs deserve.

Behind the wigs and the flares are serious musicians who love this catalogue as much as you do. When the crowd is on its feet singing every word, we know we have done the music justice. That is the whole reason we do it.
