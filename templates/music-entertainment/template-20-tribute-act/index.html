The Show

A labour of love, played note for note.

Rumours of Fleetwood began as five musicians united by an obsession: to recreate the sound, the look and the feeling of one of the greatest bands in rock history, with a respect for the music that borders on the religious.

This is not an impression — it is a recreation. Every harmony, every guitar tone, every drum fill is studied and reproduced with painstaking accuracy, so that for two hours you genuinely feel transported to the records you grew up on.
