The crew behind the curtain.

Apex Stage Co. is a full-service live event production company — stages, sound, lighting, video and the crew who make festivals and shows happen flawlessly.

Our story

How it all comes together

Apex Stage Co. exists to make live events look effortless — which, anyone in the business will tell you, takes an enormous amount of effort. From the stage you stand on to the speaker you feel, we build the show around the artist and the audience.

We are full-service by design: structural staging, line-array sound, intelligent lighting, LED video, rigging and power, all from one accountable team. One call, one plan, one crew that owns the result.

Our gear is owned, maintained and constantly updated in-house, and our crew are seasoned professionals who have toured the world. That means reliability — the show goes on, on time, every time, whatever the venue throws at us.

We produce across every scale and sector: multi-stage music festivals, headline tours, corporate launches, conferences and broadcast events. The brief changes; the obsession with getting it perfect does not.

Promoters and brands come back to Apex because we make their job easier and their event better. We sweat the load-in, the cable runs and the contingency plans so the only thing the crowd notices is a brilliant show.
