The Studio

Forty years of tape, talent and the right room.

Northlight Studios is a world-class recording and mixing facility — a vintage console, a live room with rare acoustics, and engineers who serve the song.

Our story

How it all comes together

Northlight Studios was built in 1984 inside a former chapel, chosen for one reason — the natural acoustics of the live room are nearly impossible to recreate. Engineers still travel across the country just to track drums in it.

At the heart of the control room sits a fully restored vintage analog console, paired with a deep collection of outboard, microphones and instruments gathered over four decades. We can go fully analog or hybrid, whatever the record needs.

But gear is only half the story. Our engineers are here to serve the song, not show off the rack. We listen first, suggest second, and stay out of the way when an artist is in the zone.

Northlight is trusted by major labels and bedroom producers alike. We have tracked gold records and first EPs in the same week, and we give both the same attention, because every record matters to someone.

Beyond tracking, we offer mixing, mastering, production and a residential option for artists who want to disappear into a project. Tell us what you are making, and we will build the session around it.
