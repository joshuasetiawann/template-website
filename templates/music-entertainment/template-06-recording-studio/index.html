The Studio

Forty years of tape, talent and the right room.

Northlight Studios was built in 1984 inside a former chapel, chosen for one reason — the natural acoustics of the live room are nearly impossible to recreate. Engineers still travel across the country just to track drums in it.

At the heart of the control room sits a fully restored vintage analog console, paired with a deep collection of outboard, microphones and instruments gathered over four decades. We can go fully analog or hybrid, whatever the record needs.
