The Club

Brick wall, low ceiling, legendary nights.

The Laughing Crow is a beloved comedy club serving up stand-up, improv and the funniest touring acts in the business — seven nights of laughter and a stiff drink.

Our story

How it all comes together

The Laughing Crow has been making the city laugh since 1989, from the same low-ceilinged room with the same brick wall behind the same battered microphone. Comics call it one of the best rooms in the country, and audiences agree.

We built our name on a simple promise: every night, a tight bill of genuinely funny people. No filler, no endless open-mic slog — just stand-up, improv and showcases curated by people who live and breathe comedy.

Some of the biggest names in comedy worked out their first hours on our stage, and many still drop in unannounced to test new material. You never quite know who is going to walk through the curtain on a given night.

The room seats just 180, close enough that the comic can see your face and you can feel the crowd turn. That intimacy is the whole point — comedy is a conversation, and this is a room built to have it.

Alongside the comedy there is a proper bar, a warm welcome and a team that treats a Tuesday crowd as well as a sold-out Saturday. Come early, stay late, and laugh until it hurts.
