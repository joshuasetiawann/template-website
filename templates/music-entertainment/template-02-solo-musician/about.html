Biography

A voice that learned to whisper before it learned to belt.

Cassia Vale is a singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist writing intimate, cinematic folk-pop — just a voice, a piano and the truth.

Our story

How it all comes together

Cassia Vale grew up between a piano stool and a kitchen radio in Asheville, North Carolina, learning that the smallest songs often carry the heaviest feelings. She has been chasing that contradiction ever since.

Her writing lives in the details — a half-finished letter, a porch light left on, the particular silence of a house after someone leaves. Critics call it cinematic; she just calls it honest.

After years of open mics and house concerts, Cassia self-produced her debut EP on a borrowed interface and an old upright piano. It found its way onto editorial playlists and quietly crossed a million streams.

On stage she moves between hushed solo sets and a five-piece full-band show, but the centre never changes: a single voice, completely present, telling you something true. Audiences tend to forget to use their phones.

Her new album 'Paper Tide' was recorded live to tape over nine days in a converted chapel. It is her most fully realised work — and the reason this next run of shows means everything to her.
