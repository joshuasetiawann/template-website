Biography

A voice that learned to whisper before it learned to belt.

Cassia Vale grew up between a piano stool and a kitchen radio in Asheville, North Carolina, learning that the smallest songs often carry the heaviest feelings. She has been chasing that contradiction ever since.

Her writing lives in the details — a half-finished letter, a porch light left on, the particular silence of a house after someone leaves. Critics call it cinematic; she just calls it honest.
