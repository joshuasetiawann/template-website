Our story

How it all comes together

LUMA WAVE is the creator project of artist Remy Sol, who turned a bedroom studio and a webcam into one of the most-watched live music channels online. What started as playing for nobody became a nightly show for a worldwide audience.

The sound is bright, melodic electronic-pop, made live and in the open — viewers watch tracks get built from a blank session to a finished hook, then hear them again as polished monthly releases.

But the real product is the community. Tens of thousands tune in from every time zone, request songs in real time, name the tracks and shape the channel. LUMA WAVE is less a broadcast than a nightly hangout.

Brands, labels and fellow creators noticed. LUMA now collaborates on campaigns, livestream events and crossover singles, always on the condition that it stays true to the channel and the people who built it.

Five nights a week, the light goes on and the stream goes live. Whatever else is happening, that consistency is the promise — and the reason the community keeps showing up.
