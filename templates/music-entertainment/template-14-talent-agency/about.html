The Agency

A boutique agency that knows every artist by name.

Lantern Talent is a boutique agency representing musicians, DJs and performers — and connecting promoters, brands and events with artists who deliver.

Our story

How it all comes together

Lantern Talent was founded on a belief that representation should be personal. We are a boutique agency by choice, with a curated roster small enough that we know every artist's strengths, ambitions and the rooms where they shine.

We represent musicians, DJs, bands and live performers across genres, and we match them with the promoters, festivals, brands and private clients who will treat them well and present them right.

For artists, we are managers, agents and advocates in one — handling bookings, fees, contracts and the long-term career strategy, so they can focus on the work. For buyers, we are a single trusted call.

Our reputation rests on reliability. When Lantern confirms an artist, the date is advanced properly, the rider is honest, and the show happens as promised. Promoters rebook us because we make their job easier.

We are selective about who we sign and who we work with, because trust runs both ways. The result is a roster of artists we believe in and a network of partners who keep coming back.
