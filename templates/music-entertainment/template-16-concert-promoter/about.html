The Promoter

Two decades of putting on the show.

Highrise Live is a concert promoter producing unforgettable live music events — from intimate club tours to headline arena nights and festival main stages.

Our story

How it all comes together

Highrise Live has spent two decades doing one thing extremely well: producing live music events that artists trust and audiences never forget. From a single club night, it has grown into one of the most active independent promoters in the country.

We promote across every scale — breaking acts on their first headline tour, established artists in theatres and arenas, and curated festival stages that bring it all together. The room changes; the standard does not.

Artists work with us because we do it properly. The marketing is sharp, the production is dialled, the settlement is fair and the show runs on time. Word travels in this business, and ours is good.

Audiences work with us because a Highrise show is a guarantee — a great act, a great room and a night that runs smoothly from doors to encore. We obsess over the details so the crowd never has to.

Behind every event is a team of bookers, producers and marketers who genuinely love live music. We are fans first, and that is why we keep putting on the shows we would want to be at.
