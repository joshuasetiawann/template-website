The Company

Half a century of fearless, intimate theatre.

The Saltbridge Players are an award-winning theatre company staging bold classics and striking new writing in an intimate, beautifully restored playhouse.

Our story

How it all comes together

The Saltbridge Players were founded in 1971 by a company of actors determined to make theatre that was both rigorous and alive — productions that honour the text while speaking directly to the audience in the room tonight.

We stage a balanced season: the great classics reimagined with clarity and daring, alongside new plays we commission and develop ourselves. We believe the canon and the cutting edge belong on the same stage.

Our home is the Saltbridge Playhouse, a lovingly restored Victorian theatre seating just 240. The intimacy is deliberate — close enough to see an actor think, so that every performance is a genuine exchange between stage and seat.

The company is an ensemble at heart. Many of our actors, directors and designers have worked together for years, and that shared language shows in the precision and trust of every production we mount.

Beyond the main stage, our learning and participation programme brings theatre to schools, community groups and emerging artists — because the future of the art form depends on opening its doors wide.
