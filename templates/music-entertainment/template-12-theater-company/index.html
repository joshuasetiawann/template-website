The Company

Half a century of fearless, intimate theatre.

The Saltbridge Players were founded in 1971 by a company of actors determined to make theatre that was both rigorous and alive — productions that honour the text while speaking directly to the audience in the room tonight.

We stage a balanced season: the great classics reimagined with clarity and daring, alongside new plays we commission and develop ourselves. We believe the canon and the cutting edge belong on the same stage.
