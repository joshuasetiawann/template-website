The Crew

From the cypher to the world stage.

Kinetic Mob is a championship street-dance crew — explosive choreography, sharp visuals and a stage show that turns rhythm into raw electricity.

Our story

How it all comes together

Kinetic Mob started as a handful of dancers in a community-centre car park, trading moves under a single streetlight. That cypher energy — raw, competitive, joyful — is still the heartbeat of everything the crew does.

We dance across styles: hip-hop, breaking, popping, locking and house, fused into a signature crew sound that hits hard and lands clean. Precision and power are non-negotiable; so is personality.

Years of training turned that car-park crew into world finalists. We have battled and performed on international stages, but we measure ourselves by one thing — whether the room is on its feet when we are done.

Beyond competition, Kinetic Mob is a performance act for hire: festivals, brand launches, music videos, halftime shows and live tours. We tailor every set to the stage, the brief and the crowd.

We give back through our academy, teaching the next generation the discipline and the joy of street dance. The kid in the back of the class today is the crew member on the world stage tomorrow.
