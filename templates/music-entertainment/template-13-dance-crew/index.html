The Crew

From the cypher to the world stage.

Kinetic Mob started as a handful of dancers in a community-centre car park, trading moves under a single streetlight. That cypher energy — raw, competitive, joyful — is still the heartbeat of everything the crew does.

We dance across styles: hip-hop, breaking, popping, locking and house, fused into a signature crew sound that hits hard and lands clean. Precision and power are non-negotiable; so is personality.
