The Aurelian Choir
The Ensemble

A choir defined by blend, intonation and stillness.

The Aurelian Choir was founded in 1979 with a single ambition: to sing the great choral repertoire with the precision of a chamber ensemble and the warmth of a community. Four decades later, that ambition still shapes every rehearsal.

The choir's identity lives in its blend. Forty voices are trained to sound as one instrument — seamless, balanced and capable of a hush that can quiet a cathedral. Audiences often describe the experience as more architectural than musical.
