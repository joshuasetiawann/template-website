The Ensemble

A choir defined by blend, intonation and stillness.

The Aurelian Choir is an acclaimed chamber choir performing sacred and contemporary choral works of rare beauty — a single, luminous instrument made of voices.

Our story

How it all comes together

The Aurelian Choir was founded in 1979 with a single ambition: to sing the great choral repertoire with the precision of a chamber ensemble and the warmth of a community. Four decades later, that ambition still shapes every rehearsal.

The choir's identity lives in its blend. Forty voices are trained to sound as one instrument — seamless, balanced and capable of a hush that can quiet a cathedral. Audiences often describe the experience as more architectural than musical.

The repertoire spans nine centuries, from Renaissance polyphony and the sacred masterworks of the Baroque to newly commissioned pieces written specifically for the ensemble's voices and acoustic sensibility.

Aurelian performs in concert halls, cathedrals and intimate chapels, and records for its own label, Aurelian Editions. Its recordings of contemporary choral music have introduced new works to listeners around the world.

Singing with Aurelian is a discipline and a privilege. Members audition rigorously and rehearse weekly, united by a shared belief that the human voice, gathered together, remains the most moving instrument there is.
