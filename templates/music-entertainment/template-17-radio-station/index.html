The Station

Thirty years of putting the city on the dial.

Bywater 98.3 went on air in 1994 from a spare room above a launderette, founded on a stubborn idea: that radio should be made by people who love music, for the community they live in, with no algorithm in earshot.

Three decades later we are still independent and still listener-supported. No corporate playlist tells us what to spin. Our hosts choose every record, champion local artists and follow their ears wherever they lead.
