The Station

Thirty years of putting the city on the dial.

Bywater 98.3 is independent community radio — handpicked music, unmistakable hosts and live shows broadcasting from the heart of the city around the clock.

Our story

How it all comes together

Bywater 98.3 went on air in 1994 from a spare room above a launderette, founded on a stubborn idea: that radio should be made by people who love music, for the community they live in, with no algorithm in earshot.

Three decades later we are still independent and still listener-supported. No corporate playlist tells us what to spin. Our hosts choose every record, champion local artists and follow their ears wherever they lead.

Our schedule is a tapestry of the city itself — soul and jazz in the morning, talk and culture through the day, new music and deep cuts at night, and live sessions that bring artists straight into the studio.

We broadcast live and local around the clock, but we are more than a frequency. We host events, run a community studio, and put on live shows that turn the radio into a room you can stand in.

Bywater belongs to its listeners. Members keep us on air, volunteers keep us human, and the city keeps us honest. Turn the dial to 98.3 and you are not an audience — you are part of the station.
