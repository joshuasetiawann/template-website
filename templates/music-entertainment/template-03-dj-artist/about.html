Our story

How it all comes together

NOVA SAGE is the project of producer and DJ Lior Halevi, who spent a decade learning that the best electronic music is not about the biggest drop — it is about tension, patience and the moment everyone breathes out at once.

The sound sits between melodic techno and progressive house: rolling low-end, hypnotic arps and breakdowns built to make a packed room hold its breath before the floor reopens.

A residency at Berlin's Halo club became the laboratory. Four-hour sets, no setlist, reading the room in real time — it is where the NOVA SAGE language was written and where it is still being refined.

Productions followed the sets. The 'Afterglow' EP distilled those long nights into five tracks of late-night energy, picked up by tastemaker labels and charted across underground electronic radio.

Today NOVA SAGE plays clubs and festivals worldwide, but the philosophy has not moved an inch: build it slowly, mean every transition, and never hand a dancefloor anything less than total commitment.
