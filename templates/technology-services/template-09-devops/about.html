About Pipeworks

The team behind devops & platform engineering

Deploys that are boring, rollbacks that are instant and alerts that actually mean something. We're Pipeworks — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Pipeworks started in 2018 when its founders, veterans of too many 3am rollbacks, decided shipping software shouldn't feel dangerous. We build the calm, paved-road platforms we wish we'd had on call.

Today Pipeworks brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

What we stand for
