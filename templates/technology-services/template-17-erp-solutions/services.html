How we work

A process designed to de-risk delivery

Four phases that keep work visible, accountable and on track from kickoff to handover.

Blueprint

Workshops capture your processes and requirements and translate them into a system design.

Configure & migrate

We build the configuration, extend only where needed and cleanse and migrate your data.

Test & rehearse

End-to-end and user-acceptance testing, plus a full cutover rehearsal, de-risk go-live.

Go live & support

Hypercare carries you through launch, then managed support keeps the system healthy.
