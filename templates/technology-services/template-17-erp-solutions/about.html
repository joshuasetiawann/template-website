About Coreaxis

The team behind erp implementation & solutions

On-time ERP implementations, sensible customisation and support that knows your build. We're Coreaxis — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Coreaxis was founded in 2005 by consultants who had lived through ERP projects that ran years late and twice over budget. We built a practice obsessed with scope discipline, clean data and on-time go-lives — and the track record shows it.

Today Coreaxis brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: on-time ERP implementations, sensible customisation and support that knows your build. The principles below are how we get there.

Configuration over code

We fit the system to you with the least custom code that does the job — your future upgrades depend on it.

De-risk the cutover

Rehearsed migrations and testing are why our go-lives land on time.

Know the build

Our support team supports what we built, so you're never explaining your system from scratch.

Accountable to outcomes

We measure ourselves by your results, and we say so in writing before we start.
