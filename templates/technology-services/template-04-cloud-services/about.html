About Stratoshift

The team behind cloud services & migration

A right-sized cloud you can reason about — cheaper, faster and fully automated. We're Stratoshift — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Stratoshift formed in 2018 after its founders cleaned up one too many runaway cloud bills and panicked migrations. We turned that hard-won playbook into a calm, repeatable way to move — and stay — in the cloud.

Today Stratoshift brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: a right-sized cloud you can reason about — cheaper, faster and fully automated. The principles below are how we get there.

Right-size everything

The best cloud bill is the smallest one that still sleeps soundly.

Automate the boring

If a human does it twice, it becomes code the third time.

Safety nets always

Every move has a rollback; we never bet the platform on a single cutover.

Accountable to outcomes

We measure ourselves by your results, and we say so in writing before we start.
