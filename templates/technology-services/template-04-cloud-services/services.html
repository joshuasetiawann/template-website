How we work

A process designed to de-risk delivery

Four phases that keep work visible, accountable and on track from kickoff to handover.

Assess

We inventory workloads, dependencies and costs, then score each for the best migration path.

Design landing zone

Accounts, networking, identity and guardrails are codified before anything moves.

Migrate in waves

Workloads move in safe waves, each with validation and an instant rollback path.

Optimise & operate

We right-size, automate and either hand over or run the platform for you.
