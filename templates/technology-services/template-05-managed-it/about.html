One partner for your whole IT stack, proactively watched and clearly reported. We're Sentinel IT — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Sentinel IT started in 2010 with a promise to replace the break-fix scramble with steady, proactive partnership. Fifteen years on, we still answer to that promise — measured by uptime, not invoices.

Today Sentinel IT brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.
