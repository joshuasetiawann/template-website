About Pulsemesh

The team behind iot solutions & connected devices

Reliable devices, secure connectivity and a platform that turns telemetry into action. We're Pulsemesh — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Pulsemesh was founded in 2017 by hardware and cloud engineers who were frustrated that most IoT projects stalled between a clever prototype and a fleet that actually scaled. We built one team to own the whole stack, from silicon to dashboard.

Today Pulsemesh brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: reliable devices, secure connectivity and a platform that turns telemetry into action. The principles below are how we get there.

Accountable to outcomes
