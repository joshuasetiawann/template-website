About Hostpeak

The team behind web hosting & infrastructure provider

99.99% uptime, free migrations and human support that answers around the clock. We're Hostpeak — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Hostpeak was founded in 2011 by infrastructure engineers fed up with hosting that went down and support that never answered. We built the host we'd want to use — measured in uptime and answered tickets, not marketing gigabytes.

Today Hostpeak brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.
