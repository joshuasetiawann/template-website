How we work

A process designed to de-risk delivery

Four phases that keep work visible, accountable and on track from kickoff to handover.

Discover

We map the domain, constraints and success metrics, then size the work with a senior architect.

Architect

Data model, service boundaries and a thin walking-skeleton prove the design end to end.

Build in slices

Vertical, demoable increments ship behind tests and CI every sprint, never big-bang.

Harden & hand off

Load tests, runbooks and pairing leave your team able to own and extend the system.
