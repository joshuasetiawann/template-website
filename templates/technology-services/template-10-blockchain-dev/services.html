How we work

A process designed to de-risk delivery

Four phases that keep work visible, accountable and on track from kickoff to handover.

Specify & threat-model

Build defensively

Contracts are written test-first with gas and upgrade paths considered from the start.

Audit & verify

Deploy & monitor
