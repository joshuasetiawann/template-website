About Chainwright

The team behind blockchain development

Audited contracts, formal rigour and architectures that survive adversarial conditions. We're Chainwright — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Chainwright was founded in 2019 by engineers and a cryptographer who had watched too many promising protocols drained by avoidable bugs. We built a studio where security discipline comes before shipping speed, always.

Today Chainwright brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: audited contracts, formal rigour and architectures that survive adversarial conditions. The principles below are how we get there.

Assume adversaries

We write every contract as though a motivated attacker is reading it — because they are.

Prove, don't hope

Where value is high enough, we replace optimism with mathematical proof.

Security is the product

On-chain, a beautiful protocol that can be drained is worthless.

Accountable to outcomes

We measure ourselves by your results, and we say so in writing before we start.
