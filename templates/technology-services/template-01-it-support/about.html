About HelpGrid

The team behind it support & helpdesk

We answer fast, fix at the root and report every SLA in plain numbers. We're HelpGrid — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

HelpGrid began in 2014 when three burned-out sysadmins decided support didn't have to mean long queues and copy-paste replies. We built the desk we always wished we'd had — fast, named and accountable.

Today HelpGrid brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.
