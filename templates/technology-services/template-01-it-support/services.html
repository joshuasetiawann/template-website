How we work

A process designed to de-risk delivery

Four phases that keep work visible, accountable and on track from kickoff to handover.

Audit & baseline

We inventory devices, accounts and pain points, then agree SLAs and escalation paths.

Onboard the desk

Your pod learns your stack, imports assets and sets up monitoring and intake channels.

Run & resolve

Tickets are triaged, fixed and root-caused with every priority-1 tracked to closure.

Review & improve

Monthly scorecards surface trends so we cut recurring tickets at the source.
