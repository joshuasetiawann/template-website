About Cleargrove

The team behind ux research & design agency

Research that ends arguments and design that quietly makes the hard things easy. We're Cleargrove — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Cleargrove was founded in 2016 by researchers and designers tired of watching good products fail on untested assumptions. We built an agency where evidence leads and the simplest workable design wins.

Today Cleargrove brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: research that ends arguments and design that quietly makes the hard things easy. The principles below are how we get there.

Evidence over ego

The user's behaviour outranks anyone's opinion in the room, including ours.

Simplicity wins

The best design is often the one with the least in it.

Share the craft

We measure success partly by how much research your team does without us.

Accountable to outcomes

We measure ourselves by your results, and we say so in writing before we start.
