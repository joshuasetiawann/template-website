About Linkforge

The team behind network infrastructure solutions

Resilient, segmented, well-documented networks engineered to stay up under load. We're Linkforge — and this is who we are and why clients trust us with work that matters.

Our story

Built on a simple conviction

Linkforge was founded in 2007 by network engineers who believed connectivity should be invisible — present when needed, never a topic in a status meeting. Eighteen years on, that's still the standard we hold ourselves to.

Today Linkforge brings that same standard to every engagement — pairing real expertise with the kind of accountability you can hold us to. We grew by referral, not by hype, and it shows in how we work.

Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is simple: resilient, segmented, well-documented networks engineered to stay up under load. The principles below are how we get there.

Resilience first

We design assuming things will fail, so that when they do, nothing stops.

Document everything

An undocumented network is a liability; ours come with full as-builts.

Segment by default

Flat networks are fragile — we contain risk so one problem stays one problem.

Accountable to outcomes

We measure ourselves by your results, and we say so in writing before we start.
