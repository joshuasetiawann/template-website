About UpliftMicro

The people and principles behind UpliftMicro

A community microfinance institution offering fair micro-loans, savings and training to underserved entrepreneurs.

Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

UpliftMicro was founded in 2011 on a simple belief: a small, fair loan can change a family's future. Banks overlooked market traders and smallholder farmers; we sought them out.

A hundred dollars buys stock, seeds or tools — and with weekly repayment, training and a community of peers, it becomes a business. We lend small, teach freely and grow with our borrowers.

Fourteen years on, eighty-six thousand borrowers have built livelihoods with our support, repaying 98% of the time. Small loans, big change — the mission is exactly where it started.

Regulation & compliance

Held to the highest standard

We operate under clear regulation and independent oversight — the foundation of the trust we are given.

Regulatory & compliance. UpliftMicro is a regulated community microfinance institution. Loans carry a simple flat interest rate disclosed in full before borrowing. We are committed to responsible, transparent lending and never lend more than a borrower can sustainably repay.
