Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Safeharbor Brokers opened in Hartford in 1981 with one conviction: people deserve someone on their side of the insurance table. Forty years later we are still independent and still on yours.

We never tied ourselves to a single insurer. Holding agencies with forty-plus carriers means we can shop the whole market — and walk away from a bad deal on your behalf.

Most of all, we earn our keep at claim time. When the worst happens we manage the claim with you, and we do not stop until the settlement is fair.
