Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Tickr launched in 2015 with a question: why was the stock market gated behind high commissions and big minimums that locked beginners out? They tore the gate down.

Commission-free trades and fractional shares meant anyone could start with a single dollar. A clean app made a first share feel approachable instead of intimidating.

Five and a half million investors later, Tickr has introduced a generation to the markets. The platform grew pro-grade tools, but the founding promise — investing for everyone — never changed.
