Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Northcliff Bank opened on a single Dorchester corner in 1968 with one promise: keep the neighborhood's money safe and reachable. Three generations later that promise still runs the place.

We never chased the loans that sank bigger banks. We stayed boring on purpose — strong capital, local underwriting, and a teller you can actually talk to. That discipline is why we are still here, and still independent.

Today we serve a quarter of a million households and businesses across Greater Boston, blending a genuine mobile bank with branches you can walk into. The technology changed; the handshake did not.
