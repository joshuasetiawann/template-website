Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Halberd Capital was founded in 1994 on a contrarian belief: that patience, not prediction, is what compounds wealth. We have never wavered from it.

While markets chased every fashion, we bought quality businesses at sensible prices and held them. We told clients the truth in plain letters, including when we were wrong. Trust accrued like interest.

Three decades on we steward forty-eight billion dollars for institutions and families who think in generations. The discipline that started the firm still governs every decision.
