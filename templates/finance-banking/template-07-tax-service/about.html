Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

BrightReturn began in 2015 when an enrolled agent got tired of watching software nickel-and-dime people at the end of their return. She built something kinder.

Our promise is simple: a flat fee, a friendly flow, and a real expert who signs off on your return. No mystery charges, no jargon, no dread.

Six hundred thousand returns later, we have refunded more money to more people than we ever imagined — and we still answer when you call.
