Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Pennywise began as a spreadsheet. Its founder dug her family out of debt by giving every dollar a job, then realised everyone deserved a tool that made that simple and even fun.

The app links your accounts read-only, sorts spending automatically, and turns budgeting into something you actually want to open — complete with goal bars that fill up as you save.

Today nearly two million people budget with Pennywise, saving an average of $640 a month. The mission is unchanged: help everyday people feel ahead of their money, for free.
