About Apexline

The people and principles behind Apexline

A multi-asset trading platform with commission-free stocks, deep tools and millisecond execution.

Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Apexline launched in 2016 because retail trading platforms were slow, expensive and dumbed-down. Its founders, exchange engineers, set out to give everyone professional-grade tools.

They made core stock and ETF trading commission-free, then layered on a charting suite and an API that systematic traders actually respect. Execution was measured in milliseconds, not seconds.

Today seven million orders a day flow across 8,000 instruments on Apexline. It is still built for people who take trading seriously — and still allergic to needless fees.
