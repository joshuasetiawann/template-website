Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Riverstone Credit Union was chartered in 1955 by a handful of Portland workers who pooled their savings so neighbors could borrow at fair rates. Seventy years on, members still own it.

We have never had outside shareholders to satisfy. Every dollar of surplus returns to members as better loan rates, higher savings dividends and fewer fees. That is the cooperative difference.

Today a hundred and eighty thousand members strong, Riverstone offers the digital tools of a big bank with the conscience of a cooperative. We answer to our members, and only to them.

Regulation & compliance

Held to the highest standard

We operate under clear regulation and independent oversight — the foundation of the trust we are given.

Regulatory & compliance. Riverstone Credit Union is a not-for-profit, member-owned cooperative. Federally insured by the NCUA. Equal Housing Lender. NMLS #515209. Membership eligibility and a $5 share account required.
