Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Northwind Ventures was founded in 2008 by an operator who had been a first-time founder and remembered how few investors would take an early bet. She built a firm that does.

We write first cheques at pre-seed and seed, then roll up our sleeves — recruiting, customer introductions, late-night counsel. Capital is the beginning of the partnership, not the end.

Seventeen years and two hundred and forty companies later, thirty-four have become unicorns. The thesis hasn't changed: back bold founders early, and help them build.
