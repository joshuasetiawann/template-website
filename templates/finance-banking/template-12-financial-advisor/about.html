Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Truenorth Advisory was founded in 2009 by an advisor who left the commission world for good. She was tired of advice that quietly served the seller, not the client.

Fee-only and fiduciary from day one, Truenorth earns money only from the people it advises. No commissions, no product quotas — just a plan pointed at your goals.

Sixteen years and hundreds of households later, that independence is still the whole proposition. The advice is honest because there is no other way for us to be paid.
