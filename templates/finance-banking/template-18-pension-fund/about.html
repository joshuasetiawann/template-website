About Evergreen Pension

The people and principles behind Evergreen Pension

Our story

From a single idea to a name people trust

Evergreen Pension was established in 1972 to give working people a dignified retirement they could rely on. Half a century later that purpose still anchors every decision.

A pension is the longest-horizon money there is, so we invest accordingly — diversified, patient and prudent, with glide-paths that protect members in the years that matter most.

Today we steward sixty-two billion dollars for more than half a million members. Independent trustees safeguard every pot, and the long view that founded the scheme still governs it.
