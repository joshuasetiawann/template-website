Services & Care

Most appointments are happily boring: exams, hygiene, small fixes. When more is needed, you'll see the plan before we start. Below you'll find every option in full — what it involves, who it suits, how a typical visit runs, the complete weekly timetable, honest pricing and the questions people ask before they start.

Exams & Hygiene

Six-monthly exams, digital X-rays and hygienist cleans that don't lecture.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to exams & hygiene — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a clinician who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your clinician and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The appointment

    Move through the exams & hygiene plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Photo ID and insurance card
  • Any recent scans, X-rays or referral letters
  • A list of medications or supplements
  • Comfortable clothing you can move in
  • Your questions — write them down
  • Water and a little patience for paperwork
