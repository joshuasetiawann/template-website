Our Story

How Stillpoint began

Stillpoint was founded to be the kind of place people wish they'd found sooner — calm, confidential and genuinely unhurried, behind a quiet garden entrance on Arbour Close.

Therapy works best in safety, so we built for it: soundproofed rooms, generous appointment times and therapists trained across modalities so we can match you to the right approach and person.

We see individuals, couples and small groups, supporting everything from anxiety and low mood to life transitions and relationships. The work is led by you; we simply hold the space and walk alongside.

Above all, we're a no-judgement practice. Whatever brings you to the door, you'll be met with warmth, respect and complete confidentiality.

Our mission

Our purpose is to offer a safe, confidential space to be heard and supported — meeting each person without judgement and moving always at their own pace.

Philosophy

What we believe

Confidential & safe

We match you thoughtfully to a therapist and approach, because the relationship is what makes therapy work.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities
