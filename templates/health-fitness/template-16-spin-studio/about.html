Our Story

How Afterdark began

Afterdark was built around one question: what if a workout felt like the best part of your weekend? We blacked out the windows, hung the lights and tuned the sound until the room itself did the motivating.

Every ride is choreographed to music — climbs on the build, sprints on the drop — led by instructors who ride at the front and pull the whole room with them.

Newcomers worried they'd be lost in the dark; instead they found the dark freeing. Credits, packs and unlimited months grew around a community that books the same bike and the same Friday ride every week.

It's still 45 minutes, a fixed wheel and a great playlist. But on the right night, with the right room, it's the closest a workout gets to a gig.

Our mission

Our mission is to make hard training feel like a night out — an immersive, music-driven ride where the room carries you and 45 minutes fly by.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Afterdark.

Ride the music

Every climb, sprint and recovery lands on the beat. The playlist isn't background — it's the workout.

Dark is freedom

Low light means no mirrors, no comparison and no self-consciousness. Just you, the bike and the room.

Everyone rides their ride

Resistance is yours to set. Beginners and regulars share the same room and each chase their own numbers.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

First ride? Arrive 15 minutes early so we can set up your bike and sort shoes (we lend them free). Bring water and a small towel, wear something you can sweat in, and remember: every climb and sprint is your call on the dial. Just ride your ride.
