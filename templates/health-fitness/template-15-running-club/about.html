Our Story

How Pacekeepers began

Pacekeepers started as six people meeting at a lamppost on a wet Tuesday. The rule was the same then as now: whatever your pace, you run with us, and nobody gets left behind.

We grew into pace groups so everyone has a bunch to run with — from run-walk Trailblazers to the sub-five Cheetahs — each led by a trained, friendly volunteer who knows the route and your name.

Beyond the runs, the club became a community: post-run coffees, race road-trips, parkrun takeovers and a finish-line wall of personal bests we're all weirdly proud of.

Couch-to-5K graduates, marathoners and everyone between share the same start line here. The pavement's free; the company makes it worth it.

Our mission

Our mission is to get people moving and keep them company — a run club for every pace and stage, where nobody runs alone and every personal best is a club win.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Pacekeepers.

No one left behind

Every group has a back-marker. We run at the pace of the people who came, not the fastest legs in the bunch.

Every pace welcome

Run-walkers, returners and racers all belong. Find your group today, move up whenever you're ready.

Community first

The coffee after matters as much as the kilometres before. We're a club, not just a training plan.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Pace-group system

Clearly marked groups from run-walk to race pace, each with a trained, friendly leader.

Club meeting point

A central start and finish with bag drop, so you arrive, run and refuel without the faff.

Route library

Safe, well-lit and traffic-light routes of every distance, shared ahead of each run.

Personal-best wall

A celebrated wall of members' milestones — first 5K, first marathon, first podium.

Race road-trips

Group entries and shared travel to local races, parkruns and the odd big-city marathon.

Post-run coffee

A standing date at the cafe next door, because that's where the club really happens.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Joining your first run is as simple as turning up — your first run is free. Wear trainers and clothes for the weather, arrive ten minutes early to meet your pace leader, and don't worry about speed: we'll point you to the right group and run with you the whole way.
