Sustainable Fat Loss

A 12-week pattern reset with weekly data reviews — no banned foods, no 1,200-calorie theatrics.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to sustainable fat loss — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a dietitian who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your dietitian and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The consultation

    Move through the sustainable fat loss plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Photo ID and insurance card
  • Any recent scans, X-rays or referral letters
  • A list of medications or supplements
  • Comfortable clothing you can move in
  • Your questions — write them down
  • Water and a little patience for paperwork
