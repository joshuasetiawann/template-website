Our Story

How Evergreen began

Evergreen was created on a simple, powerful idea: the right movement keeps us independent, and it's never too late to start. We opened a club built entirely around older adults — their bodies, their pace, their goals.

Every class focuses on what matters most with age: strength to stay capable, balance to prevent falls, and mobility to keep doing the things you love. All of it is gentle, scalable and genuinely social.

Instructors are trained specifically in working with older adults, so classes are safe, encouraging and adapted to every ability — chairs available, no one pushed beyond their comfort.

But ask our members and they'll mention the friendships first. Evergreen is as much a community as a club — the tea afterward is as important as the squats before.

Our mission

Our mission is to help older adults stay strong, steady and independent — with gentle, expertly-led classes and a warm community that makes movement something to look forward to.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Bright, level studio

A warm, well-lit studio with a level, non-slip floor and plenty of space to move safely.

Supportive equipment

Sturdy chairs, light weights, bands and rails so every class meets you where you are.

Balance & strength zone

A dedicated area for falls-prevention, balance and gentle strength work.

Comfortable social lounge

A cosy space for the all-important post-class tea, chat and friendships.

Step-free access

Level access throughout, accessible bathrooms and seating wherever it's needed.

Trained instructors

A team qualified specifically in exercise for older adults — safe, patient and encouraging.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Coming for the first time is easy and pressure-free. Book a free taster, wear comfortable clothes and flat shoes, and arrive ten minutes early for a cup of tea and a chat. Tell your instructor about any health conditions — every movement can be adapted, and a chair is always nearby.
