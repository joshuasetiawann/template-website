Programs & Schedule

Programs & Schedule

Every BlueFin swimmer moves through clearly assessed stages. Badges are earned in the water, never given for attendance. Below you'll find every option in full — what it involves, who it suits, how a typical visit runs, the complete weekly timetable, honest pricing and the questions people ask before they start.

Session

Splash Babies (4m–3y)

Parent-and-baby water confidence: cues, floats and first submersions, gently.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to splash babies (4m–3y) — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a coach who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your coach and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The session

    Move through the splash babies (4m–3y) plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Swimsuit and a spare towel
  • Goggles and a swim cap
  • Flip-flops for poolside
  • Water bottle
  • A snack for after the splash
  • A waterproof bag for wet kit
