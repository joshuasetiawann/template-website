Our Story

How BlueFin began

BlueFin started with a single learn-to-swim class at the Reservoir Road Aquatic Centre and a simple promise to parents: small groups, qualified instructors and a level path you can actually see.

Water confidence is built one rung at a time, so we mapped a clear progression — from blowing bubbles and floating to four strokes and squad pace — with assessments that tell you exactly where your swimmer stands.

Families came for the structure and stayed for the instructors, who learn each child's name, fears and small victories. Adult lessons and stroke-correction followed by popular demand.

Today we run learn-to-swim, stroke school and squads across the week, all on the same belief: safe, steady, encouraging teaching in genuinely small classes.

Our mission

Our mission is to give every swimmer water confidence and safety for life — taught patiently, in small groups, along a level pathway anyone can follow.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at BlueFin.

Safety before speed

Water competence and self-rescue come first. We build confident, safe swimmers, then fast ones.

Small classes, real attention

Tight instructor-to-swimmer ratios mean every swimmer is watched, corrected and encouraged every lesson.

A pathway you can see

Clear levels and honest assessments show exactly where your swimmer is and what comes next.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Warm teaching pool

A heated, shallow teaching pool that keeps young and nervous swimmers comfortable and calm.

Main lap pool

A full-length pool for stroke development, squads and masters lanes.

Level pathway board

A clear wall chart of every level and milestone, from water safety to squad.
