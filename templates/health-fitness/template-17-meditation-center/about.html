Our Story

How Aurora began

Aurora Stillness Center grew from a single weekly sit in a borrowed room. People kept coming back for the same reason: a quiet, unpretentious place to practise, with someone to guide the way.

We kept it secular and simple — no dogma, no incense-heavy mysticism, just well-taught attention practice for real, busy lives. Beginners are not only welcome here; they're the point.

The timetable opened out into guided sits, silent sits, breath-focused sessions and longer practice evenings, each held by guides trained to teach gently and clearly.

Today the center is a soft-lit room of cushions and chairs where strangers sit together in stillness and leave a little lighter. The first sit is always the hardest; we make it easy.

Our mission

Our purpose is to make meditation accessible and unintimidating — a calm, secular space to learn attention, settle the mind and simply arrive, whatever your level.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Aurora.

Beginners belong

No experience, no perfect posture, no jargon. Every session has a way in for the first-time sitter.

Secular & simple

Evidence-informed attention practice, free of dogma. Take what helps, leave the rest.

Stillness over striving

There's nothing to achieve and no way to fail. We practise arriving, not performing.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Your first sit is the hardest — and we make it simple. Arrive ten minutes early, wear comfortable clothing, and sit however you like (chair or cushion, both are fine). A guide will offer a short orientation, then gently lead the practice. There's nothing to prepare and no way to get it wrong.
