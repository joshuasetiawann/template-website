Our Story

How Aurelia began

Aurelia was founded on a single conviction: that Pilates is at its best when it is precise, unhurried and taught in genuinely small groups. We capped class sizes before we even chose the paint.

The atelier on Linden Row was fitted out one reformer at a time, each placed so that an instructor can see every spring, every alignment, every breath from across the room.

Our work spans first-timers rebuilding after injury, dancers chasing control, and busy people who simply want to move without pain. The method bends to the body, not the other way around.

We remain deliberately small — eight reformers, a handful of instructors and a standard of attention that a packed class can never give.

Our mission

Our philosophy is control over chaos: low-impact, high-precision movement, taught in small groups so every repetition is seen, corrected and made to count.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Aurelia.

Precision over speed

Pilates rewards control, not hurry. We teach a smaller range done perfectly before a larger one done loosely.

Small by design

Group classes are capped so your instructor can correct alignment in real time — the way the method intends.

Movement for every body

From post-rehab to performance, the springs scale the work to exactly the body on the carriage.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Eight studio reformers

Premium reformers spaced for full sightlines, each with its own box, straps and jump board.

Mat & tower corner

A dedicated mat and wall-tower area for foundational and complementary work.

Props library

Magic circles, soft balls, rotating discs and resistance bands for every progression.

Private session room

A separate room for one-to-one and post-rehabilitation sessions away from the group floor.

Calm changing suite

A quiet changing area with showers, robes and lockers for valuables.

Step-free studio

Level access throughout so the atelier is open to clients of every mobility.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Booking your first session? Wear fitted clothing you can move in (grippy socks are ideal — we sell them too), arrive ten minutes early, and let your instructor know about any injuries. We will start you gently and build from there.
