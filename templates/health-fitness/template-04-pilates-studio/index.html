The Method

Six principles, one sequence

Tap through the principles every Aurelia session is built upon — in the order Joseph Pilates insisted they be learned.

  1. Every movement begins on an exhale that knits the ribs and wakes the deep core.

  2. The mind rehearses the movement before the body performs it.

  3. All work radiates from the powerhouse — the corset of abdomen, hips and back.

  4. No momentum, no shrug, no shortcut. Springs reward honesty.

  5. One perfect repetition outranks ten approximate ones.

  6. Finally the sentence is read aloud: seamless, breathing, whole.
