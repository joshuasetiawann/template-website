Our Story

How KinetiCare began

KinetiCare opened at Wellfield Court to do physiotherapy the way the evidence asks for it — thorough assessment, hands-on treatment where it helps, and active rehabilitation that actually fixes the cause.

We resisted the conveyor-belt model from day one. Appointments are long enough to assess properly, explain clearly and send you home knowing exactly what to do between visits.

Our clinicians span musculoskeletal, sports and post-operative rehabilitation, and we added a rehab class studio so patients can progress strength under expert eyes, not guesswork.

Today we treat everyone from desk-bound necks to returning athletes, united by one standard: care that's explained, measured and built to get you discharged, not dependent.

Our mission

Our mission is to resolve the cause, not just the symptom — pairing hands-on care with active rehabilitation so patients recover fully and stay that way.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at KinetiCare.

Assessment leads

We diagnose before we treat. A thorough first assessment is the difference between managing pain and resolving it.

Explain everything

You'll understand what's wrong, why, and what the plan is. Informed patients recover faster and relapse less.

Active recovery

Hands-on care opens the door; targeted exercise walks you through it. We build strength, not reliance.
