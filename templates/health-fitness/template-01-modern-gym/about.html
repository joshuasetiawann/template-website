Our Story

How IRONHAUS began

IRONHAUS started in a single railway arch with two squat racks, a chalk bucket and a whiteboard of percentages. The idea was simple: build a room loud enough that nobody felt self-conscious, and structured enough that every rep counted toward something.

Word travelled the way it does in strength circles — by personal records. Within two years the arch became a unit, the unit became 2,400 square metres, and the chalk bucket multiplied. What never changed was the whiteboard: every program here is still written in blocks, waves and honest numbers.

Today the floor runs from 06:00 lifters to the 19:30 conditioning crew, with fourteen certified coaches who learn your name before they touch your bar path. Members range from first-timers in Foundations 101 to platform-bound powerlifters — the same room, the same standards, scaled to you.

We are independent, member-owned in spirit, and stubborn about the things that matter: clean plates, racked weights, no ego lifting and a coach within earshot whenever you need one.

Our mission

Our mission is plain: make serious strength training feel welcoming, measurable and a little bit loud — so that turning up becomes the easiest habit you keep.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at IRONHAUS.

Earn it with reps

Progress is a skill you practise, not a thing you buy. We coach the boring basics until they become your superpower.

No ego lifting

Leave the comparison at the door. The only number that matters is the one you beat last week.

Coached, always

Every session has a certified coach on the floor. You are never left to guess your next set alone.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Twelve competition squat racks

Calibrated bars, change plates and a dedicated platform for pulls and overhead work.

Turf and sled track

A 20-metre indoor turf lane for sleds, prowlers and carries, open outside class times.

Full conditioning rig

Rows of ergs, assault bikes and ski machines wired to the heart-rate display.

Recovery Bay

Sauna, cold plunge and guided down-shifts, bookable straight after any session.

24/7 keycard access

Members train on their own schedule — the floor never closes for those with a card.

Changing rooms & lockers

Hot showers, day lockers and a kit-wash station by the loading door.
