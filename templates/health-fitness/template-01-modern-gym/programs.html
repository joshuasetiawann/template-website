Programs & Schedule

Programs & Schedule

Six coached tracks run on repeating 8-week blocks — pick one lane or mix them all on an unlimited plan. Below you'll find every option in full — what it involves, who it suits, how a typical visit runs, the complete weekly timetable, honest pricing and the questions people ask before they start.

Strength Lab HIIT Engine Power & Speed Mobility Reset Body Comp Camp Engine Builder Foundations 101 Recovery Bay
Session

Strength Lab

Barbell-first coaching across squat, bench, deadlift and press with percentage-based waves.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to strength lab — open to experienced members chasing more.
  • People who want structure and a coach who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your coach and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The session

    Move through the strength lab plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Comfortable kit and supportive trainers
  • A full water bottle
  • A small towel
  • Arrive 10 minutes early to check in
  • Any niggles or injuries to mention
  • An open mind — we'll handle the rest
