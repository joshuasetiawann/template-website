Good to know

Pricing questions, answered

The things people ask most before they join.

Our Little Tigers stream welcomes young children, with junior and teen classes above it. We build coordination, focus and respect from the very start.

Not for your first sessions — loose, comfortable clothing is fine. We'll guide you on a gi once you decide to continue.

Students progress through coloured belts by grading, each with clear requirements. Gradings are encouraged, never rushed, and celebrated by the whole dojo.

Controlled sparring is introduced gradually and supervised closely. It's a part of training, always matched to your rank and readiness.

Tuition is month to month with no lock-in; let us know before your next billing date and we'll take care of it.
