Programs & Schedule

Programs & Schedule

From Little Tigers to senior black-belt study, each class keeps the same spine: etiquette, kihon, kata, kumite. Below you'll find every option in full — what it involves, who it suits, how a typical visit runs, the complete weekly timetable, honest pricing and the questions people ask before they start.

Session

Little Tigers (5–7)

Balance, focus and falling safely — discipline disguised as play.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to little tigers (5–7) — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a coach who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your coach and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The session

    Move through the little tigers (5–7) plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Comfortable kit and supportive trainers
  • A full water bottle
  • A small towel
  • Arrive 10 minutes early to check in
  • Any niggles or injuries to mention
  • An open mind — we'll handle the rest
