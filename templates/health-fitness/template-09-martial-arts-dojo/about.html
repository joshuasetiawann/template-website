Our Story

How Kurotora began

Kurotora opened on Garrison Street with a clean mat, a single rack of belts and a sensei who believed martial arts should build character before it builds fighters.

The belt path gave students a clear journey, but the heart of the dojo was always etiquette — bowing in, respecting partners, leaving ego at the door. Parents noticed the change in their children first.

Little Tigers, junior, teen and adult classes grew around that same culture: demanding on the mat, generous off it. Gradings became milestones the whole dojo celebrated.

We keep the traditions — the bow, the lines, the discipline — because they work. Wrapped around them is a genuinely welcoming room where everybody belongs.

Our mission

Our purpose is to develop the whole person — building skill, discipline, respect and quiet confidence on the mat that carries into every part of life off it.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Kurotora.

Etiquette first

We bow in, respect our partners and leave ego at the door. Character is the first technique we teach.

A clear path

The belt system gives every student a visible journey — small, earned steps from white belt onward.

Demanding, never harsh

High standards, delivered with patience. We push students to grow and catch them when they wobble.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Curious about training? Your first step is to begin — wear loose, comfortable clothing (we'll sort your gi later), arrive ten minutes early and simply watch how we bow in. Sensei will introduce you, place you in the right class and start you with the fundamentals.
