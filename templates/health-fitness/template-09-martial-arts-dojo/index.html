The Belt Path

From white to black, honestly graded

Nine kyu grades before dan. Each belt below shows its typical training time — there are no shortcuts on this list, and that is the point.

  1. White belt10th kyuBegin: etiquette + first kihon
  2. Yellow belt9th–8th kyu6–12 months
  3. Orange belt7th kyu12–18 months
  4. Green belt6th–5th kyu18–30 months
  5. Blue belt4th kyu2.5–3.5 years
  6. Brown belt3rd–1st kyu3.5–5 years
  7. Black beltShodan5+ years · examined externally

Dojo etiquette, briefly

The bow marks the line between the street and the mat. Bow to the dojo entering and leaving, to your partner before and after every drill.

Instructors are Sensei; senior students are Sempai. Questions are welcome — raised hands, not raised voices.

Arrive in a clean gi, nails short, jewellery off. Students sweep the mat on rotation; the rota is by the door.
