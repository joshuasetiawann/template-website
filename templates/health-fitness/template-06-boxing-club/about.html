Our Story

How Southpaw began

Southpaw took over the old fire station on Tannery Row with a couple of heavy bags and a second-hand ring. The brick was already scarred; we just added skipping ropes and a bell.

Boxing teaches you more than how to throw a punch — it teaches timing, calm under pressure and a kind of fitness you can't fake. We coach all of it, for everyone from white-collar beginners to club competitors.

The youth squad came next, then Boxfit for people who wanted the conditioning without the contact. The ring stayed busy; the community got tighter.

We're still an honest, no-frills club. Wraps on, phone away, work hard, respect the corner. That's the whole code.

Our mission

Our mission is to share real boxing with anyone willing to put the work in — building fitness, confidence and discipline whether you spar once or never.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Southpaw.

Wraps on, ego off

Everyone starts on the bags learning to move. Respect the craft and the craft gives back.

Defence first

We teach you to not get hit before we teach you to hit. Footwork and guard come before power.

Mind the corner

Coaches are your corner — honest, demanding and entirely in yours. We win and lose together.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Competition ring

A full-size roped ring for technique, padwork and supervised sparring for those who want it.

Heavy-bag floor

A row of heavy bags, uppercut bags and a floor-to-ceiling ball for timing work.

Padwork stations

Coach-led pad rounds that build combinations, distance and conditioning.

Strength & conditioning corner

Kettlebells, ropes, medicine balls and a pull-up rig for the engine work.

Youth training space

A dedicated mat area and lighter kit for the 14–17 squad sessions.

Wraps & glove wall

Loan gloves, wraps and pads for first-timers, plus a rack for members' own kit.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

Never boxed before? Good — most of our members hadn't either. Book your first session free, wear trainers and clothes you can sweat in, and turn up ten minutes early. We'll lend you wraps and gloves and start you on the bags, no sparring required.
