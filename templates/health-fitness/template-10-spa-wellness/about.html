Our Story

How Salt & began

Salt & Cedar was imagined as an antidote to busy — a low-lit retreat on Cedar Hill Lane where time slows the moment the door closes behind you.

We built around the senses: warm cedar underfoot, a salt-air relaxation room, herbal steam and treatments designed as rituals rather than appointments. Nothing here is rushed.

Our therapists were chosen for their hands and their calm in equal measure. Guests arrive frayed and leave softer; many now mark the seasons by their visits.

From a single treatment room to a full day-spa with thermal suite, the intention never wavered: create space for guests to put themselves down for a while.

Our mission

Our intention is simple — to create unhurried space for rest and repair, where every ritual is paced to your nervous system and nobody is ever rushed out the door.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Salt &.

Unhurried by design

Treatments include time to arrive and time to land. We never double-book the calm out of your visit.

Senses first

Warmth, scent, sound and touch are all considered. The room does half the work before a therapist begins.

Rest is the point

This isn't productivity in disguise. The goal is to leave softer, slower and genuinely restored.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Thermal suite

A herbal steam room, sauna and cool-down space to open the visit and ease the body in.

Salt-air relaxation room

A gently lit room with a salt wall and loungers for the slow minutes between treatments.

Cedar treatment rooms

Warm, quiet rooms for massage, facials and body rituals, each fully soundproofed.

Tea & rest lounge

Herbal infusions, soft seating and silence — stay as long as the calm asks you to.

Robes, slippers & lockers

Plush robes, slippers and private lockers so you can drift between spaces unbothered.

Couples' ritual room

A double room for shared treatments and side-by-side stillness.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

On your first visit, arrive thirty minutes early to use the thermal suite and let your shoulders drop. Bring nothing but yourself — robe, slippers, towels and tea are all provided. Tell your therapist your pressure preferences and any sensitivities, then simply rest.
