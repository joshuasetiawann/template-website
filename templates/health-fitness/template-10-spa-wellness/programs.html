Treatment

Deep Tissue Massage

Slow, specific pressure for shoulders that carry laptops and worry.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to deep tissue massage — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a therapist who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your therapist and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The treatment

    Move through the deep tissue massage plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Arrive 15 minutes early to unwind
  • Leave jewellery and valuables at home
  • Flag any allergies or sensitivities
  • Switch your phone to silent
  • Hydrate well before and after
  • A robe and slippers are provided
