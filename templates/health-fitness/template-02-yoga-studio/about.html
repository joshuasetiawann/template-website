Our Story

How Stillwater Yoga began

Stillwater began as a Sunday-morning circle of six friends and a borrowed set of mats. The loft above Alder Street had good light and creaky floors, and it turned out that was most of what a yoga home needs.

We grew slowly and on purpose. Rather than chase the loudest, hottest trend, we built a timetable that breathes — grounding Hatha mornings, flowing Vinyasa evenings, deep Yin nights and restorative classes for the weeks that ask for less.

Our teachers come from different lineages but share one habit: they teach to the people actually in the room, offering a way up and a way to soften in the same breath. Beginners and ten-year practitioners roll out beside each other here.

Today the loft holds thirty mats, a small tea corner and a wall of props for anyone who wants them. The creaky floors are still here. So is the Sunday circle — it just has a few more friends now.

Our mission

Our intention is to keep yoga unhurried and unintimidating — a steady place to breathe, move and arrive, whatever kind of day you bring up the stairs.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Stillwater Yoga.

Breath leads

Every class is paced to the breath, not the clock. You set the depth; the breath sets the rhythm.

Come as you are

No flexibility prerequisite, no perfect pose. Props, modifications and rest are always on the menu.

Teach to the room

Our teachers read the people in front of them and meet them there — gentle on the hard days, spacious on the open ones.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Daylight practice loft

A thirty-mat room with skylights, warm wood floors and soft, dimmable evening light.

Full prop wall

Bolsters, blocks, straps, blankets and bolster-towers — borrow whatever supports your practice.

Quiet tea corner

A kettle, loose-leaf teas and a few low chairs for the slow minutes before and after class.

Heated Yin nights

Gently warmed evening sessions for deep, long-held floor postures.

Cubbies & changing nook

Shoe cubbies, a private changing nook and day lockers for valuables.

Step-free access

A lift to the loft and a level practice floor so the space stays open to everyone.

First Visit

Your first visit, made easy

New to the loft? Come ten minutes early, wear something soft you can fold and stretch in, and leave your shoes at the cubbies. Mats and every prop are here for you — just bring yourself and an empty water bottle to fill.
