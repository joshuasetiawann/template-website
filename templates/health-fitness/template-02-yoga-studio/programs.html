Programs & Schedule

Every class is small, candle-warm and labelled by pace — so your first visit already feels like your hundredth. Below you'll find every option in full — what it involves, who it suits, how a typical visit runs, the complete weekly timetable, honest pricing and the questions people ask before they start.

Session

Sunrise Slow Vinyasa

Wake the spine with rounds of breath-led salutations as the loft fills with morning light.

Who it's for

  • Anyone drawn to sunrise slow vinyasa — open to all levels.
  • People who want structure and a guide who knows their name.
  • Those who'd rather progress steadily than burn out in week one.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Arrive & settle

    Get here ten minutes early, meet your guide and run through any notes or goals.

  2. 2

    The session

    Move through the sunrise slow vinyasa plan at your pace — every drill has a scale up and a scale back.

  3. 3

    Cool down & plan

    Wind down together, log what you did, and leave with one small thing to work on next.

Come prepared

What to bring

A short checklist so your first visit is smooth from the front door onwards.

  • Comfortable kit and supportive trainers
  • A full water bottle
  • A small towel
  • Arrive 10 minutes early to check in
  • Any niggles or injuries to mention
  • An open mind — we'll handle the rest
