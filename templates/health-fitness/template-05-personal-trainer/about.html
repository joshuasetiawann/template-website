Our Story

How Vale Method began

Vale Method began with a notebook of programs and a conviction that generic plans waste people's effort. The first clients trained in a borrowed studio at Riverside Mills, each with a plan written for them and nobody else.

As results stacked up, so did referrals. We added coaches who shared the same standard: measure first, program deliberately, and adjust every block based on what the numbers actually say.

Today we coach across three formats — fully online, hybrid, and one-to-one in the studio — but the spine is identical: an individual plan, regular check-ins and a coach who is genuinely accountable for your progress.

We stay small and selective. Every coach carries a limited roster so that nobody becomes a number on a spreadsheet.

Our mission

Our mission is to make personal training genuinely personal again — a written plan, honest measurement and a coach who owns your result alongside you.

Philosophy

What we believe

Three principles shape every session, plan and conversation at Vale Method.

Measure, then program

We start with assessment, not assumptions. Every block is written from your numbers, your schedule and your goal.

Accountability, not guesswork

Regular check-ins keep the plan honest. You always know what to do next and why it's on the page.

Limited rosters

Each coach takes a capped number of clients, so the attention you get is real, not theoretical.

The Space

Our facilities & amenities

Everything here was chosen to make your time with us comfortable, safe and worth coming back to.

Private coaching floor

A calm one-to-one training space with full racks, dumbbells and cable systems — no queues, no crowds.

Assessment suite

Movement screening, strength testing and body-composition tools to set an honest baseline.

Client training app

Your plan, video demos and check-in history in your pocket, updated every block.
