Our Story

How BrightShift began

BrightShift began because crash diets fail almost everyone — not through lack of willpower, but through lack of support. We built a coached program around the things that actually create lasting change.

We run in cohorts so members lose weight alongside others on the same journey, with a coach, a clear weekly plan and a group that genuinely has each other's backs.

The method is unglamorous and effective: sustainable habits, honest education, steady movement and weekly accountability — no shakes, no shame, no eliminating entire food groups.

What sets it apart is what happens after: a full alumni year of continued support, because keeping weight off is the real goal, and that's where most programs abandon you.

Our mission

Our mission is lasting change over quick fixes — a supported, science-based program that builds the habits, mindset and community to keep the weight off for good.
