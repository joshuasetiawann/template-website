1. Introduction

Marrow & Vale Technologies, Inc. (“Marrow & Vale,” “we,” “us” or “our”) provides the Vellum writing platform and related websites, applications and services (collectively, the “Services”). This combined Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (“Agreement”) governs your access to and use of the Services.

By creating an account, accessing or using the Services, you agree to be bound by this Agreement. If you do not agree, you must not use the Services. This is a sample legal document for a template and is not legal advice.

2. Definitions

“Account” means the registered profile through which you access the Services. “Content” means any text, files, images or other material you upload, create or store using the Services. “Personal Data” means information that identifies, relates to or could reasonably be linked to an identifiable individual.

“Processing” means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether automated or not, including collection, storage, use, disclosure and deletion.

3. Eligibility & Accounts

You must be at least 16 years old, or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, to use the Services. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and for all activity that occurs under your Account.

You agree to provide accurate registration information and to keep it current. We may suspend or terminate Accounts that we reasonably believe have provided false information or breached this Agreement.

4. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address and the Content you create. We also collect information automatically, including device identifiers, log data, approximate location derived from IP address, and usage analytics that help us improve the Services.

Where you connect third-party integrations, we receive only the data necessary to provide the requested functionality. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under the eligible age.

5. How We Use Information

We Process Personal Data to operate, maintain and improve the Services; to authenticate users; to provide customer support; to send service and security notices; and to comply with legal obligations. We rely on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, consent or legal obligation as our lawful bases, depending on the activity.

We do not sell your Personal Data. We use aggregated, de-identified data — which can no longer be linked to you — for analytics and research.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to secure the Services. With your consent, we use preference and analytics cookies to remember your settings and understand how the Services are used. You can manage non-essential cookies through the banner on this page or your browser settings.

Our analytics are configured to respect “Do Not Track” signals where technically feasible.

7. Sharing & Disclosure

We share Personal Data with vetted service providers who process it on our behalf under written contracts (for example, cloud hosting and email delivery). We may disclose information to comply with law, to enforce this Agreement, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users and the public.

In the event of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, Personal Data may be transferred, subject to this Agreement and applicable law.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Subject to your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your Personal Data, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain Processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly from your account settings, or by contacting us using the details below.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Acceptable Use

You agree not to misuse the Services, including by attempting to access them through unauthorized means, interfering with their operation, uploading unlawful or infringing Content, or using the Services to harass others or distribute malware.

We may, but are not obligated to, remove Content or suspend access that violates this Agreement or applicable law.

10. Data Retention & Security

We retain Personal Data for as long as your Account is active and as needed to provide the Services, then delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period unless a longer retention is required by law. We implement administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

We will notify affected users and regulators of a data breach where required by applicable law.

11. Changes & Contact

We may update this Agreement from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated version with a new effective date and, where appropriate, by additional notice. Your continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

Questions about this Agreement can be sent to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@marrowvale.example, or by post to Marrow & Vale Technologies, Inc., 4400 Quill Lane, Suite 12, Portland, OR 97204.

By using the Vellum platform you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Agreement.
