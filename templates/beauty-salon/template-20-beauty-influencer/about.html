Our story

The People & Place Behind Glow with Remi

The personal brand of a beauty creator — tutorials, collaborations, masterclasses and one-to-one glow sessions.

How we began

I'm Remi — a beauty creator who believes makeup should be joyful, inclusive and genuinely easy to recreate at home.

What started as messing about with eyeshadow on camera has grown into a community of millions who glow up together.

I partner with brands I actually love, teach hands-on masterclasses, and sit down one-to-one with people who want to find their own signature look.

No gatekeeping, no impossible standards — just real techniques, honest reviews and a lot of sparkle.

Today, Glow with Remi welcomes clients from across Los Angeles and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.
