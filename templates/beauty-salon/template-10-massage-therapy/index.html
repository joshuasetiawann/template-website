Welcome

Hands that understand the body

Wellspring Bodywork is a focused massage practice. We treat pain and tension with the same care a physio brings to movement — but with the comfort of a great massage.

Each session starts with a short assessment. We want to understand where it hurts, how you move and what your body has been carrying.
