Our story

The People & Place Behind Veil & Vow

A bridal beauty studio creating timeless, luminous looks for your wedding morning and beyond.

How we began

Veil & Vow is a bridal beauty studio devoted entirely to weddings. We create looks that feel like you — only luminous, and built to last every tear and dance.

Every bride begins with a trial. We listen, test and refine until the look is exactly right, then recreate it flawlessly on the day.

We come to you. On the morning itself we set up wherever you're getting ready, keeping the room calm, on schedule and full of quiet excitement.

From the bride to the whole party, we make sure everyone glows in the photos — and feels wonderful long after the confetti settles.

Today, Veil & Vow welcomes clients from across Charleston and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.

“Amelia made the morning serene and I looked like the best version of myself. Fourteen hours, still flawless.”
