Welcome

Fragrance as a fingerprint

Maison de Sève is a niche perfume boutique for people who believe scent is personal. We don't chase trends; we help you find what feels like you.

Our fragrance advisors are trained noses. A consultation here is a guided journey through families and notes until something simply clicks.
