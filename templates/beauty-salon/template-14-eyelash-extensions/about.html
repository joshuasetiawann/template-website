Our story

The People & Place Behind Featherlight Lash Lab

A dedicated lash lab crafting weightless, custom extensions for fuller, fluttery eyes.

How we began

Featherlight Lash Lab does one thing exceptionally well: lash extensions tailored entirely to you.

We map each set to your eye shape, lash health and the look you want — from a barely-there enhancement to dramatic mega-volume.

Our artists hand-make every fan and isolate every natural lash, so your set is weightless, comfortable and kind to your own lashes.

With a quick infill every few weeks, your lashes stay full indefinitely. Mornings become a non-event — in the best possible way.

Today, Featherlight Lash Lab welcomes clients from across Phoenix and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.
