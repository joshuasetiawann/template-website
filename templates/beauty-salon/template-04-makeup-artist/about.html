Our story

The People & Place Behind Noir Atelier

An editorial makeup studio creating bold, camera-ready looks for events, shoots and the spotlight.

How we began

Noir Atelier is a makeup studio built for impact. We create looks that read on camera, hold under lights and still feel like you up close.

Our artists come from fashion, film and editorial. That means clean technique, fearless colour and an eye for the detail a photographer will notice.

We work from a SoHo studio and on location — backstage, on set and at the kind of events where the makeup has to last from first light to last dance.

Whether it's a magazine cover or your own headline moment, we treat the chair like a stage and your face like the story.

Today, Noir Atelier welcomes clients from across New York and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.
