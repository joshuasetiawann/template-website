Our story

The People & Place Behind Wink & Arch

A friendly lash and brow studio shaping eyes that wake up gorgeous, every single morning.

How we began

Wink & Arch is a cosy studio devoted entirely to the frame of your face. We shape brows and build lashes that make mornings effortless.

We map every brow to your bone structure and every lash set to your eye shape — because cookie-cutter never suits anyone.

Our stylists are precise, patient and a little bit obsessed with symmetry. You'll leave looking like the most rested version of yourself.

Lifts, tints, extensions, laminations — whatever the look, it's low-maintenance by design. Set it once, enjoy it for weeks.

Today, Wink & Arch welcomes clients from across Denver and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.

“I genuinely don't touch my eyes in the morning anymore. The hybrid set is perfect.”
