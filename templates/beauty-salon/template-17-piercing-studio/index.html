Welcome

Piercing done properly

Lobe & Co. is the friendly, modern piercing studio — bright, spotless and a million miles from intimidating.

Our piercers are extensively trained and use only implant-grade jewellery and single-use, sterile needles. Safety is everything.

  • Needle, not gun
  • Quality metal only
