Our story

The People & Place Behind Lumen Skin Clinic

A results-driven skincare clinic offering advanced facials and medical-grade treatments.

How we began

Lumen Skin Clinic bridges the gap between a relaxing facial and clinical results. Every treatment is grounded in skin science and tailored to a plan.

We begin with a thorough skin analysis — under magnification and with your history in hand — so we treat causes, not just symptoms.

Our therapists are qualified in advanced modalities: chemical peels, microneedling, LED and professional-grade facials, all delivered safely.

We track your progress visit to visit. The goal isn't a glow that fades by morning — it's healthier, stronger skin over time.

Today, Lumen Skin Clinic welcomes clients from across San Francisco and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.
