Our story

The People & Place Behind Stillwater Spa

An urban day spa offering massage, facials and thermal rituals for deep, restorative calm.

How we began

Stillwater Spa is a sanctuary a few steps from the city's noise. Step inside and the temperature, the light and the sound all soften at once.

Our therapists are trained across deep-tissue, lymphatic and holistic techniques. Each treatment begins with a wellness check so the pressure and focus are right for you, today.

Beyond the treatment rooms lies our thermal suite — a sequence of heat, steam and cool plunge designed to ease tension before you've even lain down.

We believe rest isn't a luxury but a necessity. An afternoon here is an investment in how the rest of your week will feel.

Today, Stillwater Spa welcomes clients from across Portland and beyond. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll find the same warm welcome, the same exacting standards, and the same belief that looking after yourself should feel wonderful.
