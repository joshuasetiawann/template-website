The Cause

Why we gather

One night of generosity, a year of opportunity.

The Mission

The Lumen Foundation funds after-school learning for children in under-served neighbourhoods.

The Impact

Last year, your support reached 4,200 students across 38 schools.

The Need

This year we aim higher — to open ten new learning centres by next autumn.

The Night

Every ticket, bid and pledge tonight goes directly to that goal.
