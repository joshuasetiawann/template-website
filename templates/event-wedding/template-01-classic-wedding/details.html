In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Eleanor & Theodore, in one place.

When people ask how we met, the honest answer is: over a battered library novel that neither of us would let go. Eleanor & Theodore have been telling — and gently arguing over — that story ever since, and it still makes us laugh.

Seven summers later, here we are. We have moved cities, adopted a hopeless dog, restored half a cottage and learned that the best evenings are the quiet ordinary ones at the kitchen table.

We chose Rosewood Manor & Gardens because its gardens hosted so many of our Sunday walks. It felt right to say our vows where so much of our life together has already happened.

On Saturday, 19 September 2026 we will gather everyone we love into one place for one long, warm afternoon. There will be vows beneath the oaks, supper in the conservatory and dancing until the lanterns burn low.

Whatever the weather, it will be a day made of the people in the room. Thank you for being one of them — this page holds everything you need to celebrate it with us.

Getting There

Rosewood Manor & Gardens

A restored 1840s estate twenty minutes from downtown Charleston.

  • By Car

    Complimentary valet at the main gate; overflow parking signposted along Magnolia Lane.

  • Staying Over

    Blocks held at The Vendue and The Spectator — mention “Eleanor & Theodore”.

  • Timing

    Allow 20 minutes from the historic district; the ceremony begins promptly.

1847 Magnolia Lane, Charleston, SC 29403

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.
