In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Aero One Reveal, in one place.

Aero One has been three years in the making, and on launch night we finally take the cover off in front of the people who will use it first.

This is not a webinar with slides — it is a live reveal: the hardware on stage, the team behind it answering real questions, and the first units in the room.

We are gathering at The Foundry, an industrial space turned showroom, with hands-on stations, a keynote stage and a launch-night party to follow.

On Wednesday, 30 September 2026 the lights go down, the keynote begins, and Aero One steps into the light for the very first time.

Whether you are press, a partner or a day-one believer, this page has the programme, the tickets and everything you need for the night.
