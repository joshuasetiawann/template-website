In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Midnight 2027, in one place.

Midnight 2027 is the New Year's Eve party the year deserves: a glittering ballroom, a live band, and a countdown you will actually remember.

We built it for people who like to mark the moment properly — champagne, dancing, a midnight balloon drop and a room full of people ready to celebrate.

It all happens at The Skyline Ballroom, high above the city, with floor-to-ceiling windows framing the skyline as the clock ticks toward twelve.

On Thursday, 31 December 2026 the doors open on a night of music, fizz and good company, building to the countdown and the first toast of the new year.

Black tie, big energy, late finish. This page has the timings, the tickets and everything you need to ring it in.

The Venue

The Skyline Ballroom

The 40th-floor ballroom of the Meridian Tower, midtown Manhattan.

  • Arrival

    Valet at the Meridian Plaza entrance; rideshare drop-off on 52nd Street.

  • Transit

    57th St (F) and 7th Ave (B, D, E) are both a short walk.

  • Dress Code

    Black tie with a twist — sequins, gold and your boldest best.

5 Meridian Plaza, 40th Floor, New York, NY 10019

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.
