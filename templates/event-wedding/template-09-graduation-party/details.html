In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Class of 2026, in one place.

Four years of early lectures, late nights and questionable coffee are finally behind us, and the Class of 2026 is ready to celebrate properly.

This is the party we promised ourselves somewhere around finals week: caps off, music up and the whole cohort back together one more time.

We are gathering at Lakeside Hall, a hall big enough for all of us, our families and a dance floor that will see some action.

On Saturday, 27 June 2026 we will toast the diplomas, retell the stories that should probably stay untold, and send each other off into whatever comes next.

Whether you are heading to a job, more study or a long-overdue holiday, come celebrate the milestone. Details are all here.

Location

Lakeside Hall

A bright event hall overlooking Lake Mendota.

  • Parking

    Free guest parking in Lot 60, a short walk along the lakeshore.

  • Transit

    Route 80 stops at the door; rideshare drop-off at the north entrance.

  • Arrival

    Doors at 5:45; the toast begins promptly at 6:30.

1200 Lakeshore Path, Madison, WI 53706

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.

Good to Know

What to know

A few practical notes so the day runs smoothly for you.

Dress code

Smart casual with a celebratory edge — wear the outfit, keep the comfortable shoes for dancing.

The night

Speeches early, party late. Families are welcome for the toast and the first hour.

Buffet dinner and a bar; flag dietary needs in your RSVP and we will sort it.

Getting there

Rideshare to the main entrance; there is free parking on site for those driving.
