In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Sienna & Leo, in one place.

Sienna & Leo are engaged, and frankly we cannot stop grinning about it. Before the big wedding planning begins, we want one brilliant night with our favourite people.

This is the fun part: no formality, no seating charts, just cocktails on a rooftop and everyone we love in one place.

We are throwing the party at The Rooftop at Coral Club, where the sunset does most of the decorating for us and the playlist does the rest.

On Saturday, 18 July 2026 we will toast the engagement, tell the embarrassing how-he-asked story at least twice, and dance under the string lights.

Come thirsty and ready to celebrate. Everything you need to know is right here.
