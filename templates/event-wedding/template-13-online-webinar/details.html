The whole story

Everything worth knowing about The Growth Lab Live, in one place.

The Growth Lab Live is a focused online session for operators who want practical growth tactics, not recycled theory.

We built it for busy people: ninety minutes, three expert hosts, zero filler, and a workbook you can act on the same afternoon.

It streams live to wherever you are — no travel, no venue, just a link, a notepad and a live Q&A where your questions actually get answered.

On Thursday, 30 July 2026 we will walk through real playbooks, pull apart real numbers, and take your questions live until the time runs out.
