In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Aisha & Yusuf, in one place.

Aisha & Yusuf were introduced by family, and what began as a careful conversation has become the steady, joyful partnership we are about to make permanent before Allah.

Our nikah is, above all, a sacred promise — but it is also a celebration of two families becoming one, with all the warmth, food and laughter that brings.

We will gather at Al-Andalus Hall, a hall chosen for its calm and its beauty, where the ceremony and the walima can both be held with grace.

On Saturday, 5 December 2026 the imam will conduct the nikah, the contract will be witnessed, and then we will feast and celebrate together as one community.

We would be honoured by your duas and your presence. This page holds the timings, the venue details and everything else you may need.

The Venue

Al-Andalus Hall

A grand banqueting hall with separate prayer facilities.

  • Parking

    Free on-site parking for 200 cars; accessible bays beside the main entrance.

  • Prayer Space

    Wudu facilities and a prayer room are available throughout the evening.

  • Seating

    Family-arranged seating; please speak to an usher on arrival.

12 Coventry Road, Birmingham, B10 0RX

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.

What to know

Modest formal attire is requested. Rich colours are welcome; please ensure shoulders and knees are covered.

The nikah is conducted with reverence — please arrive in good time and silence phones beforehand.

A full walima dinner follows the ceremony; all dietary needs are halal and can be tailored on request.
