In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about ShiftStack 2026, in one place.

ShiftStack 2026 is two days for the people who build and run distributed systems — the platform engineers, the language nerds, the on-call veterans.

We started ShiftStack because the best conversations always happen in the hallway, so we built an event that is mostly excellent hallway with a world-class agenda attached.

This year we gather at Pier 48 Convention Center, with four tracks, hands-on labs, a packed expo floor and a closing party you will actually want to attend.

On Wednesday, 21 October 2026 the doors open on keynotes about the decade ahead, deep dives into the tools you use daily, and clinics where you bring your real problems.

Whether you are shipping to thirty regions or just curious about what is next, this page has the agenda, the venue and everything you need to plan your two days.

Venue

Pier 48 Convention Center

A waterfront hall with bay views and very good coffee.

  • Transit

    Muni T-Third stops two blocks away; Caltrain is a 12-minute walk.

  • Parking

    Event parking in Lot A; EV chargers on level 2.
