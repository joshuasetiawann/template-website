The Artists

Eleven points of view

A selection of the voices in Liminal. Full catalogue available at the door.

Yuki Tanaka

Painting — large-scale colour fields that dissolve at their edges.

Idris Bello

Sculpture — cast concrete forms caught mid-transformation.

Marta Kowalski

Photography — long exposures of empty thresholds at dawn.

Sun-Woo Lee

Installation — a room of shifting light you can walk through.

Amara Diop

Textile — woven works that map migration and memory.

Felix Hartmann

Mixed media — found objects suspended between use and ruin.
