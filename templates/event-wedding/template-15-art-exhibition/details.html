In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Liminal: New Works, in one place.

Liminal: New Works brings together a year of new pieces exploring the spaces between — between places, states and selves.

The exhibition is built to be walked slowly: a considered route through paintings, sculpture and sound, with room to pause and look properly.

We are showing at The Halden Gallery, whose clean, daylit rooms let each work breathe and reward an unhurried second visit.

On Saturday, 3 October 2026 we open with a private view — the artists in the room, a few words on the work, and the first chance to see it all.

Whether you come for the opening or any day after, this page has the programme, visiting details and everything you need.

The Artists

Eleven points of view

A selection of the voices in Liminal. Full catalogue available at the door.

Yuki Tanaka

Painting — large-scale colour fields that dissolve at their edges.

Idris Bello

Sculpture — cast concrete forms caught mid-transformation.

Marta Kowalski

Photography — long exposures of empty thresholds at dawn.

Sun-Woo Lee

Installation — a room of shifting light you can walk through.

Amara Diop

Textile — woven works that map migration and memory.

Felix Hartmann

Mixed media — found objects suspended between use and ruin.
