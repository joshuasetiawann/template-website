In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Robert & Margaret, in one place.

Forty years ago, Robert and Margaret promised each other forever, and somehow it has gone faster and meant more than either of them imagined.

Four decades, three children, six grandchildren and one very patient garden later, they would like to do the thing they do best: gather everyone they love and feed them well.

We will celebrate at The Old Vineyard House, a place that has hosted so many of the family's milestones that it feels like an extension of home.

On Saturday, 5 September 2026 we will raise a glass to forty years, share the stories the grandchildren have not heard yet, and dance the way they did at the very first party.

Whether you have known them four years or forty, come help mark the occasion. Everything you need is here.

Getting There
