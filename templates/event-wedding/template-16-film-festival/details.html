In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Northlight Film Festival, in one place.

The Northlight Film Festival is six nights of cinema that takes risks — new voices, restored classics and the kind of films that stay with you on the walk home.

We programme for people who love the dark of the cinema: bold features, sharp shorts, and Q&As with the filmmakers who made them.

This year we screen at The Aurora Picture House, a grand old picture house with the kind of screen and sound these films deserve.

On 19–24 November 2026 the festival opens, and from there it is nightly screenings, panels and late conversations until the closing gala.

Whether you come for one film or buy the full pass, this page has the programme, the venue and everything you need.

The Venue

The Aurora Picture House

A lovingly restored 1923 cinema on the edge of the Old Town.

  • By Tram

    West End–Princes Street stop is a five-minute walk.

  • Parking

    Castle Terrace car park is two minutes away; no on-site parking.

  • Doors

    House opens 30 minutes before each screening; latecomers seated at a pause.

8 Lothian Road, Edinburgh, EH1 2DJ

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.

Good to Know

What to know

A few practical notes so the day runs smoothly for you.

Tickets

Single screenings or a full festival pass; popular nights sell out, so book the must-sees early.

The format

Nightly screenings from 7:00 PM, with filmmaker Q&As after selected features.

Access

The Aurora Picture House is step-free to the stalls, with captioned and audio-described screenings each night.

Late shows

Midnight cult screenings run all week — strong stuff, and not for the faint-hearted.
