In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Solstice Sound 2026, in one place.

Solstice Sound is three days of music under open sky — headliners after dark, hidden gems at noon, and a tent city full of new friends in between.

We built Solstice for people who come for the whole weekend: the sunrise sets, the food trucks, the art installations and the crowd that becomes a community by Sunday.

This year the fields are at Meadowlark Fields, with multiple stages, a dance tent that never sleeps and a campsite a short walk from the music.

On Friday–Sunday, 14–16 August 2026 the gates open and the first downbeat lands; from there it is non-stop until the closing set sends everyone home glowing.

Whether you are camping the whole weekend or coming for one big night, this page has the lineup, the site map and everything you need to plan it.

Festival Grounds

Meadowlark Fields

Eighty acres of open meadow beneath the Flatirons.

  • Driving

    Pre-paid parking and campervan lots; carpool lane jumps the queue.

  • Shuttles

    Free shuttles run from downtown Boulder and the airport park-and-ride.

  • Come Prepared

    Sun, dust and altitude — bring water, a hat and a warm layer for night.

9000 Meadowlark Road, Boulder, CO 80301

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.

Good to Know

What to know

A few practical notes so the day runs smoothly for you.

What to bring

Sun cream, a refillable bottle, sturdy shoes and a layer for when the desert night turns cool.

Camping

The campsite is a short walk from the stages; pitch early and leave no trace when you go.

Food & drink

Forty vendors and water refill points across Meadowlark Fields; cashless everywhere, so bring a card.

Stay safe

Welfare tents, free water and a friendly crew are always nearby — look after each other.
