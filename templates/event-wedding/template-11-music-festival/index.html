Festival Grounds

Meadowlark Fields

Eighty acres of open meadow beneath the Flatirons.

  • Driving

    Pre-paid parking and campervan lots; carpool lane jumps the queue.

  • Shuttles

    Free shuttles run from downtown Boulder and the airport park-and-ride.

  • Come Prepared

    Sun, dust and altitude — bring water, a hat and a warm layer for night.

9000 Meadowlark Road, Boulder, CO 80301

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.
