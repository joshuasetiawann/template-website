In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about Capital Cup 2026, in one place.

The Capital Cup is a weekend of fast, friendly, fiercely contested football, with sixteen teams chasing one trophy.

We run it for the love of the game: grassroots sides, future stars and a crowd that turns up for every minute, from the first whistle to the final penalty.

Matches are played across Riverside Sports Park, on full-size pitches with proper stands, food trucks and a fan zone for the families.

On Saturday–Sunday, 8–9 August 2026 the group stages kick off, and across two days it builds to a final that, more often than not, comes down to the wire.

Whether you are playing, coaching or cheering, this page has the fixtures, the venue and everything you need for the weekend.

The Grounds

Riverside Sports Park

Eight full-size pitches along the Anacostia River.

  • Metro

    Anacostia (Green Line) is a 12-minute walk; free shuttle on match days.

  • Parking

    Free event parking in Lots C and D; arrive early for the 9 AM kick-offs.

  • Come Ready

    Bring sun cream, a chair and a team to cheer for. Pitches are grass.

2500 Riverside Drive SE, Washington, D.C. 20020

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.

Good to Know

What to know

A few practical notes so the day runs smoothly for you.

For players

Arrive an hour before your first match for check-in and warm-up; bring both kit colours.

For fans

Stands and a fan zone at Riverside Sports Park, with food trucks and a big screen for the later rounds.

Facilities

Changing rooms, physio and water stations on site; the ground is step-free for spectators.

Weather

Matches run rain or shine; bring layers and waterproofs for the stands just in case.
