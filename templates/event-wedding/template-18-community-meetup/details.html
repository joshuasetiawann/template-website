In Full

The whole story

Everything worth knowing about The Riverside Get-Together, in one place.

The Riverside Get-Together is exactly what it sounds like: neighbours, old and new, gathering by the water for a relaxed afternoon together.

There is no agenda beyond good company — a shared picnic, a few games for the kids, live music from local musicians and plenty of time to actually talk.

We are meeting at Riverside Community Green, a stretch of riverside green with shade, space to spread out and a bandstand for the music.

On Saturday, 11 July 2026 we will set out the blankets, fire up the community barbecue and spend the afternoon being the kind of neighbourhood we all want to live in.

Everyone is welcome, all ages, no ticket needed. This page has the timings, what to bring and how to lend a hand.

About the Day

Why we gather

Because the best neighbourhoods are the ones that actually know each other.

It Started Small

Three families, one barbecue, in 2019. This year we expect three hundred.

It’s for Everyone

Newcomers and old-timers, toddlers and grandparents — there is a corner for all.

It’s Run by Us

Volunteers from the street plan, cook and clear up. Hands always welcome.

It Gives Back

Donations from the cake stall go to the riverside path restoration fund.

The Plan

How the day runs

Drop in any time — it runs all afternoon.

  1. 11:00 AM

    Doors Open

    Tea, coffee and the welcome table.

  2. 12:00 PM

    The Big Lunch

    Bring-a-dish picnic on the green.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Kids’ Zone

    Bouncy castle, games and face paints.

  4. 3:00 PM

    Live Music

    Local bands and the community choir.

  5. 4:30 PM

    Bake Sale & Raffle

    For the riverside path fund.

  6. 6:00 PM

    Wind Down

    Pack up together; many hands make light work.

Where to Find Us

Riverside Community Green

The big open green by the river, behind the community hall.

  • On Foot

    A two-minute walk from the high street; the green is signposted from Mill Lane.

  • Parking

    Street parking only — please leave the close-by spaces for those who need them.

  • Wet Weather

    A gazebo village and the community hall keep things going, rain or shine.

Riverside Green, off Mill Lane, Bristol, BS3 4QR

Directions: head for the main entrance; signage and stewards will guide you on arrival.
