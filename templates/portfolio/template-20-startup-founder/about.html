The longer story

A bit more about me

I build companies from zero. Two exits and one ongoing taught me that the founder’s real job is sequencing — knowing which impossible thing to do first, and which to ignore until it is allowed to become a problem.

My pattern is the same each time: find a painful, boring problem; build the smallest thing that fixes it; sell it before it is ready; then earn the right to make it good. Relay, my current company, is that story playing out again.

I am a product-and-distribution founder more than a technical or financial one. I care most about the wedge — the one sharp use case that gets a stranger to change their workflow — and the story that makes it spread.

I lead by writing. Clear memos beat clever meetings; a company that can read the same paragraph and act is faster than one that cannot.
