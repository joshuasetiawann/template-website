How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Find the wedge

    One painful, specific problem and the smallest product that removes it. Breadth is a luxury you earn later.

  2. 02

    Sell early

    Put it in front of buyers before it is ready. Their objections are the roadmap; their yes is the proof.

  3. 03

    Build to keep

    Earn the right to add surface area by making the core genuinely loved first.

  4. 04

    Scale the story

    Distribution and narrative — the wedge spreads on a story a stranger can repeat.
