Overview

Overview

Freelancers wait weeks for money they earned in hours. Relay pays out same-day against verified invoices — boring infrastructure, life-changing latency.

Two years in: 12k freelancers paid, default rate under one percent, and a risk model that improves with every invoice. Series A closed last fall.

The brief

The challenge

Moving money fast is easy; moving it fast and safely is the company. The first year was spent on risk plumbing nobody can see.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Live the problem

    Every venture started as a spreadsheet I was personally maintaining at midnight. Founder-problem fit before product-market fit.

  2. 02

    Sell before building

    Landing page, ten hard conversations, three signed letters of intent — then code. Demand is the only spec.

  3. 03

    Ship the embarrassing version

    Launch when it solves one real workflow badly-but-honestly. Iterate weekly against retention, not roadmap.

  4. 04

    Compound or close honestly

    Double down on what retains; write the post-mortem when it does not. Both outcomes are documented in public.
