The studio

About

Osaka-based illustrator drawing with brush pens, gouache and a scanner older than some of my clients. Ten years of editorial spots, picture books and packaging that aims for ‘gentle, but not bland’.

I sketch on paper first, always. Color happens digitally, in palettes stolen from fruit stands and 1970s train posters. Picture books are the long game — two published, a third in roughs.
