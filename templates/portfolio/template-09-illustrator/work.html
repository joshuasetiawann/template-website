How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Read & sketch

    I read the piece, then thumbnail relentlessly. The first idea is rarely the right one; the tenth often is.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Two or three rough directions with rationale — we choose the image before I commit the line.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Final art in the medium the idea wants: ink, gouache or vector, built to reproduce cleanly.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Files in every size and format a client needs, plus one round of considered revisions.
