The longer story

A bit more about me

I draw for a living and have done for over a decade — editorial illustrations, book covers, and the occasional sprawling mural. My line is my voice; clients come to me for a way of seeing, not just a deliverable.

Most of my work begins with reading. An editorial piece is an argument I have to picture; a cover is a whole book compressed into one honest image. I sketch badly and often until the idea arrives, then draw it properly once.

I work in ink and gouache as happily as I do in vector. The medium follows the idea — texture where it needs warmth, clean line where it needs to carry across a poster.

I care about the brief and the deadline as much as the drawing. An illustration that misses the point beautifully is still a miss.
