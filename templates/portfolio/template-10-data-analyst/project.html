Fleet utilisation dashboard

Live utilisation and idle-time views for 800 vehicles; surfaced £1.2M of recoverable capacity.

Overview

Overview

Northgate ran 400 trucks on instinct and a wall of spreadsheets. I built the utilisation dashboard the depot managers actually open at 6am.

Live load factors, dead-leg miles and idle hours by depot, with drill-downs that answer the follow-up question before it is asked.

The brief

The challenge

The first version was ignored — too many charts. The redesign cut it to five numbers and a map, and adoption followed within a month.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Frame the question

    Decision first, metric second: what will change if this number moves? No dashboard begins without a named decision-maker.

  2. 02

    Audit the data

    Lineage walks, null censuses and a reconciliation against finance. Trust is built in the plumbing, not the chart.

  3. 03

    Model & test

    dbt models with tests on every join; experiments sized before launch and pre-registered so the readout cannot be argued backwards.

  4. 04

    Tell the answer

    One headline, three numbers, the caveat in plain sight. The deck ends with a recommended action, never with "more research needed".
