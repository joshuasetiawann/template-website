The longer story

A bit more about me

I turn messy data into decisions people actually make. Eight years across product and finance teams taught me that the hard part is rarely the model — it is the question. Get the question right and the SQL writes itself.

My work spans dashboards, experiment design and the unglamorous data plumbing that makes both trustworthy. I am happiest when a metric I built ends an argument that used to go in circles.

I am obsessive about honesty in numbers: clear definitions, confidence intervals, and a chart that does not lie by axis. A dashboard nobody trusts is worse than no dashboard at all.

I write so non-analysts can follow. The insight is only worth the work if the person who can act on it understands it in one read.
