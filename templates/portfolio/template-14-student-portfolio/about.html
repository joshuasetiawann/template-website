The longer story

A bit more about me

I am a final-year design and computer-science student who learns by building real things. My portfolio is mostly project work — some for coursework, some for clubs, some just because the problem would not leave me alone.

I sit happily between design and code, which means I can take an idea from sketch to working prototype on my own. I am at my best on a small team with a real deadline and a problem worth caring about.

I am early in my career and honest about it: what I lack in years I make up for in curiosity, speed and a genuine love of the craft. I ask a lot of questions and I ship.

Outside of class I run a small student dev group, mentor first-years, and keep a notebook of half-built ideas that occasionally turn into something real.
