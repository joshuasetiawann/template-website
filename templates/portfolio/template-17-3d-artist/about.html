The longer story

A bit more about me

I make things that do not exist look like they were photographed. Look development is equal parts art and detective work — studying how light actually behaves on a surface, then rebuilding that truth in a shader.

My work spans product visualisation, character and environment look-dev, and the occasional full CG sequence. I love the patient part: the tenth lighting iteration, the subtle subsurface tweak, the moment a render stops looking like a render.

I think in references and breakdowns. Before I build anything I gather how the real thing scatters, reflects and wears — then I match it, then I art-direct it past reality where the shot needs drama.

I optimise as I go. A gorgeous look that never finishes rendering is not finished; I build shaders and scenes that hold up at production scale.
