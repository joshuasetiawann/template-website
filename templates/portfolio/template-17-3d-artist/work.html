How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Reference

    Gather how light really behaves on the surface — reflectance, scatter, wear. You cannot fake what you have not studied.

  2. 02

    Block

    Greyscale lighting and rough materials first, to get the read and the mood before the detail.

  3. 03

    Look-dev

    Build shaders to match reference, then art-direct past it where the shot wants drama.

  4. 04

    Render & finish

    Optimised passes, denoise, comp and grade — finished frames that hold up at production scale.
