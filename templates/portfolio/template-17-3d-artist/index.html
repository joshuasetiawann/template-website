Behind the orb

About me

Gothenburg-based 3D generalist, nine years between game studios and product viz. I model clean, light cinematically, and treat shaders like a second art degree. Iridescence is a personality trait at this point.

Currently splitting time between real-time environment packs and high-gloss product film for brands that want CGI you can't immediately clock as CGI.
