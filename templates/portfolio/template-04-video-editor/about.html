The longer story

A bit more about me

I cut for feeling first. The technical craft — conform, grade, sound — is in service of one question: does this land? Twelve years in edit suites taught me that the edit is a second script, and the best cut is often the one with the most left out.

My work runs from brand films and music videos to long-form documentary. I am as comfortable building a ten-version social cutdown pack as I am living inside a ninety-minute timeline for months, hunting for the structure hiding in the rushes.

I finish what I cut: a colour grade that holds a mood, a mix that is loud where it should be and silent where it counts, and delivery masters that pass QC the first time. The polish is not separate from the story — it is the story, dressed.

I love a paper edit. Before a single clip moves, I want the beat map: the spine of the thing, written down, so the timeline has somewhere to go.
