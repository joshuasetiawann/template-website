How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Screen & log

    Watch everything, twice. Log the moments that make the room go quiet — those are the edit, the rest is connective tissue.

  2. 02

    Paper edit

    Structure on paper first: the spine, the turns, the ending. A timeline without a beat map just wanders.

  3. 03

    Assembly

    Build the story long, then cut to the bone. Two revision rounds keep it honest, not infinite.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Conform, colour grade, sound mix and delivery masters — finished to spec, passed QC, delivered on time.
