Quick questions

Do you edit and grade, or just one?

Both, usually together — but I am happy to come in for a finishing grade on someone else’s cut.

What do you need to start?

Organised rushes, a brief, and any locked music or VO. If it is a long form, an afternoon to screen everything together.

Remote or in-suite?

Either. Most reviews run on Frame.io; I am glad to be in the room for the final grade.

Rather just say hi?

No brief required. If our work might overlap, I would love to hear from you.
