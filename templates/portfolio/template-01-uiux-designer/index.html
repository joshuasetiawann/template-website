About me

I'm a Stockholm-born, Copenhagen-based designer with eight years spent untangling complex SaaS workflows. My happy place is the gap between a research insight and the first greyscale wireframe — that's where products are actually decided.

I run lightweight discovery sprints, design in systems rather than screens, and hand off specs engineers genuinely like. Lately I have been deep in design tokens and accessibility audits for enterprise dashboards.
