Before you write

Quick questions

Do you work fixed-scope or retainer?

Both. Discovery sprints and design-system starters are fixed-scope; embedded product design runs 2–3 days a week on a rolling retainer.

Can you work with our engineers directly?

Always. I design in tokens, annotate for build, and join your standups. The best handoff is the one that never needs a meeting.

What do you need to start?

A real problem, access to a few users, and someone who can say yes. I will bring the research plan and the whiteboard.

Rather just say hi?

No brief required. If our work might overlap, I would love to hear from you.
