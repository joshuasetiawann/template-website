Loopwise onboarding redesign

Cut activation drop-off 31% by rebuilding a 9-step signup into 3 adaptive screens with inline education.

  • UX
  • SaaS
Role
Lead Product Designer
Client
Loopwise
Year
2025
Timeline
9 weeks

Overview

Overview

Loopwise came to us with a nine-step signup that was bleeding trial users. I led design end to end — research, flows, UI and the experiment plan.

We rebuilt onboarding as three adaptive screens that teach the product while collecting only what the first session needs. Education moved inline, jargon moved out.

The brief

The challenge

Every team owned one of the nine steps, and each had a metric attached to it. The real work was political: proving with session recordings that fewer questions earlier meant better data later.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Discover

    A two-week research sprint: stakeholder interviews, analytics review and five-second tests to find where the experience actually breaks.

  2. 02

    Define

    Journey maps and a sharply-worded problem statement, signed by product and engineering before a single pixel moves.

  3. 03

    Design

    Greyscale wireframes first, then high-fidelity flows built from the design system tokens, prototyped in Figma.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Moderated usability rounds, WCAG checks and an annotated handoff engineers can build without a meeting.
