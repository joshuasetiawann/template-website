The longer story

A bit more about me

I design products end to end, from the first fuzzy problem to the polished handoff. Most of my work is mobile-first, which is a useful discipline: small screens punish vague thinking and reward a clear hierarchy of one thing at a time.

I came up through agencies and startups, which means I can frame a problem and also push the pixels. I like teams where design is a verb everyone does, not a department that gets handed tickets.

My happiest work lives at the seam between research and shipping: a tested prototype, a tight component set, and a metric that moved because the design got out of the user’s way. I am suspicious of beauty that does not test.

I prototype to think. A clickable flow answers questions a static mock never will — and it makes the team argue about the right things, early, while change is cheap.
