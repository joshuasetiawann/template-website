How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Turn a vague request into a sharp problem statement and a couple of testable hypotheses everyone signs.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Wide and cheap: many rough directions, mobile-first, sketched and pressure-tested before fidelity.

  3. 03

    Refine

    One direction, built from components, prototyped and put in front of real users twice.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Annotated handoff, edge cases scoped, and a follow-up to see whether the metric actually moved.
