Hello

About me

Bengaluru-based product designer, seven years across fintech and health apps. I like messy zero-to-one problems: ambiguous briefs, regulatory constraints, and users who have never been in a research panel.

My process is unfancy — sketch ugly, test early, polish what survives. I prototype in code when motion matters and keep a personal library of 200+ recorded usability sessions.
