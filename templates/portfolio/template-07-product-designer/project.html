Paisa savings app redesign

Round-up savings flow rebuilt for first-time investors; weekly retention up 24% post-launch.

  • Fintech
  • Mobile
Role
Senior Product Designer
Client
Paisa
Year
2025
Timeline
12 weeks

Overview

Overview

Paisa’s savings app served first-time savers with a screen built for traders. I led the redesign of the home and goal flows.

We rebuilt around one question — "am I on track?" — with goals as cards, deposits as one thumb action, and jargon translated into rupees and dates.

The brief

The challenge

Trust is the currency in fintech for new savers: every simplification had to be auditable, with the full ledger one tap behind the calm surface.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Ground in research

    Field studies and support-ticket archaeology before opinions. The roadmap obeys what users do, not what they say.

  2. 02

    Frame the bet

    One-page hypothesis with a measurable outcome, agreed with PM and engineering before design begins.

  3. 03

    Design in systems

    Flows assembled from tokens and shared components; new patterns proposed back to the system, never forked.

  4. 04

    Test & instrument

    Prototype tests before build, event tracking specified with the design, and a readout honest enough to kill the feature.
