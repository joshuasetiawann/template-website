How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Board

    Storyboard the idea in rough frames. If it does not read as stills, more keyframes will not save it.

  2. 02

    Animatic

    A timed, scrappy cut with scratch sound — the moment we agree the pacing before the polish.

  3. 03

    Animate

    Build it for real: weight, easing and detail, designed as a reusable motion vocabulary.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Grade, sound and delivery in every ratio a campaign needs, exported clean.
