Overview

Overview

An electronic-arts festival wanted an identity that performs rather than sits. I directed the kinetic type system for screens, stage and street.

The wordmark behaves like a signal: it stretches on bass, fractures on glitch cues, and reassembles for sponsors. VJs got a live rig, not a logo sheet.

The brief

The challenge

The identity had to survive operators improvising at 2am. We shipped behaviours with safe ranges instead of fixed animations — chaos, bounded.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Boards & rhythm

    Style frames and an animatic timed to scratch audio. If the animatic does not breathe, polish will not save it.

  2. 02

    Define the physics

    Every brand gets a physical logic — weight, drag, snap — written down before a single keyframe is set.

  3. 03

    Animate & iterate

    Hero shots first at full quality, then the system: type behaviour, transitions, loops. Daily renders, honest critiques.

  4. 04

    Package the system

    Tokens for duration and easing, exported rigs and a guideline film, so the motion survives my absence.
