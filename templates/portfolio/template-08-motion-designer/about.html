The longer story

A bit more about me

I direct motion — the title sequence, the product film, the ten seconds that has to explain a whole company. Motion is timing and weight before it is anything pretty; if the easing is wrong, no amount of polish saves it.

My work runs from broadcast and brand films to interface motion for product teams. I like the discipline of explaining something complicated in one confident move, and I like the craft of making a frame feel alive without showing off.

I design in storyboards and animatics before I open After Effects. Deciding the story in cheap, scrappy frames keeps the expensive frames focused.

I think about motion as a system, not a one-off: a vocabulary of transitions and timings a product team can reuse long after I have gone.
