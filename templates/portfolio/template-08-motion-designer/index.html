Backstory

About me

Lyon-born motion designer in Amsterdam. I design how brands move — logo behaviors, kinetic type systems, UI animation specs and the occasional broadcast package when nostalgia wins.

Half my work is animation; the other half is writing the rules so in-house teams can animate without me. Everything ships with easing curves documented, not guessed.

The road here

From broadcast idents to product motion systems, one ease-curve at a time.

  1. 2022 — Now

    Independent motion designer · Studio Moreau

    Motion identities and product animation for design-led brands across Europe.

  2. 2019 — 2022

    Senior Motion Designer · Framehouse Amsterdam

    Led motion on rebrands for two broadcasters and a global audio platform.
