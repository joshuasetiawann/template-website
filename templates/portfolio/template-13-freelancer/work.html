How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Plan

    A short call to understand the business, the customer and the one job the site must do. Then a clear fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Design

    A custom design in Figma — not a template — reviewed together until it feels like your business.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-coded, fast, responsive and accessible, with a CMS you can actually update yourself.

  4. 04

    Launch & care

    Training, a launch checklist, and an optional care plan for updates and small changes within 48 hours.
