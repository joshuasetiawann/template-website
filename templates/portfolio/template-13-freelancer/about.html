The longer story

A bit more about me

I am one pair of hands that designs and builds the whole website — strategy, design, code and the training call after launch. Small businesses do not need an agency’s overhead; they need someone who will actually finish the thing and pick up the phone.

Most of my clients are owner-run: a clinic, a cafe, a tradesperson, a studio. They want a site that works, loads fast, ranks locally and does not break the week I hand it over. I build exactly that, fixed-price, no jargon.

Doing both design and code is my advantage. Nothing gets lost in handoff because there is no handoff — I draw it, I build it, I own the result.

I believe in care plans, not abandonment. A website is a living thing; I stick around to keep it healthy, fast and current.
