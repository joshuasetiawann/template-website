The longer story

A bit more about me

I build systems that move money and events without losing either. Eight years as a backend and platform engineer taught me that the interesting problems are about correctness under load — exactly-once where it matters, graceful degradation everywhere else.

I work close to the metal of distributed systems: queues, idempotency, observability and the failure drills that prove your guarantees instead of asserting them. I would rather run a shadow migration for six weeks than cut over on hope.

I write open source and maintain it like it is in production, because some of it is. Good tooling is a force multiplier; a deploy CLI that just works saves a thousand small frustrations.

I care about the humans in the system too — runbooks people can follow at 3am, on-call rotations that do not burn engineers out, and design docs that make the hard decision legible.
