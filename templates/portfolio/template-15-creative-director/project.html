“Doors Open” — National Theatre season

Season identity that turned ticket stubs into collectible posters; subscriptions up 22%.

Overview

Overview

A season campaign for a theatre fighting the idea that it is not for everyone. "Doors Open" put the building’s backstage life on the posters.

Real prop-makers, wig rooms and fly towers shot at work — the season’s shows announced through the hands that build them. Box office followed.

The brief

The challenge

The institution wanted its stars; the strategy needed its staff. Winning that argument — politely, with research — was the campaign.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Interrogate the brief

    The first week is spent arguing with the brief until the real problem confesses. Strategy and craft start as one conversation.

  2. 02

    Find the organising idea

    One idea strong enough to brief a film crew, a typographer and a PR team without translation. If it needs a diagram, it is not it.

  3. 03

    Cast and direct the craft

    The right photographer, type foundry and edit house — then direction that protects the idea through a hundred small compromises.

  4. 04

    Land it everywhere

    From 48-sheet posters to a ticket stub: the campaign is judged at its weakest touchpoint, so none are allowed to be weak.
