The longer story

A bit more about me

I set the idea and hold the line. Twenty years in agencies and studios taught me that creative direction is mostly judgement — knowing which idea is brave enough, then protecting it through every meeting that wants to make it safe.

My work spans brand platforms, campaigns and the occasional full rebrand. I art-direct, I write when the line needs it, and I build the team that makes the thing — but my real job is keeping the work pointed at one clear idea.

I believe in taste as a discipline, not a mood. The best work is specific, confident and a little uncomfortable. Committees sand off exactly the edges that make it memorable; my job is to stop that.

I lead people as carefully as I lead work. Great creative comes from a team that feels safe enough to be brave — that culture is something you build on purpose.
