About

Twenty years in creative departments on both sides of the Atlantic, the last eight running them. I believe in big organising ideas, small egos, and presenting three routes where one is secretly a dare.

I direct writers, designers, photographers and the occasional perfumer. The work below outlived its media plans — which is the only test I fully trust.
