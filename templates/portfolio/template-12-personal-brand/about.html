The longer story

A bit more about me

I help founders and executives say one true thing clearly, again and again, until the market believes it. Personal brand is not vanity — it is distribution. The leader who can explain why their company matters in a sentence wins meetings the rest never get.

My work is part strategy, part editing, part nerve. I audit everything a leader has said, find the through-line they keep almost saying, and build the talks, posts and interviews that finally say it out loud.

I think in narrative arcs and twelve-month maps, not one-off posts. A reputation is built on repetition with discipline — the same idea, sharpened, across every stage and screen.

I am the calm voice in the room when the stakes are high: the keynote, the funding announcement, the crisis. Clarity under pressure is the whole job.
