Overview

Overview

The Patient Contrarian argues that the best founders are slow on purpose — contrarian in conviction, patient in proof. Fourteen stages booked it in a year.

The talk runs 38 minutes, no slides for the first ten. It has been rebuilt three times as the argument sharpened against audience pushback.

The brief

The challenge

A keynote earns its fee in the Q&A. The talk had to survive hostile questions from operators who lived the counter-examples.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Find the throughline

    Months of material mined for the one argument I can defend anywhere — keynotes, books and posts all serve it.

  2. 02

    Pressure-test privately

    New ideas rehearsed with operator roundtables long before any stage. If founders push back, the idea is not ready.

  3. 03

    Deliver in public

    Keynotes, essays and counsel engagements — the same thesis, fitted to the room, never read from notes.

  4. 04

    Compound the audience

    Every appearance feeds the newsletter and the next body of work. Attention is treated as capital, not applause.
