Salt Roads — Sicilian coast series

Eighteen months following salt harvesters in Trapani; 40-image essay, exhibited in Palermo and Arles.

  • Documentary
  • Travel
Role

Overview

Overview

Eighteen months on the Trapani salt flats, following the harvesters through a full season — flood, evaporation, harvest, rest.

The essay runs to forty frames: labour at dawn, the geometry of the pans, salt mountains against weather. Shot on medium format, printed at one metre.

The brief

The challenge

Trust was the project. The harvesters had been photographed before, badly, in a hurry. The first month produced no usable frames on purpose.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Scout & research

    Weeks of walking, permissions and coffee before the first frame. The picture list starts as a notebook of light and timings.

  2. 02

    Shoot patiently

    Small kit, available light where it holds, returning to the same places until people stop performing for the camera.

  3. 03

    Edit ruthlessly

    Thousands of frames down to dozens on the print wall. Sequence first, single images second — the story sets the order.

  4. 04

    Print & publish

    Colour-managed proofs, press checks and exhibition prints. A series is not done until it exists on paper.
