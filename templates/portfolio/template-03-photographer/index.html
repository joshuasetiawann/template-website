The eye

About me

Milan-born, lives wherever the assignment is. Fifteen years across editorial, reportage and the occasional stubborn personal project. I shoot slowly, mostly at eye level, and I print everything that matters.

Clients include travel magazines, NGOs and architects who want buildings shown with people still in them. Between commissions I teach a small darkroom workshop and keep a film diary nobody is allowed to art-direct.

The road here

Fifteen years of assignments, darkrooms and patient available light.

  1. 2018 — Now

    Independent photographer · Studio Marchetti

    Editorial and documentary commissions across Europe; representation for print sales in Milan.

  2. 2014 — 2018

    Staff photographer · Meridiana Magazine

    Travel and culture features; 30+ published stories, two covers a year.

  3. 2011 — 2014

    Photo assistant · Atelier Bregoli

    Lighting, logistics and large-format scanning for one of Milan’s last great still-life studios.

  4. 2009 — 2011

    Darkroom technician · Laboratorio Nove

    Printed other people’s negatives until I understood exactly what mine were missing.
