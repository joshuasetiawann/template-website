The longer story

A bit more about me

I help mid-market firms fix the unglamorous things: throughput, margins and meetings. Twenty years in operations taught me that most performance problems are not strategy problems — they are a process nobody has looked at end to end in years.

My engagements start by following the work, not the org chart. I trace an order, a ticket, a hire from start to finish and find where it waits. The waiting is almost always the problem, and the cheapest thing to fix.

I am independent on purpose. No upsell, no bench to feed — just a clear diagnosis, a short list of changes that pay for themselves, and the discipline to see them through with your team.

I leave clients more capable, not more dependent. The best engagement ends with your managers running the new rhythm without me in the room.
