How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Follow the work end to end — an order, a ticket, a hire — and find where it waits. The waiting is the cost.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    A short list of changes ranked by payback, not by how impressive they sound. Quick wins fund the hard ones.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Side by side with your managers, changing the actual process, not the slide about the process.

  4. 04

    Embed

    New rhythms, simple dashboards and the handover that leaves your team running it without me.
