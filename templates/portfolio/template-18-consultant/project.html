Throughput turnaround — Hadleigh Foods

Bottleneck analysis and shift redesign lifted line output 23% with zero capex.

  • Operations
  • Manufacturing
Role
Managing Consultant
Client
Hadleigh Foods
Year
2025
Timeline
14 weeks

Overview

Overview

A ready-meals manufacturer at 71% OEE with a full order book — leaving margin on the line every shift. We ran the throughput turnaround.

Changeover surgery, bottleneck rebalancing and a shift handover that takes eight minutes instead of forty. No capex, just sequence.

The brief

The challenge

The plant had survived three consultancies and trusted none. We started on nights, fixed the smallest grievance first, and earned the floor.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Diagnose on the floor

    The first fortnight lives in the plant, the depot, the order book — not the boardroom. Data validates what the floor already knows.

  2. 02

    Size the prize

    A baseline nobody disputes, then the value tree: every improvement priced, owned and dated before design begins.

  3. 03

    Pilot with the sceptics

    The loudest doubters run the pilot. If the change survives them, the rollout sells itself.

  4. 04

    Embed & exit

    Cadences, dashboards and trained owners — then we leave. Success is measured a year after the invoices stop.
