How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Read the site

    Sun path, prevailing wind, neighbours and ground. The first sketches are about the place, not the plan.

  2. 02

    Frame the brief

    What does the client actually need to do here, daily, for decades? We agree the problem before the form.

  3. 03

    Design in material

    Model in card and timber, test in light. The detail and the budget are designed together, never bolted on later.

  4. 04

    Deliver on site

    Drawings a builder trusts, site visits that catch problems early, and finishes that earn their place.
