The practice

About

Principal of a four-person practice in Valparaíso working between Chile and Spain. Twenty years of measured drawings, planning hearings and the slow pleasure of buildings coming out of the ground.

The studio's bias: keep the structure, change the plan. Most of our work begins with something already standing — a grain silo, a school, a house with good bones and bad additions.
