Silo Cultural Hall, Valparaíso

1920s grain silo converted to a 400-seat hall; new timber roof rides on the original concrete drum.

Overview

Overview

A decommissioned grain silo above the port, recast as a cultural hall without erasing its industrial bones. We led design from competition to opening.

The concrete bins became stacked galleries; a new timber crown holds the performance room. Eighty percent of the original structure remains in service.

The brief

The challenge

Heritage rules, seismic codes and a community that loved the ruin as it was — every move had to satisfy all three without splitting the difference.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Read the site

    Weeks of surveys, light studies and listening — to neighbours, to planners, to what the existing fabric refuses to do.

  2. 02

    Test massing

    Card models and courtyard diagrams before software. The cheapest version of every bad idea is made of paper.

  3. 03

    Develop the detail

    Material passports, 1:20 sections and the junctions that decide whether a building ages or just gets old.

  4. 04

    Build alongside

    Site visits weekly, contractor workshops, and drawings revised with mud on them. The building is the documentation.
