The longer story

A bit more about me

I design buildings that know where they are. Eighteen years of practice taught me to start with the site — light, climate, the materials within a day’s drive — and let the brief meet the place halfway. Good architecture is specific or it is nothing.

My studio works across houses, cultural buildings and adaptive reuse. I am drawn to projects with constraints: a tight budget, a protected façade, a difficult slope. Constraints are where architecture gets interesting and honest.

I draw by hand before I draw on screen. The pencil keeps the thinking slow and the proportions human. The computer is for precision, not for deciding.

I believe in building to last and to weather well — materials that age into something better, details that survive the rain, rooms that still make sense in thirty years.
