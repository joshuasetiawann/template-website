The longer story

A bit more about me

I write the sentence the whole workshop was circling for an hour. Twelve years between newsrooms and agencies taught me that good copy is mostly good thinking, lightly dressed — the line is easy once the argument is right.

My work spans naming, voice systems and campaign copy. I have named challenger banks, rescued taglines from committee, and written the manifesto that makes the rest of the deck make sense. I like brands that want to sound like a person, not a category.

I am a ruthless editor of my own work. The first draft is for getting it down; the craft is in the cutting. If a word is not earning its place, it leaves.

I write to be read aloud. Rhythm is meaning. A line that trips off the tongue tends to stick in the head — and the legal team tends to sign it, too.
