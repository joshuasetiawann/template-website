How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Interrogate

    I start with the brief behind the brief. What are we really selling, to whom, against what? Most copy problems are positioning problems.

  2. 02

    Generate

    Volume first: territories, hundreds of lines, named candidates. You cannot edit a blank page into a great one.

  3. 03

    Sharpen

    Cut, test, read aloud. The platform line earns its keep by surviving compliance and stopping a commuter.

  4. 04

    System

    Principles, before/after rewrites and a tone guide your team will actually open — so the voice outlives the campaign.
