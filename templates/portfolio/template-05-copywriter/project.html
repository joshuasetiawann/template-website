Overview

Overview

A challenger bank launching into a category that shouts. Lark wanted to sound like the calm person at the party — I wrote the launch platform.

One manifesto, forty out-of-home lines and a tone guide the lawyers signed without a fight. "Money, minus the mood" led the campaign across three cities.

The brief

The challenge

Banking copy dies in compliance. The craft was writing lines flexible enough to clear legal and sharp enough to stop a commuter.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Listen first

    Founder interviews, customer calls and the complaints folder. The voice is already in the building — my job is to transcribe it.

  2. 02

    Find the line

    Hundreds of headlines written longhand; the wall decides. Strategy is whatever survives being said aloud.

  3. 03

    Build the system

    Voice principles, do-and-don’t pairs and rewritten real examples — a guide the intern can use on day one.

  4. 04

    Read it aloud

    Every deliverable performed in the room before it ships. If the client stumbles reading it, it is not finished.
