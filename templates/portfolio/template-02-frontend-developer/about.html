The longer story

A bit more about me

I build the front of the front end: the accessible component, the render that does not jank, the bundle that ships in kilobytes instead of megabytes. Eight years across fintech and developer tools taught me that the interesting problems are rarely visual — they are about state, performance budgets and the long tail of real devices.

I am a systems person who happens to love the browser. I would rather delete a dependency than add one, rather write the codemod than file the migration ticket, and rather measure than guess. Lighthouse is a starting line, not a trophy.

Most of my work lives where design systems meet engineering reality: tree-shakeable component kits, visual-regression suites, and the boring infrastructure that lets a team ship confidently on a Friday. I write docs for engineers in a hurry, because that is everyone.

Accessibility is non-negotiable and, handled early, basically free. Semantic HTML, real focus management, and tests that fail loudly when a control stops being reachable.
