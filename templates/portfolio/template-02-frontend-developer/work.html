How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Profile the real bottleneck — bundle, render or network — on the devices users actually hold, not a flagship phone on office wifi.

  2. 02

    Architect

    Pick the smallest abstraction that survives the roadmap: component contracts, state boundaries, and a performance budget in CI.

  3. 03

    Build

    Type-safe, accessible components with visual-regression coverage and stories that double as documentation.

  4. 04

    Harden

    Codemods, office hours and adoption metrics — a kit wins on merit, never mandate.
