How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Position

    Sharpen who it is for and why now. Most growth problems are positioning problems wearing a budget.

  2. 02

    Model

    Map the funnel with real numbers — where attention leaks and what a fix is worth before we spend a euro.

  3. 03

    Run

    Campaigns and content built to the strategy, instrumented end to end, shipped in testable waves.

  4. 04

    Read

    Honest measurement tied to pipeline, not vanity reach — so the next decision makes itself.
