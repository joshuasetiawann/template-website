About me

Paris-based strategist, ten years between agencies and scale-ups. I write positioning that sales teams quote verbatim, then build the funnel math to prove it worked — or didn't, in writing, fast.

Best deployed at inflection points: new market, new product, flat growth curve. I bring a measurement plan to the first meeting and a kill-list of zombie campaigns to the second.
