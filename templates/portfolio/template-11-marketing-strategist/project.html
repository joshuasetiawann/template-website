Repositioning Cartepro B2B

From “expense cards” to “spend autopilot” — pipeline per campaign up 3.1x in two quarters.

  • Positioning
  • B2B
Role
Marketing Strategist
Client
Cartepro
Year
2025
Timeline
12 weeks

Overview

Overview

Cartepro sold expense cards as "the cheapest" in a market racing to free. I led the repositioning to an operations story: close the books in a day.

New narrative, new pricing page, new sales deck — and a rebuilt demo that opens on the month-end close instead of the card.

The brief

The challenge

Repositioning means disappointing someone. We walked away from the SMB discount-hunters, and the pipeline got smaller before it got better.

Method

The approach

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Win-loss calls, funnel forensics and a brutal audit of where money currently leaks. Strategy starts with the unflattering truth.

  2. 02

    Choose the battle

    One segment, one enemy, one promise. Positioning is decided in writing and signed before any creative brief exists.

  3. 03

    Build the engine

    Channel mix, budget model and lifecycle map built as one system — brand and performance on the same spreadsheet.

  4. 04

    Measure & retell

    Holdouts and MMM-lite measurement, then quarterly narrative reviews that keep the strategy honest as markets move.
