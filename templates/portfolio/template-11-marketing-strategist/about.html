The longer story

A bit more about me

I do growth with receipts. Positioning, funnels and campaigns, all measured to the euro — because marketing that cannot show its work is just expensive optimism. Twelve years across B2B and consumer taught me to start with the maths and the message together.

My engagements usually begin with positioning: who is this for, against what, and why should they care this quarter? Everything downstream — the funnel, the content, the spend — gets easier once that is sharp.

I am as comfortable in a spreadsheet as on a brief. I will model the funnel, then write the campaign that fills it, then read the numbers honestly when it ships.

I distrust vanity metrics. Reach is not revenue. I build dashboards that tie activity to pipeline so the next decision is obvious.
