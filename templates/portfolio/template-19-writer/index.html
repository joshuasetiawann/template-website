The author

About me

I write long things slowly. Essays and reported features for magazines, criticism when a book deserves arguing with, and a novel that is — at last — with its editor.

Born in Vancouver, based in Lisbon, usually writing about somewhere else. My beat, if I have one, is infrastructure with feelings: night markets, ferry timetables, the politics of bread.
