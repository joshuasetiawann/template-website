Quick questions

What do you write?

Essays, reported features and criticism — mostly on cities, food and the overlooked machinery of ordinary life. Plus one slow novel.

Do you take commissions?

Yes. Send the brief, the word count and the deadline; I will tell you honestly whether I am the right writer for it.

Editing or ghostwriting?

Occasionally — developmental editing for nonfiction, and the rare ghost essay where the subject is worth the disappearance.

Rather just say hi?

No brief required. If our work might overlap, I would love to hear from you.
