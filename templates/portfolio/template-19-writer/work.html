How I work

The approach

Different projects, same backbone. This is roughly how the work happens.

  1. 01

    Notice

    It starts with attention — a detail that will not let go. I keep notebooks full of them waiting for their essay.

  2. 02

    Report

    Interviews, walking, archives. Even an opinion piece earns its keep only after it survives the facts.

  3. 03

    Draft

    Write it long and wrong, then find the spine. The structure is the argument, hiding.

  4. 04

    Edit

    Cut until the prose disappears and only the reader’s attention is left. More drafts than anyone admits.
