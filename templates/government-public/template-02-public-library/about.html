Our mission & history

Serving the public since 1903

Wexford Public Library System opened its doors in 1903 with a simple purpose: to make culture, knowledge and shared spaces freely available to everyone in Wexford District. What began as a modest effort has grown into a cornerstone of community life.

Over the decades we have expanded what we offer while holding firm to our founding belief that access should never depend on means. Today, families, students, researchers and visitors of every background find a welcome here.

Our work is shaped by the people we serve. Programs, collections and improvements grow out of what the community tells us it needs, and we measure our success by how many people we reach and how well we serve them.

Accessibility and inclusion sit at the heart of everything we do. From step-free access to multilingual help and accommodations on request, we work to remove every barrier between people and what we offer.

Looking ahead, Wexford Library is committed to staying free, open and relevant — a trusted public institution that adapts with the community while protecting what it loves most.

What we stand for

Our values & transparency

The principles that guide how we serve, decide and account for our work.

Free & Open to All

Every resident can read, learn and gather here at no cost, regardless of means.

Privacy Protected

What you read is your business. We collect the minimum and never sell borrowing data.

Accessible by Design

Step-free entrances, lowered desks, large-print collections and assistive technology at every branch.

Community Owned

Programs and collections are shaped by what readers and the volunteer board ask for.
