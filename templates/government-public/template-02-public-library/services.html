Get a Free Library Card

Borrowing

Every resident, student and worker in the district qualifies for a free card that unlocks books, digital media and study spaces.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Apply online in two minutes and pick up your card at any branch, or sign up at a service desk with photo ID.

Get a card

Borrow & Reserve Books

Borrowing

Browse the catalog of 480,000 items, place holds, renew loans and have materials sent to your home branch.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Search the online catalog and place a hold; we'll email you when it's ready for pickup.

Search the catalog

Reserve a Study or Meeting Room

Spaces

Quiet study carrels, group study rooms and a community meeting room available free to cardholders.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Check room availability and book online up to two weeks ahead, or ask at the information desk.

Reserve a room

Digital Library & eBooks

Digital

Stream eBooks, audiobooks, films, magazines and learning courses to your phone or tablet, free with your card.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Sign in with your card number and PIN on the digital library portal; no app purchase needed.

Open digital library

In person Public service

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Apply online in about two minutes or visit any branch with a photo ID. Your card works immediately for digital borrowing and is ready to pick up the same day for physical materials.

Cardholders may borrow up to 40 physical items and 15 digital titles at a time. Most books loan for three weeks and renew automatically twice unless another reader is waiting.

Yes — browsing, reading in-house, WiFi and many programs are open to everyone. A card is only needed to borrow items, reserve rooms and use the digital library.

Sign in to the room-booking page with your card, choose a branch and time up to two weeks ahead, and you'll get a confirmation email. Group rooms can be booked for up to three hours a day.

All library programs — story times, classes, clubs and author talks — are free. A few hands-on workshops ask you to register in advance because space is limited.

We are a fine-free library. Overdue items simply pause new borrowing until they're returned, and we'll send a friendly reminder before anything is due.

Can't find what you need?

Our team is happy to point you to the right service or answer your questions directly.
