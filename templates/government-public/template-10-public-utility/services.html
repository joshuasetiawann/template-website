Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Pay online with the account number on your statement, set up autopay, pay by phone, or use an authorized pay station. Payments post within one business day and you can go paperless any time.

Submit a service request online at least two business days before your move-in or move-out date. You'll get a confirmation, and final bills are mailed to your forwarding address.

Report power outages and water-main breaks online or by phone at any hour. The live outage map shows confirmed faults and estimated restoration times as crews work.

Yes. Sign in to the account portal to view daily and monthly usage charts, compare periods and set high-usage alerts so a big bill never surprises you — all at no extra cost.

We offer rebates for efficient appliances, low-flow fixtures, home audits and rooftop solar. Apply online after your purchase, and book a free home water-and-energy audit to find more savings.

Help is available. Bill-payment assistance, budget billing that evens out seasonal costs, and medical-needs protection are open to qualifying customers. Apply online or by phone with proof of eligibility.
