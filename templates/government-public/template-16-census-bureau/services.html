Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Respond online with the unique code on your invitation letter, or complete it by phone or mail. The household form takes about ten minutes, is confidential, and is required by law for an accurate count.

Use the free data explorer to browse interactive tables and maps of population, housing and demographics down to the neighborhood level. No account is needed to view or download the data.

Absolutely. By law, your answers are used only to produce statistics and are never shared in a way that could identify you or your household. The privacy page explains exactly how your data is protected.
