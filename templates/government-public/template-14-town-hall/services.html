Payments

Pay property taxes, water and sewer bills and parking permits through the secure town portal.

Permits

Building, sign, fence and home-occupation permits, plus dog and business licenses.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Apply online or at the Town Clerk's counter; most simple permits are issued within a week.

Meeting Agendas & Minutes

Meetings

Selectboard, planning and zoning agendas, minutes and the schedule of all public meetings.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Read agendas and minutes online; meetings are open to the public and livestreamed.

View meetings

Request Town Records

Records

Request public records, deeds, meeting documents and assessment cards under the public-records law.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: File a records request online or in person; most are fulfilled within 10 business days.

Request records

Report a Local Issue

Reporting

Report potholes, streetlights, brush pickup, plowing concerns and other neighborhood issues.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Submit a request online with the location, or call the Public Works line during office hours.

Report an issue

Register to Vote & Elections

Information

Register to vote, find your polling place and view sample ballots for town and state elections.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Register online or at the Town Clerk's office; bring ID to update your address.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Pay property taxes, water and sewer bills and parking permits online with your account number, by mail, or at the Treasurer's window in Town Hall. Online payments post within one business day.

Apply online or at the Town Clerk's counter for building, sign, fence and home-occupation permits, plus dog and business licenses. Most simple permits are issued within a week.

The Selectboard meets on the second and fourth Monday each month at 7 p.m. in the Town Hall meeting room. Agendas are posted online the Thursday before, and meetings are livestreamed.

Submit a service request online with the location of the pothole, streetlight or other issue, or call the Public Works line during office hours. You'll get a tracking number for follow-up.

Order certified copies of birth, marriage and death certificates online, or visit the Town Clerk's counter with a photo ID. Marriage licenses are issued in person to both applicants.

Yes. Residents need an annual transfer-station sticker, available online or at Town Hall. The services page lists current hours, accepted materials and bulky-waste days.
