Our mission & history

Serving the public since 1884

Marwick County Superior Court was established in 1884 to serve the people of Marwick County with essential public services delivered fairly, efficiently and with respect. That mandate has guided our work ever since.

As Marwick County has grown and changed, so have we. We have modernized how services are delivered, bringing more of our work online while keeping in-person help available for everyone who needs it.

Transparency is central to how we operate. Budgets, decisions and performance are published for the public, and anyone can request records or attend the meetings where decisions are made.

We hold ourselves accountable for results. We track how quickly and completely we resolve what the public asks of us, and we report those measures openly so residents can judge our work.

Above all, Marwick Courts exists to serve. Whatever you need from us, our goal is to make every interaction clear, accessible and worthy of the trust the public places in us.

What we stand for

Our values & transparency

The principles that guide how we serve, decide and account for our work.

Equal Justice

Every person is treated fairly and with dignity, regardless of who they are.

Open Courts

Records and proceedings are public, and the docket is searchable online.

Access for All

Self-help, free interpreters and accommodations remove barriers to justice.

Impartial & Independent

Judges decide each case on the law and the facts, free from influence.
