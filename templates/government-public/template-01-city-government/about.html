Our mission & history

Serving the public since 1871

Ashford Bay City Government was established in 1871 to serve the people of Harbor County with essential public services delivered fairly, efficiently and with respect. That mandate has guided our work ever since.

As Harbor County has grown and changed, so have we. We have modernized how services are delivered, bringing more of our work online while keeping in-person help available for everyone who needs it.

Transparency is central to how we operate. Budgets, decisions and performance are published for the public, and anyone can request records or attend the meetings where decisions are made.

We hold ourselves accountable for results. We track how quickly and completely we resolve what the public asks of us, and we report those measures openly so residents can judge our work.

Above all, Ashford Bay exists to serve. Whatever you need from us, our goal is to make every interaction clear, accessible and worthy of the trust the public places in us.

What we stand for

Our values & transparency

The principles that guide how we serve, decide and account for our work.

Open & Accountable

Budgets, contracts and council records are published online and searchable by every resident.

Service First

From 311 to permits, we measure how fast and how completely we resolve what residents ask of us.

Equity in Every District

Investment is tracked by district so every neighborhood shares in the city's growth.

We maintain roads, water and public buildings as long-term assets held in public trust.
