Pay a Utility or City Bill

Payments

Pay water, refuse and stormwater charges, traffic citations and business-license fees through the secure resident portal.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Pay online with the account number on your bill, or in person at the Finance counter on the first floor.

Pay a bill

Apply for a Building Permit

Permits

Residential and commercial building, electrical, plumbing and demolition permits, with plan review and inspection scheduling.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Submit plans through the Permit Center portal; most residential permits are issued within 10 business days.

Start a permit

Report a Pothole or Streetlight

Reporting

Report damaged roads, broken streetlights, graffiti, illegal dumping and other non-emergency neighborhood issues.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Use the 311 service request form online or call the 311 line; crews are dispatched by district.

Report an issue

Business License & Tax Registration

Business

Register a new business, renew an annual license and file local business tax for storefronts, contractors and home-based businesses.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Register online through the Business Services portal or visit the Licensing desk with your formation documents.

Register a business

Parks & Facility Reservations

Parks

Reserve picnic shelters, sports fields, community-center rooms and the civic plaza for events and gatherings.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Check availability and book online, or call the Parks office to hold a date for a large event.

Reserve a space

Public Records Request

Records

Request city documents, council records, contracts and inspection reports under the Open Records ordinance.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: File a records request online and track its status; most requests are fulfilled within 10 business days.

Request records

Voter & Election Information

Information

Find your council district, polling place, sample ballots and upcoming municipal election dates.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Look up your district online, or visit the City Clerk's office for registration help.

Find your district

Reporting
