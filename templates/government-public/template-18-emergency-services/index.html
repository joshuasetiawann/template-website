Our mission

Government that works for you

Summit County Emergency Services serves the public with transparency, fairness and care. Our job is to make essential services easy to reach and to be accountable for how we deliver them.

Whether you are paying a bill, applying for a permit or seeking help, we aim to make every interaction clear, respectful and efficient.
