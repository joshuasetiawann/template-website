Our mission & history

Serving the public since 1981

Summit County Emergency Services was established in 1981 to serve the people of Summit County with essential public services delivered fairly, efficiently and with respect. That mandate has guided our work ever since.

As Summit County has grown and changed, so have we. We have modernized how services are delivered, bringing more of our work online while keeping in-person help available for everyone who needs it.

Transparency is central to how we operate. Budgets, decisions and performance are published for the public, and anyone can request records or attend the meetings where decisions are made.

We hold ourselves accountable for results. We track how quickly and completely we resolve what the public asks of us, and we report those measures openly so residents can judge our work.

Above all, Summit 911 exists to serve. Whatever you need from us, our goal is to make every interaction clear, accessible and worthy of the trust the public places in us.
