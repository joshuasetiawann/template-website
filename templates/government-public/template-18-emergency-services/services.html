Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Call 911 for any emergency that threatens life, safety or property — a crime in progress, fire, crash or medical crisis. Stay on the line, give your exact location, and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

Register your phone number and address online to receive targeted alerts for severe weather, evacuations, hazards and missing persons. Alerts are tailored to where you live and work.

Use the readiness checklist to build a family plan, map evacuation routes and assemble a 72-hour kit. Doing this before an event means you can act quickly and calmly when it matters.

During an event, the live shelter map shows open shelters, cooling and warming centers and accessible facilities near you. Shelters welcome service animals and provide accessible access.

It's a voluntary, confidential registry so responders know who may need extra help during an evacuation or outage — for example, people who use medical equipment. Add yourself or a loved one online.

Community Emergency Response Team training is a free course that prepares residents to help their neighborhood respond and recover, covering first aid, light search and rescue, and safety. Enroll online.
