About Reyes 2026

A fair economy, great schools and clean energy for every family.

Our mission & history

Serving the public since 2025

Reyes for Governor Campaign began with neighbors at kitchen tables who shared a conviction that our community deserves better — fairer opportunity, stronger schools and leaders who listen.

This is a grassroots movement, funded by small-dollar donors and powered by volunteers in every corner of Statewide. We answer to people, not special interests, and our independence is our strength.

Every priority we champion comes with a funded, plain-language plan. We believe voters deserve specifics they can read, question and hold us to — not slogans.

We are building a campaign that looks like the community it seeks to serve: open to everyone, rooted in respect, and determined to bring people together across differences.

The work ahead belongs to all of us. Real change comes from neighbors organizing, knocking doors and turning out to vote — and it starts with you joining in.
