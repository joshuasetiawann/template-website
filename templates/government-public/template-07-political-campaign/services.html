Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Sign up online and we'll connect you with a team near you within a day. You can knock doors, make calls, register voters, host a meet-and-greet or help at events — there's a role for every schedule.

The full platform is published in plain language with funded steps for each priority — the economy, schools, healthcare, climate, safety and housing. Read it on the platform page and decide for yourself.

This is a grassroots campaign powered by small-dollar donors. We publish our funding sources, and the average contribution is small because we answer to people, not special interests.

Browse the events list and RSVP online. We hold town halls, rallies, phone banks and meet-and-greets across the state every week, and most are free and open to all.

Use the registration link to check your status and register or update your address in minutes. Make sure you're ready well before the deadline so your voice counts.

Request a yard sign, sticker or literature through the volunteer form. We'll get materials to you or point you to the nearest field office to pick them up.

Can't find what you need?

Our team is happy to point you to the right service or answer your questions directly.
