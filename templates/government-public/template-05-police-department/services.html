Emergency & Non-Emergency Lines

Emergency

Call 911 for crimes in progress or emergencies; call the non-emergency line for past incidents and questions.

In person Public service

How to apply: Dial 911 for emergencies. Use the non-emergency number or online report for anything that is not urgent.

File a Police Report Online

Reporting

Report theft, lost property, vandalism, identity theft and other non-emergency incidents from home.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Use the online reporting tool for eligible incident types; you'll receive a case number by email.

File a report

Request Records & Reports

Records

Copies of incident reports, crash reports and background letters, released under public-records rules.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Request records online with the case or report number; most are released within 10 business days.

Request records

Crime Data & Maps

Information

Searchable crime statistics, neighborhood maps and monthly transparency reports for the whole city.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Explore the public dashboard online by district and date — no account or request needed.

View the dashboard

Community Programs

Community

Neighborhood watch, youth academy, ride-alongs and coffee-with-a-cop events to build trust and safety.

In person Public service

How to apply: Browse programs online and sign up; neighborhood watch groups can request a startup visit.

Join a program

Fingerprinting & Permits

Records

Fingerprinting for employment and licensing, plus permits for parades, block parties and large gatherings.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Book a fingerprinting slot online, or apply for an event permit at least 14 days ahead.

Book a service

Victim & Witness Support

Community

Confidential support, safety planning and referrals for victims of crime and key witnesses.

In person Public service

How to apply: Connect with an advocate online or by phone; support is free and available in multiple languages.

Get support

Property & Evidence Return

Records

Recover found property and the safe return of items held as evidence once a case is resolved.

In person Public service

How to apply: Check the found-property list online and bring photo ID to the property room to claim an item.

Claim property
