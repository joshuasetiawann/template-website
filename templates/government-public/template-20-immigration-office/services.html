Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Enter your receipt number on the case-status page to see real-time progress, the next step and an expected timeline. Sign up for email or text updates so you know the moment your case moves.

Choose your visa type, review the eligibility guidance and checklist, then apply online by uploading your documents and paying the fee. We confirm receipt by email and tell you what happens next.

When you meet the residency and eligibility requirements, apply for naturalization online. Free study guides and practice tests help you prepare for the civics test, and we'll schedule your interview and oath.

Book or reschedule your fingerprinting and photo appointment online with your receipt number. Bring your appointment notice and a valid photo ID, and arrive a few minutes early.

Many applicants can apply for work authorization while their case is decided. File online with proof of eligibility, and renew early so you don't have a gap in your work permit.

Yes. Free English classes, job-readiness help and community resources are available to help you settle and thrive. You can access these resources regardless of your immigration status.
