Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Use the online trip planner — enter where you're starting and going, and it returns the fastest routes with times, transfers, fares and step-free options. You can save favorites for one-tap planning.

The reloadable SmartGo card and mobile fare always charge the lowest fare and automatically cap your daily and monthly spending, so you never pay more than a day or month pass costs.

Yes. Every bus is ramped or low-floor, all rail stations are step-free with elevators, and door-to-door paratransit serves riders who can't use fixed routes. Apply for paratransit eligibility online.

Seniors, students, riders with disabilities and qualifying low-income residents can apply online with proof of eligibility. Once approved, the discount loads automatically to your SmartGo card.

Yes. Every bus has a front bike rack, rail cars have designated bike areas off-peak, and major stations offer secure bike parking. No reservation or extra fare is required.

Submit a lost-item report online with your route, direction and approximate time. Found items are logged daily and held at the customer center; we'll contact you if yours turns up.
