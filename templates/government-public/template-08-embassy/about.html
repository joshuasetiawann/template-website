Our mission & history

Serving the public since 1956

Embassy of the Republic of Valoria was established in 1956 to serve the people of Diplomatic District with essential public services delivered fairly, efficiently and with respect. That mandate has guided our work ever since.

As Diplomatic District has grown and changed, so have we. We have modernized how services are delivered, bringing more of our work online while keeping in-person help available for everyone who needs it.

Transparency is central to how we operate. Budgets, decisions and performance are published for the public, and anyone can request records or attend the meetings where decisions are made.

We hold ourselves accountable for results. We track how quickly and completely we resolve what the public asks of us, and we report those measures openly so residents can judge our work.

Above all, Valoria Embassy exists to serve. Whatever you need from us, our goal is to make every interaction clear, accessible and worthy of the trust the public places in us.
