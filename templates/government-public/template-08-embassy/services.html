Passport Renewal & Issuance

Passports

New, renewed and emergency passports for Valorian nationals living or traveling abroad.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Book a consular appointment online and bring your current passport and supporting documents.

Renew a passport

Citizen Registration

Citizenship

Register as a Valorian national resident abroad to receive consular notices and emergency assistance.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Register online with your residence details; registration is free and helps us reach you in a crisis.

Register as a citizen

Notarial & Legal Services

Legal

Authenticate documents, certify copies, witness powers of attorney and legalize papers for use in Valoria.

In person Public service

How to apply: Book an appointment and bring originals; most notarial acts are completed the same day.

Book notarial

Birth, Marriage & Civil Records

Citizenship

Register births and marriages abroad and request certified civil-status documents from the national registry.

In person Public service

How to apply: Submit the registration form with certified local documents at a consular appointment.

Register an event

Emergency Consular Assistance

Emergency

Help for nationals abroad facing lost passports, hospitalization, arrest or crisis situations.

In person Public service

How to apply: Call the 24-hour consular hotline in an emergency; for non-urgent help, book an appointment.

Get emergency help

Cultural & Trade Affairs

Culture

Promotion of Valorian culture, education exchange and business links between Valoria and the host country.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Contact the cultural office online to propose an event, exchange or trade enquiry.

Legal
