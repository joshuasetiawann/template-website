Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

General entry doesn't require a reservation — buy a pass online or at the gate. Campsites, backcountry trips and some popular trailheads do need reservations or permits, which you can book online.

Check the live conditions page the morning of your visit for trail status, closures, snow lines and river levels. Mountain weather changes fast, so plan for conditions to shift during the day.

Reserve frontcountry campsites and backcountry permits online up to six months ahead. A number of sites are kept first-come, first-served and fill early on weekends and holidays.

Most ranger-led hikes, campfire talks, junior-ranger activities and stargazing nights are free. Browse the schedule online and simply meet the ranger at the posted meeting point.

Keep a safe distance from all wildlife, never feed animals, and store every bit of food and scented item in provided lockers or an approved bear canister. Read the full safety guide before you go.

Yes. The park has accessible trails, viewpoints and campsites, plus a free seasonal shuttle. The accessibility map shows step-free routes and accessible facilities so you can plan ahead.
