Our mission

A welcoming place for everyone

Cascade Ridge National Park Service exists to welcome, inform and inspire the public. We open our doors to everyone, removing cost and access barriers so that every resident and visitor can take part.

From day-to-day visits to special programs, we work to make every experience meaningful, accessible and rooted in the community we serve.
