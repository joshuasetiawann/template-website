Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Find your nearest public health clinic online and book a vaccination, or walk in during clinic hours. We offer childhood, adult, travel and seasonal vaccines, many at low or no cost. Bring your record card if you have one.

Use the clinic finder to locate the nearest site for vaccines, testing, family planning and preventive care. Each listing shows services, hours and how to book online or by phone.

Read current advisories on outbreaks, air quality, heat and food or water safety on the health alerts page. You can also sign up to receive advisories by email or text so you stay informed.

Request it online with a photo ID. Immunization records are usually available the same day and are accepted for school, work and travel. We also issue birth and death certificates on request.

Look up inspection scores for restaurants, pools and public facilities online. If you have a concern about a food or public facility, you can report it for follow-up by an inspector.

WIC provides nutrition support, healthy food benefits and breastfeeding help for pregnant people, new parents and young children. Apply online or by phone, and staff will help check eligibility and book your first visit.
