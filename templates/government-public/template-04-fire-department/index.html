Services & services

Emergency response, inspections, permits and public-safety programs from your fire department.

Emergency Response (Call 911)

Fire suppression, vehicle extrication, hazardous-materials response and emergency medical first response, 24 hours a day.

Dial 911

Fire & Safety Inspections

Required inspections for businesses, multi-family buildings, schools and assembly venues to verify exits, alarms and sprinklers.

Request inspection

Burn & Fireworks Permits

Open-burning, bonfire, agricultural and special-effect permits, plus seasonal burn-ban status for the county.

Apply for a permit

Smoke Alarm Program

Free smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms installed in the homes of eligible residents, with battery checks on request.

Request an alarm

CPR & First Aid Training

Community CPR, AED and first-aid classes for workplaces, schools and neighborhood groups, taught by firefighters.

Find a class

Incident & Fire Reports

Official fire and incident reports for insurance, legal and property records, released under public-records rules.

Request a report

Our mission

Government that works for you

Ridgemont Fire & Rescue Department serves the public with transparency, fairness and care. Our job is to make essential services easy to reach and to be accountable for how we deliver them.

Whether you are paying a bill, applying for a permit or seeking help, we aim to make every interaction clear, respectful and efficient.
