Emergency Response (Call 911)

Emergency

Fire suppression, vehicle extrication, hazardous-materials response and emergency medical first response, 24 hours a day.

In person Public service

How to apply: Call 911 immediately for any fire, crash or medical emergency. Never use online forms to report an emergency.

Dial 911

Fire & Safety Inspections

Inspections

Required inspections for businesses, multi-family buildings, schools and assembly venues to verify exits, alarms and sprinklers.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Request or schedule an inspection online; most are completed within seven business days of approval.

Request inspection

Burn & Fireworks Permits

Permits

Open-burning, bonfire, agricultural and special-effect permits, plus seasonal burn-ban status for the county.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Apply online and check the daily burn-ban indicator before you light anything; permits are issued by email.

Apply for a permit

Smoke Alarm Program

Safety

Free smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms installed in the homes of eligible residents, with battery checks on request.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Request a free alarm online or by phone; a crew schedules an installation visit at your convenience.

Request an alarm

CPR & First Aid Training

Safety

Community CPR, AED and first-aid classes for workplaces, schools and neighborhood groups, taught by firefighters.

In person Public service

How to apply: Browse the class calendar and register online; group classes can be arranged at your site.

Find a class

Incident & Fire Reports

Records

Official fire and incident reports for insurance, legal and property records, released under public-records rules.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Request a report online with the incident date and address; reports are released within 10 business days.

Request a report

Station Tours & Education

Safety

Free firehouse tours, school visits and fire-safety talks for scouts, classrooms and community groups.

In person Public service

How to apply: Book a tour online at least two weeks ahead; engine tours pause if the crew is dispatched.

Book a tour

Hydrant & Sprinkler Permits

Permits

Permits and flow tests for new fire hydrants, private sprinkler systems and standpipes in new construction.

Apply online Public service

How to apply: Submit plans through the permit portal; a plans examiner reviews and schedules the required tests.

Submit plans
