Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

General admission to the permanent collection is always free. A small number of major special exhibitions carry a modest ticket, and members and district students enter those free as well.

No booking is needed for general admission — just walk in during open hours. For popular special exhibitions we offer free timed entry so you can skip any line.

Yes. The building is step-free throughout with elevators to every floor, wheelchairs to borrow, large-print guides, sensory-friendly mornings and audio description on request.

Photography for personal use is welcome in the permanent galleries without flash. Some loaned works in special exhibitions are marked no-photography to respect lender agreements.

We do, and they're free for district schools. Curriculum-linked tours and hands-on workshops can be booked online at least two weeks in advance.

Becoming a member or making a gift directly funds free admission, school programs and conservation. Members also receive previews, workshop entry and shop discounts.
