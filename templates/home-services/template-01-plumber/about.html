About us

The story behind DrainGuard Plumbing

For over 21 years, DrainGuard Plumbing has been the plumber neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

DrainGuard began in 2005 with one van, a master plumber named Marcus Vane and a simple promise: pick up the phone, show up fast and charge a fair flat price.

Two decades later we run a fleet of fully-stocked trucks across the metro, but the promise has not changed. We invest in ongoing certification, modern hydro-jetting and camera gear, and a dispatch team that treats every flooded basement like it is their own.

We are proud to be the plumber neighbours recommend — the one that answers at 3am, explains the problem in plain language and cleans up before leaving.
