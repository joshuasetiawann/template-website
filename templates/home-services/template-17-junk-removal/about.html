About us

The story behind HaulAway Junk Removal

For over 11 years, HaulAway Junk Removal has been the junk removal neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

HaulAway Junk Removal was founded in 2015 by Marcus Reed after helping a relative clear an estate and realising how stressful and overpriced junk removal could be.

He built HaulAway around doing all the lifting, quoting an honest all-in price before loading, and diverting as much as possible from landfill through donation and recycling.

Today our crews clear furniture, clutter and debris across the region — fast, friendly and eco-minded — and still treat every cleanout, especially the hard ones, with care and respect.
