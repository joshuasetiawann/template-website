About HaulAway

Your trusted local junk removal team

HaulAway Junk Removal was founded in 2015 by Marcus Reed after helping a relative clear an estate and realising how stressful and overpriced junk removal could be.

He built HaulAway around doing all the lifting, quoting an honest all-in price before loading, and diverting as much as possible from landfill through donation and recycling.

  • Licensed, bonded & fully insured
  • Background-checked, expert technicians
  • Upfront pricing — approved before we start
  • Workmanship guaranteed in writing
