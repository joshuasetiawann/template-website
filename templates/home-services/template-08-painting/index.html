About TrueHue

Your trusted local painting team

TrueHue Painting began in 2013 when founder Adrian Cole, a painter with an eye for colour, grew frustrated watching crews rush prep and leave behind drips, uneven lines and a mess.

He built TrueHue on the opposite principle: prep done properly, premium low-VOC paint, crisp lines and a crew that respects your home like it is their own.

  • Licensed, bonded & fully insured
  • Background-checked, expert technicians
  • Upfront pricing — approved before we start
  • Workmanship guaranteed in writing
