About us

The story behind Sentinel Home Security

For over 18 years, Sentinel Home Security has been the home security neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

Sentinel Home Security was founded in 2008 by Gregory Stahl, a security designer who believed protection should be engineered for the home, not sold from a one-size box.

He built Sentinel around custom system design, certified professional installation and genuine 24/7 monitoring — so families get protection that actually fits the way they live.

Today Sentinel designs, installs and monitors smart security across the region, unifying alarms, cameras and locks into one elegant app — and backing every install with a workmanship guarantee.
