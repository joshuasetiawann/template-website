About GreenHaven

Your trusted local landscaping team

GreenHaven Landscapes grew from a single mower and a love of the outdoors when founder Elena Marsh started caring for neighbourhood lawns in 2010.

As clients asked for gardens, patios and full transformations, Elena built a crew of certified horticulturists and skilled hardscapers who share her belief that a great yard is designed, not just mowed.
