About us

The story behind FreshNest Cleaning

For over 12 years, FreshNest Cleaning has been the cleaning service neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

FreshNest started in 2014 when founder Marisol Vega could not find a cleaning service that was both genuinely thorough and safe for her young family.

So she built one: friendly, vetted cleaners, plant-based products, transparent flat pricing and a happiness guarantee on every visit. Word spread fast among parents and pet owners who wanted a healthier clean.

Today FreshNest is a team of trained, insured cleaners caring for thousands of homes and offices — still using the same low-tox products and still standing behind every clean.
