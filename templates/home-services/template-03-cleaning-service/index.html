About FreshNest

Your trusted local cleaning service team

FreshNest started in 2014 when founder Marisol Vega could not find a cleaning service that was both genuinely thorough and safe for her young family.

So she built one: friendly, vetted cleaners, plant-based products, transparent flat pricing and a happiness guarantee on every visit. Word spread fast among parents and pet owners who wanted a healthier clean.

  • Licensed, bonded & fully insured
  • Background-checked, expert technicians
  • Upfront pricing — approved before we start
  • Workmanship guaranteed in writing
