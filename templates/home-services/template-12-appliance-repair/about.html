About us

The story behind ReviveTech Appliance Repair

For over 19 years, ReviveTech Appliance Repair has been the appliance repair neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

ReviveTech Appliance Repair was founded in 2007 by master technician Andre Foss on a simple conviction: most appliances do not need replacing, they need an expert.

He built ReviveTech around factory-trained technicians, trucks stocked with genuine parts, and upfront pricing — so a broken fridge becomes a same-day fix, not a costly new purchase.

Today our technicians keep kitchens and laundry rooms running across the region, and back every repair with a written parts-and-labour guarantee.
