About us

The story behind VoltLine Electric

For over 18 years, VoltLine Electric has been the electrician neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

VoltLine Electric was founded in 2008 by master electrician Owen Castellano after one too many calls from homeowners who had been overcharged and left with work that failed inspection.

Our answer was simple: only licensed electricians, only flat-rate pricing approved up front, and only work that passes inspection the first time. That standard has powered our growth ever since.

Today our trucks roll out across the region for everything from a dead outlet to a 400-amp service upgrade — and we still treat every panel as if our own family were living behind it.
