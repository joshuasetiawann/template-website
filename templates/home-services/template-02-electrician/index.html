About VoltLine

Your trusted local electrician team

VoltLine Electric was founded in 2008 by master electrician Owen Castellano after one too many calls from homeowners who had been overcharged and left with work that failed inspection.

Our answer was simple: only licensed electricians, only flat-rate pricing approved up front, and only work that passes inspection the first time. That standard has powered our growth ever since.
