About us

The story behind AquaLux Pool Service

For over 15 years, AquaLux Pool Service has been the pool service neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

AquaLux Pool Service was founded in 2011 by master technician Hector Salas, who saw too many pools left cloudy by services that skimmed the surface and ignored the chemistry.

He built AquaLux around certified technicians who understand water balance and equipment, reliable same-day routes, and a photo report after every visit so owners always know their pool's status.

Today AquaLux keeps pools crystal-clear across the region — weekly service, expert repairs and seasonal care — all backed by a clear-water guarantee.
