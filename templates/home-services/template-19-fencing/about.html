About us

The story behind IronOak Fencing

For over 22 years, IronOak Fencing has been the fencing neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

IronOak Fencing was founded in 2004 by lead builder Roy Calhoun, who had seen too many fences lean and sag within a few years because corners were cut on posts and materials.

He built IronOak on the opposite standard: concrete-set posts, quality cedar, vinyl, aluminum and steel, and crews who build a fence to stand straight for decades.

Today IronOak designs, builds and repairs fences across the region — privacy, security and decorative — and backs every project with a written workmanship warranty.
