About us

The story behind ClearView Window Cleaning

For over 12 years, ClearView Window Cleaning has been the window cleaning neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

ClearView Window Cleaning was founded in 2014 by Daniel Frost, who was struck by how much brighter a home feels once the windows truly disappear.

He invested in pure-water technology and trained, insured crews to deliver a spot-free finish without chemicals — and to never leave behind smudged sills or muddy tracks.

Today ClearView keeps homes and storefronts gleaming across the region, screens and tracks included, and backs every visit with a streak-free guarantee.
