About SummitPeak

Your trusted local roofing team

SummitPeak Roofing was founded in 2002 by Garrett Lowe after a string of storms left local homeowners with leaking roofs and unreliable contractors.

Garrett built SummitPeak on factory-certified crews, premium materials and meticulous insurance documentation — so a damaged roof becomes a solved problem, not a second headache.
