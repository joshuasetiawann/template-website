About us

The story behind FixWell Handyman

For over 11 years, FixWell Handyman has been the handyman neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

FixWell Handyman started in 2015 when founder Carl Whitman noticed how many homeowners had a growing list of small repairs and no reliable, trustworthy person to call.

He built FixWell to be exactly that: one vetted, insured craftsman, fair flat pricing, and the kind of careful work that means a job only has to be done once.

Today our pros knock out repair lists across the region — from a single mounted TV to a full seasonal maintenance checklist — and still answer to one simple test: would we be proud to leave this in our own home?
