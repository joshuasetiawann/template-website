About us

The story behind HeritageGrain Flooring

For over 23 years, HeritageGrain Flooring has been the flooring neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

HeritageGrain Flooring was founded in 2003 by master installer Walter Brennan, who believed a floor should be a piece of craftsmanship the family lives on for decades.

He paired a curated showroom of hand-picked hardwood, tile, vinyl and carpet with his own crew of master craftsmen, so one accountable team guides the choice and delivers the install.

Today HeritageGrain supplies and installs floors across the region — precise, beautiful and built to last — and backs every project with a written workmanship guarantee.
