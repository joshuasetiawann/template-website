About HeritageGrain

Your trusted local flooring team

HeritageGrain Flooring was founded in 2003 by master installer Walter Brennan, who believed a floor should be a piece of craftsmanship the family lives on for decades.

He paired a curated showroom of hand-picked hardwood, tile, vinyl and carpet with his own crew of master craftsmen, so one accountable team guides the choice and delivers the install.

  • Licensed, bonded & fully insured
  • Background-checked, expert technicians
  • Upfront pricing — approved before we start
  • Workmanship guaranteed in writing
