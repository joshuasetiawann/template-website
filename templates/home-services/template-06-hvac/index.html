About ClimaCore

Your trusted local hvac team

ClimaCore HVAC was founded in 2004 by master technician Raymond Hale on a belief that comfort should be honest — fair prices, the right-sized system, and a technician you can trust in your home.

From a single service van we have grown into a full team of NATE-certified technicians installing and maintaining heating, cooling and air-quality systems across the region.

  • Background-checked, expert technicians
