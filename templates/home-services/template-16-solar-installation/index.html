About SunCrest

Your trusted local solar installation team

SunCrest Solar was founded in 2011 by lead engineer Daniel Okafor, who believed homeowners deserved solar that was engineered properly and built to last, not sold on hype.

He assembled an in-house team of solar engineers and certified installers to design systems around each home's roof and usage, install only tier-1 equipment, and guarantee production for 25 years.

  • Background-checked, expert technicians
