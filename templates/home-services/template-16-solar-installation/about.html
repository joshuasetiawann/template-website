About us

The story behind SunCrest Solar

For over 15 years, SunCrest Solar has been the solar installation neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

SunCrest Solar was founded in 2011 by lead engineer Daniel Okafor, who believed homeowners deserved solar that was engineered properly and built to last, not sold on hype.

He assembled an in-house team of solar engineers and certified installers to design systems around each home's roof and usage, install only tier-1 equipment, and guarantee production for 25 years.

Today SunCrest designs and installs premium solar and battery systems across the region — cutting power bills, raising home values and backing every system with monitoring and a long-term warranty.
