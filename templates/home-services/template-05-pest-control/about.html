About us

The story behind ShieldPest Control

For over 17 years, ShieldPest Control has been the pest control neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

ShieldPest Control was founded in 2009 by Diego Marin, a licensed operator who was tired of seeing companies spray a baseboard, leave, and call it done.

He built ShieldPest around integrated pest management: find the source, seal the entry points, use the least-toxic effective treatment, and stand behind the result with a real guarantee.

Today our licensed technicians protect thousands of homes from ants to termites — always with pet-safe options, plain-language plans and the promise to come back free if pests do.
