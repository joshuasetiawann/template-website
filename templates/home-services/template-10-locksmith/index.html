About KeyFort

Your trusted local locksmith team

KeyFort Locksmith was founded in 2009 by master locksmith Marcus Vale after hearing too many stories of stranded people charged double once the locksmith showed up.

He built KeyFort on a fixed promise: upfront pricing quoted before the work, licensed and bonded technicians, and fast damage-free entry around the clock.

  • Background-checked, expert technicians
  • Upfront pricing — approved before we start
