About us

The story behind KeyFort Locksmith

For over 17 years, KeyFort Locksmith has been the locksmith neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

KeyFort Locksmith was founded in 2009 by master locksmith Marcus Vale after hearing too many stories of stranded people charged double once the locksmith showed up.

He built KeyFort on a fixed promise: upfront pricing quoted before the work, licensed and bonded technicians, and fast damage-free entry around the clock.

Today our mobile technicians cover the whole metro for homes, cars and businesses — and we still answer every lockout call as if someone we love is standing outside in the cold.
