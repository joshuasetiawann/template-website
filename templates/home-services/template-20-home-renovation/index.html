About StoneBridge

Your trusted local home renovation team

StoneBridge Renovations was founded in 2006 by Jonathan Reeves, who had watched too many remodels stall in the gap between a designer's vision and a builder's reality.

He built StoneBridge as a true design-build firm: one accountable team to design and construct, master craftsmen, premium materials, and white-glove project management from first sketch to final reveal.
