About us

The story behind StoneBridge Renovations

For over 20 years, StoneBridge Renovations has been the home renovation neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

StoneBridge Renovations was founded in 2006 by Jonathan Reeves, who had watched too many remodels stall in the gap between a designer's vision and a builder's reality.

He built StoneBridge as a true design-build firm: one accountable team to design and construct, master craftsmen, premium materials, and white-glove project management from first sketch to final reveal.

Today StoneBridge transforms kitchens, baths, additions and whole homes across the region — calm, on-time and beautifully built — and backs every project with a written workmanship warranty.
