About PureFiber

Your trusted local carpet cleaning team

PureFiber Carpet Care was founded in 2012 by Renzo Aquino, who saw how many families were left with carpets that looked clean on top but trapped allergens and odours underneath.

He invested in professional truck-mounted hot-water extraction and eco-safe, low-residue solutions to clean deeper, rinse cleaner and dry faster — without the sticky film cheaper methods leave behind.
