About us

The story behind PureFiber Carpet Care

For over 14 years, PureFiber Carpet Care has been the carpet cleaning neighbours trust — built on quality work, honest pricing and treating every home like our own.

Our story

From one van to your neighbourhood's most-trusted team

PureFiber Carpet Care was founded in 2012 by Renzo Aquino, who saw how many families were left with carpets that looked clean on top but trapped allergens and odours underneath.

He invested in professional truck-mounted hot-water extraction and eco-safe, low-residue solutions to clean deeper, rinse cleaner and dry faster — without the sticky film cheaper methods leave behind.

Today PureFiber refreshes carpets, upholstery and rugs across the region, and stands behind every job with a spotless guarantee that brings us back free if a stain dares to return.
