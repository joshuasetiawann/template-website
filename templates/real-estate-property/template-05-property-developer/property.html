About this property

Tower A launches the Northgate masterplan — a connected riverfront neighbourhood rising on the banks of the Colorado. One- and two-bedroom residences are designed for light, efficiency and long-term value.

Owners step out onto a landscaped plaza with retail, transit and a greenbelt at the door. Phase-one pricing reflects an early opportunity in a fast-growing district.
