About this property

Willowmere is a light, single-level cottage with two bedrooms, a private garden and your own front door — independence and privacy, with the reassurance of a caring community all around you.

Maintenance, gardening and domestic help are taken care of, and the village clubhouse, spa and country walks are moments away. Care is available on site should your needs ever change.
