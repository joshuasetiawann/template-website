Our story

The story behind Willowbrook Village

Willowbrook Village offers independent cottages and apartments plus assisted living in the Cotswolds — landscaped gardens, a wellness spa, restaurant and on-site care as needs change.

We believe later life should be rich, social and worry-free. Willowbrook combines the privacy of your own home with the security of a caring community on hand whenever you need it.

Residents enjoy beautiful gardens, a spa and a restaurant, with a calendar of activities — and the reassurance that care can step up gently if circumstances change.

From your first visit to settling in, our team makes the move calm and unhurried, and stays close throughout your time here.
