About Willowbrook

A community that grows with you

Willowbrook was created so that retirement could mean more freedom, not less. Own your home, keep your independence, and let us look after the gardening, maintenance and the worries.

And because care is available right here on site, you never have to move again as your needs change. It's peace of mind for you, and for the people who love you.

“The whole point of Willowbrook is that you only have to move once — to somewhere lovely.”
