Our story

The story behind Keyline Homes

Keyline Homes began in 2014 with a simple idea: house-hunting in Seattle should feel transparent, not stressful. We built a portal where every listing is verified, every floor area is accurate and every price is honest.

Today a team of 42 local agents covers 21 neighbourhoods, from the waterfront condos of Marina Quarter to the family streets of Greenwood. We pair the convenience of a search-and-save portal with the judgement of people who actually know each block.

We are an independent, licensed brokerage (WA-2204781). That independence means our advice answers to you and not to a listings quota — we would rather you waited for the right home than rushed the wrong one.

From your first saved search to the day we hand over the keys, one dedicated advisor stays with you. No call centres, no handoffs, no surprises at the door.
