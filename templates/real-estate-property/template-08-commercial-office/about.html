Our story

The story behind Meridian Workspace

Meridian Workspace leases premium office floors and suites across Chicago's Loop, River North and West Loop — full floor plates, ready suites and flexible terms for growing teams.

We are a landlord that thinks like an occupier. Our buildings prioritise the things teams actually feel day to day: light, air, connectivity and amenity that earns the commute.

Every floor plate is published with transparent pricing and a calculator to model your monthly occupancy cost, so leasing decisions start from real numbers.

From first tour to fit-out, a dedicated advisor manages your move and stays with you through the life of the lease.
