Our story

The story behind Maplewood Park

Maplewood Park is a new housing estate in Reading offering energy-efficient 2–5 bedroom homes — with Help to Buy, an on-site school, a village green and superb commuter links.

We are building more than houses. Maplewood is a real community: tree-lined streets, open green space, a primary school and shops, all within easy reach of the station.

Every home is built to a high energy-efficiency standard, with transparent pricing and a calculator to plan your monthly cost from the first visit.

From reservation to move-in, our customer-care team keeps your purchase clear and on track.
