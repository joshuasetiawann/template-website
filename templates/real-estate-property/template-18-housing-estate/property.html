About this property

The Ashbourne is a thoughtfully designed three-bedroom detached home on plot 12, offering bright, flexible living space with an open-plan kitchen-diner opening onto a turfed rear garden.

Built to an A energy rating with solar and a heat pump, it comes with a fitted designer kitchen, integrated appliances, allocated parking and a ten-year structural warranty. Help to Buy is available on selected plots.
