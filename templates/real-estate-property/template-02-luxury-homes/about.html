Our story

The story behind Belgrave Estates

Belgrave Estates represents an exclusive collection of the finest homes across Los Angeles and the California coast — oceanfront villas, architectural estates and trophy penthouses that rarely reach the open market.

Founded as a private atelier rather than a volume agency, we work with a deliberately small roster of clients and properties. Discretion is the default; many of our listings are shared by private dossier only.

Our advisors pair deep market intelligence with relationships built over decades — developers, architects and family offices who trust us with their most significant transactions.

Whether you are acquiring a Malibu beachfront or quietly disposing of a Bel-Air estate, every engagement is handled by a single partner with absolute confidentiality.
