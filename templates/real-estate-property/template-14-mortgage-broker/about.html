Our story

The story behind Beacon Mortgages

Beacon Mortgages is a whole-of-market broker comparing 90+ lenders for first-time buyers, remortgages, buy-to-let and self-employed clients — with genuinely expert, personal advice.

Being whole-of-market means we are not tied to any lender. We search the entire market for the deal that fits you, and we explain the trade-offs in plain English.

Our advisers handle the whole journey — decision in principle, application, paperwork and lender chasing — so your mortgage completes without the stress.

Use our calculator to model repayments, then book a free consultation with an adviser who will find you a deal you might not find alone.
