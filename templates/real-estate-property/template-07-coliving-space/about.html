Our story

The story behind Commonhouse

Commonhouse offers beautifully designed co-living rooms and studios across London — bills, cleaning and a built-in community included, with flexible monthly stays you can start this week.

We believe a home should give you more than four walls. Our houses pair private, design-led rooms with generous shared kitchens, lounges and workspaces, and a calendar of member events.

Everything is bundled: rent, utilities, fast Wi-Fi, fortnightly cleaning and access to every Commonhouse space in the city — one membership, one simple payment.

Move in this week, stay as long as it suits you, and let our community team handle the rest.
