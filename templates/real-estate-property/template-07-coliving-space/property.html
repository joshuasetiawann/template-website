About this property

The Loft Room gives you a beautifully furnished private bedroom with your own en-suite, set within a twelve-member co-living house designed by interior architects.

Everything beyond your door is shared and sociable: a chef's kitchen, co-working lounge and rooftop terrace, plus a calendar of house dinners and events. Bills, Wi-Fi and cleaning are all included in one flexible monthly price.
