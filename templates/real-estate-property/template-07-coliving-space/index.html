About Commonhouse

Designed for connection

We started Commonhouse because city living had become lonely and complicated. Our houses fix both: beautiful shared spaces that bring people together, and one simple bill that covers everything.

Each house has a community manager and a rhythm of dinners, socials and skill-shares — but never any pressure. Join in as much or as little as you like.

“The best cities are made of communities. We just build them one house at a time.”
