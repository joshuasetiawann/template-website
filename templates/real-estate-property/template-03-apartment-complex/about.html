Our story

The story behind The Aria Residences

The Aria Residences is a brand-new apartment community in Mission Bay, San Francisco — studio to three-bedroom homes wrapped around a rooftop pool, fitness centre and full-time concierge.

We designed Aria for the way people actually live in the city: light-filled layouts, generous storage, and amenity floors that feel like an extension of your home rather than a hotel lobby.

Leasing is handled in-house by a resident-experience team that stays with you long after move-in, from the first tour to your renewal.

Every floor plan is available to view, with transparent pricing and a budget calculator so you know exactly where you stand before you apply.
