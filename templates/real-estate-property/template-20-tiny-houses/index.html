About Acorn

Small homes, made with care

We started Acorn in a barn workshop with a belief that a smaller home could mean a bigger life — more freedom, less debt, lighter on the planet. Every house is still hand-built by our team.

We obsess over the details that make tiny living work: clever storage, a warm and quiet building envelope, and finishes that feel like a real home, not a glorified caravan.

“A tiny home isn't about living with less. It's about making room for what matters more.”
