About this property

The Cedar 24 packs a surprising amount of life into 240 square feet: a light great room, a full kitchen, a comfortable wet-room and a generous full-height sleeping loft reached by storage stairs.

Built on a road-legal trailer with cedar cladding, triple glazing and a heat pump, it's ready to tow, site and live in — with solar and off-grid options if you want to roam further.
