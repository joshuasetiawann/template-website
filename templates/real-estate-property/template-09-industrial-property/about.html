Our story

The story behind Irongate Industrial

Irongate Industrial leases warehouse, distribution, cold-storage and flex space across the Memphis inland port and logistics parks — with rapid highway, rail and air-cargo access.

Memphis moves the nation's freight, and our facilities are built for it: high clear heights, deep truck courts, abundant docks and power for the most demanding operations.

We publish full specifications for every facility — clear height, dock count, power, column spacing — so logistics teams can shortlist with confidence.

From site selection to onboarding, our team understands throughput, not just square footage, and stays with you through the lease.
