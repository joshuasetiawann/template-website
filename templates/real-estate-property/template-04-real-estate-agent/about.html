Our story

The story behind Hannah Ford Realty

Hannah Ford is a five-star Realtor® helping families buy and sell across Chapel Hill, Durham and the wider North Carolina Triangle — with warmth, patience and genuine local knowledge.

After more than a decade and 300+ closings, Hannah built a small, hand-picked team that shares one belief: real estate is about people first, transactions second.

Whether you are buying your first home or selling the one where you raised your kids, you get straight talk, sharp negotiation and someone who picks up the phone.

Hannah lives and works in the Triangle, so the advice you get is rooted in the streets, schools and seasons of the place you want to call home.
