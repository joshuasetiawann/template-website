Our story

The story behind Campus Nest

Campus Nest offers bright studios and en-suite rooms minutes from campus in Manchester — all bills included, superfast Wi-Fi, an on-site gym and a genuinely friendly community.

We get that student housing is about more than a bed. It is your first taste of independence, so we built spaces that are safe, social and stress-free, with everything bundled into one simple price.

Our team lives on site. That means real people to sort a parcel, fix a tap or point you to the best late-night food — not a ticket system that ignores you.

Book your room online in minutes, with a transparent price and no hidden fees. Move-in is made easy, and our community team makes sure you settle in fast.
