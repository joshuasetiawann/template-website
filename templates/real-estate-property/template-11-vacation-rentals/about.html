Our story

The story behind Azulado Stays

Azulado Stays is a curated collection of beachfront villas, bungalows and sea-view rentals across Mexico's Riviera Maya — private pools, concierge service and instant booking.

Every property is hand-selected and personally inspected. We turn down far more homes than we list, so when you book Azulado you book a guaranteed standard.

Our local concierge team lives on the coast and is on call throughout your stay — from grocery pre-stocking to private chefs, cenote tours and airport transfers.

Book instantly online with transparent nightly pricing and no hidden fees, and arrive to a home that is ready and waiting.
