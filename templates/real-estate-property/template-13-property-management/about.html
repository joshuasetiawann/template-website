Our story

The story behind Anchor Property Group

Anchor Property Group manages multifamily, single-family and HOA communities across Portland — tenant screening, 24/7 maintenance, online rent collection and transparent owner reporting.

We treat your property like an investment, because it is one. Our job is to protect its value, keep great residents in place and give you clear numbers every single month.

From rigorous screening to a 24/7 maintenance line, we handle the day-to-day so owners can step back, while a dedicated manager stays close to every property.

Owners see everything through a transparent portal — rent, expenses, maintenance and statements — with no surprises and no hidden fees.
