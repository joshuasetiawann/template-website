Who we are

Built on trust, driven by service

IronCrest Motorcycles opened its doors in 2013 with one goal: to make motorcycle dealership in Milwaukee simpler, fairer and a great deal friendlier.

What began as a small, family-run operation has grown into one of the most trusted names in the area — but our values haven't changed. We still believe in honest advice, transparent pricing and treating people the way we'd want to be treated.

Over the years we've helped thousands of customers, built a loyal community and earned a reputation we're proud of. Word of mouth remains our biggest source of new business, and that's no accident.

Today our team combines deep expertise with the latest tools and a genuinely warm welcome. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll get the same straight-talking, no-pressure service.

We're proud to call Milwaukee home, and we're committed to giving back to the community that has supported us from day one.
