SAT Prep

Most popular

Comprehensive, guaranteed SAT coaching across every section.

Program overview

Our SAT program is the gold standard of Summit prep. After a full diagnostic, your coach builds a plan targeting your weakest question types across Reading, Writing, and Math, drilling strategy and timing until your score climbs — backed by our improvement guarantee.

Course structure

  1. 01

    Diagnostic & Plan

    A full-length test and analysis set your baseline and goal.

  2. 02

    Reading & Writing

    Evidence strategy, grammar rules, and pacing for the verbal sections.

  3. 03

    Math · No & Yes Calculator

    Algebra, data, and advanced math with shortcut strategies.

  4. 04

    Full-Length Practice

    Repeated timed tests build stamina, pacing, and confidence.

Learning outcomes

  • Master every SAT question type
  • Apply timing and pacing strategies
  • Eliminate careless errors
  • Handle the toughest math problems
  • Stay calm and focused on test day
  • Hit your target SAT score

GRE Prep

Graduate

Quant, verbal, and analytical writing for grad-school applicants.

Program overview

Our GRE program prepares graduate-school applicants across the adaptive Quantitative and Verbal sections and the Analytical Writing essays. A diagnostic targets your weak areas, and our coaches demystify the adaptive format so you perform when the questions get harder.

Course structure

  1. 01

    Diagnostic & Plan

    A full GRE diagnostic and analysis set your baseline and goal.

  2. 02

    Quantitative Reasoning

    Arithmetic, algebra, and data interpretation with strategy.

  3. 03

    Verbal Reasoning

    Text completion, equivalence, and reading comprehension.

  4. 04

    Analytical Writing

    Structuring and writing high-scoring argument and issue essays.

Learning outcomes

  • Master GRE quant and verbal content
  • Navigate the adaptive test format
  • Apply timing strategies under pressure
  • Write high-scoring analytical essays
  • Build a targeted vocabulary
  • Reach your target GRE score
