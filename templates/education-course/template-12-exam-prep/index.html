About us

Test prep that’s precise, not generic

Summit was founded by tutors who were tired of generic prep books. Standardized tests are beatable — but only with a method as specific as the test itself.

Everything at Summit starts with data. A full-length diagnostic shows us exactly which question types cost you points, so every hour of prep targets your real weaknesses instead of re-covering what you already know.

Our coaches don’t just know the content — they know the test: its traps, its timing, its patterns. They teach the strategy and the mindset that turn a good student into a high scorer.

We track progress obsessively. Every practice test and drill feeds a dashboard you and your coach review together, so improvement is visible point by point.

And because we believe in the method, we stand behind it: our flagship courses come with a score-improvement guarantee, because students who follow the plan, improve.

The Summit method

Four steps from your score to your goal

Every Summit student follows the same data-driven path. Here’s how we add the points.

  1. 1
    01

    Diagnostic Exam

    You sit a full-length, official-format practice test under real conditions to establish a precise baseline score.

  2. 2
    02

    Score Analysis

    We break down every missed question by type and topic, revealing exactly where your points are leaking.

  3. 3
    03

    Goal & Plan

    Together we set a target score and build a week-by-week plan focused only on what will move your number.

  4. 4
    04

    Strategy Lessons

    Coaches teach the specific strategies, shortcuts, and traps for each section of your test.

  5. 5
    05

    Targeted Drilling

    You drill your weakest question types relentlessly until they become your strengths.

  6. 6
    06

    Timed Practice

    Regular full-length, timed practice tests build stamina and pacing under pressure.

  7. 7
    07

    Progress Review

    You and your coach review your dashboard, watching the score climb and adjusting the plan.

  8. 8
    08

    Test-Day Ready

    A final tune-up of strategy and mindset so you walk in calm, sharp, and confident.
