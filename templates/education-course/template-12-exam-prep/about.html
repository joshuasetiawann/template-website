Our story

How Summit Test Prep began

Every part of Summit Test Prep grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Summit Test Prep was founded in 2012 by top-scoring tutors who knew a secret: standardized tests reward strategy as much as knowledge. We built a program that teaches both — the content students need and the test-taking craft that turns a good score into a great one.

Our method is data-driven. Students take a realistic diagnostic, we pinpoint exactly where points are being lost, and every session targets the highest-yield improvements. Full-length practice tests under timed conditions build the stamina and calm that test day demands.

We prepare students for the SAT, ACT, PSAT, GRE, and GMAT, one-to-one and in focused small groups, in person and online. Our coaches have all scored in the top percentiles themselves, and they teach the exact strategies that got them there.

Since 2012 Summit students have lifted their scores by an average that opens doors to better colleges and bigger scholarships. We measure success in points gained and, more importantly, in the confidence students carry into the exam room.
