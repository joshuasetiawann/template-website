About us

Training that leads straight to work

TradeWorks was founded by tradespeople who saw too much demand for skilled workers and too few places teaching the trade properly, with real tools and real standards.

Everything here is hands-on. From your first week you’re in the workshop with the actual equipment of the trade — striking an arc, wiring a panel, brazing a joint — because skilled work is learned by doing it, under the eye of someone who’s done it for a living.

Our programs are short, focused, and built around the certifications employers actually ask for. We cut the filler and teach exactly what gets you certified, hired, and earning.

We work hand-in-hand with local contractors, manufacturers, and utilities, who hire our graduates, advise our curriculum, and often interview students before they even finish.

That tight industry link is why our job-placement rate sits at 92%. We don’t just train you for a certificate — we train you for a career, and we help you land the first job.

How training works

From first tool to certified pro

Every TradeWorks program follows the same proven, hands-on path. Here’s how we get you job-ready.

  1. 1
    01

    Safety & Foundations

    You start with shop safety, tools, and the fundamentals of the trade — the bedrock every skilled worker is built on.

  2. 2
    02

    Core Skills in the Shop

    Hands-on practice with the real equipment of the trade, building muscle memory and confidence under instruction.

  3. 3
    03

    Codes & Standards

    Learning the codes, specifications, and standards that govern professional work and keep it safe.

  4. 4
    04

    Real-World Projects

    Completing full projects to industry standard — a welded structure, a wired panel, a working system.

  5. 5
    05

    Certification Prep

    Focused preparation for the industry certification exam that employers require.

  6. 6
    06

    Certification Exam

    Sitting and passing the recognised credential that proves your skill to any employer.

  7. 7
    07

    Job Placement

    Our placement team connects you with hiring employers, prepares your resume, and lines up interviews.

  8. 8
    08

    Career & Beyond

    You start work as a certified pro, with the option to return for advanced and specialty credentials.
