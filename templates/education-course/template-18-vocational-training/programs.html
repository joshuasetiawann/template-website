Welding Technology

High demand

MIG, TIG, and stick welding to industry certification in six months.

Program overview

Our Welding Technology program takes you from your first arc to industry-certified welder in six months. Training is overwhelmingly hands-on in a fully equipped weld shop, covering the major processes and positions employers demand — finishing with certification prep and job placement.

Program modules

  1. 01

    Safety & Setup

    Shop safety, equipment, and reading welding symbols and blueprints.

  2. 02

    MIG & Flux-Core

    Wire-feed welding for production and structural work.

  3. 03

    Stick & TIG

    Stick welding and precision TIG across materials and positions.

  4. 04

    Certification & Projects

    Welding to code on real projects and certification testing.

Learning outcomes

  • Weld safely with MIG, TIG, and stick
  • Read welding symbols and blueprints
  • Weld in multiple positions
  • Pass an industry welding certification
  • Complete projects to code
  • Be hired-ready as a welder

At a glance

Duration6 months, day or evening
FormatHands-on weld shop
CredentialIndustry welding certification
Outcome92% job placement

  • Aged 18 or over
  • No experience needed
  • Physically able to do shop work
  • High-school diploma or equivalent

Electrical Technician

Popular

Residential and commercial wiring toward electrical certification.

Program overview

The Electrical Technician program trains you to wire and service residential and commercial systems to code. Over nine months of hands-on lab work you’ll master circuits, panels, and the electrical code, finishing certified and ready for a licensed-track career.

Program modules

  1. 01

    Electrical Fundamentals

    Circuits, theory, and safe working practices.

  2. 02

    Residential Wiring

    Wiring homes — outlets, lighting, and service panels.

  3. 03

    Commercial & Code

    Commercial systems and mastering the electrical code.

  4. 04

    Certification & Projects

    Full installations and certification preparation.

Learning outcomes

  • Wire residential and commercial systems
  • Install and service panels safely
  • Apply the electrical code correctly
  • Diagnose and fix electrical faults
  • Pass an electrical certification
  • Begin a licensed-track career

HVAC/R

In demand

Install and repair heating, cooling, and refrigeration systems.

Program overview

The HVAC/R program prepares you to install, maintain, and repair heating, ventilation, air-conditioning, and refrigeration systems. Eight months of hands-on training cover both the mechanical and electrical sides of the trade, finishing with certification and placement support.

Program modules

  1. 01

    HVAC Fundamentals

    How heating and cooling systems work, and safe practices.

  2. 02

    Refrigeration

    The refrigeration cycle, charging, and EPA handling.

  3. 03

    Electrical & Controls

    Wiring, thermostats, and system controls.

  4. 04

    Install, Service & Certify

    Full installs, diagnostics, and certification prep.

Learning outcomes

  • Install heating and cooling systems
  • Service and charge refrigeration
  • Wire and troubleshoot controls
  • Diagnose system faults
  • Pass HVAC and EPA certifications
  • Enter a high-demand trade
