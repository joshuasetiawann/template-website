Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • 18+ (or 16+ with consent for some programs)
  • High-school diploma or equivalent
  • Steel-toe boots and safety gear (guidance provided)
  • A placement chat with an advisor
  • Regular attendance in labs
  • A strong work ethic
