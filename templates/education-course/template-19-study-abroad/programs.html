Study-abroad programs in detail

Every Horizon program is credit-bearing and fully supported, from placement to homecoming. Here is what each program includes, what you’ll gain, costs and scholarships, and how to apply.

Semester Abroad

Most popular

A full semester at a partner university, credits and all.

Program overview

The Semester Abroad program places you at one of our partner universities overseas for a full term, with your credits transferring home. We arrange your placement, visa, and housing, and support you throughout — so you can immerse yourself in a new country while staying on track to graduate.

What’s included

  1. 01

    University Placement

    Admission to a partner university matched to your major.

  2. 02

    Credit Transfer

    Pre-approved courses that count toward your home degree.

  3. 03

    Housing & Visa

    Vetted housing and full step-by-step visa support.

  4. 04

    On-Site Support

    Orientation, a local team, and a 24/7 support line.

Learning outcomes

  • Study at a university overseas
  • Earn credits toward your degree
  • Adapt to and thrive in a new culture
  • Build an international network
  • Grow independence and confidence
  • Strengthen your resume globally

At a glance

DurationOne semester
Where30+ countries
CreditTransfers to your degree
Support24/7 in-country

From

From $9,800 / semester

Scholarships, transferable financial aid, and interest-free plans help cover costs.

Entry requirements

  • Enrolled university student
  • Good academic standing
  • Valid passport
  • Home-school study-abroad approval

Scholarship: Global Citizen Scholarships award up to $5,000 toward a semester abroad.

Language Immersion

All levels

Live the language with a host family in-country.

Program overview

The Language Immersion program drops you into your target language by living with a host family and studying at a local language school. From four to twelve weeks, it’s the fastest, most joyful way to real fluency — and it welcomes complete beginners as warmly as advanced speakers.

What’s included

  1. 01

    Language School

    Intensive daily classes matched to your level.

  2. 02

    Host-Family Stay

    Living with a vetted local family for true immersion.

  3. 03

    Cultural Activities

    Excursions and events that bring the culture alive.

  4. 04

    Full Support

    Orientation, a local team, and a 24/7 support line.

Learning outcomes

  • Make rapid gains in fluency
  • Live comfortably in the language daily
  • Experience authentic local culture
  • Build confidence speaking
  • Form bonds with a host family
  • Earn optional academic credit

At a glance

Duration4–12 weeks
LanguagesMany, all levels
StayVetted host families
Support24/7 in-country

From

From $3,900

Scholarships and interest-free payment plans help make immersion affordable.

Entry requirements

  • Open to all levels
  • Aged 16 or over
  • Valid passport
  • Openness to a new culture

Scholarship: Language Access Grants support first-time and first-generation travellers.

Global Internship

Career-boost

Professional experience abroad with full support.

Program overview

The Global Internship program places you in a professional internship overseas, paired with housing and cultural programming. It’s a powerful way to gain international work experience, build your resume, and grow personally — all while living in a new country with Horizon’s support.

What’s included

  1. 01

    Internship Placement

    A role matched to your field at a host organisation abroad.

  2. 02

    Housing & Logistics

    Vetted housing and arrival arranged for you.

  3. 03

    Professional Coaching

    Resume, workplace-culture, and career guidance.

  4. 04

    Cultural Programming

    Events and excursions to make the most of your destination.

Learning outcomes

  • Gain international work experience
  • Build a standout global resume
  • Navigate a foreign workplace culture
  • Expand your professional network
  • Grow independence and adaptability
  • Clarify your career direction

At a glance

Duration8–16 weeks
FieldsMany industries
WhereAcross four continents
Support24/7 in-country

From

From $5,400

Scholarships and interest-free payment plans help make internships affordable.

Entry requirements

  • Student or recent graduate
  • A resume and short interview
  • Valid passport
  • Professional commitment

Scholarship: Career Mobility Scholarships support students pursuing global internships.

Scholarships to send you further

Horizon awards Global Citizen Scholarships of up to $5,000 toward a semester abroad, plus Language Access and Career Mobility grants for first-time and first-generation travellers. Combined with transferable aid and interest-free payment plans, the world is more within reach than you think.
