Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Be enrolled in or a graduate of secondary or higher education
  • A valid passport (we help you apply)
  • Minimum GPA varies by program
  • An advising conversation
  • Visa and insurance (we guide you)
  • A sense of adventure
