Our story

How Horizon Study Abroad began

Every part of Horizon Study Abroad grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Horizon Study Abroad was founded in 2010 by people who had their own lives changed by a semester overseas — and wanted to make that experience safe, accessible, and unforgettable for the next generation. From a single partner university, we've grown into a trusted gateway to study abroad on every continent.

We handle the hard parts so students can focus on the adventure. From choosing the right program and university to visas, housing, insurance, and pre-departure preparation, our advisors guide every step, and our on-the-ground support means help is always close while students are away.

Our programs span semester and full-degree study, language immersion, global internships, summer programs, and volunteer experiences. We partner only with universities and providers we'd send our own families to, and we match each student to the destination and program that fits their goals.

Since 2010 thousands of students have crossed borders with Horizon and come home transformed — more confident, more capable, and more connected to the world. We measure success in passports stamped and perspectives widened, one student at a time.
