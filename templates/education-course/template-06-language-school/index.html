Our approach

Conversation first, always

Linguava began when two language teachers grew tired of students who could conjugate perfectly yet freeze the moment a real person spoke to them.

Our method is simple but radical: you talk from minute one. Grammar is learned in service of speaking, not the other way around, and every class of six gives you constant time on the microphone.

All of our tutors are native or near-native speakers trained in our conversational method. They correct gently, encourage relentlessly, and bring the living culture of the language into every room.

Beyond class, Linguava runs weekly conversation cafés, film nights, and cooking workshops so you practise in the wild — the way languages are really learned.

Our levels map directly to the international CEFR framework, so your progress is recognised everywhere, whether for travel, work, or study abroad.

The Linguava path

From silence to fluency, step by step

Our levels follow the international CEFR framework. Open any stage to see what you’ll be able to do.

Greet people, introduce yourself, order food, and handle simple everyday exchanges. You speak in full sentences from week one.

Describe your day, shop, travel, and chat about familiar topics with growing confidence.

Handle most travel situations, express opinions, and tell stories in past and future tenses.

Hold longer conversations on familiar topics, understand the main points of clear speech and media.

Discuss abstract topics, argue a point, and interact with native speakers without strain.

Understand idiom, humour, and register, and adapt how you speak to the situation.

Use the language flexibly for academic, professional, and social life with ease.

Express subtle shades of meaning and understand demanding, long texts and talks.

Understand virtually everything and express yourself spontaneously and precisely.

Sit a CEFR-aligned assessment at any level for an internationally recognised certificate.
