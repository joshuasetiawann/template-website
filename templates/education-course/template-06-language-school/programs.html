Programs & Curriculum

Language courses in detail

Every Linguava course is conversation-first, capped at six learners, and aligned to the international CEFR framework. Here is the curriculum for each language family, what you’ll be able to do, pricing with payment options, and how to start.

SpanishFrenchBusiness English

Spanish A1–C2

Popular

Our flagship language, from first words to genuine fluency.

Program overview

The Spanish programme carries you across all six CEFR levels with native tutors from across the Spanish-speaking world. You speak from lesson one and progress through ten-week terms, supported by weekly conversation cafés and cultural events.

Levels & content

  1. 01

    A1–A2 · Foundations

    Greetings, daily life, travel, and the present and simple past tenses.

  2. 02

    B1 · Independence

    Opinions, storytelling, and handling real situations confidently.

  3. 03

    B2 · Fluency

    Abstract discussion, idiom, and interacting with native speakers at ease.

  4. 04

    C1–C2 · Mastery

    Academic and professional Spanish with near-native nuance.

Learning outcomes

  • Hold real conversations from week one
  • Use past, present, and future tenses
  • Travel and live in Spanish comfortably
  • Discuss opinions and abstract topics
  • Understand films and native speech
  • Earn a CEFR-aligned certificate

French A1–C1

Loved

Bonjour to fluent, with Parisian and Québécois tutors.

Program overview

The French programme spans A1 to C1 with tutors from France and Canada. Pronunciation and rhythm are emphasised from the start, and you build toward confident, culturally fluent conversation across five CEFR levels.

Levels & content

  1. 01

    A1–A2 · Premiers Pas

    Everyday French, sounds and rhythm, and the core tenses.

  2. 02

    B1 · Autonomie

    Expressing opinions, narrating, and handling travel and work life.

  3. 03

    B2 · Aisance

    Debate, nuance, and understanding fast native speech and media.

  4. 04

    C1 · Maîtrise

    Flexible, precise French for academic and professional settings.

Learning outcomes

  • Speak with accurate French pronunciation
  • Converse on everyday and abstract topics
  • Use a full range of tenses
  • Understand French films and news
  • Write clear emails and essays
  • Earn a CEFR-aligned certificate

Business English B1–C2

Professional

Perform in meetings, emails, and presentations with confidence.

Program overview

Business English is built for professionals who already speak English but need to perform in high-stakes situations. Across eight-week terms you’ll master meetings, negotiation, presentations, and professional writing with a tutor who knows the corporate world.

Modules

  1. 01

    Meetings & Calls

    Leading and contributing to meetings and conference calls clearly.

  2. 02

    Professional Writing

    Emails, reports, and proposals that are concise and persuasive.

  3. 03

    Presentations

    Structuring and delivering confident, memorable presentations.

  4. 04

    Negotiation & Networking

    The language of deals, diplomacy, and professional small talk.

Learning outcomes

  • Lead a meeting in English
  • Write clear professional emails and reports
  • Deliver a confident presentation
  • Negotiate and handle pushback
  • Network and build rapport
  • Earn a CEFR-aligned certificate
