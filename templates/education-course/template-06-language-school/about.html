Our story

How Linguava Language School began

Every part of Linguava Language School grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Linguava was founded in 2009 by language teachers who were frustrated that years of grammar drills left students unable to order a coffee abroad. We built a school around a simple promise: you will speak from day one, because a language is something you do, not something you memorize.

Our method is conversation-first. Native-speaking tutors lead small classes where you talk from the opening minutes, with grammar woven in only as you need it. Real situations — markets, meetings, friendships — shape every lesson, so what you learn is what you can actually use.

We teach Spanish, French, Mandarin, Italian, and more, from absolute beginner to advanced fluency, alongside Business English and children's classes. Online and in-person learners share the same small-group warmth and the same patient, encouraging tutors.

Since 2009 thousands of learners have walked out of Linguava able to hold a real conversation — for travel, for work, for family, or simply for the joy of it. We measure success the moment a student stops translating in their head and just speaks.
