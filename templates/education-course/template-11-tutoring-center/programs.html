Math Tutoring (K-12)

Most requested

From number sense to calculus, rebuilding the foundations first.

Program overview

Math tutoring is BrightPath’s most requested program because math gaps compound. We diagnose exactly which foundations are missing, rebuild them, then move ahead — turning ‘I’m just bad at math’ into genuine, confident competence.

How it works

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    A detailed assessment pinpoints precise gaps from earlier years.

  2. 02

    Foundations

    We close those gaps first so new topics finally make sense.

  3. 03

    Current Curriculum

    Aligned support with classwork, homework, and upcoming tests.

  4. 04

    Stretch & Confidence

    Moving ahead of class to build real momentum and belief.

Learning outcomes

  • Close hidden gaps from earlier grades
  • Keep pace with current classwork
  • Approach problems methodically
  • Show working clearly and accurately
  • Tackle tests calmly and confidently
  • Believe in their own math ability

English & Reading

Core

Reading, writing, and comprehension that lift every subject.

Program overview

Strong reading and writing lift a child’s performance across the whole curriculum. Our English program builds decoding, comprehension, and clear writing, tailored to your child’s level — whether they need to catch up or push ahead.

How it works

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    Assessing reading level, comprehension, and writing skills.

  2. 02

    Reading Fluency

    Building decoding, vocabulary, and reading for meaning.

  3. 03

    Writing Craft

    Sentences to paragraphs to essays, with clear structure.

  4. 04

    Comprehension & Analysis

    Understanding, inferring, and responding to texts.

Learning outcomes

  • Read fluently and for meaning
  • Build a richer vocabulary

Study Skills Course

New

How to revise, manage time, and beat exam nerves.

Program overview

The Study Skills course teaches the meta-skills schools rarely do: how to revise effectively, manage time, organise notes, and stay calm under exam pressure. Over six weeks your teen builds a personal system that lifts results across every subject.

Course content

  1. 01

    Effective Revision

    Active-recall and spaced techniques that actually work.

  2. 02

    Time & Organisation

    Planning, prioritising, and beating procrastination.

  3. 03

    Note-Taking

    Capturing and organising information that sticks.

  4. 04

    Exam Confidence

    Managing nerves and performing on the day.

Learning outcomes

  • Revise using proven active methods
  • Plan and manage study time
  • Take clear, useful notes
  • Organise materials and deadlines
  • Manage exam stress and nerves
  • Apply skills across every subject
