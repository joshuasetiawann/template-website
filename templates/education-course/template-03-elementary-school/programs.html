Programs & Curriculum

Grade levels & learning

From the first day of Kindergarten to the big step into middle school, Sunnybrook grows with your child. Here is what each grade band explores, what your child will learn, and how to enrol.

Early YearsMiddle YearsUpper Years

Kindergarten & Grade 1

Ages 5–7

The joyful first steps of reading, numbers, and friendship.

Program overview

Our early-years classrooms are gentle, playful, and full of wonder. Through songs, stories, and hands-on play, children learn their letters and numbers, make their first friends, and discover that school is a happy place to be.

What we learn

  1. 01

    Early Literacy

    Phonics songs, letter play, and cosy story time build a love of reading.

  2. 02

    Number Sense

    Counting, sorting, and shape games with blocks and real objects.

  3. 03

    Discovery Play

    Sand, water, and building corners that teach through exploration.

  4. 04

    Friendship & Feelings

    Sharing, taking turns, and naming big emotions kindly.

Learning outcomes

  • Recognise letters and their sounds
  • Count and compare numbers to 20
  • Hold a pencil and write their name
  • Take turns and make friends
  • Follow a happy daily routine
  • Speak up in a group circle

Grades 2 & 3

Ages 7–9

Confident readers and curious young scientists.

Program overview

In grades 2 and 3, children become independent readers and writers, dive into hands-on science, and tackle bigger maths challenges. Project days let them research, build, and present, growing both skills and confidence.

What we learn

  1. 01

    Reading & Writing

    Chapter books, journals, and the first real stories children author.

  2. 02

    Maths Explorers

    Addition, subtraction, and the start of multiplication through games.

  3. 03

    Little Scientists

    Experiments, nature study, and the wonder of asking ‘why?’

  4. 04

    Project Days

    Researching a topic and proudly presenting it to the class.

Learning outcomes

  • Read chapter books with understanding
  • Write a paragraph and short story
  • Add, subtract, and begin times tables
  • Run a simple science experiment
  • Research and present a project
  • Work kindly in a small team

Grades 4 & 5

Ages 9–11

Bold thinkers ready for the next big step.

Program overview

Our oldest students take on leadership, deeper projects, and beginner coding in the Robot Lab. We prepare them — academically and emotionally — for a smooth, confident move into middle school.

What we learn

  1. 01

    Advanced Literacy

    Novels, research reports, and persuasive writing with real audiences.

  2. 02

    Maths & Logic

    Multiplication, division, fractions, and fun logic puzzles.

  3. 03

    Robot Lab

    Beginner coding and building friendly robots step by step.

  4. 04

    Leadership

    Class jobs, mentoring younger buddies, and planning school events.

Learning outcomes

  • Read and analyse a full novel
  • Write a clear research report
  • Work with fractions and division
  • Code a simple robot program
  • Lead a class job or project
  • Feel ready for middle school

Enrollment steps

How to join Sunnybrook

Joining our family is simple and friendly. Here is what to expect.

  1. Step 1

    Say hello

    Send a note or call, and we’ll book a sunny campus tour for your family.

  2. Step 2

    Visit & try a class

    Bring your child to meet a teacher and join in a real lesson.

  3. Step 3

    Complete enrollment

    Fill in a simple form and send your child’s records — that’s it!

  4. Step 4

    Welcome day

    Your child meets their class and we celebrate a brand-new Sunnybrook friend.
