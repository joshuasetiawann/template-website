Departments

How our teaching is organised

Our faculty work across focused departments, each led by experienced specialists.

Early Years (K–1)

Phonics, numbers, and lots of play as children settle into school.

Lower Elementary (2–3)

Confident reading, writing, and maths through projects.

Upper Elementary (4–5)

Deeper reading, science, and the big step toward middle school.

Specials

Art, music, robotics, and movement for every grade. 🎵

Teaching philosophy

How we teach

Our teachers believe young children learn by doing. Lessons start with a story or a question, move into hands-on projects, and always leave room for play — because a happy child is a child who learns. 🌟
