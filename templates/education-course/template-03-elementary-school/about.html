Our story

How Sunnybrook Elementary began

Every part of Sunnybrook Elementary grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Sunnybrook Elementary opened in 1996 with one big idea: that young children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and a little bit delighted. 🌈 Our first classes met in a sunny brick schoolhouse on Maple Lane, and that same warmth still greets every child at the door each morning.

We teach reading, writing, and maths with care and structure — but we teach them through stories, building, music, and lots of questions. Children move, make, and explore, because a five-year-old learns fractions faster with a pizza they cut themselves than with a worksheet.

Every classroom is a small community. Teachers loop ideas across subjects, older buddies read with younger ones, and families are part of the day, from reading mornings to the spring science fair. Kindness is taught as carefully as spelling.

Today Sunnybrook is home to around 320 children from Kindergarten through Grade 5. We stay small on purpose so every child is known by name, and so school feels like a place they actually want to be. 🍎
