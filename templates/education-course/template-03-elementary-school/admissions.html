Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Child's name and date of birth
  • Proof of age (birth certificate)
  • Up-to-date immunisation records
  • Any learning or allergy needs to share
  • A completed enrolment form
  • Emergency contact details

Tuition & fees

  • Half Day (K)$640 / moMornings for our youngest Kindergarten learners
  • Full Day$880 / moFull school day, K–5 · lunch and snacks available
  • With SupportFrom $300 / moSliding-scale places for qualifying families

We never want cost to keep a child from Sunnybrook. Sliding-scale places and a family support fund are available — just ask our office in confidence, and it will never affect your child's place. 💛
