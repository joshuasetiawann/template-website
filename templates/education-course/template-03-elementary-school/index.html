Our happy place

A safe, sunny place to grow

Sunnybrook was built around one simple belief: children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and excited to come to school.

Our classrooms buzz with reading corners, science tables, and art walls covered in real student work. Learning here is active — children build, measure, paint, and explore their way to understanding.

Small class sizes mean every teacher knows every child: their strengths, their wobbles, and exactly what makes them light up. Families are partners, welcomed into the school every week.

Beyond academics, Sunnybrook teaches kindness on purpose. Our ‘Buddy Bench’, class jobs, and weekly community circle help children become caring friends and confident speakers.

Every child finishes the day outdoors — our two-acre garden and playground turn recess into a hands-on lesson in nature, teamwork, and play.

“My daughter literally runs into school every morning. That tells me everything.”

— Rebecca Lin, Sunnybrook parent

Parent questions

Questions parents ask

Anything else? Just call or pop in — we love saying hello.

Sunnybrook welcomes children from Kindergarten (age 5) through Grade 5 (age 11). We can arrange a visit so your child can meet their future teacher.

We offer a healthy hot-lunch option and a daily morning snack, and we happily accommodate allergies and dietary needs.

The school day runs 8:15 AM to 3:00 PM, with optional before-care from 7:15 AM and after-care until 6:00 PM.

Our campus is fully secured with a single supervised entrance, and every staff member is background-checked and first-aid trained.
