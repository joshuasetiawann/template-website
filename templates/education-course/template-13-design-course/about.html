Our story

How Prism Design School began

Every part of Prism Design School grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Prism Design School was founded in 2018 by working designers who knew that portfolios get people hired, not certificates. We built a course around that truth: every week you ship real design work, and you graduate with a portfolio that proves you can do the job.

Our curriculum mirrors how design actually happens in industry. You research real users, sketch and prototype, run critiques, and iterate — learning Figma and the modern toolset by using them on genuine briefs, with mentors who do this work for a living.

We teach UX, UI, product, and graphic design, with foundations for career-changers and depth for those going further. Cohorts are small and the critique culture is honest and kind, because designers grow fastest when feedback is frequent and real.

Since 2018 Prism graduates have moved into design roles at startups and product teams, portfolios in hand. We measure success by one thing: whether our graduates can walk into an interview, open their work, and get the job.
