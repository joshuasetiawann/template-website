The 20-week path

From first wireframe to a hire-ready portfolio

Prism is relentlessly hands-on. Open any phase to see what you research, design, and ship.

Visual principles, typography, colour, and layout. You complete daily design exercises and your first style explorations.

Interviews, surveys, and synthesis. You uncover real user needs and frame a design problem worth solving.

User flows, sitemaps, and wireframes that turn research into structure.

Visual design systems, components, and pixel-perfect interfaces in industry tools.

Interactive prototypes, usability testing, and iterating on real feedback.

An end-to-end product designed from research to polished, tested prototype.

Building scalable systems and handing off cleanly to engineers.

Crafting four case studies that tell the story of your process and impact.

Portfolio reviews, interview prep, and design-challenge practice.

You present your portfolio to our hiring partners, then continue with 6 months of career support.
