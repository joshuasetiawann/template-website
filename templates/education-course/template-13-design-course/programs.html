Design tracks in detail

UX/UI Design

Flagship

20 weeks from first wireframe to a four-project portfolio.

Program overview

Our flagship UX/UI track takes complete beginners to hire-ready product designers. You’ll run real user research, design interfaces and systems, build interactive prototypes, and craft four portfolio case studies — finishing with a career sprint that gets you interview-ready.

Curriculum modules

  1. 01

    Foundations

    Visual principles, typography, and layout across the first weeks.

  2. 02

    Research & IA

    User research, synthesis, flows, and wireframes.

  3. 03

    UI & Prototyping

    Visual systems, interfaces, and tested interactive prototypes.

  4. 04

    Capstone & Career

    An end-to-end capstone plus a portfolio and career sprint.

Learning outcomes

  • Run and synthesise user research
  • Design polished, usable interfaces
  • Build and test interactive prototypes
  • Create scalable design systems
  • Craft four portfolio case studies
  • Interview confidently for design roles

Product Design

Advanced

20 weeks of UX plus product strategy and metrics.

Program overview

The Product Design track pairs the full UX/UI curriculum with product strategy, metrics, and collaboration with engineers. It’s ideal for those who want to design not just screens but successful products, working fluently with business and tech.

Curriculum modules

  1. 01

    UX Core

    Research, IA, interface design, and prototyping foundations.

  2. 02

    Product Strategy

    Problem framing, prioritisation, and product metrics.

  3. 03

    Design & Dev Handoff

    Working with engineers and shipping real features.

  4. 04

    Capstone & Career

    A product capstone plus a portfolio and career sprint.

Learning outcomes

  • Design end-to-end product experiences
  • Frame problems and prioritise with metrics
  • Collaborate with product and engineering
  • Build and test interactive prototypes
  • Craft strategy-led case studies
  • Interview for product-design roles

Graphic Design

Visual

14 weeks of typography, branding, and layout.

Program overview

The Graphic Design track builds a strong visual designer through typography, branding, and layout for print and digital. Over fourteen weeks you’ll complete a series of real projects — from identity systems to editorial layouts — that become a striking visual portfolio.

Curriculum modules

  1. 01

    Typography & Layout

    Type, grids, and composition that command attention.

  2. 02

    Branding & Identity

    Logos, identity systems, and brand guidelines.

  3. 03

    Editorial & Print

    Posters, publications, and print-ready production.

  4. 04

    Digital & Portfolio

    Digital assets and a polished visual portfolio.

Learning outcomes

  • Use typography and grids expertly
  • Design brand identity systems
  • Lay out editorial and print work
  • Prepare files for professional print
  • Create digital design assets
  • Build a striking visual portfolio
