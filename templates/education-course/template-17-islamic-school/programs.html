Programs & Curriculum

Programs in detail

Every Al-Noor program integrates a strong academic curriculum with Quran, Arabic, and Islamic studies, at the right level for each stage. Here is what each program covers, what students gain, tuition with assistance, and how to enrol.

Elementary (Grades 1–5)

Foundations

Strong academic foundations woven with daily deen.

Program overview

Our elementary program builds firm foundations in reading, writing, and mathematics while integrating daily Quran, beginning Arabic, and the beloved stories of the Prophets. Children grow academically and spiritually in nurturing, small classes.

Curriculum areas

  1. 01

    Core Academics

    Reading, writing, mathematics, science, and social studies.

  2. 02

    Quran & Tajweed

    Daily recitation and memorization of short surahs with tajweed.

  3. 03

    Arabic Language

    A structured program building toward Quranic fluency.

  4. 04

    Islamic Studies

    Aqeedah, manners (adab), and the stories of the Prophets.

Learning outcomes

  • Read, write, and reason confidently
  • Recite Quran with correct tajweed
  • Memorize a portion of the Quran
  • Build foundational Arabic
  • Practise beautiful Islamic manners
  • Love both learning and their deen

High School (Grades 9–12)

College-prep

College-preparatory academics with advanced Islamic studies.

Program overview

Our high school combines a college-preparatory academic curriculum — with honors and advanced options — and mature Islamic studies that equip students to live their faith with confidence. Dedicated college counseling from Grade 9 guides every student to the right university.

Curriculum areas

  1. 01

    College-Prep Academics

    Honors and advanced courses across the core subjects.

  2. 02

    Advanced Islamic Studies

    Fiqh, seerah, ethics, and contemporary Muslim life.

  3. 03

    College Counseling

    Personalized guidance from Grade 9 to university acceptance.

  4. 04

    Service & Leadership

    Community service, halaqa leadership, and student government.

Learning outcomes

  • Prepare for top universities
  • Study Islam maturely and confidently
  • Develop strong character (akhlaq)
  • Lead and serve their community
  • Navigate identity with confidence
  • Graduate ready for deen and dunya

Hifz Program

By placement

Memorize the Quran alongside a full academic education.

Program overview

The Hifz Program is a dedicated Quran-memorization track that runs alongside academic studies, so committed students can become huffaz without pausing their education. Guided by experienced huffaz, students progress at a pace suited to them, with strong support and care.

Program elements

  1. 01

    Memorization (Hifz)

    Structured daily memorization with experienced huffaz.

  2. 02

    Revision (Muraja’ah)

    Consistent review to retain and strengthen what is learned.

  3. 03

    Tajweed Mastery

    Refined recitation rules for beautiful, accurate Quran.

  4. 04

    Integrated Academics

    A full academic curriculum continues alongside Hifz.

Learning outcomes

  • Memorize the Quran with tajweed
  • Develop strong revision discipline
  • Recite beautifully and accurately
  • Continue full academic progress
  • Grow in patience and devotion
  • Join Al-Noor’s community of huffaz
