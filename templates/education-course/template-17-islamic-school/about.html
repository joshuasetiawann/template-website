Our story

How Al-Noor Academy began

Every part of Al-Noor Academy grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Al-Noor Academy was founded in 1995 by families who wanted their children to excel academically without choosing between faith and knowledge. From a small weekend program in Meadowvale, the Academy grew into a full Pre-K to Grade 12 school where both flourish together.

Our students follow a rigorous academic curriculum — strong in mathematics, science, and the humanities — woven together with Islamic studies, Quran, and Arabic. We believe knowledge and faith are not rivals but companions, and our graduates carry both with confidence.

Character is at the heart of an Al-Noor education. Through daily prayer, service to the community, and a culture of respect and excellence, students develop the akhlaq — the good character — that defines a person far beyond any transcript.

For nearly three decades Al-Noor graduates have gone on to leading universities and meaningful careers, grounded in their identity and their values. We measure success in scholars who are also people of integrity, service, and faith.
