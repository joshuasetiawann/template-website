About Al-Noor

Where deen and dunya thrive together

Al-Noor Academy was founded by parents and educators who refused to choose between academic excellence and Islamic upbringing — and built a school that delivers both, fully.

Our integrated curriculum holds two goals in balance: that every graduate is prepared for the best universities, and grounded in a confident, practising Muslim identity. Strong academics and a living faith are not in tension here; they reinforce one another.

Days at Al-Noor blend mathematics, science, and literature with Quran, Arabic, and Islamic studies, all in an environment shaped by Islamic values of respect, discipline, and compassion.

Beyond the classroom, our students lead halaqas, serve their community, and grow through sports, the arts, and student leadership — developing the whole child, body, mind, and soul.

We partner closely with families, because an Islamic education is a shared trust (amanah). Together we raise young people of excellent character who carry their faith and their knowledge with confidence into the world.

The Al-Noor journey

An integrated path, year by year

Our curriculum grows with each child, balancing academic rigour with Islamic knowledge. Open any stage to learn more.

Through play, song, and story, young children learn short surahs, beautiful manners (adab), and early literacy and numeracy in a warm, nurturing setting.

Strong foundations in reading, writing, and mathematics, integrated with daily Quran recitation, beginning Arabic, and the stories of the Prophets.

Tajweed-focused Quran lessons and a structured Arabic program build fluency in the language of the Quran.

A rigorous academic curriculum meets deeper Islamic studies — fiqh, seerah, and aqeedah — and growing personal responsibility.

Students lead halaqas, mentor younger pupils, and take part in community service and student government.

Honors and advanced courses prepare students for top universities, with dedicated college counseling from Grade 9.

Mature study of Islamic thought, ethics, and contemporary issues equips students to live their faith with confidence.

For committed students, a structured Hifz program runs alongside academics, supported by experienced huffaz.

A required service program and identity-building seminars nurture confident, contributing young Muslims.

Graduates leave grounded in faith and ready for university, joining an alumni community that stays connected.
