Our story

How ForgeLabs Bootcamp began

Every part of ForgeLabs Bootcamp grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

ForgeLabs was founded in 2017 by engineers who were tired of watching talented, motivated people get locked out of tech by four-year price tags and gatekeeping. We set out to build the most honest, outcomes-driven path into software engineering we could — sixteen weeks, real projects, and a job at the end.

Our curriculum is reverse-engineered from what hiring managers actually need. Students write code every single day, ship full-stack applications, review each other's pull requests, and learn the tools and habits of a working engineering team — not just syntax.

Mentorship is the engine. Small cohorts, instructors who have shipped production software, and a dedicated career team that works with you from week one to your first offer. We are transparent about outcomes because we stake our reputation on them.

Since 2017, ForgeLabs graduates have launched careers at startups and household-name companies alike, many doubling or tripling their previous income. We exist for one number: the percentage of our graduates who get hired.
