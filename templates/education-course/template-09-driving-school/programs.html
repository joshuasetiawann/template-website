Beginner Lessons

Popular

From your first time behind the wheel to test-ready, at your pace.

Program overview

Our beginner lessons take you from never having driven to confidently test-ready. Booked hourly and built around a clear structure, they let you progress at a relaxed pace with the same patient instructor throughout, in a dual-control car for total safety.

Lesson structure

  1. 01

    Stage 1 · Controls

    Cockpit drill, moving off and stopping, and basic steering in quiet areas.

  2. 02

    Stage 2 · Road Skills

    Junctions, roundabouts, gears, and reading traffic on busier roads.

  3. 03

    Stage 3 · Manoeuvres

    Parking, turning in the road, and independent driving to a sat-nav.

  4. 04

    Stage 4 · Test Prep

    Mock tests on real routes until you’re calm and consistent.

Learning outcomes

  • Control the car confidently and safely
  • Handle junctions, roundabouts, and traffic
  • Perform all test manoeuvres
  • Drive independently following signs and sat-nav
  • Stay calm under exam conditions
  • Be genuinely ready to pass first time

Intensive Course

Fast

Pass in one to two weeks with a concentrated block of lessons.

Program overview

The intensive course compresses your learning into one or two focused weeks, ideal if you need to pass quickly for work or study. It includes a fast-tracked practical test, theory prep, and a mock test, all in your training car.

Course structure

  1. 01

    Days 1–2 · Foundations

    Rapid mastery of controls and core road skills.

  2. 02

    Days 3–5 · Building Up

    Town driving, manoeuvres, and independent driving daily.

  3. 03

    Days 6–8 · Polishing

    Mock tests, weak-point drilling, and exam-route practice.

  4. 04

    Test Day

    Fast-tracked practical test in your familiar training car.

Learning outcomes

  • Reach test standard in 1–2 weeks
  • Master all skills under time pressure
  • Complete every test manoeuvre
  • Drive confidently and independently
  • Pass theory before the practical
  • Sit a fast-tracked practical test

Theory Test Prep

Online

Hazard-perception and mock tests until you’re passing every time.

Program overview

Theory Test Prep gives you everything you need to pass the theory and hazard-perception test the first time. Self-paced and online, with thousands of practice questions and realistic hazard clips, plus optional in-person coaching sessions.

What’s included

  1. 01

    Question Bank

    Thousands of official-style multiple-choice practice questions.

  2. 02

    Hazard Perception

    Realistic video clips to train your hazard-spotting reflexes.

  3. 03

    Mock Tests

    Full timed mock exams that mirror the real test exactly.

  4. 04

    Progress Tracking

    See your weak topics and watch your scores climb.

Learning outcomes

  • Know the rules of the road thoroughly
  • Spot developing hazards quickly
  • Pass timed mock tests consistently
  • Understand road signs and markings
  • Approach the real test calmly
  • Pass theory before your practical
