Our story

How GreenLight Driving School began

Every part of GreenLight Driving School grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

GreenLight Driving School was founded in 2008 by an instructor who believed learning to drive should feel calm, not terrifying. What began as one dual-control car on the roads around Easton has grown into a trusted local school known for patient teaching and a genuinely high pass rate.

We teach driving the safe way, step by step. Every lesson is structured around real skills — moving off, junctions, roundabouts, independent driving — and our instructors adapt the pace to each learner, whether they are seventeen and anxious or an adult finally getting their licence.

Our cars are modern, dual-control, and fully insured, and our instructors are fully qualified, DBS-checked, and calm under pressure. We cover the theory test as carefully as the practical, because a confident driver understands the rules of the road, not just the controls.

Since 2008 thousands of GreenLight learners have passed their test, many on the first attempt, and driven away as careful, capable drivers. We measure success not just by the pass, but by the confidence our learners carry for the rest of their driving lives.
