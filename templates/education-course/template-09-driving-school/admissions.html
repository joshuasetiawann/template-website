Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • A valid provisional licence
  • To be at least 17 years old
  • Glasses or lenses if you need them to read a plate
  • Comfortable shoes for driving
  • A theory-test pass before the practical
  • Regular lessons to keep progressing
