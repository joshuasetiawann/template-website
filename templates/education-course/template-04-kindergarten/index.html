Our little nest

Where childhood is cherished

Little Sprouts was founded by early-childhood teachers who believe the first years are precious — and best spent playing, wondering, and feeling deeply loved.

Our rooms are gentle and inviting, filled with natural light, soft corners, and open-ended toys that spark imagination. Children choose, create, and discover at their own pace.

With one teacher for every five children, every sprout is truly known. We notice the small wins — a first written name, a brave climb, a shared toy — and we celebrate them all.

Outdoor wonder is part of every day. Our garden, mud kitchen, and sandpit turn play into hands-on lessons about nature, weather, and growing things.

We work hand-in-hand with families, sharing daily photos and stories so you never miss a milestone in your child’s big little world.

“Little Sprouts feels like a second home. My son is so happy, so loved, so himself.”

— Amara Osei, Little Sprouts parent

  • A gentle 1:5 teacher-to-child ratio
  • Play-based, child-led learning
  • Daily outdoor and garden time
  • Warm, qualified educators
