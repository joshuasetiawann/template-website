Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Your child's name and date of birth
  • Proof of age
  • Immunisation records
  • Any allergies or needs to share
  • Comfort items your child loves (a toy or blanket)
  • Emergency contacts
