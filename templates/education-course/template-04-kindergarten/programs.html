Programs & Curriculum

Our rooms & learning

From wobbly first steps into our nest to confident pre-schoolers ready for big school, Little Sprouts grows gently with your child. Here is what each room explores, what your little one will gain, and how to enrol.

Toddler Room · Age 3

Youngest

Soft beginnings of play, words, and feeling safe.

Program overview

Our Toddler Room is a calm, cuddly space for our youngest sprouts. Through sensory play, songs, and lots of warm care, little ones grow language, independence, and the confidence that comes from feeling truly safe.

What we explore

  1. 01

    Sensory Play

    Water, sand, and dough that delight the senses and build little hands.

  2. 02

    First Words

    Songs, rhymes, and naming games that grow early language.

  3. 03

    Self-Help Steps

    Hand-washing, snack time, and gentle toilet learning at each child’s pace.

  4. 04

    Cosy Connection

    One-to-one cuddles and key-carer bonds that build deep security.

Learning outcomes

  • Settle happily away from home
  • Use more words and gestures
  • Take part in songs and rhymes
  • Begin self-help routines
  • Play alongside other children
  • Feel safe with a key carer

Discovery Room · Age 4

Explorers

Curious minds at full, joyful play.

Program overview

Four-year-olds are bursting with questions, and our Discovery Room gives them room to explore. Through projects, garden adventures, and imaginative play, sprouts grow problem-solving, friendship, and a true love of finding out.

What we explore

  1. 01

    Project Play

    Following children’s questions into mini projects and big discoveries.

  2. 02

    Garden Explorers

    Planting, digging, and watching nature change with the seasons.

  3. 03

    Story & Mark-Making

    Loving books and making the first marks that lead to writing.

  4. 04

    Friendship Skills

    Sharing, turn-taking, and naming feelings with kindness.

Learning outcomes

  • Ask questions and investigate
  • Care for plants and notice nature
  • Enjoy stories and retell them
  • Make marks and ‘write’ their name
  • Play cooperatively with friends
  • Name and manage big feelings

Pre-K Room · Ages 5–6

School-ready

Confident sprouts ready for big school.

Program overview

Our Pre-K Room gently bridges kindergarten and ‘big school’. Sprouts build early literacy and number skills, grow independence, and practise the routines and confidence that make starting school a joyful, easy step.

What we explore

  1. 01

    Early Literacy

    Letter sounds, name-writing, and a deep love of stories.

  2. 02

    Early Numbers

    Counting, patterns, and shapes through hands-on games.

  3. 03

    Independence

    Managing belongings, listening in a group, and trying bravely.

  4. 04

    School Readiness

    Practising the routines and confidence for a smooth start to school.

Learning outcomes

  • Know letter sounds and write their name
  • Count and recognise numbers and patterns
  • Listen and focus in a group
  • Manage their own belongings
  • Express ideas with confidence
  • Feel excited and ready for school

Enrollment steps

How to join our nest

Joining Little Sprouts is gentle and simple. Here is what to expect.

  1. Step 1

    Wave hello

    Send a note or call, and we’ll arrange a cosy tour of our nest.

  2. Step 2

    Visit & play

    Bring your child to explore a room and meet their future teacher.

  3. Step 3

    Complete enrollment

    Fill in a short form and share your child’s records.

  4. Step 4

    Settling-in week

    A gentle, gradual start so your sprout feels safe and at home.
