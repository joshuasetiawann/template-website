Our story

How Pigment & Co. Art School began

Every part of Pigment & Co. Art School grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Pigment & Co. was founded in 2011 on a simple, generous idea: that making art should be open to everyone, not just the formally trained. We opened our studio doors on Studio Lane with a few easels and a kiln, and grew into a busy community art school where the paint never quite dries.

We teach by making. Classes are hands-on from the first session, taught by practising artists who share not just technique but the habits of a creative life. Whether you have never held a brush or you sell your work, you will find a bench, a tutor, and a community here.

Our studios run day and night across painting, ceramics, drawing, printmaking, and digital art, alongside a beloved kids' art club. Members exhibit in our gallery, sell at seasonal markets, and form the friendships that keep people making for years.

More than a decade on, Pigment & Co. is a true community: thousands of makers have found their medium here, and many who arrived as nervous beginners now teach the classes they once took. We exist so that everyone who wants to make, can.
