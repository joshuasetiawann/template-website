Our studio

An art school that feels like home

Pigment & Co. grew from a single shared studio into a thriving school — but it has never stopped feeling like a place where everyone’s welcome and every mark matters.

We believe everyone is creative, full stop. Our classes are small, unhurried, and judgement-free, led by working artists who remember exactly what it felt like to be a beginner.

Painting, ceramics, drawing, printmaking, digital art — whatever pulls you, you’ll find materials, mentorship, and a bench with your name on it. Come once a week or live here; both are welcome.

Twice a year the whole school turns into a gallery. Every student — from first-timers to long-time makers — shows a piece, and the whole neighbourhood comes to celebrate.

Our community is the real magic: friendships forged over shared sinks and drying racks, critiques that build people up, and a standing invitation to keep making, always.

“I came to ‘try a class’ five years ago. Pigment & Co. is my favourite place on earth now.”

— Dana Whitfield, Pigment & Co. member

Your creative path

From first mark to first show

However you start, here’s the journey most makers travel at Pigment & Co.

  1. 1
    01

    Find Your Medium

    Try a short taster or jump into a beginners’ term in painting, clay, drawing, or digital — no experience needed.

  2. 2
    02

    Learn the Fundamentals

    Build the core skills of your medium: colour, form, composition, and the confidence to make a mess.

  3. 3
    03

    Develop Technique

    Deepen your craft with intermediate classes, new materials, and longer projects.

  4. 4
    04

    Find Your Voice

    Move beyond exercises into work that’s truly yours, guided by mentors who push you gently.

  5. 5
    05

    Build a Body of Work

    Create a small series around a theme — the beginning of a real portfolio.

  6. 6
    06

    Show Your Work

    Exhibit in one of our twice-yearly gallery shows, with friends, family, and the neighbourhood.

  7. 7
    07

    Join the Community

    Become a member with open-studio access, socials, and a standing invitation to keep making.
