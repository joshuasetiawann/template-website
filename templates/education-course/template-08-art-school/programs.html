Programs & Curriculum

Art courses in detail

Every Pigment & Co. course is small, hands-on, and judgement-free, led by a working artist. Here is what each course explores, what you’ll create, fees with payment options, and how to enrol.

Painting Studio

Loved

Acrylics, oils, and watercolour from first colour to personal style.

Program overview

The Painting Studio takes you from mixing your very first colours to developing a personal voice on canvas. Across eight-week terms you’ll explore acrylics, oils, and watercolour through guided projects, building both technique and confidence in a relaxed, social studio.

Course content

  1. 01

    Colour & Materials

    Mixing colour, handling paint, and getting to know your tools.

  2. 02

    Light & Form

    Rendering light, shadow, and three-dimensional form convincingly.

  3. 03

    Composition

    Arranging a picture that holds the eye and tells a story.

  4. 04

    Your Own Work

    A guided personal project that becomes a show-ready piece.

Learning outcomes

  • Mix and control colour confidently
  • Render light, shadow, and form
  • Compose a balanced painting
  • Work in acrylic, oil, and watercolour
  • Develop a personal painting style
  • Finish a gallery-ready piece

Ceramics & Pottery

Popular

Hand-building and the wheel, with glazing and firing included.

Program overview

Our ceramics studio is many members’ happy place. Across eight-week terms you’ll learn hand-building and throwing on the wheel, then glaze and fire your pieces to keep. It’s tactile, meditative, and deeply satisfying — and fully equipped with everything you need.

Course content

  1. 01

    Hand-Building

    Pinch, coil, and slab techniques to build forms by hand.

  2. 02

    The Wheel

    Centring, pulling, and throwing your first bowls and cups.

  3. 03

    Surface & Glaze

    Decorating and glazing for colour, texture, and finish.

  4. 04

    Firing & Finishing

    Two firings turn your work into pieces you’ll keep forever.

Learning outcomes

  • Build forms by hand with confidence
  • Throw basic shapes on the wheel
  • Decorate and glaze your work
  • Understand the firing process
  • Produce finished, functional ceramics
  • Develop your own style in clay

Digital Art & Design

Modern

Illustration and design on tablet, from sketch to print.

Program overview

The Digital Art & Design course bridges traditional craft and the tablet. Across eight-week terms you’ll learn digital drawing, colour, and layout, producing illustrations and designs that are print- and screen-ready — no prior tech skills required.

Course content

  1. 01

    Digital Drawing

    Brushes, layers, and confident mark-making on a tablet.

  2. 02

    Colour & Light

    Digital colour, lighting, and atmosphere in your work.

  3. 03

    Composition & Layout

    Designing pages, posters, and illustrations that work.

  4. 04

    Export & Print

    Preparing artwork for high-quality print and screen.

Learning outcomes

  • Draw and paint digitally with layers
  • Use colour and light on screen
  • Design clear, attractive layouts
  • Create finished illustrations
  • Prepare artwork for print and web
  • Build a small digital portfolio

Open Door Bursaries for our neighbours

Pigment & Co. holds Open Door Bursaries each term — free and reduced places for local makers who could not otherwise join. With concession rates, family discounts, and payment plans too, our door really is open to all.
