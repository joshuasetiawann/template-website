Programs & Curriculum

Music programmes in detail

Every Crescendo programme blends rigorous technique with the joy of performance, following an internationally graded curriculum. Here is what each programme covers, what you’ll achieve, tuition with payment options, and how to audition.

PianoVoiceComposition

Piano & Keyboard

Popular

From first scales to concerto repertoire, classical and contemporary.

Program overview

The piano programme is the heart of Crescendo. Through weekly private lessons you progress along the international graded path, building technique, musicianship, and a repertoire you love, with regular recital opportunities on our Steinway Recital Hall stage.

Progression

  1. 01

    Foundations · Grade 1–2

    Posture, hand position, reading notation, and first pieces.

  2. 02

    Technique · Grade 3–5

    Scales, arpeggios, and expressive playing across keys and styles.

  3. 03

    Repertoire · Grade 6–7

    Longer works across periods, accompaniment, and interpretation.

  4. 04

    Mastery · Grade 8 & Diploma

    Concerto movements, advanced technique, and recital programmes.

Learning outcomes

  • Read and perform graded piano repertoire
  • Master scales, arpeggios, and technique
  • Play expressively across musical styles
  • Sit international graded exams
  • Perform confidently on stage
  • Prepare for diploma or conservatoire study

At a glance

ScheduleWeekly 45-minute lessons
FormatPrivate, in person
ExamsInternational graded boards
OutcomeGrade 1–8 & diploma

Per lesson

$58 / lesson

Monthly membership and sibling discounts available; instrument hire keeps starting affordable.

Entry requirements

  • Open to all ages and levels
  • A relaxed playing assessment
  • Access to a piano or keyboard to practise
  • Commitment to weekly practice

Scholarship: Talent Scholarships support gifted young pianists with reduced tuition.

Voice & Singing

All levels

Classical, musical-theatre, and contemporary vocal training.

Program overview

The voice programme develops healthy technique, range, and stagecraft across classical, musical-theatre, and contemporary styles. Whether you’re preparing for auditions or simply love to sing, you’ll build breath, tone, and the confidence to perform.

Progression

  1. 01

    Breath & Tone

    Healthy breathing, support, and a free, resonant sound.

  2. 02

    Range & Agility

    Extending range, blending registers, and vocal flexibility.

  3. 03

    Repertoire & Style

    Songs across genres, language, and interpretation.

  4. 04

    Stagecraft

    Microphone technique, presence, and performing under pressure.

Learning outcomes

  • Sing with healthy, supported technique
  • Extend and control your vocal range
  • Perform repertoire across styles
  • Interpret a song expressively
  • Command the stage with confidence
  • Prepare audition and exam pieces

Composition & Theory

New

Write your own music and master the craft behind it.

Program overview

The composition and theory programme teaches you to create original music while mastering harmony, ear training, and orchestration. Across sixteen-week terms you’ll write, notate, and hear your own pieces performed by fellow students.

Modules

  1. 01

    Harmony & Theory

    Chords, progressions, and the grammar of music.

  2. 02

    Ear Training

    Hearing intervals, chords, and rhythm with accuracy.

  3. 03

    Composition

    Crafting melody, structure, and your own musical voice.

  4. 04

    Orchestration

    Writing idiomatically for instruments and ensembles.

Learning outcomes

  • Read and write standard notation fluently
  • Harmonise and develop melodies
  • Identify chords and intervals by ear
  • Compose original short works
  • Orchestrate for small ensembles
  • Hear your music performed live
