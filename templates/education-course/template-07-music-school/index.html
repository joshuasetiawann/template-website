Our story

A home built for music

Crescendo Conservatory was founded by four performing musicians who wanted a school where serious training and pure joy were never in conflict.

From a single piano studio in 1998, Crescendo has grown into a full conservatory with eight teaching disciplines, a youth orchestra, and our own Steinway Recital Hall, where students perform live every Friday.

We believe music is a craft and a joy in equal measure. Our students learn proper technique and theory, yes — but always in service of playing music they love, on a stage, for real audiences.

Performance is woven through everything we do. Every student, from beginner to diploma candidate, performs regularly, because the stage is the best teacher of all.

Our alumni play in orchestras, write for film, teach, and fill amateur ensembles with lifelong joy — proof that music, once truly learned, never leaves you.

“At Crescendo I learned that discipline and delight are the same thing in music.”

— Priya Raman, Crescendo alumna

The journey

From first note to first concerto

Our graded curriculum aligns with international examination boards. Open any stage to see the milestones.

Posture, the basics of your instrument, simple pieces, and the joy of making your first real music. Foundations of reading notation.

Steady pulse, simple rhythms, and the beginnings of playing by ear and singing in tune.

Scales, arpeggios, and pieces in several keys, with your first graded examination.

Sight-reading, dynamics, and expressive phrasing that bring a piece to life.

The music-theory grade that unlocks the higher practical grades, plus your first solo recital.

Longer works across periods and styles, and ensemble or accompaniment skills.

Demanding repertoire, interpretation, and the stamina for full recital programmes.

The highest school grade — concerto movements, advanced technique, and a public performance.

Performance diplomas, conservatoire-audition preparation, and a path to professional study.

Regular recitals, ensembles, competitions, and our annual gala on the Steinway Recital Hall stage.
