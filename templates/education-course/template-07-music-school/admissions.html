Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Choose an instrument or voice
  • A friendly audition or trial lesson
  • Your own instrument (or rent through us)
  • A regular weekly practice commitment
  • A weekly lesson time
  • Enthusiasm — all levels welcome
