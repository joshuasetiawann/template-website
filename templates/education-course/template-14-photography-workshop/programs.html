Programs & Curriculum

Photography workshops in detail

Every Aperture workshop is shoot-based, mentor-led, and capped at eight photographers, on real locations or in our daylight studio. Here is what each covers, what you’ll be able to shoot, pricing with options, and how to book.

Photography Foundations

Start here

Four hands-on sessions from auto mode to confident manual.

Program overview

Foundations is where most photographers begin. Across four hands-on sessions you’ll leave auto mode behind for good, mastering exposure, composition, and the art of seeing light — practising on real subjects every week so the skills truly stick.

The four sessions

  1. 01

    Light & Seeing

    Reading the direction, quality, and colour of light.

  2. 02

    The Exposure Triangle

    Aperture, shutter, and ISO mastered in manual mode.

  3. 03

    Composition

    Framing, balance, and leading the viewer’s eye.

  4. 04

    Putting It Together

    A guided shoot applying everything, with critique.

Learning outcomes

  • Shoot confidently in full manual mode
  • Read and use available light
  • Control depth of field and motion
  • Compose with intent
  • Avoid common beginner mistakes
  • Make photographs, not snapshots

Portrait Workshop

Popular

Two days of lighting, posing, and connection with real models.

Program overview

The Portrait Workshop is two intensive days shooting real models in studio and natural light. You’ll learn lighting setups, posing and direction, and the human skill of connection — finishing with a set of portraits good enough to start a portfolio.

Workshop content

  1. 01

    Natural-Light Portraits

    Finding and shaping window and outdoor light.

  2. 02

    Posing & Direction

    Guiding subjects and capturing genuine expression.

  3. 03

    Studio Lighting

    One-light setups and modifiers for flattering portraits.

  4. 04

    The Portrait Shoot

    A full directed shoot with a model, start to finish.

Learning outcomes

  • Light portraits in natural and studio light
  • Pose and direct subjects naturally
  • Build rapport and capture expression
  • Use modifiers to shape light
  • Choose lenses and settings for portraits
  • Produce a portfolio-ready portrait set

Landscape & Nature

On-location

A weekend of golden-hour fieldcraft on real locations.

Program overview

The Landscape & Nature workshop is a weekend in the field, chasing the best light at dawn and dusk. You’ll learn composition, filters, and fieldcraft on real locations, returning with images that capture not just a place but a feeling — and the skills to do it again.

Weekend content

  1. 01

    Planning & Light

    Reading weather, planning golden hour, and scouting.

  2. 02

    Composition in the Field

    Foreground, depth, and leading lines in landscapes.

  3. 03

    Filters & Long Exposure

    Using filters for skies, water, and motion.

  4. 04

    Dawn & Dusk Shoots

    Guided shoots at the best light of the day.

Learning outcomes

  • Plan shoots around light and weather
  • Compose strong landscape images
  • Use filters and long exposures
  • Capture dawn and dusk effectively
  • Work efficiently on location
  • Build a cohesive landscape series
