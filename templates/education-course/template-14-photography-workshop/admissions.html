Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Any camera (DSLR, mirrorless, or phone)
  • A charged battery and memory card
  • Comfortable shoes for location shoots
  • A laptop with Lightroom for editing classes
  • An eye for the world
  • A willingness to shoot a lot
