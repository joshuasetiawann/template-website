STEM Honors Track

Popular

Accelerated mathematics and science for future engineers and scientists.

Program overview

The STEM Honors Track accelerates students through advanced mathematics and laboratory science, culminating in AP Physics, Calculus BC, and an independent research seminar judged at a regional science fair. Graduates routinely enter competitive engineering and pre-med programs.

Course sequence

  1. 01

    Grade 10 · Accelerated Core

    Honors Algebra II/Trigonometry and Honors Chemistry build the foundation.

  2. 02

    Grade 11 · Advanced Placement

    AP Calculus AB, AP Biology, and an introduction to research design.

  3. 03

    Grade 12 · Capstone Science

    AP Physics C, AP Calculus BC, and a year-long independent research project.

  4. 04

    Co-curricular · Robotics

    Optional participation in the nationally ranked robotics and math teams.

Learning outcomes

  • Master calculus and AP-level physics
  • Design and run an independent experiment
  • Present research at a science fair
  • Use lab and data-analysis tools
  • Build a competitive STEM college profile
  • Collaborate on engineering challenges

At a glance

DurationGrades 10–12
FormatOn-campus, full school year
FacilitiesTwo science labs & maker space
OutcomeUp to 5 AP credits

Included in tuition

No additional fee

Covered by standard tuition; need-based assistance and payment plans apply.

Entry requirements

  • Enrollment at Riverside Prep
  • Strong grade in prior math & science
  • Placement assessment in mathematics
  • Teacher recommendation for honors

Scholarship: STEM Merit Awards recognise top entering students with renewable tuition grants.

Humanities & Writing

Honors

Close reading, persuasive writing, and a published senior thesis.

Program overview

The Humanities & Writing concentration develops powerful readers and writers through AP English Literature and Language, AP history electives, and a senior thesis published in the school journal. It is ideal for students aiming at the liberal arts, law, journalism, or public service.

Course sequence

  1. 01

    Grade 10 · Foundations

    Honors English and World History sharpen analysis and the essay.

  2. 02

    Grade 11 · AP Language

    AP English Language and AP U.S. History with a research paper.

  3. 03

    Grade 12 · AP Literature & Thesis

    AP English Literature alongside a year-long, published senior thesis.

  4. 04

    Co-curricular · Journal & Debate

    Editing the literary journal and competing on the debate team.

Learning outcomes

  • Analyse complex literary and historical texts
  • Write and revise a long-form thesis
  • Construct evidence-based arguments
  • Edit and publish peer work
  • Speak persuasively in debate
  • Build a humanities college portfolio

At a glance

Scholarship: The Writers’ Fellowship offers tuition support to exceptional young writers.

Computer Science

AP

From first code to AP Computer Science A and competitive robotics.

Program overview

The Computer Science concentration takes students from foundational programming through AP Computer Science Principles and AP Computer Science A, supported by a robotics team that competes nationally. Students build real applications and a portfolio for college and beyond.

Course sequence

  1. 01

    Grade 10 · Foundations

    Introduction to programming in Python and computational thinking.

  2. 02

    Grade 11 · AP CS Principles

    Algorithms, data, and the internet with a create performance task.

  3. 03

    Grade 12 · AP CS A

    Object-oriented programming in Java and a capstone application.

  4. 04

    Co-curricular · Robotics

    Designing, building, and coding robots for regional competition.

Learning outcomes

  • Write programs in Python and Java
  • Apply object-oriented design
  • Build and ship a working application
  • Reason about algorithms and data
  • Code and operate a competition robot
  • Assemble a coding portfolio

Tuition assistance for one in three families

Riverside Prep awards over $2 million in need-based tuition assistance each year, covering up to 70% of tuition. The confidential application is reviewed separately from admission, so applying for aid never affects your child’s acceptance.
