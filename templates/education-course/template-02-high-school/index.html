About Riverside

A close community with high expectations

Since 1994 Riverside Prep has believed that teenagers rise to the standard you set — academically, athletically, and as people.

Riverside Prep was founded by a group of educators who wanted a high school small enough to know every student by name yet ambitious enough to send them to the country’s best universities. Three decades on, both promises hold.

Our advisory system pairs every student with a faculty mentor who follows them from ninth grade through graduation, coordinating academics, college planning, and personal growth.

Beyond the classroom, more than 90% of students play a sport or join a performing arts ensemble, and our robotics and debate teams compete nationally.

A dedicated college-counseling office begins working with families in tenth grade, resulting in over $8 million in merit scholarships for the class of 2025 alone.

The four-year plan

Each grade, a clear next step

Riverside’s curriculum builds deliberately from foundations to advanced, college-level work. Open any grade to see the focus.

Core English, integrated science, algebra/geometry, world history, a language, and a wellness seminar establish strong study habits.

Every freshman joins a 12-student advisory and a club or team to build community from day one.

Students enter honors sections in their strengths, take their first AP-prep courses, and select a concentration.

The college office introduces planning, testing strategy, and summer-opportunity advising.

The heaviest AP year, with most students taking three to five college-level courses and a research project.

Campus visits, the testing window, and a personalized college list developed with a counselor.

A senior capstone in the chosen concentration alongside guided college applications and essays.

Seniors lead clubs, mentor freshmen, and complete a community-service requirement.

A spring internship or independent project bridges high school and university life.

Graduates leave with an average of four AP credits and a place at a four-year university.
