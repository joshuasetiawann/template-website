The process

From first hello to first day

A clear, friendly path — here is exactly what to expect, step by step.

  1. Step 1

    Inquire & tour

    Request information and book a campus tour with the admissions team.

  2. Step 2

    Shadow day

    Your student spends a day in classes to experience Riverside firsthand.

  3. Step 3

    Apply by Jan 15

    Submit the application, transcript, and two teacher recommendations.

  4. Step 4

    Interview

    A friendly family interview with an admissions counselor.

  5. Step 5

    Decision by Mar 10

    Receive your decision and any tuition-assistance offer.

Requirements

What you’ll need

Have these ready to make your application smooth and quick.

  • Completed application form
  • Current transcript and recent report card
  • Two teacher recommendations (math and English)
  • Admissions assessment or recent standardized scores
  • A family and student interview
  • Optional portfolio for arts applicants

Tuition & fees

  • Day Tuition$28,500 / yrGrades 9–12 · books and most activities included
  • Sibling$26,000 / yrReduced rate for a second enrolled child
  • With AssistanceFrom $9,000 / yrNeed-based grants for qualifying families

Riverside Prep awards more than $2.4 million in need-based tuition assistance each year. Roughly one in three families receives a grant, and applying for assistance never affects an admission decision.
