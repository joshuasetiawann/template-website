Our story

How Riverside Prep began

Every part of Riverside Prep grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Riverside Prep opened in 1998 with a single graduating class and a stubborn conviction: that a college-preparatory education should develop the whole student, not just the transcript. We began in a renovated mill on the Greenbrook River and have grown into a full grades 9–12 day school without losing the closeness of those first years.

Our academic program is built around Advanced Placement depth and the Harkness-style seminar, where students sit around the table and do the thinking aloud. Class sizes stay small so every teacher knows every student, and our college counselors begin working with families in the ninth grade.

Beyond the classroom, every Riverside student competes, performs, or creates. More than thirty athletic teams, a full arts program, and dozens of student-led clubs mean the campus is busy from the first bell to the last bus, and students learn the discipline that comes from showing up for a team.

Twenty-five years on, Riverside Prep sends its graduates to selective colleges across the country with a 100% acceptance record — but we measure ourselves by the confidence, character, and curiosity students carry with them when they go.

Mission & values

What we stand for

To prepare confident, principled graduates for college and life through rigorous academics, competitive athletics, and a vibrant arts program in a close community.

Scholarship

AP-level rigor and Harkness seminars that teach students to think, not memorize.

Character

Honor code, advisory groups, and service expectations that shape principled people.

Team

Thirty-plus teams and full arts seasons that teach commitment and resilience.

Belonging

Small classes and a faculty advisor for every student from day one.

Campus & facilities

Where learning happens

Spaces designed around how our students actually learn, make, and connect.

Riverside Athletic Complex

Turf fields, a field house, and an eight-lane track for our thirty-plus teams.

Science & Innovation Wing

Modern labs and a maker space supporting AP sciences and robotics.

Performing Arts Theater

A 400-seat theater for drama, music, and the spring musical.

A light-filled library and the home of our college-counseling office.
