B.A. Liberal Arts

Flagship

A four-year interdisciplinary degree in the humanities and arts.

Program overview

The B.A. in Liberal Arts is Whitford’s flagship undergraduate degree. Students read across literature, history, and philosophy, develop their writing in small seminars, and culminate in a year-long senior thesis defended before faculty. Graduates leave able to think across disciplines and argue with precision.

Course sequence by year

  1. 01

    Year 1 · Foundations

    Two writing seminars, the Examined Life great-books sequence, and a quantitative-reasoning course.

  2. 02

    Year 2 · Breadth

    Distribution across the natural sciences, social sciences, and the arts; declare a concentration.

  3. 03

    Year 3 · Methods

    Discipline-specific research methods plus an optional study-abroad term at a partner university.

  4. 04

    Year 4 · Thesis

    A two-semester independent thesis, advised by a faculty mentor and presented at the Senior Symposium.

Learning outcomes

  • Write and defend an original 60-page thesis
  • Read primary sources critically across eras
  • Construct and rebut rigorous arguments
  • Conduct archival and library research
  • Present scholarship to a public audience
  • Synthesize across three disciplines

At a glance

Duration4 years, full-time
FormatOn-campus, fall & spring terms
CohortSeminar capped at 14 students
CredentialBachelor of Arts

Annual tuition

$41,200 / year

Monthly payment plans and need-based grants available; most families pay far less after aid.

Entry requirements

  • High-school diploma or equivalent
  • Common Application with two essays
  • One counselor recommendation
  • Test scores optional

Scholarship: Hartwell Humanities Scholarships cover up to full tuition for outstanding writers.

B.S. Computer Science

Co-op

Theory, systems, and machine learning with a paid industry co-op.

Program overview

The B.S. in Computer Science blends mathematical foundations with hands-on systems and machine-learning work. A required junior-year co-op places every student in a paid six-month industry role, and the senior capstone ships a real software product for an external client.

Curriculum modules

  1. 01

    Foundations

    Programming, discrete mathematics, and data structures across the first three semesters.

  2. 02

    Systems

    Computer architecture, operating systems, networks, and databases.

  3. 03

    Intelligence

    Algorithms, machine learning, and the AI & Society seminar on ethics.

  4. 04

    Co-op & Capstone

    A paid six-month co-op followed by a client-sponsored senior software project.

Learning outcomes

  • Design and analyze efficient algorithms
  • Build full-stack production software
  • Train and evaluate machine-learning models
  • Reason about systems and concurrency
  • Ship a product for a real client
  • Work effectively on an engineering team

At a glance

Duration4 years incl. co-op
FormatOn-campus + 6-month co-op
FacilitiesGPU lab & maker space
CredentialBachelor of Science

Annual tuition

$43,800 / year

Co-op earnings (avg. $38,000) offset roughly a year of tuition; payment plans available.

Entry requirements

  • High-school diploma or equivalent
  • Precalculus or higher recommended
  • Common Application with two essays
  • Portfolio optional but encouraged

Scholarship: Suresh Computing Fellowships award $15,000/yr to students from underrepresented backgrounds.

M.B.A. Management

Graduate

A 21-month graduate degree for the next generation of leaders.

Program overview

The Whitford M.B.A. develops analytical and ethical leaders through a case-driven core, a live consulting practicum, and concentrations in finance, strategy, or analytics. Cohorts are intentionally small so every student leads at least one team project.

Term structure

  1. 01

    Core · Term 1–2

    Accounting, finance, marketing, operations, and managerial economics by the case method.

  2. 02

    Practicum · Term 3

    A live consulting engagement with a regional company, advised by faculty.

  3. 03

    Concentration · Term 4

    Deep electives in finance, strategy, or business analytics.

  4. 04

    Capstone · Term 5

    A board-level strategy project presented to executives and faculty.

Learning outcomes

  • Lead cross-functional teams
  • Build and defend financial models
  • Diagnose a company’s strategy
  • Communicate to executive audiences
  • Apply data analytics to decisions
  • Navigate ethical trade-offs in business

At a glance

Duration21 months, full-time
FormatOn-campus, weekday
CohortCapped at 60 students
CredentialMaster of Business Administration

Total tuition

$58,500 total

Two-year payment plan and graduate assistantships available; employer sponsorship welcomed.

Entry requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree (any field)
  • Two years of work experience preferred
  • GMAT/GRE optional
  • Two professional references

Scholarship: Dean’s Leadership Scholarships cover 25–50% of tuition for proven leaders.

Aid for every admitted student

Whitford meets 97% of demonstrated financial need and is need-blind for domestic applicants. Grants are awarded before loans, and merit scholarships are considered automatically — no separate application required.
