Our story

Three decades of shaping leaders

Kingsbridge was founded in 1989 on a simple conviction: that great management can be taught, but only through real decisions, real cases, and real accountability.

Our teaching is built on the case method. Students don’t memorize theory; they step into the shoes of executives facing hard, ambiguous choices, defend their thinking before peers, and learn judgment the only way it’s truly learned — by exercising it.

A Kingsbridge education is as much about character as competence. Our integrity-first culture and emphasis on responsible leadership produce executives the world can trust with consequential decisions.

Beyond the classroom, our students lead live consulting projects, compete in global case competitions, and learn from a faculty that splits its time between research and the boardroom.

The Kingsbridge network is its quiet superpower: 38,000 alumni across 90 countries who hire, mentor, invest in, and open doors for one another, for life.

“Kingsbridge didn’t just teach me to analyze a business. It taught me to lead one.”

— Olivia Chen, MBA ’11, CEO
