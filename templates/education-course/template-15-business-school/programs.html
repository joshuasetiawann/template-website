Full-Time MBA

Flagship

A transformative two-year program for ambitious professionals.

Program overview

The flagship full-time MBA is two years that change a career. A rigorous case-method core gives way to electives, a global immersion, a paid summer internship, and a live consulting capstone — all wrapped in two years of leadership coaching and access to our 38,000-strong network.

Program structure

  1. 01

    Year 1 · Core

    Accounting, economics, finance, marketing, operations, and strategy by case.

  2. 02

    Summer · Internship

    A paid internship that applies year one and often yields an offer.

  3. 03

    Year 2 · Concentration

    Deep electives plus a two-week international global immersion.

  4. 04

    Capstone · Consulting

    A semester solving a real strategic problem for a partner company.

Learning outcomes

  • Lead through ambiguity and complexity
  • Build and defend financial and strategic analysis
  • Drive cross-functional decisions
  • Communicate to boards and executives
  • Solve a real client’s strategy problem
  • Access a global alumni network

Executive MBA

Executive

A weekend MBA for senior leaders who keep working.

Program overview

The Executive MBA delivers the full Kingsbridge MBA on alternating weekends, designed for senior managers who continue to lead while they learn. Cohorts are seasoned and tight-knit, and the curriculum applies directly to the challenges you’re facing at work right now.

Program structure

  1. 01

    Term 1 · Foundations

    Accelerated core in finance, economics, and analytics for executives.

  2. 02

    Term 2 · Functions

    Strategy, marketing, and operations applied to your own organisation.

  3. 03

    Term 3 · Leadership

    Advanced leadership, governance, and an international residency.

  4. 04

    Capstone · Strategy Project

    A board-level project addressing a real challenge in your firm.

Learning outcomes

  • Lead at the enterprise and board level
  • Apply learning to live work challenges
  • Master strategy, finance, and governance
  • Build a senior peer network
  • Drive a strategic initiative at work
  • Step into C-suite responsibility

At a glance

  • A senior or management role
  • Employer support recommended

Scholarship: Leadership Fellowships support women and underrepresented senior leaders.

M.S. Finance

Specialized

A focused master's in corporate finance and markets.

Program overview

The M.S. in Finance is a focused, twelve-month specialized master's for those targeting careers in corporate finance, investment, and markets. It pairs rigorous quantitative training with applied projects and the same career coaching and network as our MBA.

Program structure

  1. 01

    Foundations

    Corporate finance, financial accounting, and econometrics.

  2. 02

    Markets & Investments

    Valuation, portfolio theory, and capital markets.

  3. 03

    Advanced Electives

    Derivatives, M&A, and financial modeling.

  4. 04

    Applied Project

    A real-world finance project with a partner organisation.

Learning outcomes

  • Value companies and assets rigorously
  • Build advanced financial models
  • Analyse markets and portfolios
  • Structure deals and investments
  • Apply finance to a real project
  • Launch a competitive finance career
