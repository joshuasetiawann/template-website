Our story

How Saffron & Salt Culinary School began

Every part of Saffron & Salt Culinary School grew from a single conviction about how people learn best.

Saffron & Salt was founded in 2013 by chefs who believed cooking is learned with your hands, not from a screen. We opened our teaching kitchen in the old Market Hall with a row of stoves and a long prep table, and the smell of something good has drawn people in ever since.

We teach by cooking. From your first class you are at a station with a knife and a pan, learning the fundamentals — heat, seasoning, technique — that turn recipes into instinct. Our chef-instructors share the real methods of professional kitchens, scaled for whoever is at the bench.

Our programs span the full diploma for aspiring professionals, patisserie and baking, plant-based cooking, and relaxed home-cook series for weekends and evenings. Every class ends the way cooking should — sitting down together to eat what you made.

More than a decade on, Saffron & Salt has trained line cooks and pastry chefs as well as thousands of confident home cooks. Whether you want a career in food or simply to cook a feast for the people you love, you will learn it here, at the stove.
