Our kitchen

Where real cooking is taught

Saffron & Salt was founded by chefs who learned the hard way that cooking is taught by doing, not watching — so here, you cook every single minute.

Our teaching kitchen is a real working kitchen: gas ranges, a wood oven, proper steel, and a full pastry bench. Classes are capped at ten so every cook gets a station, a chef’s eye, and constant feedback.

We teach technique, not recipes — knife skills, heat, seasoning, and the why behind every step — so you leave able to cook anything, not just the dish you made that day.

From a single evening class to a full professional diploma, our courses suit absolute beginners and aspiring chefs alike, all taught by working professionals who’ve run real kitchens.

And of course, you eat what you cook. Every class ends around the table, plates full, sharing the meal you made — because that, in the end, is the whole point.

“I came in unable to dice an onion. A year later I run the pass at my own pop-up. Saffron & Salt did that.”

The diploma journey

How a cook becomes a chef

Our flagship diploma builds from fundamentals to professional service. Open any module to see what you’ll master.

Knife skills, the mother sauces, stocks, and controlling heat — the unglamorous foundations every great cook is built on.

Vegetables, eggs, dairy, and the cold kitchen: salads, dressings, and pristine prep.

Breaking down poultry, meat, and fish; cooking proteins to perfection.

Bread, pastry, and the science of dough, lamination, and the wood oven.

Desserts, custards, chocolate, and plated sweet courses.

Pasta, curries, ferments, and the building blocks of global cuisines.

Designing dishes and menus, costing, and the art of the plate.

Working the line under pressure, mise en place, and real-time service.

A two-week placement in a partner restaurant kitchen, cooking for real guests.

You design and cook a multi-course menu, served to friends, family, and our chef panel.
