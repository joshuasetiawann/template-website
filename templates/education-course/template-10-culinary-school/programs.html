Programs & Curriculum

Cooking courses in detail

Every Saffron & Salt course is hands-on, chef-led, and capped at ten cooks, with all ingredients included. Here is what each course covers, the skills you’ll master, fees with payment options, and how to enrol.

DiplomaPastryHome Cook

Professional Diploma

Career-track

24 weeks of full-time training from fundamentals to professional service.

Program overview

Our flagship Professional Diploma is full-time chef training for those serious about a career in food. Over 24 weeks you’ll master every station, complete a two-week restaurant stage, and finish by designing and cooking a multi-course graduation dinner.

Programme modules

  1. 01

    Fundamentals

    Knife skills, sauces, stocks, and heat control across the early weeks.

  2. 02

    Proteins & Bakery

    Butchery, fish, bread, and pastry from the ground up.

  3. 03

    Patisserie & World

    Desserts, chocolate, pasta, ferments, and global techniques.

  4. 04

    Service & Stage

    Plating, menus, line service, and a two-week restaurant placement.

Learning outcomes

  • Master professional knife and heat skills
  • Cook every station to a high standard
  • Butcher, bake, and make patisserie
  • Plate and cost a professional menu
  • Work a real kitchen line under pressure
  • Complete a restaurant kitchen stage

At a glance

Patisserie & Baking

Loved

Breads, pastries, and plated desserts over twelve weeks.

Program overview

The Patisserie & Baking course takes you deep into the sweet and baking side of the kitchen. Over twelve weeks you’ll master bread, laminated pastry, custards, chocolate, and plated desserts, finishing able to bake and plate with real precision.

Course content

  1. 01

    Bread & Dough

    Yeasted breads, enriched doughs, and the science of fermentation.

  2. 02

    Lamination

    Croissants, puff, and Danish — the art of buttery layers.

  3. 03

    Custards & Creams

    Crème pâtissière, mousses, and the building blocks of dessert.

  4. 04

    Plated Patisserie

    Chocolate work and elegant, restaurant-style plated desserts.

Learning outcomes

  • Bake a range of breads confidently
  • Make laminated pastry from scratch
  • Master custards, creams, and mousses
  • Temper and work with chocolate
  • Plate elegant restaurant desserts
  • Understand the science behind baking

At a glance

Home Cook Series

Beginner

Six evenings that transform how you cook every day.

Program overview

The Home Cook Series is six relaxed evening classes that quietly revolutionise your everyday cooking. You’ll learn the core techniques chefs rely on — and how to apply them to fast, delicious meals — so weeknight dinners become a joy rather than a chore.

The six evenings

  1. 01

    Knife & Prep

    Confident, safe knife skills and efficient prep.

  2. 02

    Heat & Searing

    Pan-frying, roasting, and getting a proper sear.

  3. 03

    Sauces & Flavour

    Quick pan sauces, dressings, and balancing seasoning.

  4. 04

    Bringing It Together

    Building fast, complete meals from what you’ve learned.

Learning outcomes

  • Use a knife safely and quickly
  • Sear, roast, and pan-fry well
  • Make quick sauces and dressings
  • Season food with confidence
  • Cook fast, complete weeknight meals
  • Improvise from what’s in the fridge
