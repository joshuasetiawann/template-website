Keeping diversity alive, one packet at a time

Trueseed Company has been saving seed in Decorah since 1994. Open-pollinated and heirloom seeds, saved and shared to keep diversity alive.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your seeds comes from.
