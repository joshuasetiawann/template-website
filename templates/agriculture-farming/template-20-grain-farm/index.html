Our farm

From prairie field to your loaf

Goldacre Grains has been growing grain in Great Falls since 1968. Stone-milled heritage flours and whole grains from regeneratively farmed prairie.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your grain comes from.
