Our farm

Good food starts with living soil

Harvest Hollow has been growing food in Asheville since 1998. Certified-organic vegetables, eggs and honey from our family to your table.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your produce comes from.

How we farm

Practices we believe in

The everyday choices that shape everything we grow and make.

Certified organic

No synthetic pesticides or fertilisers — ever.

Closed-loop compost

Every scrap returns to the soil that fed it.

Drip irrigation

We grow more food with far less water.

Pollinator habitat

Wildflower margins keep our bees and beneficials thriving.

Solar-powered

The barn and cold store run on rooftop sun.

Fair work

A living wage and a share of the harvest for every hand.
