Our story

Field-grown, never flown

Petalfield Flowers began in 2013, when our family set out to do one thing properly: growing flowers in Hood River with honesty, patience and respect for the land.

What started small has grown season by season, but our way of doing things hasn't changed. We still believe the best flowers comes from looking after the soil, the water and the living things around us — and from never cutting corners.

Over the years we've learned that farming well is a long game. We've planted for the future, invested in doing things the right way rather than the quick way, and built relationships with the people who buy from us that we genuinely treasure.

Today Petalfield Flowers is still a hands-on, family-spirited operation. You'll find us in the fields at dawn and behind the counter by day, and we wouldn't have it any other way. Everything we sell, we'd happily put on our own table.

We're proud to call Hood River home, and we're grateful to the community that has supported us from the very first season. When you choose Petalfield Flowers, you're choosing real food, real people and real care.

What we stand for

Our values & sustainability

The principles behind how we farm, every single season.

Field-grown

Real seasonal flowers, never flown across the world.

No floral foam

We arrange with water and natural mechanics.

Pollinator-friendly

The field feeds bees as well as bouquets.

Drip-irrigated

Water goes to the roots, not the air.

Compostable wraps

Paper and twine, never plastic sleeves.

Solar cooler

Stems kept fresh on rooftop sunshine.
