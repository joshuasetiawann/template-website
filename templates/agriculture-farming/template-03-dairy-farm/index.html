Our farm

Grass, cows and honest milk

Clover Meadow Dairy has been milking cows in Tillamook since 1986. Fresh milk, butter and farmhouse cheese from a small herd of grass-fed cows.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your dairy comes from.
