Our farm

Happy birds on green grass

Sunny Run Poultry has been raising poultry in Athens since 2010. Pasture-raised eggs and chicken from happy, free-ranging birds on green grass.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your poultry comes from.
