Our story

Happy birds on green grass

Sunny Run Poultry began in 2010, when our family set out to do one thing properly: raising poultry in Athens with honesty, patience and respect for the land.

What started small has grown season by season, but our way of doing things hasn't changed. We still believe the best poultry comes from looking after the soil, the water and the living things around us — and from never cutting corners.

Over the years we've learned that farming well is a long game. We've planted for the future, invested in doing things the right way rather than the quick way, and built relationships with the people who buy from us that we genuinely treasure.

Today Sunny Run Poultry is still a hands-on, family-spirited operation. You'll find us in the fields at dawn and behind the counter by day, and we wouldn't have it any other way. Everything we sell, we'd happily put on our own table.

We're proud to call Athens home, and we're grateful to the community that has supported us from the very first season. When you choose Sunny Run Poultry, you're choosing real food, real people and real care.
