Our farm

From blossom to your basket

Brightboughs Orchard has been growing fruit in Wenatchee since 1979. Tree-ripened apples, pears, cherries and stone fruit from our hillside orchard.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your fruit comes from.
