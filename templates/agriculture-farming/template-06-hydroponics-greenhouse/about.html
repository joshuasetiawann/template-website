Our story

Fresh greens, whatever the weather

VertiGrove Farms began in 2019, when our family set out to do one thing properly: growing greens in Denver with honesty, patience and respect for the land.

What started small has grown season by season, but our way of doing things hasn't changed. We still believe the best greens comes from looking after the soil, the water and the living things around us — and from never cutting corners.

Over the years we've learned that farming well is a long game. We've planted for the future, invested in doing things the right way rather than the quick way, and built relationships with the people who buy from us that we genuinely treasure.

Today VertiGrove Farms is still a hands-on, family-spirited operation. You'll find us in the fields at dawn and behind the counter by day, and we wouldn't have it any other way. Everything we sell, we'd happily put on our own table.

We're proud to call Denver home, and we're grateful to the community that has supported us from the very first season. When you choose VertiGrove Farms, you're choosing real food, real people and real care.
