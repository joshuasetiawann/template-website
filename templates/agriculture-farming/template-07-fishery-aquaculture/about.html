Our story

Farming the bay, with the tide

Tidewell Aquafarm began in 2008, when our family set out to do one thing properly: farming the bay in Eastport with honesty, patience and respect for the land.

What started small has grown season by season, but our way of doing things hasn't changed. We still believe the best seafood comes from looking after the soil, the water and the living things around us — and from never cutting corners.

Over the years we've learned that farming well is a long game. We've planted for the future, invested in doing things the right way rather than the quick way, and built relationships with the people who buy from us that we genuinely treasure.

Today Tidewell Aquafarm is still a hands-on, family-spirited operation. You'll find us in the fields at dawn and behind the counter by day, and we wouldn't have it any other way. Everything we sell, we'd happily put on our own table.

We're proud to call Eastport home, and we're grateful to the community that has supported us from the very first season. When you choose Tidewell Aquafarm, you're choosing real food, real people and real care.

Our values & sustainability

The principles behind how we farm, every single season.

Low-impact farming

Rope and cage systems that work with the tide.

Clean cold water

Sited in pristine, well-flushed coastal bays.

Kelp co-culture

Seaweed cleans the water and captures carbon.

No-waste shells

Spent shells return to restore the reef.

Traceable to the bay

Every batch tagged from seed to shelf.

Solar barges

Our work boats run on stored sunshine.
