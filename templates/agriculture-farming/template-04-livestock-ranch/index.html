Our farm

Four generations on the range

Iron Ridge Ranch has been raising livestock in Bozeman since 1954. Grass-finished beef and pastured lamb, raised on open Montana rangeland.

We're a small, hands-on operation that believes good food and good farming go together — and that you should always know exactly where your meat comes from.
