Our story

From a simple idea to a trusted network

Nexus Supply Chain began with a clear belief: moving goods should be transparent, dependable and refreshingly easy to deal with. What started small has grown into a supply chain solutions network thousands of customers count on.

Today we combine experienced logistics people with technology that keeps everyone informed — so whether it's one order or thousands, you always know exactly where things stand.
