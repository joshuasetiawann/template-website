The Butterfold Bakehouse menu, in full

From the bench: the ovens are lit at 4am and the first trays land by seven. Our doughs ferment between 18 and 48 hours, so flavour comes from time, not shortcuts. Bakes are made in finite batches — pre-order the day before to be sure of the loaf you want.
