Our Story

A bakehouse with one oven and big feelings

Butterfold started as a Saturday market stall with forty croissants and a hand-painted sign.

Head baker Maren Ode learned lamination in a Copenhagen basement and brought the obsession home. The first forty croissants sold out in nineteen minutes; the bakehouse followed a year later.

We still do it the slow way — cold butter, long ferments, fruit from growers we can name, and a case that changes with whatever the orchard drops off.

MMaren OdeHead Baker & Owner
