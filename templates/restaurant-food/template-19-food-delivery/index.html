The Fleet

Couriers who treat food like cargo that matters

Platerunner started with one courier, one e-bike and a thesis: delivery shouldn't mean soggy.

Founder Dani Okoye spent two years as a bike courier and kept a spreadsheet of everything that arrived ruined. Platerunner is the fix: insulated twin-chamber bags, broth tins, fry vents and routes planned around the food, not just the map.

Every courier is trained on a 'cargo manifest' per cuisine — pizza rides flat, ramen rides split, bowls never tilt past fifteen degrees. The fleet is 70% e-bike and the average drop is under 24 minutes.
