The Full Menu

The Black Kettle menu, in full

From the pit: we smoke low and slow over post oak — brisket goes on at midnight and rests fourteen hours before it's sliced. Meat is sold by weight off the block and served on butcher paper, sauce on the side because good smoke doesn't need hiding. When today's brisket is gone, it's gone.
