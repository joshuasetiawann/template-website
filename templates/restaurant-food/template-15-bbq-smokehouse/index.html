Pit Story

The kettle that started the fire

Pitmaster Otis Crane smoked his first brisket in a beat-up black kettle grill behind a tire shop.

That kettle now sits welded to the smokehouse porch, painted gold by the regulars one anniversary. The pits inside are thousand-gallon offsets Otis built himself, fed post oak every forty-five minutes through the night.

There's no gas line, no pellet hopper, no thermostat — just stick-burner instinct and a logbook going back nine years. The line forms at eleven. The brisket decides everything else.
