Our Story

A physics teacher and a 1947 churn

Moonmilk started when Dee Okada inherited her grandfather's salt-and-ice churn and could not leave it alone.

Dee taught high-school physics for nine years before the churn took over the garage, then the farmers market, then this little shop with the pink awning. The 1947 churn is retired in the window; its five-gallon spirit lives on.

Milk comes from one dairy eleven miles out. Every Sunday night the team test-churns one wildcard flavor — the good ones make the board, the weird ones make the staff freezer.
