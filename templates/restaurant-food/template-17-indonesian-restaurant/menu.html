The Full Menu

The Dapur Kunyit menu, in full

Dari dapur kami: every recipe follows Ibu Sari's 1987 notebook, ground fresh on the batu giling each morning. Spice runs from gentle to galak — your server will calibrate it honestly, no false bravado. Rice is steamed all day and sambal is made in small batches. Prices are in rupiah.
