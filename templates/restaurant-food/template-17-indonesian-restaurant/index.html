Our Kitchen

Ibu Sari's notebook, page by page

The kitchen still cooks from a water-stained notebook dated 1987, Jakarta.

Ibu Sari ran a five-table warung behind the Cikini rail station. Her granddaughter Ayu carried the notebook across an ocean and reopened the kitchen here — same rendang clock, same charcoal, same stubborn refusal to pre-grind sambal.

The rendang pot goes on at seven each morning and is not discussed until two. Spices arrive whole and get toasted in-house. When the notebook says 'sabar' — patience — the kitchen listens.
