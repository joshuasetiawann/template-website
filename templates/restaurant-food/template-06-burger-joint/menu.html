The Full Menu

The Hatch & Griddle menu, in full

Straight off the flat-top: we smash 80g balls of fresh-ground brisket-and-chuck into lacy-edged patties on a 250°C griddle, then stack them on potato buns we toast in the rendered fat. Everything here is built to be eaten in two hands, fast, before the cheese sets.
