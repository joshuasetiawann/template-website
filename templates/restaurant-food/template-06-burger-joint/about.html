Our Story

A griddle older than the owners

Cousins Ray and Tonya Hatch grew up two booths apart in their grandmother’s diner, and they inherited exactly one thing from it that mattered: a slab of cast-iron flat-top, fifty years seasoned and roughly the weight of a small car.

When they finally opened their own place on Larkspur Road, the griddle came first and the building was chosen to fit it. That black, mirror-smooth surface is the whole restaurant — every patty is smashed thin onto it, lacy edges and all.

We keep the menu short on purpose. A couple of burgers done properly beat a wall of options done at half-speed. The beef is ground fresh daily, the buns are squishy and toasted in the beef fat, and the sauce recipe is the one thing we will never print.

It’s a loud, friendly, paper-tray kind of place. Order at the window, grab a picnic table out back, and don’t wear your good shirt — a Hatch smash burger is a two-napkin commitment and we wouldn’t have it any other way.

Everything that can be made in-house is: the pickles, the sauces, the milkshakes spun to order. We are not chasing fancy. We are chasing the exact burger Ray and Tonya remember eating as kids, just smashed a little harder.

How we cook

Flat is flavour — smash it like you mean it

Seasoned cast iron

The fifty-year flat-top runs screaming hot, so every patty gets a crisp, lacy, deeply browned crust. That sear is the entire point.

Ground fresh daily

We grind chuck and brisket every morning and smash it thin to order. Nothing sits, nothing is frozen, nothing is pre-formed.

Made in-house

Pickles, sauces and milkshakes are all made here. The secret sauce stays secret; everything else we’re happy to explain.
