The Full Menu

The Rosso Forno menu, in full

From the oven floor: our dough proves for 72 hours and bakes 90 seconds at 480°C over oak and beech. That leopard-spotted char is the point. We make a fixed number of dough balls each night — when they're gone, the oven goes cold, so order ahead on busy weekends.
