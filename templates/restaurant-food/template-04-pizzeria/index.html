Our Oven

The oven came first, the shop came after

Rosso Forno literally grew around a three-tonne oven shipped from Naples.

Founders Gia and Paolo Russo bought the oven before they had a lease — it sat in a storage unit for eight months while they found a corner shop with floors strong enough to hold it.

The dough is a two-day affair: a slow, cold ferment that blisters into a leopard-spotted crust in ninety seconds. Nothing on the menu takes longer to cook than it takes to find a seat.
