Our Story

A diner kid and a pastry chef walk into a lease

Yolk & Marigold is what happens when a third-generation diner kid marries a pastry chef.

Nina grew up refilling coffee in her family's diner; Theo piped choux in fancy hotels. Their compromise is this sunny room — diner warmth, patisserie standards, and a hollandaise that gets whisked fresh every forty-five minutes.

The marigolds on every table come from Nina's mother's garden between May and October. The playlist is whatever the dish pit votes for. Sundays are loud and we love them that way.
