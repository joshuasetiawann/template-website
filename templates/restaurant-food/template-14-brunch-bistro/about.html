Our Story

A diner kid and a pastry chef walk into a lease

Yolk & Marigold is a marriage in restaurant form. Nina grew up in her family’s diner — third generation behind a flat-top — while Theo trained as a pastry chef in proper, white-tablecloth kitchens. When they got married, they got a lease too.

The idea was to take the warmth and bottomless coffee of a diner and marry it to the laminated pastry and bright plates of a bistro. Brunch, they decided, is just breakfast that finally relaxed — so they built a sunny room where nobody is rushed off their chair.

Nina runs the savoury side and the flat-top, turning out the eggs, hash and stacks that taste like the diner she grew up in. Theo runs the pastry case and the baking, so the croissants and morning buns are bistro-grade and gone by noon.

Everything leans bright and generous: yolky eggs, citrus and herbs, golden pastry, peach-and-sky colours on the walls. The coffee keeps coming, the patio opens at nine, and the weekend waitlist is half the fun.

It’s a neighbourhood place at heart. Dogs get a biscuit, regulars get their corner, and the tips get split with the dish pit. Come hungry, stay for a second pot of coffee, and don’t plan anything urgent for after.
