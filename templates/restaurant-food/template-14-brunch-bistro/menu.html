The Full Menu

The Yolk & Marigold menu, in full

From the brunch pass: eggs are the whole point here, so we keep them gloriously runny and the hollandaise made to order. Bread comes from the bakery next door and the coffee is local. Brunch runs all day, every day — because the best meal shouldn't have a closing time at noon.
