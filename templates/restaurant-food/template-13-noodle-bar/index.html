Our Story

Four generations of dough slap

Head puller Wei Lin learned lamian from his grandmother, who learned it from hers.

Wei pulled his first passable noodle at age nine and his first perfect one at twenty-three — his grandmother kept score. Tangle opened with her rolling pin mounted above the bench and her rules taped inside the flour bin.

Every bowl starts as a fist of dough. Pull, fold, slap, repeat — eight times for ribbons, twelve for threads. The broth pots never leave the stove; the morning crew just hands them to the night crew.
