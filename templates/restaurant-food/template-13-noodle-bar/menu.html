The Full Menu

The Tangle menu, in full

From behind the pass: noodles are pulled to order by hand — you can watch the dough get stretched and slapped at the counter. Broths simmer for twelve hours minimum and we season each bowl to its own balance of fat, salt and spice. Tell us your heat level; galak means we don't hold back.
