Our Story

Two roasters, one corner shop

Harrow & Bean began as a weekend roastery in a garage three streets away.

Siblings June and Marcus Harrow roasted their first sacks of Yirgacheffe on a repurposed popcorn drum in 2015. Two years and one very forgiving landlord later, the corner shop on Alder Street became home.

Everything is still small-batch: coffee roasted twice a week, buns knotted before sunrise, and a brew bar where the kettle never gets to boil twice for the same cup.
