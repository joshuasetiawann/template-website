Our Story

One plancha, four wheels, zero landlords

Citrus Comet is what happens when a line cook buys a retired postal truck at auction.

Chef-owner Manny Reyes spent twelve years in other people's kitchens before the auction paddle slipped (his words). Eight months of welding, one questionable paint job and a citrus obsession later, the Comet rolled out.

The route grew stop by stop — breweries, the Saturday market, the courthouse lawn. The truck now sells out most days by 2 pm, and Manny still presses every tortilla himself.
