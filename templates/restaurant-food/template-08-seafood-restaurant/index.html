Our Story

Three decades on the same pier

Saltline & Tide opened the year the old cannery closed, in the building where the catch was once weighed.

Founder Rosa Delmar started with six tables and a handshake deal with two boats. The boats' grandchildren still tie up at our dock; the handshake still holds. The chalkboard has never once repeated a week.

Chef Tomás Rivera came aboard in 2011 and brought the raw bar with him. The rule hasn't changed: if it wasn't swimming yesterday, it isn't on the board today.
