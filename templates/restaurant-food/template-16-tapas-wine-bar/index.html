Our Story

A honeymoon that never quite ended

Cala Roja is named for the little red cove where Marisol and Ben decided not to fly home.

The plan was two weeks in Spain. It became three months, a stage in a Barcelona bodega, and a notebook of recipes copied at closing time. The notebook hangs framed by the bar, sangría stains and all.

The tiles came back in suitcases over six trips. The jamón leg arrives weekly and is gone by Sunday. The rule of the house: plates are small so evenings can be long.
