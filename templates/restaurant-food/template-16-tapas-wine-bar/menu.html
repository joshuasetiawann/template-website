The Full Menu

The Cala Roja menu, in full

From behind the bar: tapas are made to be ordered a few at a time and shared, so let the table fill slowly. The jamón is carved by hand to order, the bread is grilled over coals, and the sherry list is the soul of the place. Prices are in euros; pan con tomate appears whether you ask or not.
