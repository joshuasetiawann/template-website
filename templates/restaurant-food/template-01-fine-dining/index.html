The Chef

Cooking that begins at the fire

Chef-patron Elena Marchetti opened Lumière & Ash with one rule: every course must touch flame.

Elena cooked through the kitchens of Lyon and Copenhagen before coming home to build a restaurant around a single open hearth. The menu is written each Tuesday, after the market — never before.

Her kitchen works in near silence. Twelve cooks, one fire, and a tasting menu that moves from raw and bright through smoke and ember into sweetness and ash.
