The Full Menu

The Emberline Chophouse menu, in full

From the grill: we dry-age whole sub-primals in the cabinet by the door — you walked past 28, 45 and 90 days on your way in. Every cut is grilled over white-oak embers and rested before it reaches you. Steaks are sold by weight and finished with bone-marrow butter unless you say otherwise.
