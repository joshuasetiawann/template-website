Our Story

Started in a farm shed, sauced ever since

Rootline began as a harvest-dinner series in the shed of Marigold Acres farm.

Chef Imani Okafor cooked the first Rootline dinner for forty people on a borrowed grill, with whatever the farm pulled that morning. Three summers of sold-out sheds later, the restaurant opened in town — same farms, same fire.

The menu is written backwards: growers tell us what's at its peak, then the kitchen finds the dish. The wood grill does the heavy lifting; the ferment shelf does the plotting.
