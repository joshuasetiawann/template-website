Our Story

Born in restaurant kitchens, raised in marquees

Velvet Thyme was founded by two restaurant lifers who kept getting asked to cook at weddings.

Chef Lena Petros and maître d' Samuel Ash met working a three-star dining room and moonlighted their first wedding for forty guests in 2013. The rule they set that night still stands: no buffet trays under heat lamps, ever.

Today a crew of twenty-two cooks, captains and stylists move the kitchen to vineyards, lofts and back gardens. The tasting comes first, the menu is written second, and the timeline is rehearsed like theatre.
