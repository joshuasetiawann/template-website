Our Story

Born in restaurant kitchens, raised in marquees

Velvet Thyme started as a series of favours. Chef Lena Petros and maître d’ Samuel Ash had spent years in fine-dining rooms when friends, then friends of friends, kept asking them to cook for weddings. Eventually the favours needed a name and a kitchen.

They brought their restaurant standards out of the dining room and into marquees, gardens and warehouses. The thinking was simple: an event menu should taste like a restaurant that exists for one night only — not like catering, with all the compromises that word usually carries.

Every event is cooked to order from a production kitchen, then finished on site so nothing arrives tired. Tastings happen at the studio, menus are written around the couple or the occasion, and the team scales from an intimate dinner to a few hundred without dropping the thread.

Lena runs the kitchen and the menus; Samuel runs the floor, the timings and the thousand invisible details that make a room feel effortless. Between them they have catered enough weddings to know that the magic is in the planning, not the panic.

We travel, we plan obsessively, and we treat someone’s big day with the seriousness it deserves. The food should be the part nobody worries about — and, on a good night, the part everyone remembers.
