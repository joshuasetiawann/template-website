The Itamae

Twenty years for ten seats

Chef Sato Kenji trained in Kanazawa and Tokyo before building this quiet ten-seat room.

Sato-san spent eleven years on rice alone — washing, seasoning, reading the steam — before his master let him touch fish in service. Kanade is the room he was building in his head the whole time.

There is no music at the counter. Just the knife, the brush of nikiri, and a pace set by how fast you finish each piece. Tables are gentler; the counter is the full conversation.
