Instructors

Chefs who'd rather teach than plate

The Atelier was founded by a chef who kept catching herself explaining instead of expediting.

Chef Rosa Lindqvist ran restaurant kitchens for fifteen years and loved the teaching nights more than the service ones. She traded the pass for twelve teaching stations, a wall of copper pans and a strict no-demo-only rule.

Guest instructors rotate monthly — an itamae, a pitmaster, a third-generation pasta nonna. The recipe cards are tested by people who cook on Tuesdays, not brigades. You will wash your own pans. You will be glad.
