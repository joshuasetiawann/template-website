Our Story

A marathon, a juicer, a tiny shop

Pulp & Glow started with a hand-me-down press and a runner who hated sports drinks.

Founder Mae Santos trained for her first marathon on homemade green juice and kept getting flagged down by other runners asking for bottles. The press moved from her kitchen to a market stall to this glowing little corner unit.

Produce comes from two farms and a citrus grower with strong opinions. Pulp goes to a community compost; bottles come back for a dollar off. The wheatgrass really does grow in the window.
