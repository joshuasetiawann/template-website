Our mission

Real change, driven by people who care

Stepping Stones Fund opens the door to school, college and skills training for bright students held back by nothing but the cost of learning.

At Stepping Stones Fund, every gift is put to work where it matters most. We pair compassion with accountability — measuring our results, publishing our impact and treating every person we serve with dignity.
