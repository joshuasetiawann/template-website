Our mission

Real change, driven by people who care

Mercy Lines Health funds clinics, surgeries and frontline medical teams in regions where care is scarce — bringing treatment within reach of all.

At Mercy Lines Health, every gift is put to work where it matters most. We pair compassion with accountability — measuring our results, publishing our impact and treating every person we serve with dignity.
