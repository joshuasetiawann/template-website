About Mercy Lines Health

Compassion, accountability and a 22-year promise

Mercy Lines Health funds clinics, surgeries and frontline medical teams in regions where care is scarce — bringing treatment within reach of all.

Our story

Why we exist, and how we work

Mercy Lines Health began with a simple, stubborn conviction: that no one should be left behind, and that ordinary people, pooling their generosity, can achieve extraordinary things. What started as a handful of determined volunteers has grown into one of the most trusted names in global health.

Our work is built on a single principle — that every person we serve deserves dignity, not pity. We listen before we act, we partner with the communities we serve rather than imposing solutions, and we measure ourselves not by how much we spend but by the real, lasting change we create.

Over the years we have learned that impact is never an accident. It comes from rigorous programmes, honest reporting and the discipline to keep our costs low so that the maximum possible share of every donation reaches the front line. Our supporters trust us with their generosity, and we treat that trust as the most precious thing we hold.

Today, Mercy Lines Health works across multiple programmes — each one designed, delivered and reviewed with the same care. Behind every statistic on this page is a person whose life is different because someone, somewhere, decided to give. That is the heart of everything we do.

We are funded entirely by the kindness of individuals, community groups and partners who believe a better world is possible — and who are willing to help build it. This page is our promise to them: to stay transparent, stay focused, and never lose sight of why we started.
