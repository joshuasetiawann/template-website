Our mission

Real change, driven by people who care

Little Lanterns Foundation gives vulnerable children safe homes, schooling and the steady support they need to thrive — not just survive.

At Little Lanterns Foundation, every gift is put to work where it matters most. We pair compassion with accountability — measuring our results, publishing our impact and treating every person we serve with dignity.
