Our mission

Real change, driven by people who care

Brightpaw Haven rescues, heals and rehomes abandoned animals across the county — and finds them the families they were always meant to have.

At Brightpaw Haven, every gift is put to work where it matters most. We pair compassion with accountability — measuring our results, publishing our impact and treating every person we serve with dignity.
