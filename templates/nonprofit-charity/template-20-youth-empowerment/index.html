Our mission

Real change, driven by people who care

Ignite Youth Collective backs young people with mentorship, leadership programs and seed funding to launch the ideas that move communities forward.

At Ignite Youth Collective, every gift is put to work where it matters most. We pair compassion with accountability — measuring our results, publishing our impact and treating every person we serve with dignity.
