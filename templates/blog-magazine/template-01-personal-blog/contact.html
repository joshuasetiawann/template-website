Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

How often do you publish?

One essay or letter at a time, sent when it is genuinely finished. Some weeks that is Sunday; some weeks it is the Sunday after.

Can I reprint an essay?

Yes, for non-commercial use with a link back. Ask first for anything else — the answer is usually a delighted yes.

Do you take guest writing?

Once a season, in March. Send a paragraph about the thing you keep noticing, not a formal pitch.

Is there really no tracking?

None. No pixels, no open-rates, no funnels. Unsubscribe is one click and carries no guilt.
