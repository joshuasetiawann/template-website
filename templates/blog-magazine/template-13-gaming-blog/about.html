Our story

How this masthead came to be

Respawn Point started on a forum thread that wouldn't die — an argument about whether a 'small' game could be more important than a blockbuster. We came down firmly on yes, and built a site around it. We cover games as a craft and a culture, not as a hype schedule, and we judge a release by what it does, not what it cost to market.

We review across the spectrum: the AAA tentpole and the two-person indie, the competitive meta and the patch notes nobody else reads closely. A brilliant indie gets our full attention; a flawed blockbuster gets honest scrutiny. We finish the games we review, and we tell you how long that took.

Esports coverage here is about the play, not just the prize pool — the strat that broke a meta, the patch that reshaped a tier list, the underdog run that deserved the headline. Our hardware reviews are benchmarked on real games, not just synthetic loops, and we publish the frame times.

Respawn Point is independent and reader-supported. The Checkpoint newsletter saves your progress weekly: the one game worth your weekend and the patch note that quietly changed everything.
