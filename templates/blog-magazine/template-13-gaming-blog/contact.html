Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Do you finish games before reviewing?

Yes — or we tell you exactly how far we got and why. A first-impressions piece is always labelled as one, never as a verdict.

Do you cover small indie games?

Constantly. Indie spotlights are core to the site, and a self-published gem gets the same care as a blockbuster.

Do you take sponsored reviews?

No. Studios send codes; they get no say in the score, the framing, or whether the review runs at all.

How often is Checkpoint?

Weekly — the one game worth your weekend and the patch note that quietly changed everything.
