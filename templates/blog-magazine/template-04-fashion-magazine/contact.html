Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Do you accept sponsored coverage?

Sponsored projects are labelled clearly and kept entirely separate from editorial. A brand cannot buy a review or a cover.

Can I pitch as an emerging designer?

Yes, enthusiastically. We commission for point of view, not for budget — send the work and the idea behind it.

How do I submit images?

A paragraph and three reference images by email. If it is a fit, our photo editor will follow up about commissioning.

How often is the Dispatch?

One considered letter a week — the show that mattered, the maker worth knowing, and nothing you need to scroll past.
