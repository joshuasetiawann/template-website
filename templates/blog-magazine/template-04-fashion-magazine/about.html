Our story

How this masthead came to be

Lumière began backstage, in the ten minutes before a show when everything is safety pins and nerve. We wanted a magazine that captured that moment — the craft under the glamour — rather than just the finished walk. Fashion, to us, is a discipline before it is a spectacle.

We cover the runway, but we also cover the pattern cutter, the colourist, the stylist who solved an impossible brief at 4 a.m. Our editorials are shot like arguments and our interviews go past the press line. The clothes matter; the hands that made them matter more.

Lumière is independent, which lets us be honest about what is beautiful and what is merely expensive. We do not run a review we did not believe, and we do not pretend a trend is new because a calendar says so. Taste, here, is a point of view, not a rented one.

From the cover to the last contributor note, the magazine is made by people who love this industry enough to hold it to a standard. The Dispatch newsletter sends one considered letter a week, no haul videos included.

Editorial mission

To cover fashion as a craft and a culture — the runway and the workroom, the trend and the technique — with an independent point of view.

How we work

Three rules we don't bend

The standards behind everything we publish.

01

Backstage first

We report the craft before the spectacle — the fittings, the workroom, the decisions made in the last ten minutes.

02

Honest reviews

We run what we believe. A house we love gets the same scrutiny as one we don't, because that is what respect looks like.

03

Credit the makers

Pattern cutters, colourists, stylists and set builders are named. The walk is the smallest part of the work.
