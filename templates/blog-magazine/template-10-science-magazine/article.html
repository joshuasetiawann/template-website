Coral Larvae Navigate by Sound, Tank Studies Suggest

Played the audio of a healthy reef, week-old larvae swam toward the speakers at twice the random rate. The implications for restoration are loud.

FN

Felix Nakamura

Jun 4, 2026·8 min read

The result is small, almost rude in its modesty, and it quietly rewrites a textbook footnote. Played the audio of a healthy reef, week-old larvae swam toward the speakers at twice the random rate. The implications for restoration are loud.

What follows is less a verdict than a working argument, assembled from notes, false starts and one or two opinions I have since revised. The headline is up top; everything below it has to earn the claim or quietly take it back.

What the result actually says

The finding is small enough to fit in a sentence and stubborn enough to survive replication, which is the combination that quietly rewrites textbooks. It does not overturn the model; it corrects a footnote everyone had stopped reading.

What makes it credible is not the headline but the controls — the boring, exhaustive ruling-out of the dozen more mundane explanations. Good science is mostly the disciplined elimination of the ways you could be fooling yourself.

The temptation in writing this up is to oversell it. I will resist. This is one careful brick, laid straight, in a wall that took a century to build. The honest excitement is that the brick fits, and that the next one now has somewhere to go.

What the study did right

  • Pre-registered its hypotheses, so the result could not be quietly reshaped to fit the data.
  • Shared its data and code, inviting the scrutiny that lesser work tries to avoid.
  • Stated its limits plainly, which is the surest sign of a team that trusts its own work.

Why it matters beyond the lab

Be patient with the boring middle. Beginnings are theatrical and endings are quotable, but the middle is where the real decisions live, unglamorous and undocumented. The block below is the part I would clip and keep.

Three things to carry out the door

  • Start smaller than feels respectable; momentum is the only currency that compounds.
  • Write down the thing you are afraid to be wrong about. Revisit it in a month.
  • Borrow ruthlessly, attribute generously, and keep the version that survives contact with reality.

No single study settles anything, and this one does not pretend to. But it is honest, replicable and quietly load-bearing, and in a noisy field that modesty is its own kind of breakthrough.
