Our story

How this masthead came to be

Little Wonders began at 3 a.m., as so many parenting things do, with a search that returned either perfect families or panic. We wanted a third option: honest, warm, evidence-aware writing from people in the thick of it, who would admit what they didn't know and never make you feel like you were failing.

We cover the whole arc — Babies, Toddlers, Big Kids and Family Life — with real talk about the parts the highlight reels skip. The hard nights, the messy feelings, the small victories that don't photograph well. We cite the research when it helps and say clearly when the research is thin.

Nothing here is prescriptive. Children are different, families are different, and the goal is a few good options and a lot of reassurance, not a single right way. We are careful, kind, and allergic to shame — the last thing a tired parent needs is one more way to be judged.

Little Wonders is independent and parent-run. The newsletter lands once a week with one practical idea, one honest story, and the gentle reminder that you're doing better than you think.

Editorial mission

To support parents with honesty and warmth — evidence-aware, shame-free notes on real family life, not a single right way to do it.
