Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Is your health information reliable?

Our medical claims are reviewed by a pediatric advisor and we cite sources — but we're a blog, not a substitute for your own doctor.

Will you tell me the 'right' way to parent?

No, deliberately. We offer a few good, evidence-aware options and a lot of reassurance, because every child and family is different.

Can I share my own story?

Yes, please. Send what happened and what it taught you — honest stories from real parents are the heart of the blog.

How often is the newsletter?

Once a week: one practical idea, one honest story, and a reminder that you're doing better than you think.
