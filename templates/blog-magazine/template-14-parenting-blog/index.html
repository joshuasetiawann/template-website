Tested tips you can use before nap time ends — one per developmental chaos bracket.

0–1: The five-minute reset

Fed, dry, warm, held, fed again. When all five are checked and the crying continues, it's allowed to just be a day.

1–4: Offer two choices, both yours

Red cup or blue cup beats 'please drink water'. Toddler autonomy is a menu you quietly write.
