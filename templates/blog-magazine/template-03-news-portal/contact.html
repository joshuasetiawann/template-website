Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Who funds The Daily Meridian?

Readers, primarily, through subscriptions to the Brief. We disclose any grant funding and accept no editorial conditions with it.

How do you handle anonymous sources?

Sparingly, and never alone. Anonymous claims are corroborated by documents or additional sources before they run.

What's the difference between your sections?

Reporting states what is confirmed. Analysis explains what it means. Opinion argues a position — and each is labelled.

How often does the Brief arrive?

Every morning, with the few things that actually changed and links to the full reporting beneath each item.
