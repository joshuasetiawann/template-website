Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Do you take sponsored benchmarks?

Never. Vendors can send hardware for review, but they get no input on methodology, framing, or whether the result is flattering.

Can I reproduce your results?

That's the point. Every analysis links its configs and scripts; the methodology section is written so you can repeat it.

How often is the newsletter?

Once a week, on Fridays. One email, no drip sequences, and the list is never sold.

Will you cover my tool?

Maybe — pitch the surprising result, not the feature list. We cover decisions and trade-offs, not changelogs.
