Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Are the recipes really tested?

Three times minimum, in a home kitchen on a domestic oven. We log the failures publicly in the Test Kitchen notes.

Why grams and cups?

Because volume is unreliable for baking. We give both so you can use what you have, but we trust the scale.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Usually — most recipes list the swaps we've actually tested. If a substitution will break the dish, we say so.

How often is the newsletter?

Once a week: one weeknight recipe, one bake, and the test-kitchen note that saved us from ourselves.
