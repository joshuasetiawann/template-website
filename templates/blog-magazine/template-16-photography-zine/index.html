Editor, printer, keeper of the good thermometer

Daria shot newsroom assignments for nine years before trading deadlines for fiber paper. Aperture Null is printed in her cellar darkroom and co-edited with Emil Castaneda, who scans, sequences and defends every caption's right to be one sentence.
