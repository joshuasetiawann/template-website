Our story

How this masthead came to be

Static & Signal is a podcast made in two closets six time zones apart, plus a companion blog that exists because some sources deserve to be seen, not just heard. We started because the strangest, most human stories were hiding in the internet's overlooked corners, and nobody was reporting them with both rigour and delight.

Each episode is an investigation: a haunted shopping cart traced back to a recommendation pipeline, the people who keep 3 a.m. running, the archivist saving dead websites one fan shrine at a time. The blog carries the receipts — every source time-stamped to the minute we mention it — plus the gear confessions and the segments that didn't survive the edit.

We are independent and entirely listener-funded, which means no dynamic ad reads sneaking into your feed and no sponsor deciding what's interesting. We double-end our recordings, mail SD cards like it's 2009, and argue endlessly about jingles. The production is duct tape; the reporting is not.

Static & Signal sends The Signal Flare each week: episode drops, the one source worth your click, and a transmission from the cutting-room floor. The voicemail line is always open — three minutes max, weird encouraged.
