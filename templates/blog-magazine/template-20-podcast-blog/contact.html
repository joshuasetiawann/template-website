Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Do I need the audio to read the blog?

No — the show notes stand alone and carry every source. But the episode is where the haunting lives, so press play too.

Are there ads in the show?

No dynamic ad insertion, ever. Static & Signal is listener-funded so the sponsors never decide what's interesting.

Can I send a story tip?

Please do — by email, or call the voicemail line and ramble. Three minutes max, and weird is genuinely encouraged.

How often is The Signal Flare?

Weekly — episode drops, the one source worth your click, and a transmission from the cutting-room floor.
