About the review

Two readers, one ruler of margins

Founded on the belief that a review owes you its evidence.

EV

Eleanor Voss

Editor & chief annotator

Eleanor spent a decade as an acquisitions editor before defecting to the reader's side of the desk. She reviews with three pencils (argument, admiration, alarm) and co-writes the Review with translator Tobias Lindholm-Reyes, who handles everything with an umlaut.

House style: spoilers fenced, page numbers cited, hype quarantined.
