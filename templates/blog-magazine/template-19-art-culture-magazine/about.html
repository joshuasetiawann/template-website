Our story

How this masthead came to be

TILT started from a hunch that culture writing had gotten too polite. Everything was 'stunning' and nothing was at stake. We wanted a magazine with a point of view and the nerve to defend it — criticism that takes art seriously enough to disagree with it, and curiosity wide enough to look everywhere.

We cover Exhibitions, Studio Visits, Film & Stage, Design and Public Art, moving between the museum and the street without ranking one above the other. Our studio visits go past the artist statement to the actual work and the actual doubt. Our reviews argue; our profiles probe.

TILT is independent, which lets us be honest when an acclaimed show is hollow and generous when an overlooked one is alive. We credit the makers, name the curators, and treat design and public art as the serious cultural forces they are rather than decoration.

Made by people who'd rather have the argument than skip it, TILT sends one provocation a week in The Letter — the show, the idea, or the object worth your attention and your opinion.

Editorial mission

To write culture with a point of view — criticism brave enough to disagree, curiosity wide enough to look everywhere, from the museum to the street.
