Our story

How this masthead came to be

Stillpoint began as a reaction to the noise. Wellness had become a marketplace of extremes — cleanses, hacks, and the relentless implication that you were one purchase away from being fixed. We wanted a calmer, evidence-aware voice that treats health as a practice rather than a product, and the reader as a capable adult rather than a target.

We write about Movement, Nutrition, Sleep, Mindfulness and Habits with an eye on the actual research and a deep suspicion of miracle claims. When the evidence is strong, we say so. When it's thin, contested, or has been oversold, we say that too. Nuance is not a weakness here; it's the point.

Nothing on Stillpoint is meant to shame you or sell you a supplement. We name costs and trade-offs, we keep the scope realistic, and we are clear that we are a blog, not your clinician. The goal is small, sustainable change — the practice you'll actually keep, not the protocol you'll abandon by February.

Stillpoint is independent and gently paced. The Stretch newsletter sends one calm, useful essay a week and a single small practice worth trying.
