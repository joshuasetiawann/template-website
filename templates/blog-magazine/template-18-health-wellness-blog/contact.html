Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

Is this medical advice?

No. Stillpoint is educational and evidence-aware, but it is not a substitute for your own clinician, who knows your situation.

Why are you so cautious about claims?

Because wellness is full of oversold certainty. We'd rather give you honest nuance than a confident answer the evidence can't support.

Do you sell supplements or programs?

Never. We take no supplement money, and any sponsored content is labelled and walled off from the editorial entirely.

How often is The Stretch?

Weekly — one calm, useful essay and a single small practice worth trying this week.
