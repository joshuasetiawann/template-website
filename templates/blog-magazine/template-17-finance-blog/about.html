Our story

How this masthead came to be

Basis Point was founded on a quiet irritation: most finance writing is either trying to sell you something or trying to scare you into a trade. We wanted a calmer voice — one that explains markets and money clearly, respects your intelligence, and never confuses volatility for insight.

We cover Markets, Investing, Personal Finance, Fintech and Macro for readers who want to understand rather than gamble. Our analysis shows its assumptions, our personal-finance writing names the trade-offs, and we are honest about how much of the future nobody can know. We don't do hot stock tips, because they aren't journalism.

Independence is the whole point. We don't take payment to feature a product, we disclose any position, and we separate education from opinion. A good finance piece should leave you better able to make your own decision, not more dependent on ours.

Basis Point is reader-supported and proudly boring in the best way. The Memo newsletter sends one clear read on the markets each week and the one chart actually worth your attention.

Editorial mission

To explain markets and money without hype — clear, assumption-showing analysis that helps you decide for yourself, not a stock tip in disguise.
