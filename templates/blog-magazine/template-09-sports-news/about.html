Our story

How this masthead came to be

Full Press was founded by reporters who were tired of hot takes dressed up as coverage. Sport deserves better than outrage on a loop. We cover the match, the tactics, the transfer, and the long arc of a season with the seriousness fans bring to it — because the people reading actually know the game.

Our match reports go beyond the scoreline to the decisions that produced it — the press that broke a midfield, the substitution that turned a game, the marginal call that will be argued about for a week. We bring analysis that explains rather than merely reacts.

The Transfer Desk is built on sourcing, not speculation. We tell you what we know, how we know it, and when something is a genuine done deal versus an agent's wish. We would rather be right on a Thursday than first on a Tuesday with a story that collapses by Friday.

Full Press is independent and reader-funded. The Briefing newsletter sends the results that mattered and the one tactical wrinkle worth your morning, with no manufactured controversy attached.
