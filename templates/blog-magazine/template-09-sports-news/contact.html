Before you write

Frequently asked

A few answers that might save you an email.

How does the Transfer Desk verify deals?

Through sources we trust and can corroborate. We grade each story — done deal, advanced, or speculation — so you know what you're reading.

Do you do hot takes?

No. We cover tactics, decisions and the long arc of a season. If a take can't survive a second look, it doesn't run.

Who funds Full Press?

Readers, through the Briefing. That keeps our incentive aligned with being trusted rather than going viral.

How often is the Briefing?

Every match-day morning: the results that mattered and the one tactical wrinkle worth your coffee.
