Our story

How this masthead came to be

Golden Hour began as a love letter to the unremarkable evening — the one with good light, a slow dinner and nowhere to be. We thought lifestyle media had become a competition, all optimisation and acquisition, and we wanted to make something gentler: a magazine about living well rather than performing it.

We cover Home, Wellbeing, Style, Entertaining and City Guides with a bias toward what is real, repeatable and kind to your time and budget. The room you actually live in, the ritual you'll actually keep, the dinner you can actually host on a Wednesday. Aspiration, here, is meant to be reachable.

Nothing in Golden Hour is here to make you feel behind. We test the recommendations, we name the costs, and we are honest when something lovely is also impractical. Taste is a point of view we're happy to argue, but never a stick to beat you with.

Golden Hour is independent and made by people who genuinely enjoy a good evening. The Slow newsletter sends one unhurried letter a week — something to make, somewhere to sit, someone worth reading.

Editorial mission

To celebrate living well rather than performing it — real, repeatable ideas for home, wellbeing, style and the ordinary good evening.
