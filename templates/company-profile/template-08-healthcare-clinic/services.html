Care & outcomes

Every visit, explained before you book.

No one should walk into an appointment unsure of what happens or what it costs. Below is each Brightwater service in plain words — what the visit includes, how it runs, the fee before insurance — and three care programs with the outcomes we measured.

Services in depth

Six services, one shared record

The same six doors from the homepage tabs, opened properly. Most fees are billed direct to fourteen insurer networks.

GP Consultations

Twenty minutes with your named doctor — forty for new patients — booked same-week, guaranteed. Your GP reads the file before you sit down and stays your coordinator if a referral is needed, so nothing gets repeated and nothing gets lost.

What’s included

  • 20-minute consultation with your named GP
  • File reviewed before you arrive — history never repeated
  • Prescriptions sent electronically to your pharmacy
  • Same-building referral to our specialists when needed
  • Results explained by phone within 48 hours

How a visit runs

  1. 1Book. Online or by phone; reception answers in under a minute.
  2. 2Consult. Twenty unhurried minutes — your questions are on the agenda.
  3. 3Follow up. Written plan in hand; results call within two days.

Consultations from $95 · billed direct to most insurers

Child & Teen Health

Newborn checks through teen health in a paediatric wing with its own waiting room and clinicians who talk to the child, not over them. Vaccinations, growth reviews and school forms run to schedule — including Saturday slots for working families.

What’s included

  • Full childhood vaccination schedule, tracked and recalled
  • Growth and development checks with percentile charts
  • School, camp and sports forms signed without the wait
  • Teen check-ins with time alone with the clinician
  • Whole family on one record your GP actually reads

How a visit runs

  1. 1Pre-visit notes. You tell us concerns in advance so nothing is rushed.
  2. 2The check. Paediatric waiting area, child-paced examination.
  3. 3Parent debrief. Plain-language summary and the next due dates.

Child & teen reviews from $75 · vaccines billed to insurance

Cardiology Clinic

Consultant cardiology on site every weekday — ECG, stress testing and hypertension reviews without a hospital referral loop. Dr. Mensah’s team shares the same record as your GP, so medication changes land in one plan, not three letters.

What’s included

  • Consultant review with resting 12-lead ECG
  • Treadmill stress testing with same-day interpretation
  • 24-hour blood-pressure and rhythm monitoring loans
  • Hypertension reviews with a written titration plan
  • Findings copied to your GP on the shared record, same day

How a visit runs

  1. 1Referral or self-book. Your GP refers, or you book a review directly.
  2. 2Test & consult. ECG or stress test and the consultant in one visit.
  3. 3One plan. Medication and lifestyle plan agreed with your GP.

Cardiology consults from $180 · testing quoted before booking

Physiotherapy

Hands-on rehabilitation with gym space on site and home exercise videos that show your own programme, not a generic playlist. Most plans run six to ten sessions with measurable goals agreed at the first assessment.

What’s included

  • 45-minute first assessment with movement screening
  • Manual therapy and supervised gym rehabilitation
  • Home programme with video demonstrations of your exercises
  • Return-to-work and return-to-sport milestone letters
  • Progress reviewed against goals every third session

How a plan runs

  1. 1Assess. Baseline strength and range measured, goals written down.
  2. 2Rebuild. Weekly sessions plus your filmed home programme.
  3. 3Discharge. Goals re-tested; a maintenance plan goes home with you.

Physio sessions from $85 · packages quoted at assessment

Imaging & Ultrasound

Ultrasound and X-ray downstairs from the consulting rooms, usually within the hour of your GP requesting them. Images are read within 24 hours and the report lands on the same record your doctor opens at follow-up.

What’s included

  • Diagnostic ultrasound including pregnancy and MSK scans
  • Digital X-ray with radiologist reporting inside 24 hours
  • Most scans done within the hour of the GP request
  • Images shared to hospital teams when onward care is needed
  • Results explained by your own GP, not a letter

How a scan runs

  1. 1Request. Your clinician books the scan during your consultation.
  2. 2Scan. Downstairs, usually the same morning — no second trip.
  3. 3Report. Read within 24 hours; your GP calls with the meaning.

Ultrasound from $140 · X-ray from $90

Laboratory Services

Morning bloods from 7:30 so fasting tests fit before work, with most panels resulted the same day on our own analysers. Anything abnormal triggers a clinician phone call — not a portal notification you find a week later.

What’s included

  • Walk-in phlebotomy from 7:30 every weekday
  • Routine panels resulted same day on site
  • Annual health-check panels matched to age and history
  • Abnormal results phoned by a clinician within 48 hours
  • Results filed to the family record your GP reviews

How it runs

  1. 1Arrive early. Fasting draws first; coffee allowed straight after.
  2. 2Processed on site. Same-day analysers for the common panels.
  3. 3Explained. Numbers translated into plain words by phone.

Blood panels from $35 · health-check bundles from $120

Measured outcomes

Three programs, audited honestly

The same care programs from the homepage board, with the numbers our clinical audit signed off.

Specialist · with NH Heart Network

Hypertension Down 14% Cohort

Challenge. 380 patients with raised blood pressure, a third missing their reviews, and readings taken only when someone happened to be in the building.

Result. Nurse-led recalls, loaned home monitors and a written titration plan for every patient. Twelve months later the cohort’s average systolic pressure was down 14% and the missed-review rate had collapsed.

Family · with Portsmouth schools

Childhood Vaccination Drive 96%

Result. Nine Saturday clinics, consent and reminders run through the school newsletter, and every record reconciled onto the family file. Coverage reached 96% inside two school terms.

Diagnostics · whole-clinic program

Same-Day Labs Rollout

Challenge. Routine results were taking three to five days to come back from an external lab, forcing repeat visits and leaving patients to interpret portal numbers alone.

Result. On-site analysers, 7:30 phlebotomy and a 48-hour phone-call rule. Nine in ten routine panels now result the same day, and every abnormal result is explained by a clinician, not a notification.
