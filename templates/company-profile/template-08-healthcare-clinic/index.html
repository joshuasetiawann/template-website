About Brightwater

A neighbourhood clinic, grown carefully

Brightwater opened in 2009 with two GPs and a shared waiting room above a pharmacy. Patients kept asking for one thing: more time per visit.

So we built the clinic around it — 20-minute standard consultations, clinicians who read your file before you sit down, and follow-up calls that actually happen.

  • 20-minute standard appointments, 40 for new patients
  • Your own named GP, with team cover on their days off
  • Results explained by phone within 48 hours

Patient journey

Your first visit, step by step

  1. 01

    Register online

    Five-minute form; we request your old records with your consent so you never repeat your history.

  2. 02

    Meet your GP

    A 40-minute new-patient consultation — history, baseline checks and your questions.

  3. 03

    Tests same building

    Bloods or imaging happen downstairs, usually within the hour.

  4. 04

    Plan & follow-up

    You leave with a written plan; we call within 48 hours with results explained plainly.
