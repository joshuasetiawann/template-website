Capabilities & case files

What we build, priced and proven.

Every package below is self-performed by IronRidge crews — no subcontract roulette. Read what each scope includes, how the work sequences on site, and what three recent owners actually got, with the schedule and safety numbers pulled straight from the closeout binders.

Services in depth

Six packages, one payroll

The same six scopes from the homepage tabs, unpacked the way our estimators scope them at tender.

Tilt-Up & Steel Buildings

Warehouses, retail shells and office cores from 10,000 to 400,000 sq ft, with panels cast on the floor slab and topped out by our own AISC-certified erection crews. Because the concrete and steel gangs are on our payroll, the structure dates we put in the tender are the dates the crane shows up.

What’s included

  • Site-cast tilt panel engineering and lift design
  • Structural steel supply, erection and certified welding
  • Slab-on-grade poured and laser-screeded to spec flatness
  • Roof deck, membrane and dock equipment coordination
  • Weekly owner look-ahead schedule and crane plan

How the job runs

  1. 1Preconstruction. Budget, logistics and crane placement locked before the permit set is stamped.
  2. 2Structure. Panels cast on the slab, tilted in sequence; steel follows the same week.
  3. 3Dry-in & handover. Envelope closed, trades released, punch list on one page.

Shell & core from $72/sq ft

Interior Fit-Outs

Tenant improvements and conversions phased around live operations — night pours, dust walls and negative-air containment so your staff can clock in the next morning. Most fit-outs run six to fourteen weeks door to door.

What’s included

  • Demolition and structural openings with engineer sign-off
  • Partitions, ceilings, millwork and finish carpentry
  • Mechanical, electrical and data rough-in coordination
  • Phasing plan agreed with building management in writing
  • Closeout binder with as-builts and warranties

How the job runs

  1. 1Scope walk. Existing conditions surveyed; allowances itemised, not buried.
  2. 2Phased build. After-hours work wherever your operations demand it.
  3. 3Punch & handover. Keys back with as-builts, not excuses.

Fit-outs from $48/sq ft

Roads & Earthworks

Bulk excavation, paving and stormwater for municipal and private clients, self-performed with our own 34-machine fleet under GPS grade control. We hold subgrade to tight tolerance so the paving crew never stands around waiting on rework.

What’s included

  • Bulk cut and fill with GPS machine control
  • Asphalt and concrete paving, curb and gutter
  • Storm sewer, detention basins and outfall structures
  • Traffic control plans and utility locates managed for you
  • Compaction and density testing documented per lift

How the job runs

  1. 1Survey & model. Drone topo flown; cut and fill balanced in the model first.
  2. 2Mass works. Excavation and wet utilities trenched in one mobilisation.
  3. 3Pave & close. Surfaces laid, swales seeded, record drawings issued.

Unit-rate schedule itemised at tender

Bridges & Structures

Rehabilitation and short-span replacement — deck overlays, bearing swaps and scour repair — delivered by certified welding crews with lane closures measured in nights, not months. We work to the road authority’s calendar, not against it.

What’s included

  • Condition assessment with a load-rating engineer
  • Deck hydro-demolition and structural overlay
  • Bearing, joint and parapet replacement
  • Certified field welding by our own structural crews
  • Night and weekend closure scheduling with the authority

How the job runs

  1. 1Inspect & rate. Structure scanned; repairs priced from the rating report.
  2. 2Stage & repair. Half-width staging keeps one lane live throughout.
  3. 3Verify & reopen. Documented, signed and reopened ahead of the advisory.

Quoted per structure after inspection

Plant & Process Works

Equipment foundations, pits and heavy slabs poured to millimetre spec inside operating plants. Embed plates are set by optical survey, anchor templates are checked twice against vendor drawings, and shutdown windows are respected to the hour.

What’s included

  • Machine bases with anchor-bolt templates verified twice
  • Pits, trenches and sumps with waterstop detailing
  • Heavy slabs placed to millimetre-spec levelness
  • Embed plates and rails set by optical survey
  • Shutdown-window planning with plant engineering

How the job runs

  1. 1Template & survey. Anchor layouts checked against the vendor set.
  2. 2Pour window. Concrete placed and cured inside the agreed shutdown.
  3. 3Verify & release. Survey report issued before the millwrights arrive.

Machine bases from $9,500 each

Design-Build Delivery

Single-contract delivery with our engineering partners from permit to keys — one number, one schedule, one phone that gets answered. Best suited to owners who know what the building must do before they know what it should look like.

What’s included

  • Concept-to-permit design management
  • Guaranteed maximum price with open-book allowances
  • Permitting and authority liaison handled in-house
  • Long-lead procurement of steel and switchgear at risk
  • Single-point warranty after handover

How the job runs

  1. 1Define. Performance brief and budget agreed in writing.
  2. 2Design & price. Drawings advance with live costing — no surprise at 90%.
  3. 3Build. GMP signed, crews mobilise, owner reported to weekly.

Preconstruction from $24,000
