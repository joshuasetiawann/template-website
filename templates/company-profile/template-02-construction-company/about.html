About IronRidge

Commercial builders who still walk every site at sunrise.

IronRidge Constructions delivers commercial construction and civil works across the Midwest — on schedule, on budget, and built to outlast the warranty. Here is how we got here.

Our story

From a single excavator to a regional builder

IronRidge Constructions started in 1998 with one excavator, a flatbed truck, and a foreman named Earl who refused to leave a job until the punch list was empty. We took on site-prep work nobody else wanted — bad soil, tight access, impossible deadlines — and finished it without drama. Word travels fast on a job site, and within five years we were self-performing concrete, steel and civil works on commercial projects across three states.

We are a self-perform builder by choice. Roughly seventy percent of the trades on an IronRidge project are our own crews on our own payroll, which means we control the schedule instead of chasing subcontractors. When the weather turns or a design changes mid-pour, our superintendents solve it on site that morning rather than filing a change order and waiting.

Commercial construction lives and dies on two numbers: the date and the budget. We protect both with detailed preconstruction, transparent open-book pricing, and a project-controls team that updates the owner weekly — no surprises buried in month-end reports. Our safety record is not a poster in the trailer; it is an EMR below the industry average and a crew that goes home the same shape they arrived.

Over twenty-five years we have poured the foundations for distribution centres, municipal water plants, retail anchors and a fair number of things that are now too boring to photograph because they simply work. We are proud of the boring ones. A loading dock that drains correctly after a decade of freeze-thaw is a better advertisement than any rendering.

IronRidge is still family-run, still self-performing, and still measured by whether the owner would hire us again. They usually do — more than half of our annual revenue comes from clients we have already built for.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Self-perform the hard parts

We own our concrete, steel and earthwork crews so the schedule is ours to keep, not a subcontractor's to break.

Safety is not negotiable

Every crew member is empowered to stop work. An injury-free job site is the only acceptable kind.

Open-book honesty

Owners see real costs every week. We would rather deliver a hard truth early than a surprise at closeout.

Build it once

We detail it right in preconstruction so we pour it right on site. Rework is the most expensive shortcut there is.

The punch list is sacred

Earl's rule still stands: nobody demobilises until the last item is signed off.

Leave the site clean

How a crew treats the neighbours, the mud and the silt fence tells you how they treat the structure.
