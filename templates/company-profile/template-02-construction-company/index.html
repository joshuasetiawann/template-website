About IronRidge

Builders first, paperwork second

IronRidge Constructions started in 2002 with one crane, four tradespeople and a promise: quote honestly, pour on the date we said, and answer the phone after handover.

Today 240 builders, engineers and schedulers deliver everything from tilt-up warehouses to bridge rehabilitations — still on that same promise.

  • Self-performed concrete, steel and earthworks
  • In-house scheduling and BIM coordination
  • Dedicated site-safety officer on every job
