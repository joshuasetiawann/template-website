About Verdant Grid

Engineers first, evangelists second

Verdant Grid was founded in 2013 by grid engineers who believed the energy transition would be won on capacity factors and interconnection queues, not slogans.

A decade later we have 1.2 GW connected across three markets — every megawatt backed by production data we publish to investors quarterly, audited annually.

  • Development, EPC and operations under one roof
  • Community benefit agreements on every site
  • Production guarantees written into contracts

How a project flows

The path from option agreement to operating asset.

  1. Site & interconnect

    Land options, resource studies and a realistic read on the interconnection queue before money moves.

  2. Permit & community

    Permitting run in the open: town halls before filings, benefit agreements signed before steel.

  3. Finance & build

    Bankable engineering packages; our EPC crews build to the model the lenders priced.

  4. Operate & report

    25-year operations with quarterly investor data and annual third-party audits.
