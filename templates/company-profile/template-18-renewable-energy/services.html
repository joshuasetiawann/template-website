Battery Storage (BESS)

Standalone and co-located, revenue-stacked

Revenue-stack modeling from $18,500 per market

Standalone and co-located storage sized by dispatch model, not by brochure. We stack arbitrage, capacity and ancillary revenues per market, then write the augmentation plan into year-one economics so the battery still earns in year twelve.

Scope of work

  • Dispatch simulation across revenue stacks
  • Cell chemistry and integrator selection
  • Fire-safety design and AHJ coordination
  • Augmentation plan priced into the model

Path to power

  1. 1Model. Market simulation with conservative curves.
  2. 2Integrate. Procurement, controls and commissioning.
  3. 3Dispatch. Trading desk live, performance audited.

Cedar Flats Energy Park

Challenge. A 1,400-acre option with strong irradiance but a crowded interconnection queue and a county board burned by a developer who vanished mid-permit. The PPA window closed eleven months after financing.

Result. Queue position secured through a network-upgrade cost-share, town halls held before filings, and EPC crews mobilized the week of notice to proceed. The park energized on schedule with 60 MWh of storage shifting evening peaks.

Halvard Ridge Repowering

Challenge. Nineteen 1990s turbines past design life, a community tired of looking at a half-broken skyline, and grid rights worth keeping only if the repower closed before the interconnection agreement lapsed.

Result. Eight modern units replaced the nineteen, erected on reinforced foundations inside the existing footprint. A community ownership tranche turned hearing-room opponents into dividend recipients, and yield tripled against the old fleet's final years.
