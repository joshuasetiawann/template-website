Disciplines

Libraries, chapels and community halls — public rooms designed for fifty years of changing use.

Site-specific homes built around light studies, with detail drawings down to the door pull.

Offices that borrow the logic of workshops: daylight benches, quiet cores, honest materials.

Why choose us

Working principles

  1. 01 Light before layout

    Sun studies precede floor plans; rooms are placed where their hours are.

  2. 02 Material honesty

    Timber, lime, brick and steel used as themselves — no imitation surfaces.

  3. 03 Drawn, then defended

    We present one scheme we believe in, with reasoning, not three to hedge.

  4. 04 Built within budget

    Cost plans from week two; beauty that cannot be built is just homework.

Method

Eight months, four movements

  1. 01

    Listen & walk

    Weeks one to three: site walks at dawn and dusk, briefings with everyone who will use the building.

  2. 02

    Draw & decide

    One concept, hand-drawn and costed, defended in a single long meeting.

  3. 03

    Detail & tender

    Full detail set down to joinery; contractors price the same drawing, not a guess.

  4. 04

    Build & revisit

    Weekly site presence, then a one-year and five-year revisit — buildings are verbs.
