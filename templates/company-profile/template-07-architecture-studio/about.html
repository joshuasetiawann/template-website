About the atelier

An architecture studio that designs for the climate, the site and the century.

Atelier Northgrid makes buildings that belong — to their landscape, their light and their long future. This is the philosophy behind the practice.

Our story

Architecture rooted in place

Atelier Northgrid was founded in Oslo in 2008 by two architects who had grown frustrated with buildings that could have stood anywhere. They wanted to make architecture that answered specifically to its site — its weather, its daylight, its materials and its people — rather than the same glass box exported to every climate. Seventeen years later, that specificity is still the studio's signature.

We design in the Nordic tradition of restraint: clear structure, honest materials, and daylight treated as a primary building material rather than an afterthought. Every project begins not with a form but with a study of the place — how the sun moves across it through the year, where the wind comes from, what the ground will give and what it will take.

Low energy is not a feature we add; it is a constraint we design within from the first sketch. Orientation, thermal mass, natural ventilation and a tight, well-detailed envelope do most of the work before any equipment is specified. The most sustainable building is the one that barely needs to be heated, and that ambition shapes our plans long before a single panel is chosen.

We work as a small atelier on purpose. Each project is led by a principal from concept to the final site visit, and we take on only as many commissions as we can give that depth of attention. We would rather decline a project than reduce it to a deliverable produced by people who never visited the site.

From private houses to cultural buildings, Atelier Northgrid's work shares a quiet conviction: that good architecture is not loud, that it ages gracefully, and that it earns its place by the way it is lived in for decades after the photographs are taken.

Mission

What we value

The principles behind the work

Begin with the place

Sun, wind, ground and light come before form. The site writes the first brief.

Restraint over spectacle

Clear structure and honest materials outlast whatever is fashionable this decade.

Design out the energy

The greenest building barely needs heating. We solve that in the plan, not the plant room.

Detail is the architecture

A building is only as good as its junctions. We draw them, then we visit to check them.

Principal-led, always

The architect who imagines your building sees it through to the last site visit.

Age gracefully

We design for how a space feels in thirty years, not how it photographs on opening day.

Milestones

Seventeen years of considered work

  1. 2008

    Founded in Oslo

    Two architects start the atelier to make buildings that answer to their site.

  2. 2011

    First passive house

    Completes a certified low-energy house that sets the studio's environmental standard.

  3. 2014

    Cultural commission

    Wins its first public building — a small library that defines the studio's daylight approach.

  4. 2018

    Timber practice

    Develops a mass-timber specialism for low-carbon structure.

  5. 2021

    Design award

    Receives a national award for a coastal house rooted in its exposed landscape.

  6. 2024

    Across Scandinavia

    Now designing houses and cultural buildings across the Nordic region from one atelier.
