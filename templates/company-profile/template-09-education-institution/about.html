About the college

An independent college where character is taught alongside the curriculum.

Alder Gate College prepares young people for university and for life — with small classes, high expectations and a pastoral culture that has shaped students for over sixty years.

Our story

Sixty years of shaping young people

Alder Gate College was founded in 1962 on a conviction that has not aged: that a good education forms character as deliberately as it teaches a syllabus. The founders, a group of teachers who believed large schools were losing sight of the individual pupil, built a college small enough to know every student by name and ambitious enough to send them anywhere.

Six decades later we remain an independent college and sixth form of deliberately modest size. Small classes are not a marketing line but the foundation of how we teach — a class small enough for every voice to be heard, every misunderstanding to be caught, and every spark of curiosity to be followed rather than rushed past.

We hold high academic expectations, and our results reflect them, but we measure success more broadly than a grade sheet. We want students who think independently, argue honestly, and carry themselves with integrity — qualities cultivated through a genuine pastoral culture in which tutors know their students as people, not just as candidates.

Our sixth form is the bridge to university and beyond. We guide students through demanding subjects and the choices that follow them, with the kind of individual attention that turns potential into a confident next step. Many of our alumni stay connected for life, and a remarkable number return to tell younger students what is possible.

Alder Gate today is the same college in spirit as in 1962: small, serious about learning, and certain that the point of education is not only what a young person knows when they leave, but who they have become.

Mission

To educate young people in mind and character alike — sending them on not only with the grades to succeed, but with the integrity and confidence to lead.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Know every student

A college small enough that no young person becomes a number. Pastoral care is the foundation, not an add-on.

Expect more

High standards, kindly held. We believe in students until they believe in themselves.

Form character

Honesty, curiosity and integrity are taught as deliberately as any subject on the timetable.

Teach the individual

Small classes let us follow curiosity and catch confusion. Every voice gets heard.

Think independently

We want students who can argue a case and change their mind — not just recite an answer.

Belong for life

An Alder Gate education is the start of a relationship, not the end of one.

Milestones

Six decades of education

  1. 1962

    College founded

    Teachers establish Alder Gate to keep the individual pupil at the centre of learning.

  2. 1974

    Sixth form established

    Adds a sixth form to bridge students to university with individual guidance.

  3. 1989

    Pastoral system

    Formalises the tutor-based pastoral structure that still defines the college.

  4. 2001

    Sciences expansion

    Opens dedicated science facilities, broadening rigorous subject choice.

  5. 2015

    Bursary programme

    Launches a needs-based bursary fund to widen access to the college.

  6. 2022

    Sixtieth anniversary

    Marks sixty years with alumni returning to mentor the next generation.
