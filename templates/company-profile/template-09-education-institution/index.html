About the college

A century of small classes

Alder Gate was founded in 1924 by the linguist Edith Alder with twelve pupils, four languages on the timetable and a rule that still stands: every pupil is known by name in the staff room.

A century later, 460 pupils learn across our quadrangle campus. The languages have grown to seven; the rule has not changed.

  • 460 pupils, every one known by name
  • Seven languages, three sciences taught to lab standard
  • Bursaries fund 1 in 6 places
