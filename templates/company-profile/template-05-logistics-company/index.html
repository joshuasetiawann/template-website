About MeridianHaul

Twenty-two years on the road

What began in 2004 as two brothers and a leased box truck is now a 14-hub network moving freight for manufacturers, grocers and e-commerce brands across the central corridor.

We grew by being boringly reliable: published on-time rates, dispatchers on real phones, and invoices that match the quote.

  • Asset-based fleet of 220 tractors and 600 trailers
