Services in depth

Every service on the rate card, spelled out mile by mile.

Shippers should not have to book a call to learn what a carrier actually does. Below: all six services with what is included, how a load moves and where rates start — then three customer case studies with the on-time and claims numbers we report every month anyway.

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dry van, reefer and flatbed capacity on 260+ contract lanes, pulled by our own 220 tractors before a single partner truck is called. Drop-trailer pools at your dock end detention arguments, and the rate you sign is the rate you are invoiced.

Dry van from $2.05 per mile, contract lanes

What's included

  • Firm lane rate in two business hours — never an estimate
  • Drop-trailer pools sized to your weekly volume
  • 30-second portal pings plus a named dispatcher on the phone
  • Cargo cover to 2M USD and a published claims scoreboard
  • Photo proof-of-delivery attached to one matching invoice

How a load moves

  1. Quote. Lane, weight, dates in — firm rate back within two hours.
  2. Dispatch. Tractor booked, dock windows confirmed, driver card shared.
  3. Deliver. Tracked door to door; POD and invoice land together.

Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)

Daily consolidation runs between our 14 cross-dock hubs mean your pallets ride scheduled linehaul, not a marketplace lottery. Regional freight delivers next day; everything is scanned at every dock touch so the portal always knows which building your pallet is in.

From $98 per pallet, hub to hub

What's included

  • Daily departure schedule published for all 14 hubs
  • Pallet-level scans at pickup, every dock touch and delivery
  • Next-day delivery inside the regional hub network
  • Liftgate, residential and appointment options priced upfront

How a load moves

  1. Book. Pallet count and class in the portal; rate confirms instantly.
  2. Consolidate. Pallets join the nightly linehaul at the nearest hub.
  3. Final run. Local truck delivers with scan-verified POD.

Warehousing & 3PL

410,000 sq ft of racked, food-grade space across three buildings, with your inventory visible in our WMS from the minute it hits the dock. Cycle counts run on schedule, lot and date codes are tracked, and re-slotting happens before pick rates sag — not after.

Storage from $14.50 per pallet per month

What's included

  • Food-grade GDP racked storage with lot and date-code control
  • WMS portal access with live inventory and order status
  • Scheduled cycle counts with 99.5% inventory accuracy target
  • Pick, pack and kitting with same-day cutoff at 14:00
  • Quarterly slotting review tied to your sales velocity

How a load moves

  1. Receive. ASN matched, pallets scanned to location within four hours.
  2. Store & count. Cycle counts and exceptions reported weekly.
  3. Ship. Orders wave-picked and out on our own linehaul.

Last-Mile Delivery

Two-person home delivery teams trained for thresholds, stairwells and nervous dogs. Customers pick a 2-hour window by text, drivers photograph the placement, and damage claims are settled inside ten days because the evidence is already in the file.

From $39 per two-person stop

What's included

  • 2-hour delivery windows chosen by the customer by text
  • Room-of-choice placement, unboxing and debris removal options
  • Photo proof at the door plus signature capture
  • Damage claims acknowledged in 24 hours, settled inside ten days

How a load moves

  1. Stage. Goods cross-dock at the nearest hub the night before.
  2. Notify. Customer confirms the 2-hour window by text.
  3. Place. Two-person team delivers, photographs and closes the stop.

Intermodal & Drayage

Port and rail drayage with our own chassis pool, so your container is not waiting on equipment that never comes. Customs partners pre-clear paperwork, ramps are monitored around the clock, and per-diem clocks are managed like the cost center they are.

Quoted per container — firm drayage rate in 2 hours

What's included

  • Owned chassis pool at the three ramps we run daily
  • Last-free-day tracking with demurrage alerts before fees start
  • Customs broker coordination and document handling
  • Transload to dry van or directly into our 3PL buildings

How a load moves

  1. Pre-clear. Documents and customs status confirmed before vessel arrival.
  2. Pull. Container drayed on our chassis inside the free window.
  3. Connect. Transload or linehaul booked on the same invoice.

Freight Analytics

Lane scorecards, CO2 reporting and quarterly network reviews built from your actual shipment data — the same numbers our dispatchers are graded on. We will show you the lanes to consolidate, the dwell that is costing you and the mode shifts worth making.

Included on contract lanes; standalone reviews from $850 per quarter

What's included

  • Monthly lane scorecard: on-time, dwell, claims and cost per mile
  • CO2-per-shipment reporting aligned to SmartWay factors
  • Quarterly network review with consolidation recommendations
  • EDI and API feeds into your ERP or TMS at no extra charge

How a load moves

  1. Connect. Shipment feed wired to your systems in week one.
  2. Score. First lane scorecard lands with the next monthly invoice.
  3. Review. Quarterly sit-down: what to consolidate, shift or drop.

Greengrocer Co

Regional grocery · refrigerated FTL

Challenge. Cold-chain OTIF sat at 91% across 40 stores, and retailer chargebacks for missed reefer windows were eating the produce margin.

Result. Six dedicated reefer lanes with drop trailers at both DCs, temperature telemetry on the same 30-second pings and a dispatcher who knows the receiving manager by name.

Nordica Appliances

Appliance brand · last-mile delivery

Challenge. One in nine home deliveries ended in a damage claim or a missed window, and the brand's review score was paying for it.

Result. Two-person teams trained on appliance handling, customer-picked 2-hour windows and photo proof at the door — staged nightly through our Kansas City hub.

EV components · intermodal & drayage
