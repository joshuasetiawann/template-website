Our story

Born backstage at a failing gala

In 2016, producers Yara Velvet and Theo Comet met backstage at a charity gala collapsing in real time — no run sheet, no spare mics, a keynote stuck at the airport. They fixed it in gaffer tape and adrenaline.

Velvet Comet exists so no client ever has that night. Forty producers, riggers and show-callers now run 40+ events a year from one obsessive cue sheet.

  • One producer owns your event from brief to strike
  • Backup plans rehearsed, not just written
  • Budgets tracked live and shared weekly
