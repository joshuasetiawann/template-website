Conferences & Summits

Full production for rooms of 200 to 3,000: stage and set, broadcast-grade audio and vision, speaker care and live captioning. One producer owns your event from brief to strike, and every department — venue, AV, catering, security — runs off the same cue sheet.

In the package

  • Stage, set and LED design built to your brand kit
  • Speaker care: rehearsals, comfort monitors, a calm green room
  • Live captioning and hearing-loop accessibility as standard
  • Show-calling from one master cue sheet, rehearsed twice
  • Live budget dashboard shared with you weekly

How the build runs

  1. 1Brief & budget. Itemised proposal inside ten days — every cable a line.
  2. 2Pre-production. Cue sheet v1, speaker rehearsals, contingency drills.
  3. 3Show week. Load-in 48 hours before doors; strike before you wake.

Product Launches

A launch is one moment with an event built around it. We engineer the reveal to the second — lighting, audio, screens and confetti on a single clock — and drill the countdown until the only surprise left in the room is the product.

In the package

  • Reveal mechanics designed, built and rehearsed to the second
  • Press pit with riser, clean feeds and a mult-box
  • Countdown, lighting and audio locked to one show clock
  • Social capture crew cutting clips the same night

How the build runs

  1. 1The moment. We agree the exact second that matters and work backwards.
  2. 2Drill it. Three full countdown rehearsals, failures injected on purpose.
  3. 3Reveal. On the clock, in the pit, on every feed at once.

production from 18k USD

Festivals & Open-Airs

Multi-stage builds on real ground with real weather: power, permits, crowd flow and noise curfews handled before the first truss goes up. Omar's permit file opens a year out, and every outdoor quote ships with weather plans B through D and the trigger times to call them.

In the package

  • Multi-stage staging, site power and distribution plan
  • Permits, licensing and noise-curfew management end to end
  • Weather plans B–D with written trigger times
  • Crowd flow, barriers and accessible viewing platforms
  • Vendor village layout, power and waste plan

How the build runs

  1. 1Site & city. We walk the ground and open the permit file early.
  2. 2The village. Stages, power, vendors and sight lines on one site map.
  3. 3Show weekend. Show-called from a tower with eyes on every gate.

production from 60k USD
