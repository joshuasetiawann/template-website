About Velvet Comet

An event production studio that sweats the details you'll never see.

Velvet Comet Events produces experiences — launches, galas, festivals and brand moments — where the magic on the night rests on the logistics behind it. Here's who we are.

Our story

Magic is just invisible logistics

Velvet Comet Events was founded in 2014 by two producers who had spent years backstage, watching beautiful events nearly collapse because someone treated logistics as an afterthought. They knew a secret the audience never sees: the magic of a great event is entirely built on the boring, relentless detail behind it — the timing, the contingency, the cable nobody trips over. Velvet Comet was built to obsess over exactly that.

We produce experiences across the spectrum — product launches, galas, conferences, festivals and the kind of brand moments people photograph and remember. Whatever the scale, our job is the same: to make something feel effortless and spontaneous on the night by planning it with military precision in the months before. Spontaneity, ironically, takes the most rehearsal.

Production is our craft, not just our service. We manage the run of show to the minute, build a contingency for every moving part, and coordinate the dozens of suppliers, performers and crew so that on the day they move like one team. When something goes wrong — and at a live event, something always does — the audience never knows, because we planned for it before it happened.

We believe an event is an emotional experience, not a schedule of components. A launch should make people feel something; a gala should give them a night they retell; a festival should feel alive rather than administered. We design for that feeling first, then engineer every invisible detail in service of it. The spreadsheet exists so the wonder can.

A decade on, Velvet Comet has produced hundreds of events for brands, institutions and private clients who wanted a night that ran flawlessly and felt unforgettable. We are still obsessive about the details you'll never notice — because those are precisely the ones that make the ones you do notice possible.
