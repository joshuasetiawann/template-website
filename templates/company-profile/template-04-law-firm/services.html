Practice depth & proof

Six practices, examined properly.

The homepage states what we do; this page shows how a mandate actually runs. Each practice below sets out scope, sequence and fee terms — followed by three matter briefs with the numbers our clients permitted us to print.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Buy-side and sell-side counsel from the first term sheet to the last integration covenant. We staff lean — one partner, two associates — and we negotiate the indemnity that matters rather than all forty that do not. Cross-border structures, regulatory clearances and management rollover terms are handled in-house, not referred out.

Fixed-fee scoping at engagement — terms in writing before diligence opens

What the mandate includes

  • Term sheet and exclusivity strategy
  • Due diligence and disclosure schedules
  • Antitrust and foreign-investment filings
  • SPA drafting and negotiation
  • Management rollover and incentive terms

How the matter runs

  1. 1Conflicts & scoping. Cleared within 24 hours; fee letter follows.
  2. 2Diligence & drafting. Weekly issues list, never a surprise memo.
  3. 3Signing to close. Conditions tracked daily through completion.

Commercial Disputes

Arbitration and litigation built for leverage before filing. Most disputes we take end without a public hearing, because the early case assessment told both sides what a tribunal would. When trial is the right answer, we have stood up in court for fifty-seven years.

Staged budgets — each phase approved in writing before work begins

What the mandate includes

  • Early case assessment memo with settlement range
  • Pre-action strategy and protected correspondence
  • ICC and LCIA arbitration advocacy
  • Interim relief and asset preservation
  • Award enforcement across jurisdictions

How the matter runs

  1. 1Assessment. Ten-day memo: merits, exposure, leverage points.
  2. 2Pressure. Negotiation from a filed-and-ready position.
  3. 3Resolution. Settlement executed — or the hearing we prepared for.

Capital & Funds

Fund formation, LP negotiations and secondaries documentation for managers from first close to fund four. We have sat on both sides of the LPA, which is why our side letters are short and our closings are quiet.

Capped fee per close — agreed at term sheet, reconciled at final close

What the mandate includes

  • Fund structuring and LPA drafting
  • LP side-letter negotiation and MFN elections
  • Carry, GP commitment and economics advice
  • Secondaries and continuation vehicles

How the matter runs

  1. 1Structure. Vehicle, domicile and tax memo inside two weeks.
  2. 2Negotiate. Anchor LPs first; the rest follow the precedent.
  3. 3Close. Signature packets, capital call mechanics, done.

Governance & Investigations

Board advisory, internal investigations and regulator response, conducted under privilege and without theatre. When the call comes at seven before a board vote, a partner answers — that is the practice, not the slogan.

Quoted per mandate after a 24-hour conflicts check

What the mandate includes

  • Board and special-committee advisory
  • Privileged internal investigation protocol
  • Document holds and witness interviews
  • Regulator response and remediation plan
  • Whistleblower procedure design

How the matter runs

  1. 1Contain. Privilege established, holds issued on day one.
  2. 2Investigate. Facts before narrative; findings tested twice.
  3. 3Resolve. Board report, regulator file, remediation calendar.

Private Client & Estates

Succession, trusts and family-office structures across jurisdictions, drafted to survive both the tax authority and the family dinner. Discretion is the entire product: no client lists, no announcements.

Fixed plan fee or annual retainer — set at engagement

What the mandate includes

  • Estate and succession planning instruments
  • Trust formation and trustee advisory
  • Family-office and holding structures
  • Cross-border probate coordination

How the matter runs

  1. 1Map. Assets, family and intent set out on one page.
  2. 2Draft. Structures explained in plain language first.
  3. 3Maintain. Annual review; amendments within the retainer.

Executive contracts, separations and restrictive covenant disputes handled with discretion on both sides of the table. We negotiate the exit nobody reads about and litigate the covenant everybody watches — usually the first.

Fixed fee per contract suite; disputes on staged budgets

Matter briefs

Three results, told properly

The same three matters summarised on the homepage — here with the challenge, the result and the figures we may print.

M&A

Industrial group carve-out

For a European industrial holding — sell side, three jurisdictions

Challenge. A family-held group needed to sell its components division across three jurisdictions, with shared IT, intertwined pension liabilities and two antitrust regimes — on a timetable set by the buyer's fund life, not by comfort.

Result. We ran the carve-out and the auction in parallel: separation agreements drafted alongside diligence, antitrust cleared in two first-phase filings, and the pension split agreed with trustees before signing. Completion arrived eleven weeks after signature.

Disputes

Shareholder deadlock resolved

For one of two founding families — manufacturing holding company

Challenge. Two founding families held the board in deadlock for fourteen months; dividends froze, a key plant investment stalled, and a public unfair-prejudice trial would have priced the company in open court.

Result. Our early case assessment priced both downside scenarios, which became the negotiating table. A structured buyout in three tranches — funded against future dividends — settled the matter nine months after filing, with no public hearing and the plant investment approved that quarter.

Funds

Growth fund III closed

For a technology growth manager — third-generation fund

Challenge. An oversubscribed technology growth fund faced forty-plus LP mark-ups of the LPA, conflicting MFN demands and a hard regulatory deadline in one anchor investor's home jurisdiction.

Result. We negotiated anchors first and let the precedent carry the book: side letters held to a four-page standard, MFN elections reconciled in a single schedule, and the fund reached final close above target with every LP on identical core economics.
