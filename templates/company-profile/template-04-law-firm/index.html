The firm

Fifty-seven years of quiet results

Founded in 1969 by Eleanor Hartwell and Samuel Crane, the firm built its name on matters other firms called unwinnable — and on never confusing publicity with outcome.

Today thirty-eight lawyers serve founders, funds and family offices from one office, by design: one conflicts desk, one standard, one phone number that reaches a partner.

  • Partner-led on every matter, no leverage pyramids
  • Fixed and capped fees on defined mandates
  • Conflicts cleared within 24 hours

How we practice

Four commitments, kept since 1969.

  1. A partner answers

    Every matter is led hands-on by a named partner; associates support, never substitute.

  2. Prepared before retained

    We read the file before the first meeting — you pay for judgment, not orientation.

  3. Fees without theatre

    Defined mandates carry fixed or capped fees, agreed in writing before work begins.

  4. Discretion as default

    No client lists, no deal toys, no press releases unless you ask for one.
