About the firm

A corporate and disputes firm built on judgment, not volume.

Hartwell & Crane LLP advises founders, boards and institutions through the decisions that define them. We measure our work by outcomes, not billable hours.

Our story

A practice founded on a simple principle

Hartwell & Crane LLP was founded in 1987 by two litigators who left a large firm over a disagreement about how law should be practised. They believed clients were being sold hours rather than judgment, and that the partner who won the work too often vanished once the associates took over. The firm they built was a correction to both.

We are, by design, a partner-led firm. The lawyer who advises you in the first meeting is the lawyer who argues your case and signs your agreement. We keep our ranks deliberately senior, take on fewer matters than our size would allow, and decline work where we cannot add genuine judgment rather than mere capacity.

Our practice sits at the intersection of corporate counsel and commercial disputes — the two moments a business most needs a steady hand. We structure the transaction and, when it is necessary, we litigate the consequences. Sitting on both sides of that line for nearly four decades has taught us to draft agreements that survive the courtroom, because we are the ones who will be standing in it.

We are candid to a fault. A client who needs to hear that a claim is weak, or that a deal is a mistake, will hear it from us early and plainly. We have lost short-term fees by talking clients out of litigation; we have kept clients for decades for exactly the same reason.

Thirty-eight years on, Hartwell & Crane remains a single-office partnership advising founders, boards and institutions through their most consequential decisions. Our reputation is the only marketing we have ever needed, and we guard it accordingly.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Judgment over volume

We take fewer matters so the ones we take get a partner's full attention.

Candour first

If your case is weak or your deal is wrong, you'll hear it from us early and plainly.

Partner-led, always

The lawyer who wins your trust is the lawyer who does the work — not a name on the letterhead.

Draft for the courtroom

Because we litigate, we write agreements built to survive a dispute, not just to close one.

Discretion as a duty

Our clients' most sensitive decisions stay between us. Confidentiality is not a clause; it is the practice.

Earn the relationship

We keep clients for decades by being right when it matters, not by being available when it doesn't.
