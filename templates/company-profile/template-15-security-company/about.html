About Sentinel Axis

A cybersecurity team that assumes the breach and plans for it.

Sentinel Axis runs cybersecurity and risk operations for organisations that can't afford to be wrong — detection, response and resilience built by operators, not box-tickers. Here's how we think.

Our story

Built by operators, for the worst day

Sentinel Axis was founded in 2013 by a group of incident responders who had spent their careers cleaning up other people's breaches. They had watched too many organisations buy a wall of security products and assume they were safe, only to discover during an attack that nobody was actually watching the alerts. Sentinel was built on the opposite assumption: that a determined attacker will get in, and the only question that matters is how fast you detect, contain and recover.

We are an operations company, not a software reseller. Our work is the unglamorous, around-the-clock discipline of monitoring, threat hunting and incident response — the parts of security that only matter when they are done relentlessly. We measure ourselves on detection time and response time, because those are the numbers that decide whether an intrusion becomes an incident or a catastrophe.

Assume breach is more than a slogan for us; it is the design principle behind every engagement. We help organisations build layered defences, then we test them like an adversary would — red-teaming our own clients, rehearsing the incident before it happens, and making sure the response plan is a practised reflex rather than a document nobody has read.

Trust in this field is earned through discretion and competence, never through marketing. Our clients operate in finance, critical infrastructure and government-adjacent sectors where a leak is as damaging as the breach itself. We say very little about who we protect, and that silence is itself part of the service.

Over a decade on, Sentinel Axis runs a 24/7 security operations centre and a battle-tested response practice for organisations that treat security as survival, not compliance. The threats have evolved relentlessly, and so have we — but the founding belief holds: hope is not a control, and the breach you plan for is the one you survive.
