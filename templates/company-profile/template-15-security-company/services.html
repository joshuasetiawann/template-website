Scoped offensive engagements that end in proof-of-concept chains, screenshots and reproduction steps — never a re-skinned scanner export. Findings are ranked by exploitability, retested free after you fix them, and explained to engineering in their own language.

Scoped engagements from $14,000 — fixed price per scope

What ships

Protocol

Ransomware contained at patient zero

Response. The watch floor isolated the host at minute eleven, revoked the session chain, and swept for persistence while the company slept. Monday's shift change was the first time most staff heard about it — from a closed-incident report.
