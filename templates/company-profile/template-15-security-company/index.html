Why choose us

Engagement protocol

Every engagement runs the same four-phase protocol — rehearsed, timed, audited.

  1. Phase 01 — Recon & baseline

    Two weeks mapping your attack surface, identities and crown-jewel data flows before anyone changes anything.

  2. Phase 02 — Harden & instrument

    Quick-win hardening plus sensor deployment; detection rules tuned to your stack, not a generic feed.

  3. Phase 03 — Watch & hunt

    SOC goes live with weekly threat hunts; every alert resolves to a ticket, never a shrug.

  4. Phase 04 — Test & report

    Quarterly red-team probes score the blue team; the board gets one page, engineering gets the chains.
