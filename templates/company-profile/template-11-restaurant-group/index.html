The famiglia story

From one stove to three dining rooms

Rosa and Enzo Fiorelli opened a twelve-seat trattoria in 1987 with one stove, borrowed chairs and Rosa’s ragù — the recipe her grandmother smuggled out of Bologna in a hatband.

Their three children now run three rooms within four miles of that first doorway. The chairs are paid for. The ragù is unchanged. The argument about basil continues.

  • Pasta rolled fresh at noon, gone by ten
  • Produce from four farms inside the county line
  • Every manager is a Fiorelli — by blood or by twenty years

From farm to forno

A day in the kitchen

  1. 01

    6:00 — fire & flour

    The oven is lit and the first dough of the day is shaped while the city sleeps.

  2. 02

    9:30 — the farm run

    Marco drives the county loop: tomatoes, greens and whatever made the farmers proud.

  3. 03

    12:00 — pasta bench

    Three hundred portions rolled, cut and dusted by hand. When they are gone, they are gone.

  4. 04

    17:00 — doors open

    Family meal first, then yours — service runs until the last table laughs itself out.
