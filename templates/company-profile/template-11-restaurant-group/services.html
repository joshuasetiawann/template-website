La carta completa

Six ways to eat with us, told properly

Every room, the truck and both ways we cook at your place — opened up like a menu, with what arrives at the table, how booking works and the price before you ask. Further down: three stories from our dining rooms, with the covers counted.

Trattoria Fiorelli

The 1987 original on Mulberry Row: forty-two seats, the wood oven Rosa lit on opening day, and the ragù that has outlasted three mayors. Pasta is rolled at noon for that evening only — when the bench is empty, the kitchen says so honestly.

On the table

  • Hand-rolled pasta made at noon, served that night
  • Rosa's ragù, unchanged since the hatband recipe
  • Wood-fired bread baked through the afternoon
  • The one-page wine list — a third under 40 dollars
  • Sunday pranzo: four courses, one long table, twenty seats

How a table happens

  1. 1Book. Call or write the host desk; parties of 6+ get a call back.
  2. 2Sit. Bread and olives land before the menu does.
  3. 3Stay. Service runs until the last table laughs itself out.

mains from 16 USD · Sunday pranzo 48 USD/guest

Fiorelli Forno

Lucia's bakery-pizzeria two streets over, built around an oven that hits 480 degrees by opening. Neapolitan pies blister in ninety seconds; the porchetta turns all Sunday morning and sells out by two. Counter seats watch the dough get slapped.

On the table

  • 90-second Neapolitan pies from a 48-hour dough
  • Sunday porchetta until it runs out — it always runs out
  • Morning bread and biscotti for the whole group
  • Whole-pie takeaway boxed to survive the walk home

How a table happens

  1. 1Walk in. No bookings at the Forno — the line moves fast.
  2. 2Order at the counter. Watch your pie hit the stone floor of the oven.
  3. 3Ninety seconds. Blistered, lifted, at your table before the drinks.

pizzas from 13 USD · whole porchetta plates 17 USD

Terrazza by Fiorelli

Gianni's seventy-seat rooftop above the old cannery, open May through October. Aperitivo starts at five, coastal plates follow, and the sun does the decorating. Rain plan: the glass pergola closes in four minutes — we have timed it.

On the table

  • Aperitivo hour with spritzes and cicchetti from five
  • Coastal plates: crudo, grilled fish, lemon pastas
  • Golden-hour tables bookable by the half hour
  • Glass pergola and heaters for the shoulder months
  • Whole-rooftop buyouts for up to 90 standing

How a table happens

  1. 1Check the season. May to October, Tuesday through Sunday.
  2. 2Pick your hour. Aperitivo, golden hour or late — say which.
  3. 3Watch the sky. If weather turns, the pergola closes, dinner does not.

plates from 11 USD · rooftop buyouts from 38 USD/guest

The Polpetta Truck

The red truck follows Marco's Saturday farm loop and parks where the neighbourhood already is — markets, ballparks, street fairs. Meatball sandwiches and arancini, fried to order, from whatever the farms handed over that morning.

From the window

  • Polpetta sandwiches on Forno bread, four to a roll
  • Arancini fried to order — ragù or saffron centres
  • Saturday market schedule posted at the host desk weekly
  • Private hire for block parties and ballgames, 3-hour minimum

How the truck books

  1. 1Find it or book it. Chase the schedule, or bring the truck to you.
  2. 2Pick the menu. Two sandwiches and one arancino travel best.
  3. 3We park early. Window opens on the hour, rain or shine.

from 9 USD · private hire from 650 USD per 3 hours

Private Famiglia Feasts

One long table, twelve to sixty people, in the brick cellar under the Trattoria or out in your backyard. Family-style platters land in waves; a Fiorelli stays in the room the whole night. Built for birthdays, weddings-adjacent dinners and teams that need to become people again.

In the feast

  • Four family-style courses, porchetta or ragù centrepiece
  • The cellar room — brick, candles, one table, no neighbours
  • Backyard version: we bring table, linens and the kitchen
  • Wine pairings from the one-page list, poured generously
  • A Fiorelli hosting in the room, start to finish

How a feast happens

  1. 1Talk. Date, headcount, occasion — and any family arguments to seat apart.
  2. 2Taste. You and one guest come eat the proposed menu first.
  3. 3Feast. Courses in waves; nobody checks a phone after the second.

from 55 USD/guest · cellar room hire included

Catering & Office Lunch

Trays of lasagne, salads and tiramisù delivered hot with setup, serving spoons and labels for every allergy at the table. Weekly office runs get a standing menu and a standing discount; one-off occasions get Rosa's checklist call two days before.

In the delivery

  • Hot trays for 8 to 200, delivered and set up
  • Every tray labelled — allergens, vegan, gluten-free
  • Tiramisù portioned so nobody has to do the math
  • Same-day pickup of trays and stands, no chasing

How catering happens

  1. 1Count heads. Tell us the number and the room; we size the trays.
  2. 2Confirm. Rosa's checklist call — allergies, timing, elevator codes.
  3. 3Deliver hot. Set up twenty minutes before your people arrive.

from 14 USD/head · weekly office runs from 12 USD/head
