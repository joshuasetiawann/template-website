About the family

Three generations, one table, and a sauce that never changed.

Casa Fiorelli Group runs a small family of restaurants built on recipes carried from Italy and a belief that hospitality is the whole point. Here is our story.

Our story

From one trattoria to a family of tables

Casa Fiorelli began in 1979 when Nonna Lucia and her husband Aldo opened a twelve-table trattoria with the recipes she had carried from her village outside Bologna. They had no business plan and no investors — only a sauce that took all morning, a dining room they treated like their own home, and a conviction that nobody should leave hungry or unwelcome.

Forty-five years later we are a small group of restaurants, but we are still, stubbornly, a family business. The second generation runs the kitchens and the third now waits the tables and learns the recipes, exactly as it should be. We have grown only by opening rooms we could pour ourselves into — never by franchising a name or cutting a corner the family would notice.

The food is honest. We make the pasta, we let the sauce take its time, and we buy ingredients we would serve our own children because, most nights, we do. Nonna Lucia's ragù recipe has not changed in forty-five years, and it never will — the day we shorten it to save an hour is the day we stop being Casa Fiorelli.

But the food is only half of it. Hospitality is the whole point of a restaurant, and we measure ourselves on whether a guest felt like family from the door to the goodbye. We remember regulars' tables and their grandchildren's names. We celebrate your anniversaries and feed you when you are heartbroken. That is the job.

Today Casa Fiorelli welcomes families who have eaten with us across three generations of their own. The menus have grown and the dining rooms have multiplied, but the table is the same one Lucia set in 1979 — and everyone is still welcome at it.
