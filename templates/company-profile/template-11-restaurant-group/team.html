Leadership

The Fiorelli family

In the kitchen and on the floor — the people who make every guest feel at home.

LF

Lucia Fiorelli

Nonna & Founder

Opened the first trattoria in 1979. Nonna Lucia still guards the ragù recipe and inspects the Sunday sauce.

MF

Marco Fiorelli

Executive Chef

Lucia's son runs the kitchens. Marco protects every recipe while letting the menu breathe with the seasons.

SF

Sofia Fiorelli

Group Host & Director

Lucia's daughter leads the floor. Sofia remembers regulars' tables — and their grandchildren's names.

GF

Giulia Fiorelli

Pastry Chef

The third generation. Giulia learned dolci at Nonna's elbow and now ends every meal on a sweet note.

AR

Antonio Russo

Head Chef, Osteria

Family by kitchen, not blood. Antonio has cooked the casual room's classics for fifteen years.

EC

Elena Conti

Sommelier & Floor Manager

Pairs the cellar to the table. Elena makes sure every bottle suits the dish and the occasion.
