About the studio

An interior design studio that designs how a room makes you feel.

Maison Ochre & Fen creates considered, liveable interiors — rooms with soul, built around the people who live in them. Here is the studio's philosophy.

Our story

Rooms with soul, built to be lived in

Maison Ochre & Fen was founded in 2007 by two designers who shared a quiet rebellion against interiors that looked perfect and felt like nobody lived there. They had seen too many beautiful rooms that were really photographs waiting to happen — styled, immaculate and joyless. Their studio was built on the opposite conviction: that the measure of an interior is not how it photographs, but how it feels to live in on an ordinary Tuesday.

We design considered, liveable interiors for private homes and the occasional hospitality project. Our work begins not with a mood board but with the people — how they actually use a space, where the morning light falls, what they reach for, what makes them feel at home. The design serves the life, not the other way around. A room that fights its inhabitants has failed, however lovely it looks.

Our aesthetic is warm, layered and tactile — the ochres and earthy greens that give the studio its name are no accident. We favour natural materials that age well and gather character, honest textures you want to touch, and a layering of old and new that gives a room depth and history rather than the flat newness of a showroom. We design rooms that will be more beautiful in ten years, not less.

We work closely and slowly, taking on a limited number of projects so each receives genuine attention. We are in the rooms, sourcing the pieces, refining the details — because the soul of an interior lives in the specifics: the patina of a chosen material, the exact warmth of the light, the imperfect object that makes a space feel real. Those decisions cannot be delegated to a template.

Nearly two decades on, Maison Ochre & Fen creates interiors for clients who want a home rather than a showroom — spaces that hold a life, age gracefully, and feel unmistakably theirs. We remain small, warm and devoted to the difference between a room that impresses and a room that embraces.

The principles behind the work

Feeling over photographs

A room is judged by how it feels on an ordinary day, not how it looks in a magazine.

The life comes first

We design around how people actually live, where the light falls, what they reach for.

Materials that age well

Natural, honest, tactile. We choose pieces that gather character rather than wear out.

Layer old with new

Depth and history make a room feel real. Flat newness belongs in a showroom.

Soul is in the specifics

The patina, the warmth of the light, the imperfect object — the details no template can choose.

Work slowly, fully

We take few projects so each gets our hands, our eyes and our genuine attention.
