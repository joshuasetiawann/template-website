Philosophy

Notes on inevitability

A good room answers the question before it is asked: where do I sit, where does the light land, where does the day end.

We design with the house, not against it — period bones respected, additions confessed honestly.

Beauty that photographs well but lives poorly is scenery. We make rooms.

Why choose us

The studio manner

  1. 01 Listen to the house

    Survey first, moodboard second — period bones and sightlines set the brief.

  2. 02 Materials with memory

    Lime plaster, aged oak, undyed wool: surfaces that improve with fingerprints.

  3. 03 One palette, deep

    Olive, ochre, clay and bone — depth comes from texture, not from more colours.

  4. 04 Budget candour

    Costed scheme at concept stage; we redesign on paper, never on invoices.
