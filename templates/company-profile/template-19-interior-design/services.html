Services & projects

The studio, set out
in full.

A scheme is easier to trust when you can see what it includes and how it is made. Below is each service the studio offers, written out properly — the deliverables, the way a commission actually runs, and an honest word on fees. Further down: three finished houses, and what they were worth to the people living in them.

The whole offer

Six services, unpacked

The same six lines as the homepage list, opened up. Fees are scoped at concept stage and agreed on paper — we redesign on drawings, never on invoices.

01

Full Residential Design

scoped from £45k

Whole-home design from spatial planning to the last switch plate, with the studio on site weekly through works. We design with the house, not against it: period bones respected, additions confessed honestly, and the day-to-day choreography — where you sit, where light lands, where the day ends — settled before a wall moves.

What we deliver

  • Spatial plan and sightline study, room by room
  • Full material, lighting and joinery schedule
  • Costed scheme at concept, redesigned on paper if needed
  • Weekly site visits and contractor coordination through works
  • Final styling and art placement on completion

How it runs

  1. 1

    Survey first — period bones and sightlines set the brief.

  2. 2

    Costed scheme — concept, materials and a number you can live with.

  3. 3

    Works and styling — weekly on site until the room is right.

02

Hospitality & Boutique Hotels

scoped per key

Guest rooms and public spaces with operational sense — beauty that survives housekeeping and a full season. We design the lobby for the photograph and the back-of-house for the staff, because a hotel that only works on opening night is scenery, not a business.

What we deliver

  • Guest-room prototype built and pressure-tested first
  • Public-room and lobby design briefed for operations
  • FF&E specification with contract-grade durability
  • Roll-out documentation so every room repeats cleanly

How it runs

  1. 1

    One room first — prototype, test, cost it honestly.

  2. 2

    Public spaces — the rooms guests judge you on in ten seconds.

  3. 3

    Roll out — documented so housekeeping never fights the design.

03

Kitchens & Bathrooms

scoped from £18k

The two hardest rooms, detailed to joinery level with our cabinet-makers. Plumbing runs and appliance logic are drawn before anything is chosen for looks, so the beautiful kitchen is also the one that works at seven in the morning with everybody in it.

What we deliver

  • Cabinetry drawn to joinery level with our makers
  • Appliance, plumbing and lighting logic resolved up front
  • Stone, tile and brassware sampled in your own light
  • Install supervision so the details survive the fit-out

How it runs

  1. 1

    Function first — runs, reach and the morning rush drawn out.

  2. 2

    Samples in situ — stone and tile judged in your own light.

  3. 3

    Make and fit — our cabinet-makers build, we supervise the install.

04

Furniture & Joinery Commissions

from £2,400 per piece

One-off pieces drawn by us and built by sixteen partner workshops in oak, lime-plaster and bronze. When the right thing does not exist, we draw it: a reading stair, a plaster console, a bed that fits the only wall it can. Materials are chosen to improve with fingerprints.

What we deliver

  • Bespoke design drawings and a material sample set
  • Made by one of sixteen vetted partner workshops
  • Oak, lime plaster, undyed wool and bronze, honestly aged
  • White-glove delivery and placement in the room

How it runs

  1. 1

    Draw the gap — the piece the room needs and cannot buy.

  2. 2

    Choose the hands — the workshop matched to the material.

  3. 3

    Build and place — delivered, set and adjusted in the room.

05

Colour & Material Consultations

half-day from £750

Half-day studies that rescue rooms from expensive almost-right choices. One palette, deep: olive, ochre, clay and bone, with depth coming from texture rather than from more colours. You leave with a scheme on paper and the confidence to stop second-guessing the swatches.

What we deliver

  • A half-day in the room, light and orientation read first
  • A tight palette — paint, wood and textile, named and sourced
  • Large samples to live with before anything is bought
  • A written scheme sheet you can hand a decorator

How it runs

  1. 1

    Read the light — aspect and hours decide more than taste.

  2. 2

    Set the palette — one family, deep, texture doing the work.

  3. 3

    Leave it on paper — samples and a sheet, no almost-right spends.

06

Styling & Art Placement

from £1,200 per room

Final-layer styling, art sourcing and the restraint to stop one object early. This is the difference between a finished build and a room that feels lived in: the books that belong, the bowl that earns its shelf, the painting hung where the eye already wanted to rest.

What we deliver

  • Art sourced from our gallery and maker contacts
  • Object and book styling using what you already own
  • Hanging and lighting of art, done on site by us
  • A restraint pass — we remove as much as we add

How it runs

  1. 1

    Inventory — what you own that already belongs here.

  2. 2

    Source the gaps — art and objects, never filler.

  3. 3

    Edit on site — place, hang, light, then take one thing away.

Finished rooms

Three houses, and what they were worth

The same projects from the portfolio, told as the clients lived them — what refused to work, what we did, and the result, with no staging.

I

The Fen House — Norfolk

Full residence · lime plaster & estate oak

The brief: A Georgian farmhouse the Bell family loved and could not live in — cold, dark in the wrong rooms, and four hundred metres of new cable to hide without gutting the bones.

The result: A full residential scheme that kept every original cornice, rerouted services invisibly, and shifted the day-to-day to follow the light. “The house feels older and works newer,” Charlotte Bell put it — and a planned move was quietly cancelled.

11rooms reworked, none rebuilt
400mof cable hidden in period fabric
0original cornices lost
II

Hotel Santal — 24 keys

Boutique hotel · public rooms & suites

The brief: Marcus Finch’s new boutique hotel needed twenty-four rooms that photographed beautifully and survived a full season of housekeeping — on a furniture budget that had already caused one argument.

The result: One prototype suite, tested and costed, then rolled out across all keys with contract-grade specification. “Our lobby photographs itself now,” he says — occupancy is up and the furniture argument is over.

24keys rolled out from one prototype
+12%occupancy in the first season
1furniture argument, retired
VI

Villa Ochra — Mallorca

Holiday house · shade, stone, siesta

The brief: A private client wanted a holiday house translated — not how the magazines live, but how they actually wanted to: three colours, one rule, no clutter, and shade engineered for the hours that matter.

The result: A single deep palette of olive, ochre and bone, stone underfoot, and shade placed for the siesta hours. “They translated how we want to live, not how magazines do,” Asha Patel told us — the house now books out to friends every summer.

9shaded hours engineered in
