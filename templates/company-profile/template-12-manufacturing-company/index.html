The works

Three shifts, one standard

Vulkan started in 1996 grinding die components in a rented bay. The founding rule was written on the first job traveler and is now etched by the door: measure twice, ship once, never surprise a customer.

Today 180 machinists, welders and quality engineers run the floor around the clock — same rule, more zeros on the tolerances.

  • ISO 9001 and AS9100D certified facility
  • CMM inspection reports with every shipment
  • Design-for-manufacture feedback inside every quote

Capabilities

Capability matrix

Six core capabilities; the first cell is where most customers start.

5-Axis CNC Machining

Simultaneous 5-axis milling of aluminum, titanium and superalloys up to 1,200 mm envelopes — prototype to 50,000-piece runs with in-cell probing.

Wire & Sinker EDM

Burr-free internal geometries and hardened-steel features to ±2 µm.

Certified Welding & Fab

AWS-certified TIG and robotic MIG cells for frames, skids and enclosures.

Sheet Metal & Laser

Fiber laser cutting to 25 mm with CNC bending and PEM insertion.

Assembly & Kitting

Clean-room sub-assembly, torque-logged fastening and serialized kits.

Metrology & Quality Lab

Climate-controlled CMM lab, FAIR/PPAP documentation and full material traceability from mill cert to box label.
