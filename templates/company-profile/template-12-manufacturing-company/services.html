Every cell on the homepage matrix has a file behind it: what the cell delivers, how a job travels through it and what the pricing logic looks like before you send a print. Three documented production cases follow, numbers straight off the travelers.

File 01

5-Axis CNC Machining

Quoted per part — prototype lots from $1,450

Simultaneous 5-axis milling of aluminum, titanium and superalloys in envelopes up to 1,200 mm, with in-cell probing that re-measures every critical feature mid-cycle. The same program runs the prototype and the 50,000-piece production order, so first-article data stays valid for the life of the job.

Deliverables

  • DFM notes returned with every quote
  • Fixture design and CAM programming in-house
  • In-cell probing with SPC logs per operation
  • FAIR-documented first article before production
  • Lights-out capacity for repeat orders

How the job travels

  1. 01Quote & DFM, 72 h. Engineer-reviewed print, firm price.
  2. 02Program & first article. FAIR shipped for sign-off.
  3. 03Production & probing. SPC charted, serialized, packed.
File 02

Wire & Sinker EDM

Quoted per detail — typical features from $620

Burr-free internal geometries, sharp corners and hardened-steel features held to ±2 µm. Wire for profiles and punches, sinker for blind cavities — both run submerged with thermal compensation, so Friday's part measures like Monday's.

Deliverables

  • Profiles and punches to ±2 µm
  • Electrode design and burn strategy
  • Hardened tool-steel and carbide work
  • Surface-finish certification on request

How the job travels

  1. 01Feature review. Burn time and finish class confirmed.
  2. 02Setup & burn. Submerged, thermally compensated.
  3. 03Verify. CMM report attached to every detail.
File 03

Certified Welding & Fab

Quoted per weldment — frames from $2,800

AWS-certified TIG for the joints an inspector will photograph and robotic MIG for the two hundred that come after. Frames, skids and enclosures arrive square, stress-relieved and tagged with welder ID and procedure number.

Deliverables

  • AWS D1.1 / D1.6 qualified procedures
  • Robotic MIG cells for repeat weldments
  • Post-weld machining on the same floor
  • Weld maps and NDT coordination
  • Powder coat and wet paint via audited partners

How the job travels

  1. 01Fixture & WPS. Procedure matched to alloy and code.
  2. 02Weld & relieve. Distortion controlled, documented.
  3. 03Square & ship. Final machining, weld map in the box.
File 04

Sheet Metal & Laser

Quoted per part — from $14 at production volume

Fiber laser cutting to 25 mm plate, CNC bending with offline-programmed tooling and PEM insertion on the same traveler. Flat-pattern feedback comes back with the quote, so the bend that cannot exist never reaches the brake.

Deliverables

  • Fiber laser to 25 mm, nested for yield
  • CNC bending with first-bend verification
  • PEM insertion, tapping and countersinking
  • Grain-matched cosmetic stainless work

How the job travels

  1. 01Flat-pattern check. Bend reliefs and K-factors confirmed.
  2. 02Cut & form. Nested, lasered, bent to print.
  3. 03Hardware & finish. PEMs torqued, finish verified.

Assembly & Kitting

Clean-room sub-assembly with torque-logged fastening, leak testing and serialized kitting that matches your line-side sequence. One box, one kit, one scan — receiving inspection becomes a formality.

Deliverables

  • ISO 7 clean-room sub-assembly
  • Torque logging on every fastener
  • Serialized kits in line-sequence order
  • Vendor-managed hardware programs

How the job travels

  1. 01BOM lock. Sources, revisions and torque specs frozen.
  2. 02Build & log. Each station scans, torques, records.
  3. 03Kit & ship. Sequenced boxes, scannable manifests.
File 06

Metrology & Quality Lab

FAIR / PPAP packages from $850 per part number

A climate-controlled CMM lab that documents what the floor produced: FAIR and PPAP packages, gauge R&R studies and full material traceability from mill cert to box label. Also available as a standalone service for parts we did not make.

Deliverables

  • CMM inspection at 20 °C ±0.5
  • AS9102 FAIR and full PPAP submissions
  • Gauge R&R and capability studies
  • Serial traceability, mill cert to label

How the job travels

  1. 01Plan. Characteristics ballooned, gauges assigned.
  2. 02Measure. CMM programs run, soak time respected.
  3. 03Certify. Package signed by the quality director.
