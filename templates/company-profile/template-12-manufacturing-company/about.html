About Vulkan

A precision shop where the tolerance is the whole product.

Vulkan Precision Works machines and fabricates components for industries where close enough is a failure. Here is the discipline behind the parts.

Our story

Built on tenths, not promises

Vulkan Precision Works was founded in 1991 by a machinist who believed a part is only as good as the tolerance it holds under inspection. He started with two manual mills and a granite surface plate in a rented bay, taking the jobs other shops called too tight, and built a reputation one measured component at a time. That obsession with the number is still the company.

We machine and fabricate precision components for aerospace, medical and industrial customers — fields where a part that is close enough is simply a part that failed. Our work lives or dies by the inspection report, so we built the company around metrology: climate-controlled measurement, documented first-article inspection, and full traceability on every lot we ship.

Precision is a system, not a hero. It comes from disciplined process control, well-maintained machines, and operators who treat a tenth of a thousandth as a promise rather than a suggestion. We invest in capability before capacity — a new five-axis cell, a CMM, a hardening process — because the ability to hold a tolerance reliably is worth more than the ability to make more parts badly.

We are a build-to-print and build-to-spec shop, and we treat a customer's drawing as a contract. If a tolerance cannot be held, we say so before we quote, not after we ship. That candour has cost us the occasional job and earned us customers who have trusted us with flight-critical and life-critical work for decades.

Over thirty years on, Vulkan still measures everything, still owns its process, and still believes the inspection report is the only marketing that matters. The machines are newer and the tolerances tighter, but the standard has not moved a micron.
