About the studio

A design and motion studio that refuses to be put in one.

Pigeonhole Studio makes identity, design and motion that moves — work too playful to ignore and too sharp to dismiss. Here's the studio behind it.

Our story

The studio that wouldn't sit still

Pigeonhole Studio was founded in Lisbon in 2015 by a designer and an animator who were tired of being filed under one discipline. Clients wanted a logo from one agency, a website from another and a film from a third, then wondered why none of it felt like the same brand. Pigeonhole was the answer: one studio that designs the still and the moving as a single voice. The name is a joke we intend to keep proving wrong.

We work across identity, digital design and motion, and we treat them as one craft rather than three departments. A brand we build will have a logo that knows how it animates, a typeface that knows how it moves on screen, and a website that feels like the film and vice versa. Coherence is the luxury most brands never get, and it is the thing we are best at delivering.

Our work is unapologetically bold. We believe the biggest risk in design is being forgettable, so we make things that are a little too playful, a little too sharp, a little too much — then we sweat the craft until 'too much' becomes exactly right. The electric blue and acid green in our own brand are a promise: we will not give you beige.

We are a small studio by design, which means the people who pitch your project are the people who design and animate it. There is no account layer translating your brief into a game of telephone. You talk to the makers, the makers make, and the work is better for the short distance between an idea and the screen.

Ten years in, Pigeonhole has built brands and made films for culture, tech and hospitality clients who wanted to be impossible to scroll past. We are still small, still playful, still allergic to beige — and still refusing to be filed under just one thing.
