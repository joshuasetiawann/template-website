Small print, said loudly

Questions people ask before paying us

Kickoff with the maker on your project (not a manager), a timeline you can screenshot, and a teardown of the brief. By Friday you get our hot-take deck: what we believe, what we doubt, what we will test.

That is the recommended door. If you move on to a full identity or site within six months, half the sprint fee is credited — the thinking carries straight over, so you should not pay for it twice.

Included: design, build where listed, sources, handover workshop and two rounds of honest revisions. Not included: paid media, print costs and stock licences — those are passed through at cost with the invoices showing.

Projects split 50/25/25 — kickoff, midpoint, delivery. Sprints are paid up front because they start Monday and end Friday. No surprise line items; if scope grows, we say a number out loud first.

Got a gnarly bit of your own?

Skip the formal brief. Tell us what you are making and which part is scary — a maker replies within two days, probably with questions and a GIF.
