Services in depth

Six services, fees in plain figures, results on the record.

An estate agency should be as transparent about its own terms as it asks vendors to be about their chain. Here is each of our six services with what is included, how it proceeds and what it costs — followed by three instructions we are able to write up in full.

Residential Sales

A strategy-priced listing rather than a flattering one: comparable evidence sets the asking figure, professional photography and floorplans present the house honestly, and the negotiator who valued your home is the one who sells it. Vendors receive a written report every Friday, viewed or not.

Sole agency from 1.25% + VAT — 28-day terms, no tie-in

What's included

  • Evidence-backed pricing strategy with buyer-pool sizing
  • Professional photography, floorplans and EPC arranged
  • Accompanied viewings six days a week
  • Weekly written vendor report — viewings, feedback, next steps
  • One negotiator from valuation through to completion

How it proceeds

  1. Appraise. Valuation with comparables and a pricing strategy in writing.
  2. Launch. Media, listing and the first viewing weekend inside ten days.
  3. Negotiate. Offers qualified, chain checked, sale chased to completion.

Lettings & Management

Full management as a landlord would run it themselves: referenced tenants, quarterly inspections with photographs, and a 24-hour repair line answered by people who can authorise a plumber. Rent arrives on the same date each month with a statement that reconciles.

Full management from 9% of monthly rent; let-only from £950

What's included

  • Tenant referencing, right-to-rent checks and deposit protection
  • Quarterly photographed inspections with a written condition note
  • 24-hour repair line with vetted local trades
  • Gas, electrical and EPC compliance diarised and chased
  • Monthly statements and an annual rent review with evidence

How it proceeds

  1. Appraise. Rental valuation and compliance audit of the property.
  2. Let. Marketing, referencing and a tenancy signed on solid terms.
  3. Manage. Inspections, repairs and renewals handled without drama.

Investment Sourcing

Off-market acquisitions found through the network three generations of local trading builds. Every opportunity arrives with gross-yield modelling, refurbishment costings and the local letting evidence behind both — before you spend a morning viewing it.

Sourcing fee from 2% of purchase price, payable on completion

What's included

  • Investment brief agreed in writing — yield target, area, budget
  • Off-market and pre-market opportunities with full yield models
  • Refurbishment costings from trades we use ourselves
  • Negotiation and purchase progression to completion

How it proceeds

  1. Brief. Yield target and risk appetite set the search terms.
  2. Source. Modelled opportunities presented, usually before market.
  3. Secure. We negotiate, then hand to lettings if you wish it managed.

Valuations & Appraisals

RICS-aligned market appraisals with the comparable evidence attached, not alluded to. Since 2015 every valuation has come from our data desk as well as the valuer's eye: recent sales, buyer-pool sizing and time-to-offer for homes like yours, in writing.

Market appraisals free of charge; formal written reports from £350

What's included

  • Comparable sales evidence from the last eighteen months
  • Buyer-pool sizing and realistic time-to-offer estimate
  • Pricing strategy: launch figure, review points, floor
  • Formal written reports for probate, divorce and tax planning

How it proceeds

  1. Visit. The valuer walks the property; the data desk pulls evidence.
  2. Report. Figure, evidence and strategy delivered in writing within 48 hours.
  3. Decide. Sell, let, hold or refinance — the report works for all four.

Land & New Homes

Site appraisal, plot pricing and launch marketing for small developers across the Cotswold corridor. We price plots from the buyer's side of the table — what households will actually pay, phase by phase — and run show-home weekends that convert reservations rather than footfall.

Quoted per site at appraisal — fee agreed before instruction

What's included

  • Site appraisal with plot-by-plot pricing schedule
  • Phased launch plan matched to build programme and cash flow
  • Show-home weekends staffed by our own negotiators
  • Weekly developer report: reservations, exchanges, pipeline

How it proceeds

  1. Appraise. Plot pricing tested against live buyer demand, not hope.
  2. Launch. Phase one released with media, hoarding and show home.
  3. Convert. Reservations progressed to exchange on a weekly rhythm.

Relocation Service

For households moving into the area blind: we walk you through villages at school-run hour, time the commute from the actual platform and shortlist homes you can trust before you have unpacked a single box. Most relocating families offer within three accompanied days here.

From £1,800 per household, credited against fees if we sell to you

What's included

  • Area orientation day: villages, schools, commute timings
  • Written brief and a shortlist drawn from on- and off-market homes
  • Accompanied viewing days arranged around your travel
  • School, solicitor and removals introductions that answer the phone

How it proceeds

  1. Brief. A long call about how your household actually lives.
  2. Orient. A guided day in the villages that fit the brief.
  3. Settle. Shortlist, viewings and an offer negotiated on your behalf.

Developer · land & new homes

Challenge. A fourteen-plot scheme outside Cirencester was coming to market just as mortgage rates climbed; the lender's appraisal assumed sales the open market would not support.

Result. We re-priced plot by plot against live buyer demand, launched in two phases with show-home weekends, and reported reservations to the lender weekly — the scheme funded itself through to the final plot.

Investment fund · sourcing & acquisition

Challenge. A family office wanted £4m deployed into Cotswold residential without bidding against itself at auction — and without a single acquisition that flattered the spreadsheet.

Result. Eleven homes acquired over twenty months, nine of them off-market, each with yield modelling our lettings desk then had to live up to. The fund renewed the mandate for a second tranche.

Wyndham Park

Estate · lettings & management

Challenge. A sixty-home estate portfolio arrived with an eleven-month repairs backlog, voids running over 6% and tenants who had stopped reporting faults because nothing happened when they did.

Result. Every home inspected and photographed in the first quarter, the backlog cleared through our own trades, and rent reviews handled with evidence rather than letters. Voids are now an exception that gets a name attached.
