About Foxglove

An estate agency that treats a home like the biggest decision it is.

Foxglove Estates handles residential sales and property investment with the patience, honesty and local knowledge a life-changing decision deserves. Here is who we are.

Our story

Local knowledge, honestly applied

Foxglove Estates was founded in 2001 by an agent who had grown tired of an industry that treated the most important purchase of a person's life like a transaction to be closed quickly. Homes were oversold, valuations were inflated to win the instruction, and buyers were rushed toward a decision. Foxglove was built to be the patient, honest alternative.

We are a local agency by conviction, not limitation. We know our streets — which ones flood, which face the morning sun, which school catchments are real and which are wishful thinking. That knowledge means our valuations are honest rather than flattering, and our advice to buyers is grounded in fact rather than commission.

An honest valuation sometimes costs us the instruction, because a seller down the road will always promise a higher number. We are at peace with that. A property priced correctly sells; a property priced to win the pitch sits on the market until it is discounted anyway, and the seller pays for the flattery in lost time and lower offers.

We work in residential sales and in property investment, advising landlords and investors with the same candour we give a first-time buyer. We will tell an investor that a yield does not add up as readily as we will tell a family that a house is right for them. Trust, in this business, is the only asset that compounds.

Nearly twenty-five years on, Foxglove remains an independent agency where a large share of our business comes from people we have helped before — and the friends and family they send us. In an industry people love to distrust, that is the result we are proudest of.
