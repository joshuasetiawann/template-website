About Foxglove

Local since 1998, data-driven since 2015

Three generations of the Foxglove family have matched houses to households here since 1998 — long enough to remember what every street sold for, and why.

In 2015 we added an in-house data desk. Now every valuation arrives with comparable evidence, buyer-pool sizing and a pricing strategy — not a flattering guess.
