About Nexcore

A product-minded engineering company, eleven years in.

Nexcore Systems designs, builds and operates cloud platforms for ambitious product teams — from first commit to global rollout. This is the longer version of who we are.

Our story

From two desks to sixty senior engineers

Nexcore Systems began in 2014 when three platform engineers left a payments company that had just survived a brutal Black Friday outage. They were convinced the failure was avoidable, and that most outages are not technology problems but ownership problems — code thrown over a wall, runbooks nobody reads, and vendors who disappear the moment the contract is signed.

We started with two desks and one rule: the people who design a system are the people who run it at 3am. That rule still governs how we staff every engagement. We grew deliberately — never hiring a bench to keep utilised, never selling a junior at a senior rate — until we reached the sixty-person engineering company we are today, serving fintech, health-tech and logistics scale-ups across three continents.

Our work sits where reliability and revenue meet. We carve monoliths into services without a maintenance window, build internal developer platforms that turn a one-day onboarding into an afternoon, and wire SOC 2 evidence collection into the pipeline so audit week stops freezing the roadmap. None of it is flashy. All of it is measured, and the numbers are in the case studies on our services page.

What has not changed is the shape of a Nexcore squad: small, senior, and accountable for an outcome rather than a ticket queue. We pair with your engineers instead of replacing them, hand over documentation rather than dependencies, and consider an engagement finished only when your team can run what we built without us in the room.

Eleven years on, the founders still review architecture decisions, the company is still entirely employee-owned, and we still turn down work we cannot staff with senior people. That discipline is the reason clients renew — and the reason this page exists rather than a glossy brochure.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Senior by default

The engineer who pitches the work writes the code. We do not staff a discovery with one architect and deliver it with five graduates.

Own the outcome

We measure ourselves on deploy frequency, latency and uptime — not on hours billed or tickets closed.

Transparent velocity

Weekly demos, a public roadmap and burn-up charts. No surprise invoices, no status theatre.

Hand over, don't hold on

Everything lives in your repositories and cloud accounts from day one. Our access ends when the engagement does.

Boring on purpose

We favour observable, well-trodden tooling over fashionable architecture. Fewer pagers, longer weekends.

Security as a habit

Threat models and evidence collection are part of delivery, not a panic before the audit.
