Cloud Architecture

We design multi-region AWS and Azure landing zones as code — account structure, networking, identity and guardrails — sized against your actual traffic forecasts rather than vendor reference diagrams. Every blueprint ships with a cost model and a failover plan that has been rehearsed, not just drawn.

Starting from $9,800 — fixed two-week discovery

What's included

  • Landing-zone blueprint in Terraform with peer-reviewed modules
  • Network, IAM and encryption guardrails mapped to SOC 2 controls
  • Monthly cost model with reserved-capacity recommendations
  • Region-failover runbook plus a live game-day exercise
  • 90 days of architecture support after handover

How it runs

  1. Audit. Two weeks mapping workloads, spend and failure domains.
  2. Blueprint. Architecture review board signs the design with your team.
  3. Build & prove. Zones deployed by code; failover rehearsed before go-live.

Platform Engineering

We build internal developer platforms that make the right way the easy way: golden-path templates, self-service environments and paved deployment roads. Teams stop filing tickets for infrastructure and start shipping the day a repository is created.

Starting from $34,000 per squad-sprint engagement

What's included

  • Service catalog with golden-path templates for your top three stacks
  • Self-service ephemeral environments with automatic teardown
  • Scorecards measuring lead time, deploy frequency and MTTR
  • Platform docs and onboarding lab your engineers co-author

How it runs

  1. Listen. Developer-experience survey plus delivery-metric baseline.
  2. Pave. First golden path live within three sprints, adopted by a pilot team.
  3. Scale. Remaining stacks templated; platform team coached to own it.

DevOps & SRE

CI/CD pipelines, observability stacks and on-call playbooks designed for the team you actually have — not the one in the conference talk. We instrument golden signals first, automate the toil second, and leave a rotation your engineers volunteer for.

Starting from $18,500 — pipeline & observability build

What's included

  • CI/CD pipelines with progressive delivery and automatic rollback
  • SLOs, error budgets and dashboards wired to alerting that respects sleep
  • Incident-response playbooks and blameless post-mortem templates
  • Two shadowed on-call rotations before we hand over the pager

How it runs

  1. Baseline. Measure deploy frequency, MTTR and alert noise for two weeks.
  2. Automate. Pipelines and golden-signal dashboards replace manual gates.
  3. Hand over. Joint on-call until your rotation runs a full cycle solo.

Security & Compliance

Threat modelling, hardening and SOC 2 evidence automation baked into the delivery pipeline instead of bolted on before the audit. Controls map to code, evidence collects itself, and your auditor gets a portal rather than a panicked screenshot folder.

Starting from $14,000 — SOC 2 readiness assessment

What's included

  • Threat model and risk register for every internet-facing service
  • CIS-benchmark hardening applied through infrastructure code
  • Automated evidence collection for SOC 2 Type II controls
  • Secrets management and dependency-scanning rollout
  • Audit-week support, sitting on your side of the table

How it runs

  1. Assess. Gap analysis against SOC 2 and your customer contracts.
  2. Harden. Fixes land as pull requests with evidence wired in.
  3. Attest. Auditor walkthrough with live control evidence on screen.

Data Engineering

Streaming pipelines and warehouse models that turn product events into decisions the same day they happen. We favour boring, observable tooling over fashionable architectures — your analysts get tested models, not a swamp with a dashboard on top.

Starting from $22,000 — pipeline & warehouse build

What's included

  • Event schemas and streaming ingestion with exactly-once guarantees
  • Tested warehouse models with lineage and freshness monitors
  • Data contracts between product teams and analytics
  • Cost-per-query review and storage-tiering policy

How it runs

  1. Map. Source systems, owners and the five decisions data must serve.
  2. Pipe. Ingestion and core models live in four to six weeks.
  3. Trust. Tests, alerts and contracts make the numbers defensible.

Performance Tuning

Profiling and load testing that find the scaling ceiling before your launch day does. We reproduce production traffic in a disposable environment, fix the three bottlenecks that matter and leave the harness behind so every release gets the same treatment.

Starting from $7,500 per load-test cycle

What's included

  • Replayable load-test harness modelled on real traffic shapes
  • Flame-graph profiling across services, queries and caches
  • Ranked bottleneck report with fixes shipped as pull requests
  • Cloud-spend delta showing what the tuning saved

How it runs

  1. Model. Capture production traffic shape and set latency targets.
  2. Break. Load tests find the ceiling in a mirrored environment.
  3. Fix & keep. Bottlenecks patched; the harness joins your CI.
Case studies

Three engagements, with the numbers left in

Clients from the wall on our homepage, written up the way we report internally: the problem, what shipped, what changed.

OrbitalPay

Fintech · payments platform

Challenge. A monolithic payment switch deployed monthly, and every release window risked card-authorization downtime the business could no longer absorb.

Result. A strangler migration carved the switch into services behind a routing layer, with progressive delivery and automated rollback — rebuilt mid-flight, without a maintenance window.

22deploys per month, up from one
44%lower p95 authorization latency
0minutes of downtime during migration

MedSync

Health-tech · clinical data exchange

Challenge. Enterprise hospital deals stalled on security review: SOC 2 evidence lived in screenshots, and audit preparation froze the roadmap for nine weeks a year.

Result. Controls were mapped to infrastructure code and evidence collection automated into the pipeline; the next Type II audit ran from a live portal while the roadmap kept moving.

12days of audit prep, down from nine weeks
240+control evidences collected automatically
0critical findings in the Type II report

Cartful

E-commerce · checkout platform

Challenge. Checkout p95 latency doubled every peak campaign and the team had spent a year guessing at the ceiling with dashboards that disagreed.

Result. Our discovery sprint reproduced peak traffic in a mirrored environment, found a connection-pool ceiling and two query hot spots, and shipped the fixes — six weeks from kickoff to a calm sale day.

61%lower checkout p95 at peak load
6weeks from kickoff to fix in production
3.4×peak traffic absorbed without paging anyone
