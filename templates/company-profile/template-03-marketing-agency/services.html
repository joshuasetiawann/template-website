01

Brand Strategy & Positioning

From CHF 28,000

Eight weeks from kickoff to a positioning your whole company can repeat without notes. We interview the customers who chose you and the ones who ignored you, map the category's empty seats, and write a promise sharp enough to disagree with.

What's included

  • 14–16 customer and stakeholder interviews
  • Category map with the empty seats marked
  • Two positioning territories tested with real buyers
  • The one-page promise plus messaging hierarchy
  • Naming and tagline routes where the brief needs them

How it runs

  1. 01

    Listen — interviews, sales-call shadowing, store walks.

  2. 02

    Sharpen — territories argued down until one survives.

  3. 03

    Commit — one page your CFO and copywriter both sign.

From CHF 36,000

Identity built as a kit, not a PDF that dies in a drawer. Logo, type, colour and motion rules arrive as working templates your in-house team can run without phoning us — though the phone line stays open.

What's included

How it runs

  1. 01

  2. 02

    Design — two rounds, presented in context, never on white.

  3. 03

    Kit & handover — files, rules and a working session.

Proof

Three case studies

Picked from the homepage index. Challenge, result, and the numbers a client would sign off on.

Alpenmilch — dairy rebrand

FMCG · Dairy

ChallengeA 90-year-old dairy cooperative losing shelf space to private labels. The identity disappeared at two metres, and retail buyers had stopped returning calls.

ResultNew identity and packaging system rolled out across seven SKU lines in one season. In-store retesting showed shoppers finding and lifting the brand dramatically faster; the rebrand paid for itself within eight months.

41%More shelf pickup, first season
7SKU lines relaunched
8Months to payback

Kavio — fintech go-to-market

Fintech · B2B

ChallengeStrong product, invisible category position. Acquisition costs were climbing every quarter while the board's patience moved in the opposite direction.

ResultPositioning first, then creative-led paid media with a weekly testing matrix. Two quarters in, acquisition cost had fallen by more than a third and qualified pipeline had tripled.

38%Lower customer acquisition cost
3×Qualified pipeline growth
11Weeks to first profitable cohort

Helvetia Rail — employer brand

Transport · Employer brand

ChallengeEleven hundred open roles, an ageing applicant pool and recruitment advertising nobody could recall seeing — including the people who approved it.

ResultAn employer platform and film series built around the people who keep the trains moving. Applications surged, time-to-fill dropped by nearly three weeks, and offer acceptance hit a company record.

64%More applications year on year
19Days cut from time-to-fill
87%Offer acceptance rate

Before you brief us

Buying questions, answered straight

With a two-week paid diagnostic (CHF 4,800): we read your numbers, talk to a handful of customers and come back with a point of view. If we go on to work together, the fee is credited against the first project.

Strategy and identity run as fixed-scope projects. Media, content and analytics run monthly — with the quarterly kill-fee checkpoint described on the homepage. If the numbers are not moving by quarter two, we help you stop paying us.

The people on the team page. Founders still write and art-direct; the strategist in your kickoff is the strategist in your final review. There is no account layer to absorb the blame or the budget.

Most clients invest CHF 120,000–600,000 a year across brand and media. The starting fees above are floors, not averages — if your budget is well below them, we will say so in the first call and suggest a smaller first move.
