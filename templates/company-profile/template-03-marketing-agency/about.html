About the studio

A brand and performance studio that refuses to choose between the two.

Studio Vermilion builds brands that look unmistakable and campaigns that actually move the number. Most agencies pick a lane. We never did — and here is why.

Our story

Born out of a false choice

Studio Vermilion was founded in 2011 by a creative director and a media strategist who were tired of the same fight. The creatives said performance marketing made everything ugly; the media buyers said brand work was unaccountable decoration. Both were half right, and both were missing the point — a brand people remember is the cheapest performance channel there is.

So we built a studio that does both under one roof. Brand strategists sit next to performance analysts. The team that designs the identity is briefed by the team that buys the media. When a campaign underperforms, we do not blame the creative or the channel in separate meetings — we fix it together in one.

That integration is the whole product. It is why our rebrands ship with a measurement plan and why our paid campaigns are art-directed rather than templated. We name the colour the brand will own, then we prove it lifts click-through. We write the line, then we A/B test it. The vermilion in our name is not decoration; it is the discipline of making bold choices and standing behind the numbers they produce.

We stay deliberately small — a senior studio, not a holding-company pyramid. The people who pitch your brand are the people who design it, run it and report on it. We turn down work we cannot staff with senior people, and we have never won a client by being the cheapest in the room.

Fourteen years in, we have launched brands across retail, hospitality and consumer tech, and the work that makes us proudest is the work that became unignorable — the identity a competitor quietly copied, the campaign people screenshot. That is the bar.

The principles behind the work

Bold is a strategy

Forgettable is the only real risk. We make the brave choice and stand behind the data it produces.

Beauty and accountability

A campaign can be art-directed and measured. We refuse the idea that you must trade one for the other.

One team, one room

Strategy, design and media sit together. No silos to hide an underperforming idea behind.

Senior hands only

The people who pitch the work make the work. We do not sell a director and deliver an intern.

Own a colour, own a line

Show the numbers

Every engagement reports on what moved. We would rather lose an argument to data than win one with taste.
