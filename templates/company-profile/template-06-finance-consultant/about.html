About Aldergate

Independent financial advice, free of anyone else's incentives.

Aldergate Advisory gives families, founders and trustees clear financial counsel with no products to sell and no commissions to chase. Here is the thinking behind the firm.

Our story

Independence, by design

Aldergate Advisory was founded in 2009, in the long shadow of a financial crisis that had just taught a generation what conflicted advice costs. Our founder had spent years inside a firm that paid its advisers to recommend its own products, and left convinced that the only honest financial advice is advice that earns nothing from the recommendation itself.

So Aldergate is fee-only and independent. We sell no products, accept no commissions, and answer to no parent bank. We are paid for our judgment and nothing else, which means the advice you receive is the advice we would give our own family — because, in several cases, it is.

We work with people at the moments money gets complicated: a business sale, an inheritance, a retirement, the responsibilities of a trust. Our job is to bring order and a long view — to model the decision, stress-test it against the bad years as well as the good, and explain it in plain language until it is genuinely understood, not merely signed.

We deliberately keep our client numbers low. An adviser at Aldergate carries a fraction of the relationships a commission-driven firm would expect, because real planning requires time you cannot bill against a product. We would rather turn away a prospect than dilute the attention our existing clients are owed.

Sixteen years on, Aldergate remains a small, independent, fee-only firm advising clients many of whom have been with us since the beginning. Our reputation for candour is the only thing we have ever had to sell.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Independence is non-negotiable

No products, no commissions, no parent bank. We are paid for judgment, never for a recommendation.

Candour over comfort

If a plan is unrealistic or a purchase is unwise, you'll hear it plainly and early.

Plan for the bad years

We stress-test every decision against downturns, not just the optimistic case.

Plain language always

If you don't fully understand the plan, we haven't finished explaining it.

Few clients, full attention

We cap relationships per adviser so real planning gets the time it needs.

The long view

We optimise for decades and dignity in retirement, not this quarter's performance.
