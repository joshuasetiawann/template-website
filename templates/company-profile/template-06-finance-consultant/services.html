Services in depth

Every engagement, priced before it starts

The same six services from our front door, opened up: what you receive, how the work runs, and the flat fee — written down before you commit. Below them, three client outcomes told honestly, with names changed and numbers real.

Comprehensive Financial Plan

The 25-page roadmap is built over three meetings, not pulled from a software template. It covers cash flow, tax, insurance and investments in one document your household can actually act on, and every recommendation comes with an owner and a deadline on the 90-day list.

What's included

  • Net-worth and cash-flow baseline across every account
  • Review of your last two tax returns for missed elections
  • Insurance gap analysis with the payout math shown
  • Written investment policy statement you can take anywhere
  • 90-day action list with owners and deadlines
  • Annual plan review included — no meter running

How it runs

  1. 1Discovery. The free 45-minute review, then one secure document checklist.
  2. 2Build. We draft and pressure-test the plan over about three weeks.
  3. 3Delivery. A walkthrough meeting; you leave with the plan and the 90-day list.

Flat fee — from 2,400 USD

Portfolio Second Opinion

An independent audit of what you already own — no products to sell means no reason to flatter your current adviser or scare you into moving. Most households discover fees they never agreed to and overlap they never intended.

What's included

  • Fee X-ray across every account, fund by fund
  • Overlap and concentration map of your holdings
  • Risk score measured against your stated tolerance
  • Plain-English findings memo, four pages or fewer
  • 60-minute walkthrough call with next steps

How it runs

  1. 1Statements in. Upload recent statements to the secure portal.
  2. 2Audit. Ten business days of fee, overlap and risk analysis.
  3. 3Findings call. The memo, the math, and what we would change.

Flat fee — from 950 USD

Retirement Readiness

One question, answered with arithmetic instead of optimism: can you stop, and when? We map every income source, sequence the withdrawals account by account, and run your Social Security timing at 62, 67 and 70 so the choice is a decision, not a guess.

What's included

  • Retirement income map across pensions, accounts and benefits
  • Withdrawal-sequence modelling, written as an order of operations
  • Social Security timing scenarios at 62, 67 and 70
  • Healthcare bridge costs from early exit to Medicare
  • Stress test against a poor first decade of returns

How it runs

  1. 1Inventory. Every account, pension and benefit on one page.
  2. 2Scenarios. Three retirement dates, modelled side by side.
  3. 3Decision meeting. Pick a date; leave with the written sequence.

Flat fee — from 1,800 USD

Equity & Founder Planning

Options, RSUs and founder stock reward planning years before the liquidity event. We build the exercise calendar around the tax math — AMT exposure, QSBS qualification, concentration risk — and coordinate directly with your CPA so nothing is filed twice or missed once.

What's included

  • Option exercise calendar with the tax math attached
  • QSBS qualification review, documented for your records
  • Concentration unwind schedule you can live with
  • Liquidity-event cash plan for taxes, giving and life
  • Direct coordination with your CPA and counsel

How it runs

  1. 1Grant review. Every grant, strike price and vesting date mapped.
  2. 2Tax modelling. Exercise scenarios across two to four tax years.
  3. 3Calendar. Dated actions, shared with you and your CPA.

Flat fee — from 2,900 USD

College & Family Goals

Education funding that starts from the school bill, not the brochure. We size the goal per child, choose the right vehicle, model the financial-aid impact, and put the contributions on autopilot so the plan survives busy years.

What's included

  • 529 plan selection and a dated funding schedule
  • Grandparent gifting rules, explained and documented
  • Financial-aid impact check before any account opens
  • Sibling-by-sibling funding map for fairness later

How it runs

  1. 1Goal sizing. Real school costs, per child, per year.
  2. 2Vehicle choice. 529, custodial or taxable — with reasons.
  3. 3Autopilot. Contributions scheduled and reviewed yearly.

Flat fee — from 750 USD
