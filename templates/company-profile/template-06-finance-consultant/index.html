Our story

Founded after the fine print

Aldergate began in 2011 when founder Ruth Aldgate left a brokerage rather than sell another commissioned annuity. She opened a one-room practice with a flat-fee menu taped to the door.

Fifteen years later the menu is still on the door. Eight advisers now serve 540 households, and not one has ever taken a commission.

  • Flat, published fees — never a percent of products sold
  • Fiduciary duty in your contract, not just our values page
  • Annual plan reviews included, no meter running
