About Mosaic

An HR consultancy that treats people as the strategy, not the overhead.

Mosaic People Partners helps organisations hire well, lead well and grow well — practical HR and talent consulting that puts humans at the centre. Here's what we believe.

Our story

People practices that actually work

Mosaic People Partners was founded in 2010 by two HR leaders who had grown frustrated with their own profession. Too much of corporate HR had become policing and paperwork — compliance dressed up as strategy — while the actual work of helping people do their best work went undone. Mosaic was built to put the human back at the centre of human resources.

We are a practical consultancy, not a theory shop. Our clients are growing organisations that have outpaced their people practices: a forty-person company suddenly needing real hiring processes, a founder-led team learning to manage, a culture that worked at twenty and is straining at a hundred. We bring the structure they need without the bureaucracy they fear.

Our work spans the whole employee journey — attracting and hiring the right people, developing managers who can actually lead, designing fair pay and progression, and building cultures where good people choose to stay. We do not believe in importing a generic playbook; we believe in understanding your specific organisation and building practices that fit it, then handing them over so your team can run them.

We treat people as a strategy because the evidence is overwhelming that they are. The companies that hire deliberately, manage humanely and grow their people don't just feel better to work for — they perform better, retain more, and spend less replacing the talent they failed to keep. Good HR is not a cost centre; it is one of the highest-return investments a growing company can make.

Fifteen years on, Mosaic partners with organisations across sectors who want to grow without losing the culture that got them here. We are still practical, still human-centred, and still convinced that the best workplaces are built on purpose, not by accident.
