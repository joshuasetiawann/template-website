Services in depth

What we fix, how we fix it, and what it costs.

No mystique here: every Mosaic engagement below lists exactly what you get, the three moves it takes and the price it starts at. Then three client outcomes — Tidepool, ByteHarvest and Juniper Health — with the before-and-after numbers their founders let us publish.

Fractional People Lead

A senior people partner embedded in your leadership team two to ten days a month — owning the people roadmap, sitting in the hard conversations and building only what your stage actually needs. You get the judgment of a VP of People without the full-time loaded cost.

From $4,200/month for two days — the math shown per day

What's included

  • Quarterly people roadmap tied to your hiring and revenue plan
  • A named partner in your leadership meetings and Slack
  • Hands-on handling of the hard weeks: exits, conflicts, reorgs
  • Playbooks written down as we go — they stay when we leave
  • Help hiring our replacement when you outgrow fractional

Three moves

  1. Listen. Two weeks of 1:1s and data before any recommendation.
  2. Prioritise. One quarter, three people problems, owners named.
  3. Embed. Cadence days each month until your team runs it alone.

Hiring Engine Build

Structured interviews, scorecards and a pipeline your team can actually run — built in six weeks with your hiring managers, not handed down as a binder. Candidates get a process they thank you for; you get offer-accept rates that stop embarrassing the forecast.

From $12,500 for the six-week build, tools included

What's included

  • Role scorecards and structured interview kits for live openings
  • Interviewer training with calibrated practice debriefs
  • Pipeline stages, SLAs and candidate communication templates
  • Offer playbook: comp framing, references, close conversations
  • A funnel dashboard your recruiter reviews weekly without us

Three moves

  1. Audit. Score the current funnel against your last ten hires.
  2. Build. Scorecards, loops and training shipped on live roles.
  3. Calibrate. Two hiring cycles shadowed, then it is yours.

Compensation & Pay Bands

Benchmarked, explainable pay bands with equity refresh logic your CFO will sign and your managers can defend in a 1:1. We close the quiet gaps before they become loud ones, and we leave a refresh model that survives your next funding round.

From $9,500 to first published bands, typically five weeks

What's included

  • Level framework mapped to your actual org, not a template
  • Cash and equity bands benchmarked to your market and stage
  • Pay-equity audit with a costed remediation plan
  • Manager talking points for every band conversation
  • Annual refresh model in a spreadsheet your finance team owns

Three moves

  1. Map. Levels and current pay plotted, gaps surfaced privately.
  2. Band. Benchmarks set the ranges; leadership signs the logic.
  3. Tell. Managers briefed, bands published, questions welcomed.

Culture & Engagement

Surveys with teeth: we ask the questions teams actually answer, report the uncomfortable finding to your face and then fix it with you. No 40-page deck, no heat map left to rot in a drive — one finding, one plan, one re-measure.

From $6,800 — survey, finding and action plan

What's included

  • Engagement survey tuned to your size, with honest anonymity rules
  • Focus groups run by us so people say the quiet part
  • The one-page uncomfortable-finding memo for leadership
  • A 90-day action plan with owners and a re-measure date

Three moves

  1. Ask. Survey and focus groups inside two weeks.
  2. Face it. Leadership hears the finding, chooses the fix.
  3. Re-measure. Same questions, 90 days later, delta reported.

Manager School

A six-week cohort for first-time managers: feedback that lands, 1:1s worth keeping, and the hard calls — underperformance, pay questions, the day someone cries in a meeting. Built on your real situations, rehearsed out loud, never a webinar.

$1,450 per seat, cohorts of 8–14 managers

What's included

  • Six live workshops: feedback, 1:1s, delegation, hard calls
  • Practice scenarios drawn from your company, names changed
  • Peer pods that keep meeting after the cohort ends
  • Manager-effectiveness pulse before and after, shared with you

Three moves

  1. Baseline. Each manager's team rates them before week one.
  2. Practise. Six weeks of rehearsing the conversations that matter.
  3. Prove. Re-rate at week twelve; pods carry the habit on.

HR Compliance Tune-Up

Handbooks, contractor classifications and offboarding flows audited before they bite — in plain language, ranked by actual risk. You get a fix-first list and the documents rewritten so humans read them, not a 200-row spreadsheet of theoretical exposure.

Fixed-fee audit from $4,900, findings in three weeks

What's included

  • Handbook and policy review against the states you employ in
  • Contractor and exemption classification check, role by role
  • Onboarding and offboarding flows tightened end to end
  • Risk-ranked fix list with templates rewritten in plain language

Three moves

  1. Collect. Documents and a short ops questionnaire, one week.
  2. Audit. Findings ranked by likelihood and cost, not alphabet.
  3. Fix. Top items rewritten with you; the rest scheduled.

Tidepool

SaaS · 84 people · hiring engine

Challenge. Tidepool was losing two of every five offers and every interviewer ran their own private process — candidates compared notes, and the notes were not kind.

Result. A six-week Hiring Engine Build put scorecards, trained loops and an offer playbook on live roles. Offer-accept jumped within one quarter and candidates began thanking the team for the process — in writing.

ByteHarvest

Agritech data · 130 people · pay bands

Challenge. Five years of negotiate-your-own-salary had produced quiet pay gaps, loud Glassdoor reviews and a COO doing comp by spreadsheet archaeology before every offer.

Result. Levels mapped, bands benchmarked and published, the equity refresh model handed to finance. Two years on, ByteHarvest still runs the same model — without us, which was the point.

100%of roles levelled and banded in five weeks
96%of offers made inside band a year later
0equity-refresh disputes since publication

Juniper Health

Digital health · 96 people · manager school

Result. Two Manager School cohorts trained 24 first-time managers on feedback, 1:1s and the hard calls, rehearsed on Juniper's real situations. The next engagement survey moved, and so did regretted attrition — downward.
