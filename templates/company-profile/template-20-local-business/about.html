About Hearthside

A family hardware store where they still know your name and your project.

Hearthside Hardware & Home is the neighbourhood's family-run hardware store and home-services team — real advice, fair prices, and a handshake behind every sale. Here's our story.

Our story

Four decades on the same corner

Hearthside Hardware & Home opened on the corner of Main and Third in 1984, when Walt and Eleanor Hearth decided the neighbourhood needed a hardware store that treated people like neighbours instead of foot traffic. They stocked what folks actually needed, learned everyone's name and project, and sent people home with the right part and the knowledge to use it. Forty years later, their grandchildren run the same store on the same corner with the same handshake.

We are family-run and proudly local, which is not nostalgia — it is the whole advantage. When you walk in with a leaking tap, a half-finished deck or a problem you can't name, you talk to someone who has fixed it themselves and will spend ten minutes making sure you leave with the right answer. We would rather sell you the two-dollar washer that solves it than the fifty-dollar gadget that doesn't.

Over the years the neighbourhood asked us to do more than sell the parts, so we built a home-services team: trusted local trades for the jobs you'd rather not tackle yourself — repairs, installations, the odd weekend rescue. The same standard applies whether we are selling you a tool or sending a crew to your door: honest advice, fair prices, and the job done right by people you'll see again at the school fair.

Fair dealing is the entire business model. We are not the cheapest on every single item — a warehouse three towns over might shave a dollar — but we are honest, we are here, and we stand behind what we sell. When something goes wrong, you don't navigate a phone tree; you walk back in and we make it right, because we will be on this corner long after the receipt has faded.

Four decades on, Hearthside is still the neighbourhood's hardware store and home-services team, still family-run, and still built on the radical old-fashioned idea that knowing your customers and treating them fairly is good business. We have watched the kids who came in for their first bike repair bring in their own kids. That is the measure of the place.
