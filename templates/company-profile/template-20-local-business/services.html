Services & stories

Every service, and the same prices that are on the board.

What we do, in full

Six services, off the board

The same six from the homepage, opened up — and the advice that comes with them is still free, like always.

Key Cutting & Locks

keys from 3.50 USD

House, padlock and most car keys cut while you wait — under four minutes, guaranteed — from a wall of four hundred blanks. Lock re-keying happens at the bench, so a new place or a lost set does not mean a locksmith call-out fee.

What you get

  • House and padlock keys cut while you wait, four minutes
  • Most car and transponder keys cut and paired at the bench
  • Lock re-keying and matching one key to many doors
  • Free re-cut if a key we made ever sticks

How it works

  1. 1Bring the key. Or the lock, or both — we will figure out which.
  2. 2Cut at the counter. Watch it happen; under four minutes is the rule.
  3. 3Test it on the spot. We check the bite before you walk out.

Tool & Mower Repair

bench fee 15 USD

A drop-off bench for power tools, mowers and bikes, run by June, who has been fixing things behind this counter since 1989. The deal is simple: fixed, or no fee. If it cannot be saved, we say so before the parts go in, not after.

What you get

  • Power tools, mowers, trimmers and bikes on the bench
  • A written estimate before any part is fitted
  • Fixed or no fee — the bench charge waived if we cannot help
  • Blade and tool sharpening while you shop

How it works

  1. 1Drop it off. Tell us what it is doing, or not doing.
  2. 2We diagnose first. A call with the number before we fit a thing.
  3. 3Pick it up working. Or pay nothing if it could not be saved.

Handyman Service Van

from 65 USD/hr

A licensed handyman for the jobs that ladders and lost weekends were not solving — the storm door that will not latch, the shelf that needs studs, the faucet that drips through the night. Cole runs the vans, and they can usually come the same week.

What you get

  • A licensed, bonded and insured handyman at the door
  • Doors, shelves, fixtures, leaks and the small mounting jobs
  • Parts from our own shelves, charged at the shelf price
  • Honest hours billed — if it takes thirty minutes, you pay thirty

How it works

  1. 1Describe the job. Call the counter or send a photo of the trouble.
  2. 2We book a window. Usually same week, with an honest time estimate.
  3. 3Fixed and tidied. The van leaves it working, the offcuts taken away.

Screen & Window Repair

screens from 24 USD

Re-screening, glass cuts and sash-cord fixes, usually ready next day. Bring the frame or the measurements; we cut to fit so the porch is bug-free before the first warm evening, and the old double-hung that fought you all spring slides again.

What you get

  • Window and door screens re-meshed to fit the frame
  • Glass cut to size for cabinets, frames and shelves
  • Sash-cord and balance fixes so old windows slide again
  • Most jobs ready the next day, pet-proof mesh on request

How it works

  1. 1Bring it in. The frame, or just the height and width on a slip.
  2. 2We cut to fit. Mesh or glass measured against your numbers.
  3. 3Collect next day. Usually within a day, sooner in a pinch.

Grill & Heater Tune-Ups

tune-up 39 USD

Seasonal safety checks, igniter swaps and a propane exchange out back, so the first cookout of the year does not start with a dead burner. Maribel runs the seasonal bench; bring the grill or the patio heater and it leaves lighting first try.

What you get

  • Burner, hose and regulator safety check
  • Igniter and burner-tube swaps with parts in stock
  • Certified propane tank exchange out back, any time we are open
  • Patio-heater checks before the cold sets in

How it works

  1. 1Roll it in. Grill or heater, season opening or closing.
  2. 2Check and swap. Safety pass, igniter sorted, tank exchanged.
  3. 3Lights first try. We fire it before it goes home with you.

Local Delivery

flat 12 USD in town

Lumber, mulch and anything too heavy for the trunk, dropped at your curb the same day for one flat in-town fee. Order at the counter or over the phone; if the van is already on Maple Street, it is often there before you are home.

What you get

  • Lumber, mulch, bags and the heavy, awkward orders
  • One flat in-town fee, no per-mile surprises
  • Same-day curb drop on most in-town orders
  • Phone orders welcome — we will read you the total first

How it works

  1. 1Order it. At the counter or by phone, town address ready.
  2. 2One flat fee. Twelve dollars in town, told to you up front.
  3. 3At your curb. Same day on most orders, stacked where you want it.
