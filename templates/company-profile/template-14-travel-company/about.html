About Solana

A boutique travel studio that designs the trip around you.

Solana Voyages crafts boutique journeys and small-group tours built on first-hand knowledge and genuine local relationships — never a template. Here's who we are.

Our story

Journeys that fit one traveller at a time

Solana Voyages began in 2012 when its founder returned from a year of slow travel convinced that the package-tour industry had it backwards. It sold the same itinerary to everyone, optimised for the operator's logistics rather than the traveller's experience, and called the result a holiday. Solana was built to do the opposite: design each journey around the person taking it.

We are a boutique studio, not a booking engine. Every Solana trip is planned by a designer who has either walked the route themselves or works hand in hand with the local guides and hosts who live it. That first-hand knowledge is the difference between a guidebook list and a journey that knows which café is worth the detour and which 'must-see' to skip.

Our small-group tours stay small on purpose. A group of forty is a logistics problem; a group of eight is a shared adventure. Smaller groups mean access the big buses can't get, meals at tables that don't take coach parties, and the flexibility to follow a good day where it leads. We would rather run a trip that fills slowly than pack one that disappoints quickly.

Relationships are the quiet engine of good travel. The guesthouse owner who saves us the best room, the guide who has become a friend over a decade, the cook who opens her kitchen — these are not transactions but trust, built over years of returning. They are also why a Solana trip feels less like tourism and more like being shown around by someone who loves the place.

Over a decade on, Solana designs journeys across coastlines, mountains and cities for travellers who want the real version, not the brochure one. We are still boutique, still personal, and still planning each trip as if it were the only one that mattered — because to the person taking it, it is.

What we value

The principles behind the work

Design for one

No two travellers are the same, so no two trips should be. We build around you, not a brochure.

Go first, then recommend

We only send you where we or our guides have been. First-hand beats second-rate every time.

Keep groups small

Eight is an adventure; forty is a queue. Small groups mean access and the freedom to wander.

Relationships over transactions

A decade of returning earns the best room and the open kitchen. Trust is our supply chain.

Slow beats packed

We'd rather you remember one perfect afternoon than survive ten rushed ones.

Leave it better

We travel lightly and spend locally, so the places we love stay worth loving.
