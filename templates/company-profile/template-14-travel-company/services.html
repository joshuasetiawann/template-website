Trips & stories

Every journey, opened up before you book.

Nobody should wire a deposit on a paragraph. Below is each Solana Voyages service in plain words — what the journey includes, how the planning actually runs, and the price modelled honestly with fees in. Scroll on for three travellers who let us tell their trip with the numbers attached.

Services in depth

Six ways to travel, unpacked

The same styles from the homepage tabs, opened properly — every route scouted by our own team first, every guide one we have hugged.

Signature Group Journeys

Twelve travellers, one local guide and eight to twelve days of the parts you cannot Google. Departures are fixed-date and capped at the size of one long dinner table, so the group books the back-room restaurant and still hears the guide at the back of the bus.

What’s included

  • Groups capped at 12, with a single local guide throughout
  • Family-run stays scouted in person, no chain lobbies
  • All ground transport, park fees and most meals priced in
  • No single supplement, and rooms shared by request
  • 24/7 trip line answered by our own team mid-journey

How it runs

  1. 1Pick a date. Choose a scouted departure; we hold your place on a deposit.
  2. 2Pre-trip call. Fitness, food and pace, so nothing surprises you on day one.
  3. 3Travel. Your guide leads; the office stays on the line behind them.

From $3,180 per person · flights excluded, no single supplement

Festival Departures

The signature journeys timed to land on lantern festivals, harvests and the shoulder-season light photographers chase. We book the rooms and the table reservations a year out, because the good ones go the day the dates are announced.

What’s included

  • Dates locked to the festival, not the brochure calendar
  • Rooms with the view, reserved a year ahead
  • A guide who has worked the festival for years, not a brochure
  • Quiet vantage points away from the coach-tour crush

How it runs

  1. 1Register early. Festival rooms are the first to sell, so the list opens a year out.
  2. 2We hold the dates. Reservations and permits lined up before you pay in full.
  3. 3Arrive on the right night. No guessing the timetable; we have done it before.

From $3,640 per person · limited seats per festival

Tailor-Made Itineraries

Your dates, your pace, built across three drafts with a route designer who has walked the ground. We start from how you actually like to travel — slow mornings or first-light starts — and design the days around that, not around a template.

What’s included

  • A named route designer from first sketch to home again
  • Three drafts: the changes happen on paper, not on the road
  • Private guides and drivers, or self-drive where it is saner
  • One honest cost model — transfers, tips and fees all shown
  • Reschedule up to 21 days out with no change fee

How it runs

  1. 1Sketch call. Who is going, what “perfect” smells like, what to avoid.
  2. 2Draft and redraft. Three rounds until the days feel like yours.
  3. 3Go, fully held. Every booking confirmed; the trip line behind you throughout.

From $4,200 per trip designed · quoted before you commit

Honeymoons & Milestones

Celebrations engineered quietly: the upgrades, the surprises and the zero-logistics promise that the day belongs to you, not to a confirmation inbox. We brief every hotel and guide on what you are marking, so the right things happen without you arranging them.

What’s included

  • Room upgrades and arrival surprises arranged in advance
  • One private dinner staged somewhere you will not forget
  • Anniversary and milestone notes briefed to every host
  • A concierge contact who handles the changes you do not see

How it runs

  1. 1Tell us the occasion. What it is and how loud you want it to be.
  2. 2We brief the ground. Hotels and guides get the surprise list, not you.
  3. 3Just turn up. The moments land; the logistics never reach your table.

From $4,900 per couple · private dinner included

Trek & Trail

Hut-to-hut and coast-path walking with the luggage that meets you at the next bed, not on your back. Daily distances are listed honestly so you can pick the right grade, and a local walking guide knows the weather windows and the lunch stops.

What’s included

  • Bags transferred between huts and inns each day
  • Honest daily distances and ascent listed before you book
  • A local walking guide reading the weather windows
  • Half-day options built in for the legs that need a rest

How it runs

  1. 1Pick a grade. Match the daily distances to honest fitness, no bravado.
  2. 2We move the bags. You walk with a daypack; the luggage goes ahead.
  3. 3Walk it. Guide sets the pace, the weather plan stays one step ahead.

From $2,480 per person · bag transfers included

Sea & Paddle

Island-hopping by kayak and schooner with licensed skippers who know which cove is calm when the wind turns. Small craft, short crossings and nights ashore in fishing villages — the kind of water trip that does not need a cruise terminal.

What’s included

  • Licensed skippers and guides for every crossing
  • Sea kayaks, safety kit and a daily wind briefing
  • Nights ashore in fishing villages, not on a cruise deck
  • A weather-led route that swaps coves rather than cancels

How it runs

  1. 1Skill check. A short paddling-confidence note shapes the route grade.
  2. 2Daily briefing. The skipper reads the wind and picks the crossing.
  3. 3Hop the islands. Short legs, calm coves, a village bed each night.

From $2,950 per person · all craft and safety kit supplied

Traveller stories

Three journeys, told with the receipts

The same departures from the homepage board, retold with what changed for the people on them and the post-trip survey numbers left exactly as they came back.

Americas · Signature Group

Oaxaca: Markets & Mezcal — 9 days

Challenge. A group of nine, half of them nervous first-time Mexico travellers, wanted markets, mezcal and a village wedding feeling — without the food fear and the “is this a tourist trap” doubt that usually comes with it.

Result. Guide Mariana rerouted a whole day around a village wedding the group was invited to, hit three working markets before the crowds, and kept every meal on the right side of adventurous. Eight of the nine have since rebooked another Solana journey.

Europe · Trek & Trail

Dolomites Hut-to-Hut — 8 days

Challenge. Two couples of mixed fitness wanted the high huts and the long views, but one walker had a knee that ruled out the brochure’s biggest ascent days, and nobody wanted to carry a full pack.

Result. We graded the route to honest distances, swapped two punishing legs for a cable-car and a valley walk, and moved every bag hut to hut. The whole party finished on their feet, smiling, and the knee never became the story.

62 kmwalked across eight gentler days
4/4walkers completed every planned stage
100%of bags arrived ahead, every day
Asia · Tailor-Made

Vietnam North Loop — 12 days

Challenge. The Lindgren family of five, two of them teenagers, wanted the north loop without the bus-tour boredom — real homestays, a genuine local dinner, and enough flex to bail on a day if the weather turned.

Result. A tailor-made route with three homestays, one dinner cooked in a family kitchen, and built-in rest days that absorbed a typhoon afternoon without drama. For three days they were the only travellers they saw — and the teenagers asked to come back.

Before you book

Questions the map room hears most

Stays, ground transport, guides, park fees and most meals, with every exception marked in plain text in the brochure. Flights are the main thing we leave out — we will happily advise, but we do not mark them up.

Any signature route can run as a private departure on your own dates, often with the same guide. It costs a little more per head below eight travellers, and we quote it honestly before you decide.

If you can walk 12 km with a daypack and smile at the end, you are fit enough for the standard grade — and every itinerary lists honest daily distances and ascent so you can pick a gentler one.

Reschedule free up to 21 days out and cancel to a travel credit up to 7 days out. The full policy is one paragraph, not nine pages, and the trip line can talk you through it any time.
