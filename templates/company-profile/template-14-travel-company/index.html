About Solana

Founded on a missed bus in Oaxaca

In 2015 our founders missed the last bus to the coast and were driven four hours by a stranger named Beto, who narrated every hillside. The agency exists to bottle that ride.

Ten years on, 41 guides like Beto lead our journeys across 17 countries. Every route is walked by our own team first — if we would not send our mothers, we do not sell it.

  • Every itinerary scouted in person by our staff
  • Guides paid triple local average, tipped optional
  • 1% of every booking funds trail and reef care

FAQ

Before you pack

If you can walk 12 km with a daypack and smile at the end, you are fit enough; every itinerary lists honest daily distances.

A third of our travellers are solo; rooms can be shared by request and there is no single supplement on group departures.

All stays, ground transport, guides, park fees and most meals; the brochure marks every exception in plain text.

Reschedule free up to 21 days out; cancel to credit up to 7 days out. The full policy is one paragraph, not nine pages.
