Shipping & returns

How delivery works

Free refrigerated delivery on orders over £35 within our regional zones, in next-day slots you choose at checkout. Perishables aren’t returnable, but if anything arrives less than perfect we refund or replace it, no fuss. Cool-packs and crates are collected on your next delivery for reuse.

Good to know

Frequently asked

How fresh is the produce?

Most of it was in a field the day before it reaches you — we pack the morning of dispatch and deliver refrigerated.

Can I customise my harvest box?

Yes — swap items you don’t want and set dislikes in your account. Boxes flex with the week’s harvest.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Our regional zones around the South West; enter your postcode at checkout to see slots. We’re adding areas regularly.

What if something arrives damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours and we’ll refund or replace it on your next order — no need to send perishables back.
