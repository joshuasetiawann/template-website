Our story

How we got here

Greengate Grocer started as a single farm-stall in 2015, run by a grower and a chef who were appalled at how far a supermarket tomato had to travel to taste of nothing. We built a shop around the opposite idea: short distances, full ripeness, fair prices for the people who grow.

We buy direct from a network of farms within a day’s drive and we pay them properly. Every order is packed the morning it ships, so leaves are crisp and fruit is picked at its peak rather than picked early to survive a long haul. Seasonal isn’t a buzzword here — it’s the whole catalogue.

We fight food waste at both ends. “Wonky” produce gets sold at a discount instead of dumped, surplus goes to a local food bank, and our harvest boxes are built around whatever the farms have most of that week — so nothing rots in a field for being the wrong shape.

Refrigerated delivery, recyclable packaging, and a real person who knows where your eggs came from. Greengate is the farmers’ market, made as easy as a weekly shop.

Farm & freshness

On sourcing

Our produce comes from named regional farms practising low-spray and organic methods; dairy and eggs are from pasture-raised herds and flocks nearby. Everything ships refrigerated in recyclable, plastic-light packaging with returnable cool-packs. Surplus goes to a community food bank and food scraps to a composting partner — a closed loop from field to doorstep.
