Our story

How we got here

Apex Motion launched in 2018 from a strength-and-conditioning gym, founded by a coach and a textile engineer who were tired of “performance” kit that looked the part and quit by week six. We set out to build gear that survives the actual training, not the lookbook.

We test on athletes, under load. Every fabric is trialled in real sessions — squats, sprints, long runs, hot yoga — and judged on how it wicks, recovers and holds its shape after fifty washes. If it pills, sags or chafes, it doesn’t ship. Our fit is built on movement, not mannequins.

Durability is performance you can keep. We use four-needle flatlock seams that don’t rub, high-recovery knits that don’t bag at the knee, and reinforced stress points where gear usually fails first. Kit that lasts seasons means less landfill and more value per wear.

Recycled performance fabrics, a repair-and-replace ethos, and a team who actually train in what they sell. Apex Motion: honest gear for people who count reps, not likes.

Fabric & fit

On materials

We knit from recycled performance yarns engineered for moisture-wicking and four-way stretch, with high-recovery construction that resists bagging. Seams are four-needle flatlock to prevent chafe; stress points are bar-tacked. Everything is tested to fifty wash cycles before launch and ships in recycled, plastic-light packaging. Worn-out kit can come back for repair or textile recycling.
