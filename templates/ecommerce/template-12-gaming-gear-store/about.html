Our story

How we got here

HexRig was founded in 2019 by an esports coach and a hardware engineer who were sick of “gaming” gear that was all RGB and no response time. The first product, a 4kHz wireless mouse, was prototyped on a player’s desk during a bootcamp and tuned until the latency disappeared.

We engineer for competition, not vibes. Every peripheral is measured on real latency rigs — click latency, polling stability, sensor tracking — and the numbers are published. We co-develop with pro players who will tell us, bluntly, when something costs them a round.

Durability is part of performance. Our switches are rated for 80 million clicks, our cables are flex-tested to destruction, and our keycaps are doubleshot PBT that won’t shine away mid-season. Gear that fails in month three isn’t gear, it’s a liability.

We ship firmware that keeps improving — new polling modes, remappable everything, on-board profiles so your setup travels to any rig. Patch notes, not planned obsolescence. Built with players, for the win.

Build & spec

On the hardware

Shells are lightweight reinforced polymer; switches are optical or rated mechanical at 80M actuations; keycaps are doubleshot PBT; cables are flexible paracord with gold-plated contacts. Wireless models run a 4kHz low-latency link with swappable batteries. Packaging is recycled board, and dead units can be sent back for parts harvesting and recycling — performance without the e-waste.
