Our story

How we got here

Vetted & Co. launched in 2020 as the antidote to the endless, unfiltered marketplace. Founded by a retail buyer and a quality engineer, we built a platform where a human being approves every brand and inspects every category before a single product reaches the floor.

We curate ruthlessly. Brands apply to sell with us and most don’t make it — we check provenance, materials, ethics and after-sales support before approval, and we mystery-shop our own listings to keep them honest. The result is a floor where you can buy on trust, not on guesswork.

Quality is the product. Every item is checked against our standards for materials and construction, every seller commits to our returns and warranty terms, and our editors publish honest, independent picks — never paid placements. If something slips below standard, the brand comes off the floor.

Premium doesn’t have to mean opaque. We disclose our seller standards, protect every purchase with buyer guarantees, and put real specialists behind the support desk. Vetted & Co.: the considered alternative to buying blind.

Standards & trust

On our standards

Our standards cover provenance, materials, construction, ethics and after-sales support, and every seller signs up to them before listing. We mystery-shop our own floor, publish independent editor’s picks, and remove brands that slip below the bar. Purchases are protected by buyer guarantees and seller-committed returns. Curation, transparency and recourse — the things a marketplace should never have dropped.
