Our story

How we got here

Aurum & Crown was founded in 2012 in Bienne, at the heart of Swiss watchmaking, by a movement-finisher and a designer who wanted to make serious mechanical watches without the serious markup. The first series was 100 hand-numbered moonphases; it sold out by word of mouth.

We assemble in small series and we sign our work. Every watch is built around a Swiss automatic movement, regulated in-house to chronometer tolerances, and cased by hand with finishing — brushed flanks, polished bevels, applied indices — usually reserved for pieces several times the price.

Transparency is our quiet rebellion. We publish the movement, the case material, the water resistance and the real assembly origin, and we price openly so you pay for horology, not heritage marketing. A sapphire caseback means you can watch the rotor that you paid for.

We make to last generations and we service for life. Cases are built to be opened, gaskets to be renewed, movements to be regulated — so an Aurum & Crown bought today is still keeping time, and still being cared for, decades from now.

Case & movement

On the watch

Cases are machined from 316L surgical steel or solid bronze that develops its own patina; crystals are double-domed sapphire with anti-reflective coating; straps are vegetable-tanned leather or a fitted bracelet. Inside is a Swiss automatic, decorated and regulated by hand. Every watch ships in a reusable lacquered box with a full service manual — no foam, no waste.
