Shipping & returns

How delivery works

Free shipping on orders over £40, dispatched within two business days in recyclable, plastic-light packaging. Free 60-day returns — we know babies arrive on their own schedule and sizes change fast. Unwashed, unworn items with tags can be returned; for hygiene, opened care items are final sale. Gift receipts available.

Good to know

Frequently asked

How do I choose the right size?

Babies vary, so our charts go by weight and age with room to grow. Unsure? Email us and we’ll help, or size up — our 60-day returns have you covered.

Is the gear independently safety-tested?

Yes — every piece of gear is lab-tested to exceed stability, materials and small-parts standards. Certificates are summarised on each page.

Is the cotton really organic?

All our cotton is GOTS-certified organic, dyed with OEKO-TEX-approved dyes — gentle on the most sensitive newborn skin.

What’s in the Nest bundle?

The genuine first-month essentials — sleep, clothing, bath and changing basics — gathered so new parents don’t have to guess.
