Our story

How we got here

Little Lark was founded in 2017 by two parents who spent the newborn fog reading ingredient lists and safety labels at 3am, and decided baby shopping should never be that stressful. We built a shop around one promise: everything here is something we’d put on our own child.

We choose organic and we test everything twice. Our clothing is GOTS-certified organic cotton, soft enough for newborn skin and free from harsh dyes; our gear meets and exceeds the toughest safety standards, independently lab-tested for stability, materials and small parts. Nothing ships on a hunch.

Newborn life is overwhelming enough, so we design for tired hands. Poppers that line up, sizes that allow for growth, machine-washable everything, and a Nest bundle that gathers the genuine first-month essentials so new parents don’t have to guess. Practical kindness, basically.

OEKO-TEX dyes, recyclable packaging, hand-me-down-ready durability, and a team who remember those early weeks and answer like humans. Little Lark: gentle on baby, easy on parents, made to be passed to the next little one.

Fabric & safety

On materials

Clothing is GOTS-certified organic cotton coloured with OEKO-TEX-approved dyes, free from formaldehyde and harsh finishes. Gear is independently lab-tested against stability, small-parts and materials standards, and exceeds them. Everything ships in recyclable, plastic-light packaging. Pieces are built to survive many washes and many babies — gentle on skin, kind to the planet, easy on the next family.
