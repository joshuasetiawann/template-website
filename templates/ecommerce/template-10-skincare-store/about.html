Our story

How we got here

Eunoia Skin began in 2018 when a formulator and a dermatology nurse grew tired of ten-step routines that overwhelmed skin and underwhelmed results. We set out to make a small, focused range where every product earns its shelf space and every ingredient has a reason to be there.

We formulate minimally and we cite our sources. Each product lists its key actives and the concentration they’re used at — 10% niacinamide, 1% bakuchiol, 5% panthenol — because skincare is chemistry, and you deserve the numbers, not just the marketing adjectives.

Less is genuinely more for the skin barrier. Our range is deliberately short: a cleanser, a couple of serums, a moisturiser and an SPF that actually work together, fragrance-free and tested on sensitive and reactive skin. No filler, no fragrance theatre, no twelve-product funnel.

We are cruelty-free, dermatologically tested, and refill-friendly. Eunoia is the quiet routine that calms your skin and your bathroom shelf — proof that doing less, done well, is the most effective thing of all.

Inside the bottle

On ingredients

We build around well-studied actives — niacinamide, bakuchiol, panthenol, ceramides, zinc SPF — at concentrations the evidence supports, in fragrance-free bases buffered to skin-friendly pH. Bottles are recyclable PCR or glass with refill pouches on our heroes, and every batch is dermatologically and stability tested. Effective, transparent, and gentle on barrier and bin alike.
