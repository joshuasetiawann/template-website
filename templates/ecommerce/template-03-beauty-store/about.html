Our story

How we got here

Rosaline began in 2017 in a small lab in Grasse, the perfume capital of France, when a cosmetic chemist and a make-up artist decided to formulate the products they kept wishing existed: pigment-rich, skin-kind, and built on actives that earn their place.

We formulate clean, but we never use the word as a marketing flourish. Every Rosaline product is fragrance-allergen transparent, dermatologically tested, and free from the short list of ingredients our chemists won’t work with. What goes in is there to do a job — hyaluronic acid to hold water, squalane to soften, niacinamide to calm.

Colour and care are not separate departments here. Our tinted serums treat while they cover; our balms condition while they shine. We test every shade on a deliberately wide range of skin tones before it ships, because a product isn’t finished until it flatters more than one face.

We are cruelty-free, certified, and proudly small. Rosaline is still formulated in Grasse, still batch-tested by the same chemists, and still answered by people who can tell you exactly why a serum is the texture it is.

Inside the formula

On ingredients

We build around proven actives at meaningful concentrations — hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, squalane, vitamin C — and refuse a short list of ingredients we consider unnecessary. Pigments are skin-graded and PFAS-free; bottles are recyclable glass with refill pods on our hero serums. Every batch carries a code you can trace back to its formulation date.
