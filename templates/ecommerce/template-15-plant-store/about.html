Our story

How we got here

Fern & Loam grew out of a polytunnel on the edge of the city in 2017, started by a horticulturist and a designer who were tired of houseplants that arrived stressed, root-bound and doomed. We decided to grow our own, the patient way, and send them out genuinely ready for a new home.

We propagate and grow on-site, never drop-ship. Our plants are raised in peat-free, sustainable substrate, hardened off properly, and inspected leaf by leaf before they’re boxed. You get a plant that’s been cared for since it was a cutting, not one freighted halfway round the world the week before.

Packing a living thing is its own craft. Each plant travels in a custom, plastic-light box with its roots secured and its leaves cushioned, plus a care card written for that exact species. If it doesn’t survive its first 30 days despite your best efforts, we replace it — no debate.

Peat-free growing, recyclable packaging, and a care team who will happily diagnose a yellow leaf from a photo. Fern & Loam: a nursery that sends plants ready to thrive, not just to survive the journey.

Soil & care

On growing

We grow in peat-free, coir-and-bark substrate, feed with organic slow-release nutrients, and pot into recyclable or plant-up nursery pots. Plants are hardened off and inspected before dispatch, then boxed in recyclable, plastic-light packaging with a species-specific care card. Spent substrate is composted on-site — a nursery that gives back to the soil it grows in.
