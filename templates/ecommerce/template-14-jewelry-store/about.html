Our story

How we got here

Ondine & Or was founded in 2015 by a goldsmith and a gemmologist who believed beauty and ethics should never be a trade-off. Our first collection was a set of solitaire rings cast entirely in recycled gold, set with stones we could trace to the mine.

We work only in recycled precious metals and responsibly-sourced stones. Every diamond is conflict-free with documented origin; coloured stones come from mines with fair-labour certification; and our gold is 100% recycled, refined without a single ounce of newly-mined material. Provenance is part of the piece.

Each item is made by hand in our atelier and fully hallmarked. We cast, set and finish in-house, which means we can offer bespoke commissions, resize for life, and stand behind every claw and clasp. A piece of Ondine & Or is meant to be worn daily and handed down eventually.

We price transparently and we keep our runs small, because fine jewellery should be an heirloom, not an impulse. From engagement to anniversary, we make the quiet, lasting pieces you reach for every single day.

Metal & stone

On materials

We cast in 100% recycled 18ct gold and platinum, set with conflict-free, origin-documented diamonds and ethically-mined coloured stones. Every piece is hand-finished and hallmarked at assay, and arrives in a reusable lacquered box with its gemmological details. Off-cuts and polishing dust are reclaimed and refined back into new pieces — a closed loop, by design.
