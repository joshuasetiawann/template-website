Our story

How we got here

Wag & Whisker started in 2016 when a vet nurse and a dog-obsessed designer realised the pet aisle was full of clever packaging and questionable ingredients. We built a shop around the opposite priority: what’s actually good for the animal comes first, always.

Our food is vet-formulated and our toys are tested to within an inch of their squeak. Recipes are built with a veterinary nutritionist, named-protein first with no nasty fillers; toys and gear are non-toxic, choke-checked, and chew-tested by a panel of enthusiastic, well-treated office pets.

We back responsible pet ownership, not just spending. Every order includes care guidance, our welcome kit walks new owners through those first overwhelming weeks, and a slice of every sale goes to local shelters and rescue partners. Happier pets, supported owners, a few more adoptions.

Recyclable packaging, honest ingredient lists, and a team who will happily talk you through your nervous rescue’s first month. Wag & Whisker: the pet shop that puts the whisker before the markup.

Food & play

On the goods

Recipes use named, traceable proteins and whole ingredients, formulated with a veterinary nutritionist and free from artificial colours and cheap fillers. Toys are made from non-toxic, pet-safe materials and safety-tested for chew and choke hazards. Packaging is recyclable and plastic-light, and treat bags are resealable to keep things fresh — good for the pet, gentle on the planet.
