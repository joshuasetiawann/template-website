Our story

How we got here

Hearth & Hue began in 2016 as a small gallery-shop, founded by an interior stylist and a ceramicist who believed a home should be filled slowly, with objects that mean something, rather than flat-packed in a single weekend. We curate the way we’d furnish our own homes.

We work with makers, not factories. Most of what we sell is produced in small workshops by named artisans — hand-thrown ceramics, mouth-blown glass, hand-loomed textiles and turned wood. We visit the studios, we know the people, and we tell their stories on every product page.

Natural materials age beautifully, so that’s what we choose: stoneware, linen, solid wood, wool and stone, finished without plastic shortcuts. These are pieces designed to patina rather than wear out — a vase that looks better in ten years, a throw that softens with every wash.

Considered curation, honest provenance, and packaging that protects without plastic. Hearth & Hue: a shop for filling a home with intention, one well-made object at a time.

Material & make

On materials

We favour hand-thrown stoneware, mouth-blown glass, hand-loomed linen and wool, and solid wood finished in natural oil — materials that develop character with use. Pieces are made in small batches by studios we partner with directly, and ship in recyclable, plastic-free packaging cushioned with moulded pulp. Seconds and samples are sold off rather than binned — beautiful things deserve a home.
