Shipping & returns

How delivery works

Free UK shipping over £30; roasted to order and dispatched within 48 hours with a roast date on every bag. Subscriptions skip, pause or swap any time. Beans are perishable so we don’t take returns on opened bags — but if a coffee disappoints, tell us and we’ll make it right.

Good to know

Frequently asked

How fresh will my coffee be?

We roast to order and ship within 48 hours, so it reaches you days off the drum. The roast date is printed on every bag.

Whole bean or ground?

Both — choose your grind at checkout, from espresso to French press. Whole bean keeps best if you have a grinder.

How does the subscription work?

Pick a coffee and a cadence; skip, pause or swap any time from your account. Subscribers get first access to reserves.

Do you supply cafes and offices?

Yes — email the roastery for wholesale pricing and we’ll dial in a profile for your setup.
