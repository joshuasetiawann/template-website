Our story

How we got here

Kiln & Crema fired up its first 12-kilo drum roaster in a railway arch in 2018, founded by a barista and a green-buyer who believed great coffee was being ruined by two things: roasting too dark and shipping too late. We set out to fix both.

We buy at origin and we name our farmers. Every bag tells you the producer, the altitude, the varietal and the price we paid — a price that sits well above the commodity market, because a coffee this good starts with a grower who can afford to grow it well.

We roast to the bean, not to a house style. Lighter for the florals of a washed Ethiopian, a touch deeper for the cocoa of a Brazilian natural, always developed enough to be sweet and never so far that the origin disappears. Each batch is cupped before it leaves the arch.

Freshness is the whole point, so we roast to order and ship within 48 hours, with a roast date — not a use-by date — stamped on every bag. The coffee that reaches you is days, not months, off the drum.

Bean & roast

On the coffee

We buy specialty-grade green from washing stations and single estates we’ve visited, paying a transparent farm-gate price. Roasting happens in a 12-kilo drum, profiled per lot and logged batch by batch. Bags are recyclable, valve-fitted to protect freshness, and our subscription resealable pouches are home-compostable. Spent grounds go to a local mushroom grower.
