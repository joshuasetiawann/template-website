Our story

How we got here

Nebula Vault launched in 2020 to fix two things that drive digital creators mad: confusing licenses and disappearing files. We built a marketplace where the terms are plain, the downloads are permanent, and the makers actually get paid the lion’s share.

We curate, we don’t flood. Every asset in the Vault is reviewed by a real designer for quality, originality and file hygiene before it goes live — properly organised layers, named styles, working variable fonts. No dumped exports, no AI-scraped knock-offs, no broken zip files.

Licensing is radically simple: one clear, royalty-free license per product, covering commercial use, written in language a human can read. Buy once, use forever, on as many projects as you like. Updates to a product land in your library automatically and free.

Creators keep the majority of every sale, and your purchases live in a permanent library you can re-download any time, on any machine. Built by makers, for makers — a vault, not a landfill.

Files & formats

On the assets

Products ship as clean, well-organised source files — layered design files, variable fonts with full character sets, documented UI kits and tested presets — in the formats the tools expect. Everything is delivered as instant download, version-tracked, and malware-scanned. Nothing physical ships, nothing expires, and your license terms travel with every file in plain text.
