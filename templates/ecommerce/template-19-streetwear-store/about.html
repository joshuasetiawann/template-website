About Static Youth

Streetwear with something to say

Static Youth is an independent streetwear label built on limited drops, heavyweight blanks, and graphics that mean something — made ethically, worn loudly.

Our story

How we got here

Static Youth started in 2019 screen-printing tees in a bedroom, founded by a graphic artist and a skater who wanted to make the kind of streetwear they couldn’t find: heavyweight, ethically made, and actually saying something instead of just borrowing a logo.

We design in-house and we drop in limited runs. Every graphic is original artwork — no clip-art, no recycled hype — printed on heavyweight, garment-dyed blanks that feel like something. Drops are intentionally small so the work stays special and the racks don’t end up in a landfill outlet.

Ethics aren’t an afterthought on the label. Our blanks are organic cotton from audited, fair-wage factories, printed with water-based, plastisol-free inks. We’d rather make less and make it right than chase fast-fashion margins on the backs of people we’ll never meet.

Community is the whole point. We collaborate with local artists, drop with the people who actually wear it, and keep prices honest by selling direct. Static Youth: loud graphics, heavyweight cotton, zero compromise on how it’s made.

Blanks & print

On materials

We print on heavyweight (240gsm+) organic cotton blanks, garment-dyed for depth and sourced from audited, fair-wage factories. Inks are water-based and plastisol-free, so prints stay soft and the wastewater stays clean. Drops are small-batch, packed plastic-free in recycled mailers, and offcuts and misprints are upcycled into patches and one-offs rather than dumped.
