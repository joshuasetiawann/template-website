About The Marginalia

A bookshop that reads before it sells

The Marginalia is an independent bookseller built on real recommendations — every title on the shelf has been read, argued over, and chosen by a person, not an algorithm.

Our story

How we got here

The Marginalia opened in 2013 in a narrow shopfront with a ladder and far too many stacks, founded by two booksellers who believed the best recommendation engine ever built is a well-read human being who asks what you loved last.

We are fiercely independent and proudly opinionated. We don’t pay-to-place bestsellers at the front; we hand-sell the books we believe in, write the shelf-talkers ourselves, and will happily talk you out of a title that isn’t right for you. Our Book of the Month is chosen by staff vote, not by a publisher’s cheque.

We champion small presses, translated literature, debut voices and the backlist gems that the chains let slip out of print. A third of our shelves are books you won’t find stacked by the supermarket till — and those are usually the ones you’ll still be thinking about a year later.

Books are made to be passed on, so we run a thriving second-hand section, host readings in the back room, and pack every order in recycled board with a hand-written note. Slow retail for people who still dog-ear the good bits.

Shelves & ethos

On the books

We stock new and carefully-chosen second-hand titles, weighted toward independent publishers and literature in translation. Orders ship in 100% recycled, plastic-free board with compostable wrap and a hand-written card. Unsold stock goes to local schools and libraries rather than being pulped — a book’s life doesn’t end on our shelf.
