Our story

How we got here

Nordvolt started in a converted boat workshop on the Oslo fjord in 2016, when two acoustic engineers got tired of headphones that sounded brilliant in a demo and fell apart in a backpack. The first prototype was a single pair of over-ears, milled from a block of aluminium because the plastic ones kept cracking in the cold.

We are still an engineering company first. Every product begins as a spec sheet and a thermal model, not a colourway. We publish real measurements — frequency response, battery capacity in mAh, charge rates in watts — because a number you can verify is worth more than an adjective you can’t.

Our lab in Oslo runs a punishing test rig: 10,000 hinge cycles, a salt-fog chamber, drop tests onto oak, and a freezer that holds at minus twenty for a week. A product does not ship until it survives all of it. That is why we can offer a three-year warranty on hardware most brands cover for one.

We sell direct so the engineering, not the retail markup, is what you pay for. No mystery middlemen, no annual cosmetic refresh — just gear we update when the silicon genuinely gets better, and support that speaks fluent firmware.

How we build

Four engineering principles

01

Measured, not marketed

Published specs you can verify — response curves, real mAh, true charge rates. No invented units.

02

Built to outlast

Aluminium housings, replaceable batteries and a three-year hardware warranty as standard.

03

Firmware for life

Devices get features and security patches for years, not until the next model ships.

04

Repair over replace

Modular internals and a parts store so a dead cell never means a dead device.

Materials & lab

On materials

We machine housings from recycled 6063 aluminium, use cobalt-reduced cells from audited suppliers, and braid cables in recycled nylon rated for 30,000 bend cycles. Every device is designed for disassembly — standard screws, no glued batteries — and ships in moulded-pulp packaging with zero single-use plastic. Retired units can be sent back for credit and responsible recycling.
