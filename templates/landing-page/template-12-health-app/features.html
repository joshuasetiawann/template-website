Features designed to be kind to you

Wellfern is a calm daily check-in for how you actually feel. It notices patterns, suggests small resets and never shames you. Here is how each piece works.

Feature breakdown

Six gentle tools for everyday wellbeing

Not a clinical dashboard — small, kind nudges that fit a real life.

Daily check-ins

A thirty-second check-in for mood, energy and sleep, with a tap not a form. Wellfern asks once, gently, and builds a picture over time instead of demanding constant logging.

  • Thirty-second tap check-in
  • Mood, energy, sleep
  • One gentle prompt a day
72%

Patterns, not pressure

See how your week really went and what tends to lift or drain you — caffeine, late nights, a long walk — shown as kind insights, never red-alert health scores.

  • Weekly mood trends
  • What lifts and drains you
  • No alarming scores

Guided resets

On a hard day, a two-minute breathing or grounding reset is one tap away, chosen to match how you said you feel. Small tools for the moments that need them.

  • Two-minute resets
  • Matched to your mood
  • Breathing & grounding

Gentle goals

Set intentions like 'walk after lunch' or 'lights out by eleven' and track them softly, with streaks that forgive a missed day. Progress that encourages, never scolds.

  • Soft intention tracking
  • Forgiving streaks
  • Encouraging nudges

Kind reminders

Reminders that learn your day and stay quiet when you are busy or asleep. Wellfern nudges at the right moment and never piles on guilt for a missed check-in.

  • Smart timing
  • Quiet hours
  • No guilt for misses

Private by design

Your wellbeing data is end-to-end encrypted and never sold. Wellfern is a private journal that happens to notice patterns — not a product mining your moods.

  • End-to-end encrypted
  • Never sold or shared
  • Export or delete anytime
100%
