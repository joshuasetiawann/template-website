Feature breakdown

Six tools for a community that lasts

Not another noisy server — a calm, owned space your members actually return to.

Spaces & threads

Organize your community into spaces with slow, readable threads instead of a firehose chat. Conversations stay findable and welcoming, so new members are not lost on day one.

  • Topic-based spaces
  • Readable threads, not chaos
  • Searchable forever

Live events

Host calls, AMAs and workshops right inside Glowroom, with RSVPs, reminders and replays. The moments that turn followers into a community happen in one place, not five tools.

  • In-app calls & AMAs
  • RSVPs and reminders
  • Automatic replays

Member profiles

Rich profiles let members introduce themselves, find each other and build real connections, so the value comes from the people, not just the host. Belonging starts with being seen.

  • Rich introductions
  • Member directory
  • Connections, not followers

Moderation tools

Keep it kind with role permissions, gentle automod, reporting and a clear code of conduct enforced consistently. The difference between a community and a comment section.

  • Roles & permissions
  • Gentle automod
  • Reports handled fairly

Memberships

Charge for access, tiers or events and keep the vast majority of the revenue, with one clean checkout. Turn a community into something that funds the work behind it.

  • Paid access & tiers
  • Low platform fee
  • One simple checkout

Calm notifications

Members choose a weekly digest or real-time, so Glowroom never becomes the app that burns them out. Engagement that respects attention keeps people for years.

  • Digest or real-time
  • Per-space controls
  • No guilt-tripping
