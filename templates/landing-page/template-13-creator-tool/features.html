Feature breakdown

Six tools that replace your editing weekend

From a two-hour podcast to ten posted clips, without opening a timeline.

Auto clip-finding

Drop in a long video and Brightreel finds the hooks, punchlines and complete thoughts most likely to perform — then hands you a ranked shelf of clips to pick from in minutes.

  • Finds the best moments
  • Ranked by hook strength
  • Complete thoughts, not cut-offs

Animated captions

Word-by-word captions in your style, auto-timed and 99% accurate, with emphasis on the words that land. The single biggest lift to watch-time, done in one click.

  • Word-by-word timing
  • Your fonts and colors
  • Emphasis on key words

One-tap reframing

Reframe any clip to vertical, square or wide and Brightreel keeps the speaker centered automatically, tracking faces as they move. No keyframing, no jump cuts.

  • 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9
  • Auto face tracking
  • No manual keyframes

Brand kits

Save fonts, colors, intros, outros and lower-thirds once, then apply them to every clip with a tap. Your reel looks like you across hundreds of posts, effortlessly.

  • Saved fonts and colors
  • Reusable intros & outros
  • Consistent everywhere

Schedule & post

Queue finished clips straight to your channels on a posting schedule, with captions and hashtags ready. The whole pipeline, from upload to posted, lives in one tab.

  • Multi-channel scheduling
  • Captions & hashtags ready
  • Best-time suggestions
