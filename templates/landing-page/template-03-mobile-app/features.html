Feature breakdown

One app for mornings, focus and nights

Six features that root for you instead of scolding you.

Ritual stacking

Anchor habits to a time or to each other — after coffee, stretch; after stretch, journal. Daybloom shows one card at a time so mornings start themselves instead of overwhelming you.

  • Time and habit anchors
  • One-card-at-a-time mornings
  • 60+ science-backed rituals

Grace days

Twice a month a missed day is automatically forgiven and your streak continues. Research shows one lapse does not break a habit — so neither do our streaks.

  • Two grace days a month
  • Soft streaks, never zeroed
  • No red badges, ever

Focus sessions

Start a 25 or 50 minute bloom session and watch distractions get politely batched until you are done. Deep-work hours earn streaks too, not just tasks.

  • 25 / 50 minute timers
  • Notification batching
  • Streaks for focus hours

Evening wind-downs

A gentle two-minute flow dims the app, queues tomorrow from today and closes your day. A sleep-friendly night palette keeps the screen calm.

  • Two-minute shutdown
  • Tomorrow auto-planned
  • Sleep-friendly palette
