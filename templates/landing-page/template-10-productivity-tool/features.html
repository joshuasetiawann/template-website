Timeline planning

Drag tasks across a timeline to plan a quarter at a glance, with dependencies that nudge dates when reality shifts. Planning becomes a five-minute habit, not a spreadsheet ordeal.

Recurring tasks

Set work to repeat daily, weekly or on a custom cadence and let Plannery regenerate it automatically. Standups, reports and chores stop slipping through the cracks.

  • Any recurrence rule

No-code automations

When a card moves to Done, notify the channel, set the date, assign the next owner — built from plain if-this-then-that rules anyone can write, no admin required.
