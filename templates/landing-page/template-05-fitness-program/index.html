The program

What is inside the forge

Six pillars. No fluff, no 'shred in 7 days', no 200-exercise confusion.

Progressive overload engine

Loads auto-adjust weekly from your logged sets, so every session is hard enough — and never reckless.

Coach form checks

Weekly video reviews on the big four lifts with timestamped corrections from certified coaches.

4-day split that fits life

Two strength days, one power day, one engine day. Sessions cap at 60 minutes, warm-up included.

Fuel, simplified

A one-page nutrition protocol with protein targets and three plate templates — no weighing rice at midnight.

Tested, not guessed

Weeks 1, 6 and 12 are testing weeks. Watch your estimated one-rep maxes climb on real numbers.
