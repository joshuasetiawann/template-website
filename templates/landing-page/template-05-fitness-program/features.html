The program

Six pillars, zero fluff

No 'shred in 7 days', no 200-exercise confusion — just the engineering of getting strong.

Progressive overload engine

Loads auto-adjust weekly from your logged sets, so every session is hard enough and never reckless. The calendar does the deciding — you just show up and lift.

  • Auto-progressing loads
  • Built from your week-1 baseline
  • Auto-deload when needed

Coach form checks

Upload one lift video a week and a certified coach returns timestamped corrections within 24 hours. This is the part no app or PDF can fake — and the part that keeps you safe.

  • Weekly video reviews
  • 24-hour turnaround
  • Big-four lift focus

A 4-day split that fits life

Two strength days, one power day, one engine day. Sessions cap at 60 minutes including warm-up, so training survives early shifts and busy weeks.

  • 60-minute session cap
  • Home or gym track
  • Auto-reflow on a missed day

Fuel, simplified

A one-page nutrition protocol with a protein target and three plate templates. No banned foods, no weighing rice at midnight, no separate tracking app required.

  • One protein target
  • Three plate templates
  • Weekly check-in

Tested, not guessed

Weeks 1, 6 and 12 are testing weeks, so you watch your estimated one-rep maxes climb on real numbers instead of vibes. Proof beats hype, every cycle.

  • Three testing weeks
  • Estimated 1RM tracking
  • Real PR history kept forever

The cohort floor

Train alongside your start-week cohort, swap PRs and keep each other honest until graduation. Accountability without a 6 AM bootcamp shouting at you.

  • Start-week cohort
  • PR swaps and check-ins
  • Graduate paths after week 12
