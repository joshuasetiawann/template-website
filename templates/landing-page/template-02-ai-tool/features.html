Instant cited answers

Ask in plain language and get a direct response with the exact passages it came from, every time. When sources conflict, Mindgrid shows both and says so instead of guessing.

  • Linked source passages
  • Conflict detection
  • Confidence signals

Auto-drafts

Turn bullet points into briefs, release notes or replies that match your team's saved tone of voice. Drafts cite the internal docs they drew from so reviewers can verify fast.

  • Briefs, notes and replies

Meeting recall

Drop in a transcript and query it like a database: decisions, owners and deadlines on demand, with timestamps. Never relitigate a meeting you half-remember again.

  • Decision & owner extraction
  • Timestamped quotes
  • Searchable across meetings

Connected sources

Wikis, tickets, PDFs and shared drives sync on a schedule and stay searchable in one grid. Stale or conflicting pages get flagged before they mislead anyone.
