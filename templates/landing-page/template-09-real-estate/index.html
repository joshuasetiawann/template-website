Philosophy

Most listings are advertised. Very few are understood.

The conventional market runs on volume: photograph wide, list loud, hope fast. A home becomes twelve angles and a floor plan, and its actual life — morning light, the neighbor's piano, the way winter behaves in the garden — never makes the brochure.

Buyers feel this. It is why viewings so often end in polite silence: the listing promised a photograph, and a photograph is not a place.

⁂

We represent twelve homes at a time. On purpose.

Every Aurelia residence is walked at three different hours of the day, checked against our seventeen-point standard, and documented honestly — flaws included. We decline more homes than we accept, which is precisely why our clients tour so few before finding theirs.

When you enquire, you speak with the partner who has stood in the rooms. Tours are private, unhurried and scheduled around light, not lunch breaks.

The standard

Seventeen checks. Here are seven

What every residence passes before it joins the portfolio.

01

Light, three times a day

We walk every home at morning, mid-afternoon and dusk. Photographs are taken at its honest best, not its staged one.

02

The five-minute life

Coffee, green space, school, transit, quiet — we map what your actual week looks like from the front door.

03

Bones before beauty

An independent structural read on every listing, summarized in plain language before your first visit.

04

What you will hear

Traffic at rush hour, the bar on Fridays, the morning train. Measured, noted, disclosed.

05

Provenance + paperwork

Title, permits, renovations and warranties assembled into one clean dossier before offers open.

06

The honest flaw list

Every dossier names the three things we would change. Buyers tell us this page is why they trust the rest.

07

Off-market first

Roughly half our placements never list publicly. Registered clients see them first, quietly.
