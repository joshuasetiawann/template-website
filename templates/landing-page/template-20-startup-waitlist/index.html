Manifesto

Running a startup now means babysitting eleven dashboards.

Cash lives in the bank tab. Burn lives in the spreadsheet. Metrics live in three analytics tools that disagree politely. Commitments live in investor email threads, and decisions live nowhere at all — which is why you re-litigate them every six weeks.

Founders do not lack information. They lack a meridian: a single line through the noise that says where the company actually stands today, and which of the eleven fires is real.

⁂

We are building the line through the noise.

Meridian connects your bank, billing, analytics and docs, then renders one screen: runway with honest assumptions, the three metrics that matter this quarter, every promise you have made, and a decision log your future self will thank you for.

It is opinionated. It will not let you track forty KPIs, because nobody can steer by forty stars. It took us two years to make one screen this quiet — and that is exactly why there is a line at the door.
