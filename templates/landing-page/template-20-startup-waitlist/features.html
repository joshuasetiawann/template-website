What's inside

Six things Meridian gets right

A small set of sharp tools, not a kitchen sink you have to configure.

One focused today

Meridian shows you a single, honest 'today' — the few things that matter, not an infinite backlog. It is built to reduce decisions, so you spend energy doing instead of organizing.

  • A single today view
  • No infinite backlog
  • Fewer decisions, more doing

Calm planning

Plan a week in a couple of minutes with gentle structure — no Gantt charts, no ceremony. Meridian nudges what is realistic and quietly reflows when the week shifts.

  • Two-minute weekly plan
  • Realistic, not aspirational
  • Auto-reflow

Deep-work sessions

Start a focus session and Meridian dims the noise, batches notifications and protects the block. The point of the tool is the time you spend not looking at it.

  • Protected focus blocks
  • Notification batching
  • Time tracked gently
70%

Notes that connect

Capture quickly and link ideas without a folder system to maintain. Your notes become a small, navigable web that surfaces the right thing when you need it.

  • Quick capture
  • Linked, not foldered
  • Surfaces relevant notes

Small-team mode

Share a project with a few people and keep the same calm surface — no admin console, no permission matrix. Built for tiny teams, not enterprise org charts.

  • Share a project
  • Same calm surface
  • No admin overhead

Yours, private

Your data is encrypted, exportable and never sold. Meridian is a tool you pay for, not a product that monetizes your attention or your work.

  • Encrypted & exportable
  • Never sold
  • You pay, so you're the customer
100%
