The method

Six things that make learning stick

Not video lectures you abandon — structure, mentors and real projects.

Project-based curriculum

You learn by building real things — a portfolio piece every module — because skills stick when you use them, not when you watch them. Theory shows up exactly when a project needs it.

  • A portfolio piece per module
  • Skills used, not just watched
  • Theory on demand

Mentor-led cohorts

Small cohorts move together with a working-professional mentor who reviews your work and unblocks you weekly. The accountability that keeps people from quitting in week three.

  • Small, supportive cohorts
  • Working-pro mentors
  • Weekly work reviews

Career coaching

Resume, portfolio and interview prep run alongside the curriculum, with mock interviews and a coach who has placed hundreds. You graduate ready to apply, not wondering how.

  • Portfolio & resume reviews
  • Mock interviews
  • Placement-experienced coaches

Progress that's visible

A clear path shows exactly where you are, what is next and how your skills are growing — so motivation comes from momentum, not willpower. No more wondering if it is working.

  • A clear learning path
  • Skill-growth tracking
  • Momentum, not willpower
68%

Verified credentials

Finish with a credential employers can verify and a portfolio that proves you can do the work. A certificate that means something because the work behind it is real.

  • Employer-verifiable
  • Backed by real projects
  • Shareable proof of skill

Real support

Stuck at 11 p.m.? Mentors, a community and human support are there, because dropping out is usually about feeling alone, not about the material. Nobody learns in a vacuum here.

  • Community + mentors
  • Human support
  • Catch-up help built in
